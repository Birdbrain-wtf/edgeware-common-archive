There has been consistent community interest in finding additional venues for liquidity for Edgeware. This proposal follows the same process and form of [Exchange A](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/827-proposal-top-15-exchange-by-cmc-integration-exchange-a). For this proposal, a **top 5 exchange** by normalized volume by CMC has approached us about advancing a community proposal for exchange listing. Similarly, this exchange has requested confidentiality on matters relating to this proposal.

**Part 1: Deposit and Marketing**

The community has been able to negotiate away a listing fee, however, the exchange has asked for an amount of EDG to be used for marketing and a refundable deposit (that may be “slashed” if certain liquidity constraints are not met).

Similar to the Exchange A proposal, while the deposit is relatively straightforward, we’ve been asked to keep the exact amount confidential. Naturally, this makes a community vote difficult. To balance the dual need for confidentiality and transparency, a treasury proposal will ask for a “buffer” amount of EDG that is between 1.2x - 1.7x the deposit and marketing campaign.

While the marketing campaign component of this is directly in EDG, which the exchange will use at it’s discretion for user onboarding. The deposit requires a USD-equivalent stablecoin to be sent to the exchange. Similar to last time, an EDG-to-Stablecoin transfer will be made to fit the requirements of the exchange.

Also similar to the previous proposal. A 2-of-3 multsig will be created to hold funds before going to an exchange. This should allow the community to monitor any transaction between the multisig and the exchange. After one year, if there is otherwise no 'slashing' action, 50% of the deposit will be returned, and after another year the remainder will be returned.

**Part 2: Additional Market Maker Liquidity + Using Existing Balance in Multisig for Additional Monthly Fee**

For an additional exchange, Efficient Frontiers charges a total of $5k for two exchanges (so this would be on top of the $2k/month currently allocated). Since the previous proposal included funds to pay for a year of market-making for one exchange, I think it's reasonable to use those for this exchange, effectively paying for market making _for half a year_ on two exchanges rather than one exchange for two years. After this, a community vote can take place to decide if this is worth continuing to furnish.

However, it would be useful to allocate some additional EDG to provide liquidity on this exchange, something like $150k in EDG. We will plan to include this amount in the proposal.
