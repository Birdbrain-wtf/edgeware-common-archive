There has been consistent community interest in finding additional venues for liquidity for Edgeware. Exchange C, a top 15 exchange by normalized volume by Coingecko has approached us about advancing a community proposal for exchange listing.

Like previous proposals for [Exchange A](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/827-proposal-top-15-exchange-by-cmc-integration-exchange-a) and [Exchange B](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1004-proposal-exchange-b-edg), There are two components to this proposal:

## Proposal Pt 1.
**Exchange Integration and Marketing Cost**

The exchange has asked for an amount of EDG that covers:

1. Integration Fee (half in EDG and half in a stablecoin)
2. Marketing Campaign funds, to be distributed in a marketing campaign


Like previous proposals, while the fee is relatively straightforward, we’ve been asked to keep the exact amount confidential. Naturally, this makes a community vote difficult. To balance the dual need for confidentiality and transparency, a treasury proposal will ask for a “buffer” amount of EDG that is between **1.2x - 1.7x** the integration and marketing campaign.

This buffer amount will be allocated to 2-of-3 multsig, where only one member of the multisig is on the Commonwealth team. This should allow for a third party to monitor any transaction between the multisig and the exchange.

From here, the full amount will be deposited into the exchange. While the integration and marketing will be allocated to the exchange, the remainder of the buffer could be used for market making. After a set amount of time of at least 6 months, the buffer amount would be able to be returned to the Edgeware Treasury.

## Proposal Pt. 2
**Market Making Deposit **

Separately, to help foster a healthy market on this exchange and in accordance with generally accepted best practices, Commonwealth Labs has engaged with several professional market makers around ensuring a tight bid-ask spread.

For this exchange, Efficient Frontiers is charging $1.5k/month. Because this deposit may be used for market making, it's not necessary to fund this proposal with additional liquidity, as we have done previously for other Exchange proposals. This proposal, like Exchange B, will cover 6 months of market making. 

If this market-making deposit proposal passes, half of the funds would be exchanged to a stablecoin and half kept in EDG and both would then transferred to the exchange.

**Next steps**

We invite community comments for the next five days. Afterward, we'll create two proposals, one to fund the Exchange C Multisig, while I will establish, to complete this listing, and another to top up the multisig titled 'Exchange A Market Maker Multisig' to complete the second half of this proposal. Any OTC deal would be performed at the 7-day TWAP average price at the time that the proposal is fully funded.

**If passed**

This exchange is quoting a week-turn arouund time **at best**, with 4 weeks being the longest estimate.
We look forward to your comments and input!

Proposal  Amounts  (with the above mentioned buffer) and Destination address are currently being developed for public sharing and will also be on the final, onchain proposal (obviously we cant write blank checks through gov.)
