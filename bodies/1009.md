1. **A bit of Background Research**


Most of the stuff we watched/listened to fell into one of the following categories:

- Crypto influencers 
- Focus on price speculation 
- News report style delivery 
- Recorded seminars / long panel discussions
- Long episodes / not always easily digestible 


With more and more people discovering and learning about blockchain we want to make sure the Edgeware content is right for the community and also accessible and interesting for those seeking to learn more and explore a whole new world. 
 
Channels such as The Defiant and Chico Crypto are good examples of this. They are informative but are also decent entertainment. A lot of content - whilst interesting is not a great viewing experience. There are so many interesting stories within the Blockchain space but there is a lack of high quality media that brings a unique personality and emotional tone that resonates with a wider audience- regardless of interest, and there are definitely a lack of diverse voices!!!!

We believe there is an opportunity for Edgeware to create several content series which really stand out from all the existing content and engage people.

Our expertise is creating engaging and visually imaginative films. ([view our showreel](https://vimeo.com/240492037))
Our background in music videos and documentary films for TV/brands/bands etc. means we can create something really unique and exciting for the Blockchain space. 


**2. Series 1 Animated Explainers**

What it says on the tin. 

We want to create a resource of sharable vids which use simple, dope animations to explain key elements of the Edgeware eco-system and wider Blockchain terms too.

These would be a resource for Edgeware community members but also for potential new members and the wider DeFi/Blockchain space.

We love to create simple, yet stunning animations ([REF](https://vimeo.com/178449529)) and have a range of styles ([GFX REEL](https://vimeo.com/333957571)) and will work with the new Edgeware Brand Identity to create something compelling and shareable.
