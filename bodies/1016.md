Given our roadmap, it would be prudent to prioritize unique runtime improvements not only to our assets pallets, but also the Treasury.

Going to list my items here, please reply to this thread to discuss or add items.

**Treasury Explorer**
An explorer to better view data, history and current actions:, eg. [https://www.dotreasury.com/](https://www.dotreasury.com/) Currently, we have no way of looking at an account and detecting a treasury disbursement.

**Treasury Subaccounts**
Assists in budgeting, guarentees available funds for future use to certain props and sets of milestones.

**Treasury as a Service **
This is a new category and is still under development.  It might involve programmatic rules for asset management, automated agrements with the treasury like Flash Loan servicing, token-swaps for funding smaller projects, working group awards for reaching activity or member levels, or ways for stablecoins to obtain sufficient collateral.
