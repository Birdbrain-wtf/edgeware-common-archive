*1. What is your experience as a validator or node operator?

PureStake has extensive experience running secure and reliable public blockchain infrastructure, validators and nodes in a distributed architecture. We currently provide infrastructure services for the Algorand network in the form of relay nodes, participation nodes, and a high availability RPC API endpoints.  We will be launching validation services for Kusama and Polkadot shortly. Our team is made up of 13 full-time employees with significant infrastructure, DevOps, and engineering expertise.  We are globally distributed and dedicated to delivering 24x7x365 availability for our infrastructure services.

*2. What's your technical setup? (cloud vs. on-premise; software stack; key management)

PureStake has deployed a network specifically designed for proof-of-stake networks with a broad global user base. Our network features 23 points of presence at strategic locations across the globe. We use automation to drive the setup and configuration of network, security, compute, storage and blockchain application infrastructure into the points of presence on our network. We employ an infrastructure as code multi-cloud strategy across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, and we use software engineering best practices around the testing, automation, and deployment of change to our services. Our security controls include DDoS protection, firewalls, IDS, HA load balancers, automated patch management, daily vulnerability scanning, log aggregation and analysis, secure secrets management, network segmentation and isolation, and centralized and audit logged multifactor authentication services.  Our services and infrastructure are managed and monitored 24x7x365. We manage change, incidents, and other management processes using a mature set of operational practices conforming to the ISO27K standard.  Our validator setup will employ a sentry architecture deployed across all three cloud environments we support.

*3. Why are you interested in validating?

We believe in a public, multi-chain future and the mission of our company is to provide infrastructure to support this future.  We further believe in Polkadot and substrate as key enabling technologies to make this happen.  In our opinion, Edgeware has a unique opportunity to play a key role in this shift by becoming the de-facto smart contract platform on Polkadot.  We are interested in helping Edgeware with our infrastructure expertise and services, in the form of validation services to start, but depending on demand, possibly expanding to other as a service offerings such as APIs as a service, explorers, developer tooling and content, collation as a service, and enterprise smart contract application infrastructure and services.

*1. What are your positions on issues that governance will be expected to handle? 

We believe governance should focus on figuring out how to fund initiatives that will drive developer adoption and community engagement.

*2. What proposals on the network are you most interested in seeing? (e.g. initial parachain offerings, DAOs, DeFi protocols, stablecoins, prediction markets, insurance)

We are most interested in how Edgeware can join the Polkadot network as a parachain when it launches.  We are further interested in initiatives that make Edgeware a competitive alternative to Ethereum for developers, including tooling, educational materials, etc.  In terms of use cases, defi and DAOs are of particular interest to us.

*3. Is there an overall long-term vision for the network you are most excited about?’

We want to help support Polkadot/Edgeware become a competitive alternative to Ethereum for smart contract development.
