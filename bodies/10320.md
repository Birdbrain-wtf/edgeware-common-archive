## Summary
	In Edgeware’s journey to becoming a sustainable ecosystem from just a governance experiment, lots of talented enthusiasts joined the efforts as solo contributors or as teams/entities. After the reduced contributions and involvement from the founding team Commonwealth Labs, the initiative called Edgeware Agency (by ex-CW Labs’ member Thom Ivy) became the largest collective looking after the majority of the operations, maintenance and (business/technical) developments for a year. And then the Agency itself got decentralized which accelerated contributors’ onboarding as solo contributors with/without the newly introduced SCU framework to avoid governance bottlenecks. 
	As of today, we have 25+ contributors of different contribution types and diverse backgrounds. The on-chain treasury indirectly the inflation (6.2% currently) has been the backend of the sustainability of the Edgeware ecosystem without any kind of token sales or VC backings. To complement the most recent ‘[Re-rating framework for contributors](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/8791-rerating-framework-for-contributors)’ i.e. to increase the sustainability scope even further, this framework aims to explore the possibility of outsourcing the community-judged & proven talent which is cultivated within the Edgeware ecosystem to other web3 projects looking for contributors/employees so the contributors can rely less on the incentives from the on-chain treasury supporting the Edgeware ecosystem in the process.


## Clauses for contributors:
- Need at least one treasury tip/proposal approved by the community.
- Newer responsibilities shouldn’t affect your existing contributions.
- Mention your role, description, commitment availability in hours, bandwidth, incentives per month, EDG vesting ratio (VR: minimum 30%) and your demonstrated experience/expertise (could be a link to your past proposal or citizen appreciation post) as a comment on this thread.
- Update your comment when you’re out of bandwidth.



## Clauses for talent recruiters:
- Mention the roles you're looking for recruitment or the contributors’ names from the comments and the curator’s name with fees(CF: maximum 2%) {if applicable}. 
- Send [100-(VR+CF)]% of the incentives directly to the contributor(s), curator fees (CF) to the curator and the buy EDGs as per the contributor’s preferred vesting ratio (VR) and send to the corresponding contributor(s) but with 2 years of vesting (by yourself or through the collective of curators).
- Maintain public logs for incentives of the recruited talents.



## Clauses for curators:
- Should be familiar with encoding vested transfer transactions.
- Need at least two treasury tips/proposals approved by the community.
- Guide/assist the talent recruiters regarding the process.



**Glossary**
- VR: Vesting Ratio {for EDG bought from the monthly incentives corresponding to the outsourced talent(s).}
- CF: Curator Fees {optional, capped at 2% as this will be monthly (periodic).}


(Potential improvement for future talent matching: EdgeEVM or remark-based dashboard, something contributors can build in any of the upcoming hackathons.)
