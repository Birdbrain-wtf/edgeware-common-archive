Kulupu's going to have a baby! It's called [Neatcoin](https://neatcoin.org/) and it 'll be a PoS chain supporting a variant of Diem's (former Facebook's Libra) smart contract language Move called Outmove. It will also bring Grin's Mimblewimble privacy transactions to Substrate.

Kulupu holders will be able to get $Neat via a lockdrop. A cool feature is that holders can repeatedly lock their already locked coins for potential new drops and also use them for governance.

To enable trading between both coins the[ atomic swap](https://github.com/paritytech/substrate/tree/master/frame/atomic-swap) pallet will be integrated. Note that Kulupu will integrate Paritytech's asset pallet and not Acala's [ORML pallet](https://github.com/open-web3-stack/open-runtime-module-library).
