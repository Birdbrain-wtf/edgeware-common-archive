## Problem statement and Proposed solution

The success and growth of the Polkadot ecosystem depend on attracting talented individuals and teams to build on top of it. However, in India, not many people are aware of the potential of Polkadot and the opportunities it offers. This lack of awareness is what this proposal aims to address.
This proposal will also bring awareness to EdgewareDAO by nature of its support and traffic back to the EdgewareDAO podcast via YouTube cards.
