## Discussion

The purpose of our weekly calls is to allow members of our builders guild community to engage in conversation with one another about what specific projects / technology they're working on or learning. It allows for the arousal of new thoughts / ideas to occur and allow for constructive conversation to bring everyone on the same page and walk away with some new potentially helpful information.

Today, Ricardo kicked things off by explaining his involvement in the space. Smart Cities and algorithmic governance is where he comes from and he was drawn to Edgeware and wanted to learn more. 

- Max G gave a demo of Hypha DHO [https://dho.hypha.earth/#/](https://dho.hypha.earth/#/) . He explained the different functionalities such as Endorsing, the treasury, Multi Sig and the different role / badge assignment utilities that are included. They've received a letter of intent from Peru in making a coffee / cacao cooperative by leveraging DAOs as a means of avoiding COVID.


- The conversation then transitioned into the Web Mixer that Drew is working on and how it's approaching version 1. It would be extremely quick as compared to ETH. 


- He has also requested that we find 1 or 2 Solidity Devs for tech support on the EVM side. If anyone is able to contribute to that, or knows someone in the community that would be willing to tap in on that, it would be much appreciated.


- Micro-bounties through Telegram would likely be a viable incentive for new developers that are joining the telegram builders group. This could involve identity creation, small engagement tasks, social media posts etc. Any suggestions on the matter will be considered. 


- ZK Rollups are enabling Plasm Network to support various dApps. A team will be needed to do this on Edgeware. A meeting will also need to be set up with Tor on Multi Party Computation (MPC). Furthermore, a quick start documentation / guide for developers that are new to Edgeware and are looking for somewhere to start without getting lost would be very beneficial.

- Tutorials - videos, informative articles, media posts.
- Tier guides - different level competency guides so that developers / interested individuals can start their a learning track according to how competent they are with the technology.
