[https://medium.com/the4thpillar/4thtech-2-0-update-crucial-adoption-leap-forward-b0823a1b7b5b](https://medium.com/the4thpillar/4thtech-2-0-update-crucial-adoption-leap-forward-b0823a1b7b5b)

Besides the Ethereum and HashNet, the 4thtech 2.0 update adds Polkadot support **in the form of *Edgeware parachain***. Due to Polkadot’s ability to bridge blockchains, 4thtech compatibility with Polkadot plays a vital role in future cross-chain interoperability.
