I've been following Terra Protocol ($LUNA) closely for some time. Like Edgeware, they are a new smart contract chain inside a multichain ecosystem. They also support WASM smart contracts, and support fair launches. Mirror, their synthetic assets protocol, was launched late last year with 100% of Mirror tokens being distributed to the community.

Unlike Edgeware, their base layer token $LUNA is used to mint a stablecoin called [TerraUSD](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/terra-usd), which uses a model similar to Basis. Also unlike Edgeware, they have raised a lot of money for the base layer, which is necessary because they have to stabilize the dollar and avoid the kinds of booms and crashes that have happened to [Empty Set Dollar](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/empty-set-dollar) or [Basis Cash](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/basis-cash).

Most interesting to me is that the Terra team is focused on building a platform for applications, rather than base layer development of the Cosmos SDK. **This is really similar to how we're not focused on core development of Substrate, and so, there are a lot of interesting lessons we could learn.**

My general takeaways are:

- You only need a handful of good applications to make a blockchain network feel like an active and exciting place. Terra has Mirror, Anchor, and a couple of others (payments rails that run on UST), and they are approaching a $10b market cap.
- dTrade might have the potential to become our Mirror — a real-world application which encourages people from outside the chain to come and lock up a large amount of assets. According to dTrade's website, there should be several forms of mining available to dTrade users, including [insurance mining, options mining and staking](https://dtrade.org/).
- For example: One use case of Mirror is for people migrating their existing stock holdings to the protocol, so they can hold the same market positions while also collecting yield farming rewards. dTrade could encourage people to do something similar with options. There's an instrument called a LEAP which is a long dated option, which [cost-efficiently approximates taking a leveraged position on a stock](https://www.reddit.com/r/options/comments/ip64no/intro_to_leaps/), usually an equity index or mega cap like MSFT or AAPL. This is already something that people do; in the future they could do it on Edgeware, and be able to do so more easily than in the real world. Normally, options are sold on units of 100x the underlying shares so fractional ownership of options is actually very useful here.
- Otherwise, a chain like Edgeware should fund new projects to launch useful infrastructure. Ideally we could reserve some of the infrastructure tokens to be distributed to EDG holders. These lockdrops or liquidity mining activities would help align EDG holders with the newly launched tokens and create a committed and interlinked community.


A lot of this requires some more work on our side.

- We need a stablecoin. I have heard there's a project to bring an EVM-based stablecoin to Edgeware.
- We should also be able to bridge over assets from Ethereum, and also other chains. There's work being done on ChainBridge to these ends.
- We need a DEX but that will be much more straightforward. We could easily make proposals to other existing DEXes who aim to be multichain, to deploy to Edgeware.
- We should fund development of mobile wallets. Terra has Terra Wallet (official) which is really quite basic. A wallet doesn't need to be super feature rich, but supporting e.g. WalletConnect by injecting the Polkadot JS API + supporting viewing your balance and making transfers would go a very long way. (They support staking but I never use it.)
- The [Terra smart contract documentation](https://docs.terra.money/contracts/#developer-tools) is well built out for such a new platform. We should definitely invest in improving the quality of documentation for EVM and WASM. Our support for EVM contracts should be a huge advantage here.


**How we are different**

Our unique advantages are around our philosophy, being community-oriented, and being a friendly platform for new developers. It's...just different from other blockchains which are focused on BD and established teams.

The norm that is emerging around collectives forming around an interest area, and then motivated members of those collectives making proposals to build out applications, like Decent is doing with NFT infrastructure, are really valuable.

- We should invest in writing up more of the process for making proposals to the treasury, to encourage new teams to do so. A lot of that process has been ad hoc and rather opaque, so only super committed community members have been able to do it. Learning how to propose to Edgeware will be as important as deploying smart contracts. The process should be just as easy to understand. There should be examples and narratives that walk through how past proposing teams have done this.
- We could also highlight a few community members who are interested in assisting new teams with getting onboarded to Edgeware & assisting them with drafting proposals (cc [@dillchen](/edgeware/account/hwJpK8k6Q1KR8F87u5KHfBghRRit7p5UYWC1ubPDwsjdh6s) [@mrbulb](/edgeware/account/jXC7ghviUzVcVySg3eD8prB7C9m6VzK6cw2MTyMTDTUye5q) who have also been super active in doing so, and this is also something I'm interested in).
- As a side note, it's interesting that both [@dillchen](/edgeware/account/hwJpK8k6Q1KR8F87u5KHfBghRRit7p5UYWC1ubPDwsjdh6s) and [@mrbulb](/edgeware/account/jXC7ghviUzVcVySg3eD8prB7C9m6VzK6cw2MTyMTDTUye5q) have their own sort of incubator / corporate structures going on, that are organizing pools of capital, and that meshes well with being able to onboard teams to Edgeware...


Anyway, these feel like the critical points for us to cover. 1) Infrastructural chain applications including stablecoin, bridge, and DEXes/wallets 2) developer documentation, and 3) proposal documentation including examples and walkthroughs of past proposals. And a couple of killer apps (dTrade and NFTs?), and then we should have a thriving ecosystem and one which is very uniquely differentiated by our philosophy and organizational structure.
