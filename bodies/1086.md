The understanding of blockchain technology in Africa keeps growing but the more we that already have prior knowledge to some extent mingle with early adopter within the ecosystem the better we will be able to deliver appropriate information and increase its adoption the more.

We will start to study Edgeware product one after the other and monitor our progress in this area.

I will appreciate the agency members and the Edgeware team to keep suggesting the best way to grow this initiative. Education is the key to the adoption of the Edgeware use case.
