[@dillchen](/edgeware/account/hcgMhR61jUS55naSR5uW4JjN92zdFp3V91TtSCJmdnuxZAL) just posted an update to the [Edgeware 2020 Roadmap discussion](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/509-edgeware-2020-roadmap-discussion) that includes among other things a note on BuilDAO - you can view the original [BuilDAO incubator paper here](https://www.notion.so/BuilDAO-Incubator-Paper-35d451f814014f9cbff96b6c8ab443a4). 

When Edgeware launched a core use case was to become a place for 'governance experiments' - you can find ideas from some of the initial community in this ["Edgeware: Why are you here?"](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/536-why-are-you-here-edgeware-founding-values-culture-and-focus) post. This context is important for new-comers since BuilDAO itself exists as an interesting (but perhaps unintentional) 'governance experiment' with plenty of lessons to be learned.

**BuilDAO was funded with 50m EDG** pretty much the day I arrived in the project, in fact I think I walked right into a heated debate between councillors Dillon and Remz about the project. As far as history goes it was contentious not necessarily due to the contents of the project, more due to it being proposed by then council member[ @Dialectic - Ryan](/edgeware/account/jXCCBdq4Nzbv313i9VRFzm8VP4mtcEBcUsqBenh2apCR4e1) at a time when proposals put forward by the council only required a 7/13 majority to pass. 

At the time Commonwealth Labs [@dillchen](/edgeware/account/hwJpK8k6Q1KR8F87u5KHfBghRRit7p5UYWC1ubPDwsjdh6s),[ @raymondz](/edgeware/account/mae7JWE1EEMLcno2ZSB8dahwPqVPEyh8zik6dgNpQTvvucq),[ @drewstone](/edgeware/account/hwR8hAatmmdupBLXQSxLUPBa8GhRomLD9hf6iRtFeXs8fcY) and[@Thom Ivy](/edgeware/account/jKauGVPCQkteGTDKuuNwhYhMBrj5QuytgybEC4A5cS4Bki5) and their investors[ @Dialectic - Ryan](/edgeware/account/jXCCBdq4Nzbv313i9VRFzm8VP4mtcEBcUsqBenh2apCR4e1),[ @Michael Anderson](/edgeware/account/jGu8XvvYbcdFzF7vggP61cXYy7i8RiHEkzbbCsiCT66z8Ng) (Framework Ventures) and[ @Nick Tomaino](/edgeware/account/hc3YJEtCtzf8UEeZqr4GeDEc7JAGpFrJQmdKoWTSfuPHXMc) (1Confirmation) controlled 7 seats. Irrespective of the quality of the proposal, this setup an unfortunate series of events. 

Some (including remz) and myself felt that there should be a little more time for people to digest the contents of the proposal whilst others - Commonwealth team wanted to move faster and this was a way of getting ahead of the pack. 

There were good arguments on both sides, but as always moving fast can have issues down the road. 

In the end and perhaps for the good of the community we've all ended up back where we started with a chance to rethink, reset and reimagine what 50m EDG could be used for. 

It's important to not brush Edgeware's history under the carpet (it's all on-chain anyway) as there are always good lessons to be learned. As has been noted elsewhere it's ok to make mistakes, it's not ok to not learn from them. 

My view is that we should go right back to basics and just address some simple stuff before we get any deeper into legal structures, projects to be funded etc. 

- There is 50m EDG in a multisig
- Signers [@Dialectic - Ryan](/edgeware/account/jXCCBdq4Nzbv313i9VRFzm8VP4mtcEBcUsqBenh2apCR4e1) [@dillchen](/edgeware/account/hwJpK8k6Q1KR8F87u5KHfBghRRit7p5UYWC1ubPDwsjdh6s) [@_JAM_](/edgeware/account/n5wjtDexXJ2zbBq9R3aB1mxfdQtroHCd23v7ZUhKwiMeET7)
- Question - are we going to transfer this into a new multisig?
- If so, to who and why?
- Question - could we return the funds to the treasury? 
- Why not just wipe the slate clean?


Alongside those questions this is also an open call for anyone to put forward their views on the merits of an 'investment DAO'.
