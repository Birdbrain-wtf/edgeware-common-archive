Per [the BuilDAO post](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1091-rethinking-buildao-lessons-in-onchain-governance), the decisions made at the start of big initiatives cannot be unpicked... it's therefore incredibly important to be deliberate and cautious about decisions, even if there is a faster route to funding.

One big question that still remains and has been articulated by a few people is **"What does EDG get"?**

The concept of a metaDAO relies entirely on solving this Rubix cube - to give distinct advantages to individual chains, their token holders and cultures, whilst still remaining objectively independent.
