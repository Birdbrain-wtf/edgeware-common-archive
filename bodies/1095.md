## Background of The Relaychain Model and the Challenge

In the invention of Substrate and Polkadot, a core concept is that solo-chains become parachains. They 'share' their validation work, and therefore share the costs of network security. The inventor of the Polkadot model, Gavin Wood, has said that you can think of a relaychain like Polkadot like the United Nations: a collection of sovereign groups who come together for their common defense. 

However, the deployment of the relaychain has come with some surprise challenges in 'swapping' the operation of Edgeware Mainnet's consensus to the Polkadot side. Additionally, there are questions about what might happen if and when the chain ever decides to off-board from Polkadot- whether the chain would have to hard fork; something that we use on-chain upgrades and governance to avoid. 

## Launching The Edgeware DOT Bridge Zone (Parabridge?)

Most of the chains participating in the upcoming parachain slot auctions are not yet mainnet, and so will they launch with their successful bonding. As Edgeware is already live and on mainnet, we need something different to match our goals.

Edgeware should proceed with the planned [Parachain Offering](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/830-draft-proposal-edgeware-parachain-bonding), and when we are successful, we launch a **new** Edgeware chain into that slot. It will serve as a dedicated bridge, passing transactions from Edgeware Main to the DOT Bridge Zone, and then onto the Polkadot ecosystem.  First we bond offer, then we win, then we launch Bridge Zone (the parachain), then we bridge that zone to EDG Mainnet.

This gives Edgeware Main a clear path to participating in the Polkadot ecosystem without disrupting the current mainnet, with teams already building and a healthy validator set. It also ensures that if Edgeware decides Polkadot is not for whatever reason (usually this is cost) right for Edgeware, we do not have to offboard our mainnet but just one infrastructure piece.

## Conclusion
The game is fierce. Substrate pallets mean that innovation solely on the features of a chain is not a competitive 'moat', so we must also innovate in the ecosystem. The model here, if successful, would create something very new. We are the oldest live Substrate chain. We have significant organizing knowledge and experience, and I believe we should use this  to create a truly next-level Edgeware Network, further growing the value present within the networks and the community.
