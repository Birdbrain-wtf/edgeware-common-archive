### **Introduction**

Our team has held initial discussions with Thom Ivy to understand the benefits of using Edgeware as well as the networks’ need for public good infrastructure and specifically community-development infrastructure that reaches more of Edgeware’s stakeholder groups. This grant seeks to provide a solution within this scope: 

**90% of crypto users are missing out on significant investment opportunities. Why? Because creating a crypto investment portfolio is complex, technical and time-consuming to those who don’t dedicate their lives to crypto. **

Our solution is targeted towards the non-top 10% of DeFi users. The objective is to create an investment platform simple enough to bring relative novices and intermediate crypto users into the DeFi space.

Defolio democratises crypto investing by enabling users to implement a thematic asset management strategy through a simple drag and drop process, on a user-centric interface that will support interoperability and eventually enable social trading-like and gamification features to improve the user experience.

The Defolio protocol is Edgeware-based and through external integrations (with exchanges, lending platforms, automated market makers and asset protocols) it enables the user to create a range of thematic one-click investment strategies. ** **


### **Initial MVP to VC Investment stage (2-3 months): Scope of Work**
When the first stage of the application is complete, a user will be able to:
1. Use EDG to purchase a simple portfolio through one-click on a web-based platform. 
2. Log onto Metamask
3. Click the ‘buy portfolio’ button and number of EDG to be invested.
4. Wait for the trades to confirm and return assets to the metamask as a ‘portfolio’




### **MVP once VC funds have been received: Scope of Work**
When the application is complete, a user will be able to:
1. Purchase a choice of thematic portfolios through ‘one click’ investing.
2. Use Metmask, Coinbase, others 
3. In the EU, use their bank account to purchase portfolios using FIAT 
4. Decide what thematic portfolio to invest in (Choice of nine)
5. Low, med, high return
6. Themes - NFT’s, Polka, BSC, DeFi, etc




### **Next Stage: Scope of Work**
When the application is complete, a user will be able to:
1. Purchase a choice of thematic portfolios through one click investing.
2. Use Metmask, Coinbase, others, plus FIAT (in EU) 
3. Decide what thematic portfolio to invest in (Choice of nine)
4. Low, med, high return
5. Themes - NFT’s, Polka, BSC, DeFi, etc
6. Invest in copy portfolios (eToro-style)
7. Hover over portfolios to see ‘performance statistics’ (gamified)
