**LexBridge**
_Lexbridge is a technological bridge to jurisdictions in the US - launch a DAO, and get a Series LLC mapped to that DAO._
LexBridge has started development on additions to Commonwealth, to enable any Edgeware account to be attached to a Series LLC. The initial milestone will focus on personal accounts, offered through WyDAO LLC in a manner similar to www.otoco.io 's model, of minting LLCs tied to ERC20 tokens. While the legal templates have been secured, the actual PDF generation package and optimal on chain write/read strategies are still being determined. Mekyle is an additional point of contact for the project. Ongoing updates are available through the [bimonthly DAO working group call.](https://calendar.google.com/event?action=TEMPLATE&tmeid=NG1hMGFnZXA0dWswN2ZwaWZrZzRsM3EyYzdfMjAyMTAzMjlUMTUwMDAwWiBjb21tb253ZWFsdGguaW1faWhiczg5MnBxdWJ2b2d0b2ltNmMxaGY3Y2tAZw&tmsrc=commonwealth.im_ihbs892pqubvogtoim6c1hf7ck@group.calendar.google.com&scp=ALL)
