**Summary:  **This post outlines an approach to returning more value for EDG holders, treating the chain's native token like a social token capable of forming partnerships, signaling identity and status, and granting access to products and services.

## EDG Citizen Benefits

In my personal vision, Edgeware has been a platform for advancing the concept and execution of the _network state,_ a futuristic vision of a generalized union of members collectively bargaining for whatever they deem appropriate, potentially arising to a non-territorial government.  That road is long, but we've made amazing progress in the management of our public goods, the development of our financial tools, and towards a stronger awareness of our community, values, and ideas.

Part of that vision then utilizes our collective bargaining power and our community-managed treasury to advance the welfare of EDG holders.

Today, I'm happy to share an ongoing effort to deliver tangible advantages to being a part of our happy few.

## Partnerships for Welfare

As a digital network with an unknown number of humans tied to our EDG accounts, we are especially well-aligned with infinitely scalable products and services that naturally are digital, as they do not require further effort on the part of the producer to serve our ever-increasing userbase. My initial requests and outreach has been towards the following benefits:

- Education along a variety of topics.
- Wellness Resources, both physical and mental.
- Community Tooling


These projects would offer access to their resources or platforms for EDG holders through an application process, or eventually through native crypto-integration of proving that one is the holder of EDG. Hold EDG?** Free access to apps, eduction programs, and more.**

## Communities for Adoption and Experimentation

In addition to EDG holders being granted access to valuable digital goods,  they also ideally gain access to an increasing number of communities. I have also designed these proposals to bring users onto our infrastructure, provide for dogfooding of our new tech, and source user feedback from onboarded teams.  In short, we grow, they grow, and both sides benefits from user experimentation. 

## Collectively Bargained

In return, proposals will fund the development, growth, or other costs of those goods and services as a public subsidy. Per usual, these proposals will be fully subject to public engagement and the referenda process (barring trivial costs, as recently discussed through the Council 'fast-track' grant concept for lower-value requests.)

## A more competitive and diverse funding ecosystem

Formerly, for-profit products and services have had limited routes for funding as the treasury has been directed towards true public goods. Contributions to the Citizens Benefit allow for-profit teams a framework for delivering public goods in return for public funding, increasing their options for development on our network and creating a more directly competitive funding ecosystem within EDG. 

Finally, I'm excited to share that the first such proposal is in drafting and we hope to bring it to the public for engagement and review over the next week. 


## Part 2: Aligning Network Security with $EDGPass
We have several challenges related to staking at this time on the network:
1. Low staked EDG to circulating EDG ratio, which will become a greater concern when liquidity increases from market-making activities.
2. Providing node support from third-parties is not as viable as a business currently due to low staking demand.
3. Lack of easy tooling and infrastructure for staking. This was improved on the awareness side with the launch of EDG on Staking Rewards.

To improve all of these, I propose that we work with [Staking Wizard ](https://app.stakingwizard.com/)to integrate EDG using a white-label solution, or work within our community to develop a similar solution. This interface will not only help users understand the benefits of staking monetarily but will also mint them a non-transferable token called EDGPass that will remain in their account as long as the address is associated with a minimum level of EDG staked.
**Succinctly, if you stake n EDG, you get an $EDGPass, and that gives you access to the set of Citizen Benefits.**
