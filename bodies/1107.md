## Ecosystem Updates

**Commonwealth.im**
_The multi-chain governance and community interface._
The Commonwealth team has finished v1 for contracts on Commonwealth Protocol. This week and next, they’re testing + auditing the contracts. Design work is complete but not implemented yet and boilerplate structures for integration have been built. The landing page and mobile app are being worked on by an external agency. The landing page is expected to ship within the next two weeks.
 
**DTrade**
_DTrade is a platform for trading perpetuals and options on Edgeware._
No major updates this week, just building.
 
**Decent Partners**
_Decent is a 'metadao' that seeks to create crypto-native public media._
Decent [shared their recent progress in crafting their offer](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/831-decent-an-edgeware-ecosystem-project-update-150321) to Edgeware users in return for an upcoming treasury proposal to fund digital arts and media assets in the public's interest.
 
**LexBridge**
_Lexbridge is a technological bridge to jurisdictions in the US - launch a DAO, and get a Series LLC mapped to that DAO._
No major updates this week, just building.
 
**Webb**
_Webb is a privacy technology designer launched from Commonwealth Labs._
The Webb team has built new designs for the landing page, written job descriptions for potential new hires, and are targeting first integration of modules in ERUP4 (Edgeware Runtime Upgrade 4)
 
**4thTech**
_EU-Identity compliant document notarization and transfer service._
[4thTech](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1063-4thpillar-technologies-has-integrated-edgeware-for-their-ecommerce-and-document-exchange-platform) deployed the smart contract for their 2.0 platform on Edgeware earlier this week. They expect to open the platform to the public by the end of this week or next week.

- Passed, moved to referendum [Proposal to Fund ERUP4 (Edgeware Runtime Upgrade 4)](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/democracyproposal/29-treasuryapproveproposal29)


[Referendum #33](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/referendum/33-treasuryapproveproposal29) Treasury to approve proposal to fund ERUP4
_Active_
Proposal to fund ERUP 4 (Edgeware Runtime Upgrade 4) by Drew Stone and Jake Naviasky. Expected time to complete the upgrade is 1-3 months.

[Referendum #32](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/referendum/32-treasury-reward-reduction-to-0-edgblock) Treasury reward reduction to 0 EDG/block  
_Active_
Proposal sets inflation from the treasuryReward module to zero, resulting in treasury inflation of ~5% or $1.5m/year (from staking inefficiencies).

[Referendum #30](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/referendum/30-treasuryapproveproposal26) Exchange B + EDG
