# Edgeware Grant Programme Structure

## Background

Edgeware's on-chain treasury now controls c. $30m in EDG, whilst other layer 1 protocol DAOs such as Kusama and Polkadot control another $400m more, whilst ETH based smart contract treasuries such as Uniswap control billions. 

![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/Eym8cenXAAcyHXk?format=jpg&name=medium)

The challenge is no longer fundraising, these projects have enough capital to build for decades. The challenge is now sourcing, developing and delivering quality proposals and rewarding and retaining those that contribute over the long term.

Put simply, capital is no longer a scarce resource, instead the most successful projects will be those that move beyond a speculative and pure product mindset towards the hard work of building cohesive community economies that super-serve their citizens ([see Thom's recent post](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1101-concept-edg-citizen-benefits)) through well managed organisational structures.  

This might seem like a simple mental shift - but in practice it is a profoundly different way of approaching the funding, development and projects that expand the economy, culture and resources of the chain.

The big question now moves to the scalabiltiy of our funding programme - we need to develop standards, else we will be reinventing the wheel with every new concept or team and have no way to evaluate what is / isn't working which will result in wasted time and money.

## **EDG Grant Programme **

We have had a mixed history with EDG's early funding which is to be expected, but it now feels like the community is at a point where we should discuss and evolve some basic funding structures and processes.

We also have an eclectic mix of individuals, teams, interests and ideas in varying stages of development and no standards or processes for how best to organise and structure funding.

One of the key challenges has been responding to people asking **"what is the proposal process?"** 

A structured grant programme will have the following benefits.

1. Clearer understanding of the way Edgeware differentiates for newcomers and community members alike
2. Demonstrates EDG's 'full stack'  chain treasury, governance / CW system / emerging network of DAOs / organisational structures and project teams.
3. Easier onboarding for new teams and projects
4. Quicker to test and learn on smaller grants 
5. Easier to judge and manage relative progress or development of ideas and teams.
6. Builds a strong R&D culture that outputs useful work that others can build on
7. Ability to scale projects that work, kill those that don't but learn from the process
8. Building on-chain reputation in a more structured way that then can play into our plans around NFT based achievements / memberships.
9. Ability to attract quality, not quantity and drive a builder rather than speculator mentality at the core of the community.

With this in mind here's where I think we currently sit with regard to a grant structure 

It's a people centric approach (as is everything in Edgeware) which helps to see how this might play out.

**1. WORKING GROUP GRANT**

- You arrive in the community and don’t see a working group you like and want to set up one.
- You have engaged in a few calls, setup a CW ID, read documentation, posted to CW a few times or have engaged with the community in some meaningful way
- It becomes obvious you have some domain expertise, network or interest area and are self-motivated but are not yet sure of the type of project Edgeware should fund.


- Present a brief outline of your WG and plan
- **Request c. $1000 to kickstart a WG **


**2. RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT GRANTS**

A working group lead or member has initial idea or concept that seems interesting but has very little structure, or the idea might be more fully formed, but there is a lack of resource to deliver a project. 

There is agreement that more research and development is needed.

- **request $3-$50k for a tightly focused R&D grant **
- funding dependent on stage
- dependent on complexities
- dependent on team / rates
- can have a flexible timeline and scope with room for exploration
- can be funded as a single payment.
- can also have phased R&D grants, eg you start with $3k for some initial work and then request follow up before your funding runs out. 
- rates are important $3k is not a lot, then again it is a lot if it is 1 days work
- **should deliver a report or conclusion including recommended next steps.**


These payments should be quick and easy to approve and should be an easy way for groups to arrive and get started and produce viable specs in whatever domains they have interest.

**3. PROJECT FUNDING**

- Follows on from initial work - either funded or unfunded. 
- I would always say that people who show up and start without needing pay are the best people to attract. Those who demonstrate their value and commitment ahead of any funding requests
- Our aim should be to establish a missionary over mercenary culture.
- For exampl[ @Johan](/edgeware/account/jmoxayjwtZozJ6rr64ikaXhsZa7SZm7ihPwajvtodqXrmnj) with the website, he started in his evenings.  
- This is what community contribution means and is how you actually build a meaningful culture over the long term.


- **Funding request can be for ANY amount (within sensible limits).**
- Milestones based
- Reporting required
- We should weight previous community contribution higher when judging the commitment and reputation of the proposer. 


**4. CHAIN WORKERS AND LEADS**

- Community members who contribute across a range of areas and help 'glue' together the collective intelligence and resources of the community.
- Help 'steer the ship' 
- Operationalised by the Agency and Builders Group
- Chain leads discussed [here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/880-decentralizing-edgeware-with-chain-leads) by [@dillon](/edgeware/account/iCLdev9eJBSaUSD1qnbq1roqcV6jZmfLpbLJjTdmUocixcY) 
- Funded via ongoing payments as chain 'employees' 
- Payments should be standardised into a few levels that refer to ongoing contributions to the chain rather than 'meatspace' qualifications. Our aim is to build native competency in this new world of work, not hire based on CVs or background.
- _Student - RATE TBC_
- _Junior - RATE TBC_
- _Mid - RATE TBC_
- _Senior - RATE TBC_


**5. ECOSYSTEM FUNDS/DAOs**

- Should be developed by teams following the successful delivery of 1,2 and 3 when trust has been earned and larger commitments can be granted.
- This is the most time consuming of all and is kind of where BuilDAO and Decent's Web3 media ecosystem sits. 
- This is where we are delegating large amounts to separate groups who have a domain or network that can exist as a satellite entity to the project. 
- These DAOs should leverage the infrastructure built by prior project teams.
- Can and should take months (years) to develop and is very hard to scope. 
- Requires a lot of time and a lot of discussion.
- Governance structure is key 
- Layer 2 DAOs need different incentives to Layer 1 DAOs. 
- Needs nuanced relationship to mother chain.


## HOW IT ALL WORKS TOGETHER

By developing a cohesive but agile structure for the phased funding of Edgeware's emerging economy and society we can ensure that there is a clear development path for anyone in the project to follow and that can expand to any ambition.

It also lays a path for Ecosystem DAOs such as BuilDAO or Decent to recruit talent from, that can ensure we onboard new talent to the ecosystem and skill them up in the emerging culture of the chain, ensuring collective intelligence and community spirit can scale through any number of organisations that make their home in and around the ecosystem.

Something like BuilDAO would now have training wheels for teams and projects, whilst documentation would be created as research is completed, standards are set and culture is developed. 

- Education series and curriculum
- Dedicated hackathons
- Dedicated accelerators


## **FINAL THOUGHTS**

Unlike Bitcoin or Ethereum, protocol DAOs such as Edgeware that possess native treasuries and proposal systems enable EDG holders to contribute directly to the development of the network, without having to rely completely on the often perverse incentives that other sources of funding bring and demand of teams. 

The vast majority of crypto is venture funded, whilst the media surrounding it is built on a Web2 based advertising model that priorities attention over quality.

This is why speculative bubbles abound and there is often an inverse correlation between the marketcap of a network and it's actual productivity, culture or innovations.

Put simply, the market is designed to reward short term thinking and hype based marketing, something that will change radically once we have Web3 media that can fund quality research, analysis and education. 

It is my firm belief that the projects building with this in mind, will persist long into the future.
