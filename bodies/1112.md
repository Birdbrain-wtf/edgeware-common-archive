**What’s the reason to have the Agency?**
Edgeware, unlike most chains, did not have a centralized launch. As a result, it also does not have a traditional foundation empowered to sign contracts, pay traditional companies, or conduct basic business activities. Commonwealth Labs, who helped coordinate the launch of Edgeware and has thus far solely supported the development of the chain and several business development functions, is now ready to focus on specific applications on Edgeware.

The mission was always for Edgeware to fund and sustain itself. As a result, a set of longtime and some new community members have figured out how to work together to empower and enforce standards that center community-ownership in Edgeware and to drive core community engagement and adoption. Importantly, 

Without this entity, we will currently have no group that markets, coordinates, manages news, media, or guides interested projects on grant proposals, facilitates community engineering tasks, supports setting strategies, or cat-herds our several projects and large community. 


## Compensation Changes

As it stands, the Agency continues to employ our initial staff set at their initial wages, as they were negotiated at the launch of our first Brand Working Group proposal. 

Based on feedback around compensation, but recognizing that agency staff are paid in EDG that covers their costs and several operate solely on this wage, we believe it is important that we coordinate a second proposal that addresses the equity of our staff in the system. 

In startups, early employees are usually afforded equity for their high-risk involvement in the venture. In our spirit of making Edgeware the best place to work, we will propose a vesting schedule for our workers, to better enable them to remain in our ecosystem as they build these valuable skills, and to do so in a way that enables all of our team to participate. At this time, this is not part of the proposal, and all staff, including Thom, will be paid in TWAP-EDG at the end of a month, as we have done so for the past months, and commensurate with or less than market standards for our industry.

Additionally, we wanted to clarify that Rich’s payment is a mere continuation of our original agreement with him - and he will offboard from the Agency and our payroll as he brings the Decent proposal forward. 

## Agency External Strategy

**Key Metrics:**

- Community members (The goal is to reach 20,000 over the next year in our Discord/Community)
- Projects (Dapps and DAOs) on Edgeware
- Measures of value on the network - e.g. TVL



**The current strategy for Edgeware from the Agency is to use:**

**Partnership development- ‘Ecosystem partners,’ **-- technically skilled, reputable and value-aligned firms and partners that will advance Edgeware’s utility in specific use cases, increasing our resilience and independence on any single technical partner, and growing use cases that align with Edgeware’s values and experience. 

**Narrative development based on demonstrating innovatively and 'living' our community’s values. **This involves media and marketing campaigns and case studies into our uses that align strongly with our vision of the future, akin to lifestyle marketing, to rapidly discover Edgeware-aligned future holders. 

**Unique technical differentiation within the Substrate ecosystem, **as well as supporting core development, which we are calling the ‘parachain factory model,’ This model grows out of Thom’s writing on multi-chain trends and nested relaychains. This view uses the following pipeline:

- [ ] Onboard projects as smart contracts.
- [ ] Projects build traction and find product fit
- [ ] Those projects that achieve high status in the chain are afforded parachain slots in the Edgeware Relaychain model, becoming dapp-specific parachains and transitioning to palettes. 


This model is unique in Substrate, leverages our dual contracting abilities, and is compatible with pursuing a DOT parachain slot, and accrues value to EDG in the same function as DOT, at a likely more accessible price point, and potentially offers a variation in the existing crowdloan model - additionally, as DOT and KSM do not have smart contracts, they cannot use this community-proving system. 

**Community Engineering and Support.** A set of activities that variously unblock and improve the experience of community members using the network, enabling better scaling. Many of these for example can be seen in the [deliverable list.](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1MFX8mCvkuXHXmrEaG19wbhZYyqbiVrRcEW2WfuzShSQ/edit#gid=0)

## Agency Internal Strategy
The strategy here is to be a force-multiplier for teams in the ecosystem, speed onboarding and marketing to all stakeholders, and inevitably use our smart contract technology to decentralize the functions of the Agency itself. The best example of this is to imagine a ‘Stripe Atlas’ for Edgeware, allowing a pipeline for teams looking to build on EDG to establish legal entities via LexBridge, sort into funding pipelines, and automate community engagement to high standards. 

Additionally, the roles within the Agency are ideally converted to open, curator and bounty-based participations - decentralizing the the agency, allowing contribution to be permissionless, verifiable, and open.
