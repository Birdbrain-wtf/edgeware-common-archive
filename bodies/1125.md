## Ecosystem Updates

**Commonwealth.im**
_The multi-chain governance and community interface._
The Commonwealth team has finished v1 for contracts on Commonwealth Protocol. This week and next, they’re testing + auditing the contracts.

**DTrade**
_DTrade is a platform for trading perpetuals and options on Edgeware._No major updates this week, just building.

**Decent Partners**
_Decent is a 'metadao' that seeks to create crypto-native public media._
Decent is onboarding new teams directly to the chain to help remove blocks ahead of decent protocol (e.g. they need basic NFT infra before other stuff can happen). Rich, from Decent Partners, will be posting a phased NFT roadmap strategy outline in the coming days for feedback.

**LexBridge**
_Lexbridge is a technological bridge to jurisdictions in the US - launch a DAO, and get a Series LLC mapped to that DAO._
No major updates this week, just building.

**Webb**
_Webb is a privacy technology designer launched from Commonwealth Labs._
The team has added multi token support based off ORML. They’ve begun the development of our p2p node and brought on another developer to help there.

**4thTech**
_EU-Identity compliant document notarization and transfer service._
[4thTech](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1063-4thpillar-technologies-has-integrated-edgeware-for-their-ecommerce-and-document-exchange-platform) deployed the smart contract for their 2.0 platform on Edgeware last week, it is now open to the public.
