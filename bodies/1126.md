I've just joined this community and was looking for the legal working group after seeing multiple mentions of legal issues arising in relation to DAOs and the creation of business entities to help drive growth.

I think it's fair to say that this industry is at a point where these questions are becoming more pressing as communities look for safe ways to manage and distribute treasury funds and deal with the regulatory changes that are taking place around the world.

There is also the important question of providing developers the necessary protection both in terms of the work output and for the platform itself. There are a significant number of legal issues associated with these platforms and it would be good to find a place to explore those issues and identify novel ways to address them.

If there are any other lawyers who would like to collaborate (or any devs interested in these topics), please let me know and we can start up a WG on Discord.
