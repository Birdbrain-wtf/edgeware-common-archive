## Summary

New-Futures (N-F) is contributing to the Edgeware community by launching a highly curated and editorial driven arts and culture platform utilising NFT and DAO functionality. 

The N-F collective are helping to shape the way people use Web3 media, by building, _from the inside out_, through community led engineering and product management in the Edgeware ecosystem and _from the outside in_, channelling established and emerging artists, designers and musicians into the Edgeware ecosystem. 

We will be battle-testing NFT minting functionality, simplifying onboarding for creators and experimenting with advanced use-cases enabled by the Substrate native [RMRK](https://rmrk.app) standards.

This grant seeks to fund: 
- **New-Futures (N-F) Platform**, a boutique curated art collective. 
- A space to showcase innovative art and creator culture, utilising Substrate native Web3 tools, NFTs and DAO collectives. 


Our vision, extensive experience and growing collective of influential figures from the creative industries mean we are neatly positioned to establish a creative arts arm of the Edgeware ecosystem and bolster Edgeware’s position as a cultural driving force as it incubates a multi-chain ecosystem with the launch of Kusama parachain candidate Kabocha.  

_**UPDATE 28.07.21 - RMRK focus**_

N-F provides a platform for Edgeware to experiment with [RMRK](https://rmrk.app) - a native NFT standard on Substrate. 

With Edgeware’s gradual evolution towards [a multi-chain incubator](https://medium.com/coinmonks/edgeware-a-multi-chain-reality-not-just-a-solo-chain-5769d95638c5) that seeks to carve out a unique space in the Polkadot ecosystem and currently some issues facing EVM compatibility, we are initially focusing on delivering a platform that builds on Substrate’s unique advantages.

In simple terms this is a bet on engaging new users, rather than bringing in users from Ethereum, though in the future there will be compatibility with marketplaces such as Opensea - which is itself becoming [a multichain ecosystem](https://opensea.io/blog/announcements/announcing-our-100m-raise-led-by-a16z/). 

With that in mind we have developed an active strategic partnership with the RMRK team and founder Bruno Skvorc, formerly of Web3 Foundation. You can dig deeper into the exciting possibilities of RMRK in this post ‘[The Dawn of Art Legos’](https://app.subsocial.network/@rmrkapp/dawn-of-the-art-legos-18289) or an in depth video explanation at [url.rmrk.app/rmrkcc](https://url.rmrk.app/rmrkcc) which outlines why focusing on Substrate based standards ahead of EVM will unlock rapid innovation and lots of exciting new opportunities. 

We will bring our creative expertise and network to drive innovation of their technology. 

N-F will also aim to be one of the first projects to leverage RMRK 2.0, a Substrate standard that is currently limited to utilising Substrate’s remark functionality (graffitiing the chain), before adopting the RMRK pallets which will be included in Kabocha’s - our Kusama parachain candidate) - initial runtime deployment and we would aim to upgrade Edgeware to accommodate. This would lead into compatibility with WASM/Ink smart contracts as we develop additional functionality and offer composability of our New-Futures projects. 

With RMRK currently having only two marketplace deployments - [Kanaria](http://kanaria.rmrk.app/) and [Singular](https://singular.rmrk.app/), and RMRK 2.0 pallets being the first non parity libraries to be adopted by a number of projects, we feel that we have the chance to carve out Edgeware’s status as true Web3 media pioneer in the Polkadot ecosystem. 

**Key technical collaboration points**

- RMRK commits to actively working on integration of RMRK protocols into N-F with local testing and experimentation, helping with bridge construction to the best of RMRK team's ability, and RMRK also intends to add full support for N-F NFTs into Singular, so users can manage all of their RMRK NFTs regardless of ecosystem.


- Edgeware’s solo chain system presents an opportunity to bridge to Kabocha and then into the Kusama ecosystem, something that aligns with Ramsey’s work on [parabridges and bridges](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1327-edgeware-road-parabridges-core-development).
- This work will be the subject of a separate EDG grant to top up his existing work.
- It may be possible in the future to bridge EDG >< KSM, but this is on a longer timeline so this is not something we are scoping at this stage.


- N-F commits to actively participate in the engineering and optimization of RMRK core pallets with feedback and code review whenever able.
- For example, here's the spec of the emote pallet: [https://github.com/rmrk-team/pallet-emotes](https://github.com/rmrk-team/pallet-emotes) and here's an idea for optimizing emote storage: [https://hackmd.io/JjqT6THTSoqMj-_ucPEJAw](https://hackmd.io/JjqT6THTSoqMj-_ucPEJAw)

## New-Futures

New-Futures (established in September 2020) has been working extensively within the creative industries building out artists collectives. 

Utilising iterations of the platform design and core branding we have been building trust within the artist community and establishing New-Futures as their trusted partner and gateway into crypto. 

We resonate with Edgeware’s mission. We will pioneer the first creative collectives (DAOs) on Edgeware, which will chaperone creators through visual communication, inspiration and education, creating empowered decentralised collectives
 
Our core values of equity, collaboration, and transparency drive our mission to inspire and incentivize shared ownership, independent creative expression, and value distribution through Web3 media.  
 
We aim to create stunningly simple user experiences and user interfaces that drive adoption, retain attention and inspire creation and collaboration.
