**The following projects are all a part of the Edgeware ecosystem, follow our [weekly newsletter](https://blog.edgewa.re/)** **for updates from the teams behind these initiatives.**

- [dTrade](http://dtrade.org/) is a platform for trading perpetuals and options on Edgeware.


- [Decent Partners](https://www.decent.partners/) is a 'metadao' that seeks to create crypto-native public media.


- [Webb Protocol](https://commonwealth.im/webb/proposal/discussion/1122-new-welcome-to-the-webb) is a privacy technology designer launched from Commonwealth Labs.


- [Tidal Finance](https://tidal.finance/) allows users to buy and sell over-leveraged covers across multiple chains


- [Paralink Network](https://paralink.network/) offers a multi-chain oracle platform for DeFi and other blockchain applications.


- [Kylin Network](https://kylin.network/) is building a cross-chain platform powering the data economy on Polkadot


- [Zondax](https://zondax.ch/) is a team of software developers, engineers and cryptographers with diverse technical backgrounds that work together to build innovative tech products for companies from all kinds of industries.


- [LexBridge](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/948-proposal-lexbridge-legal-entitydocument-panel?comment=body) is a technological bridge to jurisdictions in the US - launch a DAO, and get a Series LLC mapped to that DAO.


- [4thPillarTech](https://the4thpillar.io/technology/) is an EU-Identity compliant document notarization and transfer service.


- [Covalent](https://www.covalenthq.com/) provides a unified API bringing visibility to billions of blockchain data points.


- The [ICON Network](https://icon.foundation/?lang=en) comprises various institutions ranging from: financial institutions, insurance companies, hospitals, universities and more.


- [Chainlink's](https://chain.link/) decentralized oracle network provides reliable, tamper-proof inputs and outputs for complex smart contracts on any blockchain.


- [OnFinality](https://onfinality.io/) is a SaaS platform that provides infrastructure services for the Polkadot/Substrate community.
