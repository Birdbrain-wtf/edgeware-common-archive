**INTRODUCTION**
Zien is a gateway to contemporary art and (soon to be) digital co-operative for the vanguard of visual culture. WhatsApp is used as a distribution mechanism to drop NFTs (called 'Scarce Editions') to subscribers that can be claimed from £0. An MVP has validated the core user experience and value proposition. This grant seeks to migrate Zien into the Edgeware ecosystem, align around shared values and accelerate on the objective to transform the relationship between contemporary art and a new generation of art collectors.

Zien has been in production since October 2020 on Ethereum Mainnet, releasing drops with renowned contemporary artists including Ed Fornieles and Petra Cortright, and is active within influential emerging and contemporary art networks.

We have held initial conversations with Thom Ivy to understand more about how Zien could fit into the Edgeware ecosystem. The grant will fund a well networked, committed team to advance—in collaboration where possible—the state-of-the-art for NFTs and their application from an art and artist first perspective. 

The core team and existing support network includes deep knowledge and experience within the cultural and blockchain ecosystems, many of whom have been active at this intersection since 2015/6. Progress to date has been entirely bootstrapped by choice and we now seek integration with an ambitious, execution focused community building out Web3 protocols and ecosystems.

**PRODUCT**
An MVP of the Zien distribution channel validated the core user experience around releasing _Scarce Editions_—NFTs with a license and instructions to produce a physical work—in drops via WhatsApp (with a freemium revenue model).

- A small number of works were available to buy at full-price, the remainder were available to claim from £0
- Sharing the drop with friends or upgrading (£10/£15) enabled subscribers to queue jump and claim either 20 or 40 minutes early
- Bid on original work related to Scarce Editions in maximum bid auction (shipping with Drop 05)

- Growth: 26% subscriber growth / drop (running 2 drops / month)
- Engagement: 60% conversion between drop notification and first action
- Scarce Editions were claimed within an average of 5 minutes
- Full-priced (buy now option) works sold out in Drop 04 (3 at £750)


Product roadmap highlights:
- X10 cheaper transactions with transaction batching to reduce gas / increase efficiency
- Drops and auction; combined edition drops with auction of an original artwork; use drops to drive presale attention to the work / artist
- Artist tools
- Custom licenses; programme works (NFTs) with resale conditions / limitations
- Log in; inventory of works and their statuses
- Secondary market; public resale of artist works (in accordance with license) 
- Buyer Features
- Messaging services; add support for different messaging services
- Concierge service; 1-2-1 service for customers
- Manage collection on iOS / Android; full custody of works / mobile wallet
- Carbon Impact of NFT minting; offset carbon via a transaction fee to Regen Network
- Location based drops; IRL events around the release of works
- Upgradability; allow live Scarce Editions to be upgraded to latest version of our spec as it evolves


12-18 month objectives enabled by proposed scope of work:

- Maintain average 25% subscriber growth each drop / auction
- Prove the drop / auction and operational model on the way to financial break-even
- 100,000+ subscribers



**ALIGNMENT WITH EDGEWARE**
We believe the proposal—and Zien’s broader objective of redefining access to visual art—can align well with an ecosystem transforming how people coordinate to build and evolve social goods (perhaps within the conceptual framework of an [Auxiliary State](https://twitter.com/thom_ivy_1/status/1391900642709544967)). While in the near term we are focused on developing traction and improving the overall user experience for artists and collectors, we are very keen to explore how Zien can integrate with Edgeware in line with the [Permissionless Subsidiary](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/958-frmr-subdaos-permissionless-subsidaries) model.
