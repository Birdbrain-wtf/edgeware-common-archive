## **Exit Theory: A Content and Community Project**
Grant Proposal
Author: Justin Murphy

## Introduction
This grant proposes funding for a content-creation and community-building project that will drive adoption of the Edgeware chain, deliver creative assets to the chain, provide user feedback from the creator perspective, document the journey for a public audience, and add value to Edgeware’s Citizen Benefits. I've held meetings with Thom Ivy and the Edgeware NFT Strategy WG to understand how creators are needed to drive adoption, usage, and improvement of the Edgeware chain.

I run a solo creator brand called Other Life, which publishes analysis and lifestyle content on philosophy, science, and internet culture. Other Life publishes weekly across [a newsletter](https://otherlife.co) (6k subscribers), [a podcast](https://podcast.otherlife.co/) (9k downloads/month), and [a Youtube channel](https://www.youtube.com/justinmurphy) (10k subscribers). I also run a premium membership community for independent intellectuals working outside of traditional institutions, called [IndieThinkers.org](https://indiethinkers.org).

The funds will support a content-creation agenda that culminates in a book, provisionally titled _Exit Theory_, which brings together much of my research and writing in recent years (e.g., [1](https://otherlife.co/hard-forking-reality-part-1/), [2](https://otherlife.co/technology-class-and-ideological-realignment/), [3](https://otherlife.co/barbarians-past-the-gate/)) . In the process, we’ll spin out several intermediate products for the public good inside and outside of Edgeware, as outlined below.

This grant will cover my living expenses for 6 months, plus help from a collaborating artist and two part-time assistants.

## Details
Strategic priorities for Edgeware include attracting creators, bringing in-demand creative assets on chain, growing and developing Edgeware’s community infrastructure, and increasing the circulation of public goods.

As a successful full-time creator and owner of a profitable membership community for creators, I am well positioned to make valuable contributions on all of these fronts. In exchange, Edgeware will provide me the resources to produce a high-quality, long-form product—rather than chase clicks—for a 6-month period. The quality and impact of my book will, in turn, drive the value of the creative assets we deliver to Edgeware.

- I will announce and publicize the initiation of my next book project, including a series of corresponding NFTs on the Zien platform.
- I’ll build a properly executed email list and launch strategy
- I have close relationships with digital artists in my community who will deliver creative assets on commission
- I offer 50 IndieThinkers.org Pro memberships to EDG holders.
- I will publish one launch video
- I will publish three lifestyle vlogs about being a creator on Edgeware, with a “behind the scenes” angle explaining what we’re doing and how these platforms work, inspiring others to follow.
- I will write the book.
- While writing the book, our team will be active contributors to the DAO working group.
- One of the two part-time assistants will have this as their chief role, to support and grow the DAO working group.
- Where appropriate, my community’s ERC-20 token $LIFE can be used to experiment and give feedback on Edgeware and Commonwealth initiatives.
- I publish the book.
- I publish a final post-publication video
