# What is the Edgeware community fund? 

Edgeware has an on-chain public treasury composed of funds from several sources, including transaction fees, validator fees, and inflation. 

It is one of the very first examples of a _Layer 1 Protocol DAO_ - a digital community with it's own economic engine and organisational infrastructure, enabling the funding of creative contributions from a decentralised work-force. 

The blockchain uses the [Substrate SDK](https://substrate.dev) and is part of the emerging Polkadot ecosystem and was fairly launched without a funding event in 2018 as part of the [lockdrop](https://medium.com/commonwealth-labs/whats-in-a-lockdrop-194218a180ca) that gave it one of the [widest and fairest distributions in crypto](https://blog.edgewa.re/the-edgeware-lockdrop-debriefed/).

You can view the current holdings of the Community Fund by opening [Polkadot JS](https://polkadot.js.org/apps) which is an interface that offers live chain data and switching to Edgeware and clicking into the Treasury tab in the Governance drop down.  

Anyone can put forward a proposal to the community and as a result can be used for practically anything that benefits the development, utility and awareness of Edgeware - either directly or indirectly, the argument is yours to make.

# **Edgeware Proposal process **

In general, the process takes 9 days from proposal launch on-chain, but the drafting and engagement periods average 1-1.5 months, or even longer for larger requests or more complex projects. Word to the wise... in these early stages it takes longer than you think, so be patient and use the time to refine your project and push for feedback. 

## Step 1: Draft your proposal.

- See the proposal template above and create a Google Doc or [HackMD](https://hackmd.io/) post so others can collaborate on your responses openly. 
- Post a thread to Commonwealth.im/Edgeware with a link to the Google Doc and a short intro. This is a great chance to make a good first impression if you haven't been very involved in the community. 
- Take good notes of others feedback, and likely, integrate some ofthat feedback in the review of your draft. You may benefit from several rounds of this, which, while it can be tedious, go a long ways to satisfying the community.
- Once you've polished your proposal sufficiently, announce a final day for remarks. 
- We usually let 2-4 weeks pass in discussion, 4 for larger or more feedbacked proposals.
- After the final call for feedback, you're ready to prep for the on-chain steps.


## Optional: Creating a Multisig Escrow

- For proposals with requests greater than 5000 USD, you must use a multisig.
- A multisig will be established with one or more member of the Edgeware Council or reputable person in the community. 
- A multisig is a wallet where cooperation between multiple people is required to act. 
- In this case, we usually use a 2-of-3, meaning 2 people out of 3 total people can agree and the wallet action, usually a transaction, will occur. This multisig will be different than the wallet you conduct your projects' transactions from. 
- The multisig will hold the total funds for the project, and the representative (a 'curator') will create and approve disbursements from the wallet to your team or person's individual wallet, as milestones are reached.
- This helps make sure that the total amount is budgeted for, responsibly managed, and that milestones are fully incentivized. In the past, teams have failed to fulfill their agreements, but were able to walk away with the total amount regardless. 
- This multisig setup helps protect the Edgeware community from fraud, and helps ensure your team has the total funds budgeted, rather than asking the treasury repeatedly (a slow process.)
- Determine your co-signatories.
- The Agency member will construct the wallet and do a test transaction.
- Add the wallet to your DotApps (instructions coming)
- Provide the address of the wallet to the Agency if they don't already have it. 
- This address will go into the proposal technical details.


## Step 2. Construct and submit the proposal.
In Edgeware, treasury proposals have two stages:

- **Description On-chain: **The treasury proposal, which details the recipient and amount.
- **Community Approval:** The referendum/democracy proposal, where the entire community of Edgeware votes on approving or rejecting that treasury proposal.


## Create the Treasury Proposal
## 
**Sponsored Proposals**
Conducting a treasury proposal is technologically difficult and requires familiarity with the interface. As a result, the Agency or another Ecosystem Partner such as [Commonwealth Labs](https://commonwealth.im) or [Decent Partners](https://decent.partners) tends to sponsor proposals it supports, and creates the on-chain proposals for your project.

**Bond**
The Agency typically covers the 'bond' for proposals. The bond exists to prevent people from abusing the proposal system and protects against frivolous proposals or spamming. The bond is 5% of the total amount of funds requested.

## **Creation of the Proposal**
- In Governance>Democracy, select 'Submit preimage'.
- Under 'propose' option, select 'treasury' and 'proposeSpend' sub-option.
- Enter the funding request value in EDG. One can use [the automated tool](https://replit.com/@thomivy1/7-Day-Trailing-Avg-dollarEDG) for calculating the 7-Day trailing average to further calculate the proposal value in EDG.
- Under 'beneficiary', input the recipient address. (which will be the multi-sig address in most of the scenarios.) 
- Then copy and store the 'preimage hash' before clicking on 'Submit preimage'.


## Push the Proposal for Referenda
- This stage should be performed by the entity who is sponsoring the 5% bond amount. 
- In Governance>Democracy, select 'Submit proposal'.
- Enter the 'preimage hash' obtained from the previous stage.
- Set the signaling threshold amount (One can keep the default {100 EDG} as it is) and select 'Submit proposal'. Thus the proposal will be added to the signaling queue aka the 'Launch Stage'.


# Go Live
## Timeline (11-13 days total)
1. **2 days** for Launch Period aka Signaling Phase (independent cycle)
2. **7 days** for Referendum (Voting)
3. **1 day **for Dispatch Period
4. **3 days** for Treasury Spend Period (independent cycle)


Inform the community
- This pre-governance stage is very crucial to understand the sentiments of different Edgeware ecosystem stakeholders from their feedback. As this stage doesn't have any definitive time-period, the proposer can revise the proposal multiple times.


Launch Stage
- Once the Proposal is pushed for Referenda, it goes into siganling queue. Anyone can signal the proposal(s) by locking signaling threshold amount, usually 100 EDG by default but can be set higher. From the siganling queue, proposal with highest signals(kind of intend indicating secondary votes) will become the next referendum once the current cycle of Launch Period concludes. Thus other proposal(s) will need to wait for another Launch Period Cycle (+2days).


Referendum Stage
- Depending upon the voting outcome as per Referendum type (SimpleMajority or SuperMajority), the voting result gets finalised once the 7 days' Referendum Period is concluded.


Dispatch Stage
- Dispatch Period is a buffer of 1 day and is the last stage where the proposal can be reverted. Dispatch of an approved proposal confirms the treasury funding.


Treasury Spend Stage
- Treasury Spend Cycles (of 3 days) are continuous and thus the treasury funding of the proposal will vary based on when the cycle is ending. For example, if the Dispatch Period and Treasury Spend Period is concluding together, due to exact overlapping the next Treasury Spend Cycle will be considered and the whole on-chain process will take 13 days in total (max).  If 2 days of Treasury Spend Period are already passed at the time of Dispatch Period's conclusion, same Treasury Spend Cycle will be considered and thus the whole on-chain process will take 11 days in total (min).

## Receiving the Funds

**Grants Larger than 5000 USD**
You will be using a multisig with a minimum of 2-of-3 threshold. You will need to work with that Curator (a community representative on the multisig) and inform them of milestone achievements or funds needed.

**Grants smaller than 5000 USD**
If the grant is lower than 5000 USD, you probably will receive the disbursement directly to the account shown in your proposal template. You may control and use these through traditional transaction methods. (Link to Docs, Sending a Transaction)

**What if there are issues with our funds?**
Cutting-edge technology comes with growing pains. Sometimes, funds can be sent to the wrong address, the public market valuation of your EDG may change drastically, or other losses may occur. These will be judged on a case-by-case basis, but for the most part, there are three avenues to explore:

- **Active:** Re-proposal
- You may create a new treasury proposal and explain the need to cover the loss in a new post, follow the above guidance like the first proposal.
- **Not Yet Available**: Decentralized insurance
- Not on network currently. Would apply for smart contract cover primarily.
- **Plausible but unresearched, not available.:** Council action?
- Needs research. Playing with balances and transactions is dangerous. Not sure if technically possible.
