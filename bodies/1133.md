# Edgeware's Key Differentiators / Benefits

We recently funded the PR firm Market Across as one part of the Agency's developing communication initiatives. It is a 3 month contract and will help us drive awareness and interest around the project.

As part of their onboarding process, we have been asked to complete their Q&A document which helps them understand our priorities as a community.

Unlike the vast majority of projects, there is no central authority or team in Edgeware - only an emerging ecosystem of partners and projects who are bootstrapping the utility and awareness of the network from the ground up.  

In our continuing quest to ensure an open, accessible and participatory environment we will be developing this in the open.

To kickstart I have detailed a few elements that I believe offer's Edgeware a unique proposition.

**Important note:**

We do not need a thousand specific characteristics - we need a few things that offer the project a 10x advantage that can be communicated via our ongoing outreach and can form the foundations for the challenge of making "Edgeware a meme". 

When viewed through this lens, it is worth weighting the advantages you believe the community has when considering its emerging traits.

- **10x benefit**
- a benefit that is difficult if not impossible to replicate
- this benefit should ideally compound over time
- in general this is about long term thinking
- **example: the lockdrop distribution **
- **5x benefit**
- a benefit that puts the community into a select category of projects
- this benefit may be sustained over time
- in general this is about mid term thinking
- **example: the community treasury **
- **2x benefit**
- a benefit that is currently a small benefit over other projects
- this benefit is likely to disappear relatively quickly
- in general this is about short term thinking
- **example: the EVM **
- **example: Commonwealth.im**


## **10x: Wide, fair and engaged distribution via the lockdrop  **

Edgeware did not operate an ICO or any other fundraising event.

Instead the project was bootstrapped using the lockdrop, where holders of ETH could lock their tokens for 3, 6, 9 or 12 months in return for receiving EDG. 

It was also possible for smart contracts holding ETH to signal on behalf of their holdings, enabling Binance, Coinbase and Aragon to receive distributions. 

This approach resulted in a wide, fair and engaged distribution that makes the project one of the five best distributed projects in existence - ref [@dillon](/edgeware/account/iCLdev9eJBSaUSD1qnbq1roqcV6jZmfLpbLJjTdmUocixcY) [@Thom Ivy](/edgeware/account/jKauGVPCQkteGTDKuuNwhYhMBrj5QuytgybEC4A5cS4Bki5)'s [gini-coefficient analysis](https://main.edgeware.wiki/edgeware-runtime/lockdrop/gini-coefficient-of-edgeware). 

> The total ETH locked in the standard process finalized at 1,199,728 ETH, with signaled funds at 4,346,544 ETH. This demonstrates strong confidence and interest in both the contracts underlying the lockdrop, but also the advance in blockchain technology that Edgeware represents. Today there exist over 4000 different Edgeware addresses largely created in the lockdrop process, from over 10,000 transactions.

## **5x: Community treasury and organisational structures**

As noted in both the [Edgeware Grant Programme Structure post](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1110-edgeware-grant-programme-structure-discussion) and in the [MetaDAO](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/513-metadao-pooling-onchain-funding-for-public-good-proposals) thread, Edgeware exists as one of a small number of **layer 1 protocol DAOs, **in a sea of well capitalised smart contract DAOs on Ethereum such as Uniswap, Balancer and API3. 

> There is an opportunity for a certain group of projects to benefit from the (crypto) mainstreaming of DAOs with **sustainable funding**, **governance via proposal systems** and **decentralised contributions.**
> This narrative is well underway as evidenced by the last couple of months hype/speculation around 'governance tokens' and more specifically the arrival of [Substrate](https://substrate.dev/) based chains, all of which have their own treasuries, governance and multi-million $ treasuries. 
> For most people, the first generation of DAOs primarily consisted of DAOstack, Moloch and Aragon - an area that has failed to live up to adoption expectations. 
> All have all been donation / individual based and therefore are not sustainable in nature since there is no perpetual funding mechanism of the sort pioneered by [Decred](https://decred.org) that gives the ability to invest consistently over the long term. 
> The number of projects with self-sustaining on-chain treasuries will increase exponentially over the next 12 months with [Cosmos SDK](https://cosmos.network/sdk) being the base for Aragon's [own chain](https://aragon.org/chain). 
> This also doesn't include existing projects with treasuries / founder rewards such as DASH and ZCash, whose issues with transparency and impact have been noted [elsewhere.](https://medium.com/@richardred/observations-of-the-dash-treasury-dao-c94231b2b5c4)

## **2x: EVM compatibility  / Commonwealth.im**

**EVM compatibility**

Right now Edgeware is also one of the only chains in the Substrate ecosystem with a working EVM, but that benefit will soon be adopted by many other chains.

Outside of Substrate it is already being competed away as NEAR, Solana and many other chains add compatibility.

This means that Edgeware can rapidly onboard projects that have launched on Ethereum and this will confer some short term advantages, however these partnerships are no substitute for building projects that offer unique new characteristics or in building our own culture.  

**Commonwealth IM**

Commonwealth.im - the interface / organisational infrastructure on which you are reading this is a vital component of the project and gives us the ability to discuss, organise and fund people and projects. 

Although this interface is open to any project, the connection between Commonwealth Labs - the creators of the interface and indeed the launch team behind Edgeware gives the project and its community a chance to pioneer the use and the capabilities of the interface.

Edgeware and its community are power users of the interface, by far the most active project and as a result this enables us to figure out 'decentralised organisation' from the ground up, dog-fooding innovations ahead of the wider market.  

This dogfooding has led to the creation of the [Edgeware Agency](https://edgeware.agency) - a decentralised community foundation, run by community contributors and funded by the chain's community treasury.
