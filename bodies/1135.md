## **Intro**

**Challenge: **_Crypto is booming, and so are the media companies who cover it. Newsrooms and publishers are applying conventional media models and discovering that it works when it’s applied to a high growth vertical like crypto._

Why does this matter? A healthy information landscape is vital to a fast-moving space like crypto. Investors, builders and users need high-quality information to understand the space they’re in, and where it’s going. Strong media companies are an important part of that information landscape. 

As the media opportunity in crypto grows—and media elsewhere falters—there will be growing demand among investors and founders to understand how to cover the crypto landscape. That’s why we’re creating this podcast: to bring transparency to the media business, and to guide the influx of new capital and energy towards building high-quality and robust models. 

As a community run project with no central team or authority, Edgeware highly values publicly accessible research and development that can benefit both the collective intelligence of the project’s emerging community and the wider ecosystem as a whole. 

The grant to create this first limited run series will be a key contribution to not just the Edgeware community, but the wider crypto markets as a whole. It will also set the stage for future explorations into Web3-native media models. 

## **Solution **
How To Make It in Crypto-media

**Subtitle**
Crypto is booming, and so are the media companies who cover it. Here’s a guide to how they did it, and what comes next. ✅ 

**Podcast Description ✅ **
Crypto is a hyper-growth industry, going from nothing to a market value of $2 trillion in just over a decade. But it’s also a cyclical business, where the booms can create fortunes, and the busts can leave players wrecked.

How to Make it in Crypto Media will take you through the main pillars of a media business—from distribution to editorial and sales—applied to the fascinating vertical crypto. We’ll talk to publishers and editors in chief, rank-and-file reporters and salesmen, about what they did right—or wrong—to navigate the complexities of an industry that has blitz-scaled.  

Joon Ian Wong has covered crypto since 2013 and has seen the industry from the newsroom and the business side. He’s founded the industry’s most important annual gathering, and a non-profit for journalists covering it. He’ll be your guide through the thriving world of crypto media.
