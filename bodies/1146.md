## Highlights
- Designer [Sean Ford](https://twitter.com/_seanford) completed the journey of the [Edgeware Brand Identity Process](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/975-edgeware-brand-identity-process-concluded).
- AMA with [Zien.io](https://zien.io/) got a huge response from the community especially for their [unique proposal](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1129-proposal-bring-zien-nfts-via-whatsapp-to-edgeware) aiming to make art easily accessible via Whatsapp messenger.
- Welcome to the new ecosystem partner project ‘[Knit Finance](https://blog.edgewa.re/kedg-by-knitfinance/)’ which aims to deploy wrapped Edgeware tokens aka kEDG on multiple chains.

- [Shared Node Services](https://onfinality.medium.com/onfinality-providing-free-shared-node-services-to-edgeware-26144fcb9170) by OnFinality are now live!
- Introducing DEX on Edgeware EVM: [PicoSwap](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1138-introducing-picoswap-and-pico)
- Aniket Jindal from Biconomy [opened a discussion](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1143-meta-transactions-on-edgeware) for a grant proposal to fund the deployment of Biconomy’s Meta Transaction infrastructure in Edgeware’s EVM environment.
