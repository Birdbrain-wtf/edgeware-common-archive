New [thread](https://twitter.com/thom_ivy_1/status/1391900642709544967) from Thom about his vision for Edgeware:

- Edgeware becomes a community token for the real world & digital world
- $EDG grants access to real-world services like gyms, coworking spaces, and community events
- And digital services like Zien NFTs, online spaces, and access to paid online tools


Holding (and perhaps locking/staking) EDG would grant citizenship. We'd onboard new members to the network using treasury grants. As the community grows, the network would become more powerful.

Edgeware becomes an appealing place to incubate new projects and protocols because we do a good job of supporting builders with PR, distribution, and community.

People use exchanges, lending protocols, NFTs, and other $EDG-based tech because we incubate thoughtful, ambitious builders (maybe out of the coworking spaces and community events sponsored by the network).

---

This is just my take - I've been helping connect the dots behind the scenes a little.

I'm excited about it for a few reasons. It brings us a new core team and a *lot* of talent and potential for new partnerships.

It also complements Commonwealth's vision for the network, which is focused on Edgeware as a forward-thinking Polkadot smart contract chain.

I don't think it distracts from our focus and I think the expenses are reasonable.

We are not the only group building these kinds of virtual organizations either. There's an 500-person group I know that is working on something similar and fielding a very strong team. Other thoughtful people in the space will be publishing more research soon.
