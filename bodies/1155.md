## My angle:
I believe that the fair distribution of Edgeware makes it the go-to EVM compatible substrate layer 1. 

But in order for Edgeware to achieve maximum success, it should be Ethereum aligned. This alignment will allow certain projects that are “Ethereum first” to successfully migrate over to Edgeware when they want to get exposure to the Polkadot/Substrate ecosystem. 

While new novel projects that never existed outside of Ethereum are still desirable (and arguably necessary for the success of any L1), the simple narrative aspect of alignment can pay dividends. This can best be seen by the recent adoption of Polygon, which is (in current form) a Proof of Stake EVM fork that is ‘Ethereum Aligned’ long term.

## How can I help?
While the Agency has done excellent work including a killer recent rebranding; there are still things missing that the community would likely benefit from. 

I believe that my content creation skills mixed with my deep experience actively using all things crypto / decentralized applications makes me an excellent candidate to develop, host, record and distribute an Edgeware Community podcast.

## Format:
While this ultimately will change over time, I believe the most optimal is a weekly podcast (with video as well) in interview format highlighting a single Edgeware project, community member, announcement...etc.

The best comparable would be [Relay Chain](https://twitter.com/RelayChain), which is funded by Parity Foundation. They have ~40 minute episodes highlighting projects within the Substrate and Polkadot ecosystem.

## Distribution:
The audio version will be distributed to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Google Podcasts, Stitcher, iHeart Radio, TuneIn, Alexa, Overcast, PocketCasts, Castro, Castbox, and Podchaser.

The video version will be distributed to YouTube and additional venues per community request.

Both versions will be distributed to Twitter, for further distribution by the community.

## Transcription:
For those who prefer reading, a transcript will be included for each episode.

## Objectively Neutral:
This podcast would not accept sponsorship money, as it’s completely and directly funded by the community treasury. 

This aligns incentives to only support/promote things that directly benefits the Edgeware ecosystem and community.
