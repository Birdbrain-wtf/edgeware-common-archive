On behalf of the Edgeware Agency, I have drafted this [Edgware Culture & Values survey](https://forms.gle/z7JX4sfXv93PkaAS8). Our hope in launching this survey is to collect information about our community's beliefs and values. We will consider _all_ contributions as we work to make our cultural values legible and design, refine practices to emphasize these values.

It's necessary to differentiate from other protocols and Edgeware's strength has always been in our community. We hope to lean into our values to polarize and attract community members who resonate with our community's ideals.

The results of this survey will be summarized in a way that is accessible and valuable. This survey will not be the only opportunity to share your perspective but it will be the foundation of this process.
