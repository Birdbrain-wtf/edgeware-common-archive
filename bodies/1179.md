## Q2 Update

For this period (since the end of April 2021) following our [delivered proposal](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/democracyproposal/30-onfinality-public-nodes-for-edgeware) for the initial setup of this pool of Edgeware nodes, we are running a single cluster of node pools in Tokyo. In the Edgeware node pool, we’re running 3 large nodes (1.5 vCPUs and 4.8GB ram). 

We’re currently serving more than 300,000 API requests daily for our single Edgeware cluster. It’s inspiring for our team to see the growth and usage of this service so soon after launch. I wanted to give the Edgeware Community an update on where we are, and what our next steps could be.

## Optional Enhancements 

This post discusses a few enhancements that may be considered by the Edgeware community. 

### Intelligent Routing
The first one is to use our [delivered intelligent routing](https://kusama.polkassembly.io/treasury/72) enhancements to double the number of clusters to 2 (perhaps Ireland and Tokyo). Intelligent geolocation routing allows us to maximise the use of different pools of nodes across our user base in different geographic regions, and allows us to quickly fall over to alternative pools in the case that one goes offline. This gives the following benefits: 

- Global scale and reduced latency for better performance 
- Increased service resiliency (if one cluster goes down we can fall over to the other) 
- Additional scale to support >1m daily requests 

A second cluster will double the costs (excluding traffic from a single cluster). As a result, we would see Edgeware’s monthly cost double from USD $1,161.50 to $2,323.00 in EDG tokens. 

### Test Network Support 

The second is to add support to Edgeware’s testnet, Beresheet. We can add another separate clusters to our Tokyo region to support Beresheet. A shared node API service reduces the barriers of entry to trying out Beresheet in terms of cost, time, expertise. We manage all the nodes for our users and distribute them across the world to achieve global scalability from day 1, we monitor each node and ensure that they meet certain service levels. In summary, we try to reduce the pain of getting started on Beresheet and minimise the costs of accessing secure nodes. 

A Beresheet cluster will almost double the costs (depending on the storage requirements of Beresheet).

**We're interested in hearing the community's thoughts around these two potential enhancements ahead of our next proposal for the Q2 costs of running our existing Edgeware Cluster**
