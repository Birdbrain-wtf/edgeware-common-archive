### Big Roadblocks for Telegram Open Network 

Telegram Open Network (TON) has had a tumultuous month. On October 11th, the United States Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) filed an emergency restraining order to stop the network from releasing it tokens to initial investors, declaring that the initial coin sale (which hit $1.7 million) was illegal. On October 12th, the TON Telegram Channel saw all messages from board members deleted and the announcement of an immediate, temporary hold on development (Partz). 

When TON originally commenced their token sale, investors were told that they would receive their money back if the network did not launch on the promised date of October 31, 2019. Since the SEC injunction, the initial investors have agreed to allow the launch date to be shifted to April 30, 2020. An SEC hearing that was scheduled for October 24th has also been moved to February 2020, with Telegram describing the shift positively. However, investors are not all so positive. According to a Coindesk interview with an anonymous investor, the source believes that TON will not be able to settle with the SEC, and the network will eventually lose the US as a market, as well as other huge communities in China, Russia, and around Europe (Foxley). 

According to Coin Telegraph, the SEC’s issue is with the way Telegram filed for the right to sell a security. In February of 2019, Telegram filed with the SEC for this right and received approval, but with the caveat that the security token can only be sold to accredited initial investors. Despite the fact that the initial coin sale was made to accredited investors, the SEC believes that the coins will easily be sold to other, non-accredited investors, which they argue puts Telegram in violation of their regulations (Linver). 

Experts across the field stand on both sides of how strong this claim is. There is certainly a feeling that the SEC would not usually make this sort of case for other traditional types of securities, and that this is a purely reactionary move against growing cryptocurrency power. However, given the recent example of Block.One being able to settle with the SEC for not registering their tokens as securities at all (they reportedly stated on their site that US citizens were prohibited from taking part in the initial coin offering, but didn’t actually engage in prevention), some think that it is possible that a deal can be made (Palley).

### TON in Context: Comparisons to Libra & Potentially Novel Structure

In light of the attention the SEC has given to the sale, many comparisons have been drawn between Telegram Open Network and the Libra network. SEC scrutiny is not the only thing these networks have in common, though. Both projects have a huge potential initial user base, due to their current successful sister applications. Obviously, Facebook has a vast global reach, but Telegram is also one of the most popular messaging applications in the world with an estimated ~500 million users (Binance Blog). 

However, despite these comparisons, some key differences exist between the proposed protocols. While Libra is a stablecoin meant to change the infrastructure of money, TON goes much further than offering a way to shift how money is moved. According to a Binance Research report on the network, TON “to radically advance the entire blockchain stack,” with nine infrastructural elements, including:

- A state-channel platform used for payments and off-chain value transfers
- A Domain Name System (DNS) that creates domain names in a human-readable format
- A queryable distributed hash-table that allows for locating other nodes in the network 
- Other elements meant to allow for application interaction across browsers and devices
- A proxy to hide IP addresses of users and nodes 
- A peer-to-peer network
- A distributed database to store information
- An interface for external application integration of TON on-chain applications (Binance Research)

The Binance report describes the innovation behind these features as marking “the birth of the 5th generation of blockchains, with features such as dynamic sharding, “tight-coupling” for blockchain interoperability, and multichain networks.” The network also highly focuses on its scalability, claiming the ability to process millions of transactions a second due to its sharding structure (Binance Research). However, both the Binance report and many others question this claim.

Beyond the usual drawing of parallels to Libra, which the Binance Research group starkly rejects, the privacy element of Telegram Open Network also begs its comparison to projects like Elixxr. Elixxr, introduced by David Chaum, is a response to the lack of digital sovereignty over personal information in traditional online applications (Web3 Foundation). Much like Telegram’s TON will seek to do, Elixxr mixes messaging and payments, but without any metadata leaks. According to Chaum, Elixxr will implement a novel consensus algorithm, using teams of nodes for batches of messages. The project, like TON, is designed with the goal of also expanding a dApp ecosystem. 

### More Red Flags in TON’s Evolution

There is much more unknown about the TON model than other comparable networks. For instance, the TON whitepaper is intentionally withholding about its asynchronous Proof of Stake (PoS) variant. Moreover, the code for the project has been largely hidden, not freely available online, making it difficult to identify potential attack vectors. This fact, along with the design of smart contracts using a fairly isolated language, Fift, suggests that the TON developer community will remain small, homogenous, and centralized (Binance Research). These are all serious problems for a chain that clearly wants to compete with other ecosystem-designing projects like Polkadot. 

### Outlooks 

It seems that development on TON is stagnant for now. Only time will tell whether the project can become a successful privacy communications and payment app, rivaling David Chaum’s, or even go as far as the initial goal of serving as an ecosystem builder like the Polkadot network.
