There seems to be general alignment on some proposals - primarily around those bringing new tech and builders to the network. There also seems to be more disagreement around proposals to do with determining the network's strategy, and how we make strategic financial allocations.

With this in mind, I'm tailoring Stage 2 to a few governance priorities:

- **More detailed feedback on proposals.**
- Gathering feedback on proposals is one of the most important functions of our governance system, and our current feedback system relies on forum-length responses.
- It's difficult to keep track of issues in a long forum thread.
- Sometimes we write proposals in separate documents, like Google Docs, but that poses a number of problems - they get updated out of sync with the thread, and sometimes deleted as people move between organizations.
- Commonwealth is a community space and docs are private spaces - it's less natural to provide detailed feedback outside of the "framework" that Commonwealth provides - vs. how people seem pretty comfortable answering Google Docs when they are explicitly cc'ed for feedback.
- Right now, in depth conversation around longer proposals seems to happen productively in private - not really in public.
- **Governance redesign / Providing a clear history of Edgeware governance.**
- It was suggested in the Stage 1 proposal, that Stage 2 would include redesigning our governance pages.
- Some of this has been done already, which you can see on the proposals/referenda/bounties pages. The harder backend work has been done, and you can now see a history of REFs, PROPs, treasury proposals, and bounties.
- The goal is to have a detailed backlog of all proposals that have gone through the system, to inform future proposers coming to the chain.


**As is, Stage 2 focuses on Google Docs-style inline commenting on proposals. **There have been requests for threading and the ability to reply directly underneath comments - this is a variation on that which I think will work better.

**The larger goal is to support more in-depth discussion of proposals before they go to voting.** The goal is to get community members reading proposals with a highlighter, so they can jump into any deep dives or potential issues immediately.

This feature could also be used as a way to poll the community and collect better feedback - for example, polling the community by writing a list of questions in a thread & asking everyone to comment on each question.

## Summary

Expand the ability of the community to present nuanced feedback to proposals, so proposal writers can evolve their ideas in response.

## Stage 2 Deliverables

- [ ] **Inline comments on threads.**
- Adding the ability to create inline comments, by highlighting text of a thread and commenting in a popup.
- Inline comments should appear to the right of the main thread (on desktop) and in a pop-up (on mobile).
- [ ] **Table editor for threads.**
- Adding support for tables to the Commonwealth editor.
- [ ] **Governance pages redesign.**
- [x] Gather more consistent data in governance pages, including a full record of historical proposals, linked threads, and linked threads' titles.
- [x] Link proposals to threads, and vice versa.
- [x] Link REFs to PROPs to treasury proposals.
- [ ] Finish redesigning the governance pages to provide better presentation of this new information.
- **Improving the new-user experience / user experience for pinned threads.**
- [ ] Redesign pinned threads, to make it more clear that pinned threads are **announcements** or **community pages**, for multiple people to edit.
