# **Edgeware DGov **

This thread is an outline and primer on projects related to Edgeware and DGov. This is not a funding proposal. 

**What is Edgeware DGov?**

[DGov Network ](https://dgov.network)is a nascent chain that aims to develop governance in Web 3.0, founded by Ramsey. **Edgeware DGov** is the projects and proposals toward shared strategic objectives. DGov chain can also be the first out of Edgewares chain factory "incubator/accelerator".

## **Objectives**
There are two main technical objectives for Edgeware DGov currently: a) enabling cross-chain governance and b) enabling layer 2 governance for sub-communities. 

**What will it take for a substrate chain to stake governance into another substrate chain? **

The parity-bridge-common pallet working group has delivered functionality that enables two substrate chains to relay information to each other, without necessarily being connected through a relay network.

This bridging is a more simple version of the relay chain that trades off efficiency and low-cost transactions with a simple way of communicating validated data across chains.

Gavin made a promise that some DOTs could be sent to Kusama. A team was set up to build something that allows for bridging to happen. They did, and [here](https://github.com/paritytech/parity-bridges-common) you can test their pallet and communicate data between two test chains.

**How can we starting experimenting with these new Substrate features?**

This idea enables some interesting use cases. The first interesting - and useful - use case that comes to mind is sharing voting power between chains. 

**What will it take for Edgeware to have a governance stake in the DGov chain?**

Initial aims and objectives:

Send test DGov to EDG multi-sig wallet. 

This sounds like an interesting objective. Some questions that come to mind: after achieving the initial aim, which is to send some dGOV tokens to a multi-sig owned EDG account, what will it take for a significant amount of dGOV tokens to be managed by Edgeware's main on-chain governance (treasury)? What possibilities will emerge from this? After this is achieved, what if DGov's on-chain governance also had a stake in Edgeware's governance? A sort of bi-directional governance. We can envisage a web of governance between chains. Would this be the beginnings of decentralized unions and confederations?

**Retaining talent by letting them go**
Substrate made it simple for individuals and small groups to set up their own blockchain. Perhaps it's optimistic to attract and retain all the talented engineers in one chain, and perhaps unnecessary. Gone is the day where there needs to be one community for only one chain. What if there was one community for multiple chains? Please excuse me if i'm late to the party. Reading some of the other threads from Edgeware Agency and Commonwealth, it seems they are also thinking roughly along these lines.

As we can see in parity more than a few of their talent go work there and then eventually move out doing something else. Given this natural progression, how can we attract talent and help them to build their own thing and then take a get a voting stake in their project?

## **Chain Factory**
As discussed over various chats and calls with other Edgeware members. A chain factory model, where Edgeware is essentially a launchpad for new substrate chains, would be a great way to incubate and channel talent and projects. Chain Factory could be part of the treasury funding thesis. Y Combinator's [Startup School ](https://startupschool.org)and [Pioneer](https://pioneer.app) are interesting accelerator gamified systems that we could draw inspiration from and partner with in order to drive talent through Edgeware. DGov could be the first chain to emerge from Edgeware's Chain Factory. Chain Factory could also invite other treasuries to stake shares in Edgeware to fund new chains... further exploration and development required.
