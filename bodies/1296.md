As I look at other Polkadot and Substrate chains, I detect that we are the only chain (that the have data on,)  that does not institute a hard cap on supply.

[https://www.parachain.live/](https://www.parachain.live/)

I am wondering whether it would be beneficial for us to set an interim (soft?) cap, obviously subject to future governance action, that will help us  convey more accurately the spirit if not the letter of our disinflationary monetary policy.  

I am especially curious how this intersects with Parachain status: 

- Inflation is used to incentivize network security, something we will pass to DOT as a parachain.
- Inflation also feeds the treasury, but in an ideal setting this is funded purely from a portion of transaction funds, more effectively aligning incentives between treasury growth and network growth. 


An interim cap could be established that effectively targets the date of parachain initiation, so as to not interfere with network security, raise interest in staking today - as there will be a planned cut-off,  and to improve investment thesis on the network.
