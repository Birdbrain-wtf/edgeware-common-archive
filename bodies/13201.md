General updates: 
The Diversification Fund has been active for a bit over the year now, and wow has it been a crazy experiment in DAO governance, community strategy, communications and quantitative reasoning. The markets have DONE A LOT (the caps are a real understatement) being influenced by regulators, scammers, bad code and just sketchy executing teams. 

Throughout this time we have managed to steer clear of a lot of these past year’s market pitfalls and not get too ahead of our skis in earmarking some of the funds into assets that would push the risk for higher APY and asset gains. 

Fund updates + strategy: 
Our policy has varied through this year’s experiment with allocations that would increase or decrease depending on the tumultuous markets; however we have remained committed in allocating for underlying L1 assets along with the specific blue chip protocols across DeFi and Metaverse categories.

Future steps: 
Despite a community call discussion that was held last week to continue the experiment for another year, we feel that the Diversification Fund should be shut down and the assets be either held in the DAO treasury or liquidated to stable and held in the DAO treasury. We made this decision after a lot of thought, as our ability to properly steward the experiment will diminish due to increasing commitments of time in our respective jobs and projects. 

We believe that we have accomplished what we set out to do in the experiment in exploring the idea of assets being trusted to a subgroup of individuals in the DAO and helping manage the treasury, while continuing on the educational mission of learning more about the respective ecosystems, markets and industry as a whole. Furthermore we are extremely grateful to the community for allowing us to conduct this experiment with special thanks to Shankar, Thom and Zovern for continuing to engage and support each of us and the experiment with input and guidance. 

Thank you.

**Plan for return on remaining funds:**


As we know, the ‘Diversification Fund’ initiative completed one year and the team wants to return the funds to the Edgeware Community. The decision to end this initiative was taken due to time constraints faced by the fund managers in order to actively manage the fund. Based on further discussions, we have 4 ways to return the funds to the community.

We’ve arranged them in a flowchart-like model and based on the result of this poll one of the 3 remark-based referendums will be created.

1st remark-based referendum
- Aye: Transfer of the custody to active contributors
All the funds will be transferred to a newly created address, which will have a volunteer from the community(preferably Shankar/Zovern). These funds can be further distributed to the active contributors or be used for other purposes via a proposal.
**If this option is suitable, propose for referendum 1 to be created.**
- Nay: 2nd remark-based referendum
If the above option is not suitable, read choices for referendum 2.

2nd remark-based referendum
- Aye: Full liquidation of the fund to EDG
All the funds will be transferred to a newly created address, which will have a volunteer from the community(preferably Shankar/Zovern). These funds will be then converted to EDG.
**If this option is suitable, propose for referendum 2 to be created.**
- Nay: 3rd remark-based referendum
If the above option is not suitable, read choices for referendum 3.

3rd remark-based referendum
- Aye: Liquidation of just the stable tokens
All the funds will be transferred to a newly created address, which will have a volunteer from the community(preferably Shankar/Zovern). Only stablecoins will be then converted to EDG.
- Nay: Liquidation of just the non-stable tokens
All the funds will be transferred to a newly created address, which will have a volunteer from the community(preferably Shankar/Zovern). Only tokens other than stablecoins will be then converted to EDG.
**If any of these options are suitable, propose for referendum 3 to be created.**
