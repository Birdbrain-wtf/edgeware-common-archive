## My strategic distillation of the responses
[These comments are my own and I anticipate disagreement from members of the community at-large _and _members of the Agency. Please be civil - ask questions, state what you disagree with and why. I am coming from a somewhat-outsider perspective, you likely have perspective that I don't.]

**Anticipated meaningful contributions to society and priorities for positioning:**

DAOs and Governance, Web 3 Media, and Interoperability - everything else will be people leveraging the smart contract functionality to realize their own visions.

We stay on the cutting edge as far as Substrate and build specialized tooling to support these other differentiating priorities

**What value is Edgeware currently providing:**

Respondees do not feel like that chain is currently providing much value. It's unclear to them how we're currently solving users' problems other than that we can distribute token they can sell to cover their runway. They are still here and contributing but this emphasizes the need to focus on aligning people with the longer-term vision._ I am optimistic that this process will help to clarify an alignment between our values and strategic vision._

Potential topics to consider moving forward:

_What specific problems will we be solving in 1-2 years? Who are we solving them for?_

_What specific problems will we be solving in 5 years? Who are we solving them for?_

**Top 3 Values to lean into:**

- Experimental/Frontier-mentality/Cutting-edge
- Honesty/Trust
- Community-first

_Next steps:_

1. Agree that these are generally the top 3 values of our community, if we were forced to choose
2. Refine the language of each of the 3, ie. Honesty should be the value, not Trust, because <reasons>
3. Elaborate on those terms with copy which can be added to the website and reinforced internally within our community through various channels
4. Discuss how we can **act out** these values by creating **practices and rituals** which embody our values, 
5. ie. We host a group hack-a-thon/sprint to deliver on the implementation of a feature which leverages the cutting-edge of Substrate - including having people organize an inspired marketing campaign to tell the story
6. ie. We create a template to construct CW posts in a respectful manner. If we have concerns, we ask specific, genuine questions in good faith which we believe will either resolve our concerns or confirm our suspicions. 


**My beliefs about our vibe/brand positioning:**

_Lean into friend:_

We want to be a pleasure to work with and focus on being welcoming [treat people like they're a future friend until they're acting like an asshole].

_Lean into Young & Innovative:_

We're Edgeware, not Matureware. We need to provide a secure chain and stay up-to-date with Substrate but we should be pushing the envelop. We don't want to be chaotic and not have clear priorities. We should be proposing hypotheses and making calculated, experimental bets.

_Mixed strategy between Playful and Serious:_

The audience of people who are interested in our use-cases are mostly young people. We want to be cool and capture people's attention. But we should keep the mission-driven aspects of our work front and center

_Mixed strategy on Mass Appeal and Elite:_

Funds and time should be primarily allocated to those who are elite. We want to be recruiting excellent people to deliver both technical and media projects. Right now, our resources are scarce and spending too much time dealing with low quality contributors is not ideal. However, we don't want to be too exclusive. We do want to foster an open, polycultural environment and create a great UX. Edgeware should be accessible to people who share our values and vision.

**Niche targeted audiences to reach out to:**

As products launch which tangibly solve audiences' problems, we should figure out ways to connect and bring them in.

Several people mentioned the reality that we need more engineering talent to continue to develop the chain. Honestly, this makes me think that we should be doing our best to lean into the Polkadot community and try to engage some of their developers, pique their interest. There may be some issues in the past but folks at Parity certainly want to have utility in their ecosystem just as badly as we do. Edgeware should be trying to capture their imagination - they can receive EDG as upside for completing bounties, or payment for supporting projects we've allocated money for already. They likely already have DOT so they're incentive aligned.

**Please do not hesitate to ask any questions or make suggestions for ways in which we can further engage y'all in this process. This should not consume too many of our scarce resources but can be beneficial for differentiating our brand and culture from rival protocols. These kinds of activities can anchor a community: enabling us to make more-aligned strategic decisions, recruit people who share our values, and coalesce around a shared identity (not just our shared bags).**
