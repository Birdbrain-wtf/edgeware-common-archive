## Introduction

The mainnet launch has been disappointing in light of the successful Lockdrop campaign. The complexity, instability and novel character of the Substrate tech stack has been underestimated. Consequently a rather active community with high expectations went into hibernation mode (waiting for a relaunch). 

We propose to test and fix bugs under an incentivized regime before mainnet launch codenamed Burnt Oak.

[https://commonwealth-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/47ec08bf-a31d-4d77-9ee1-fad40b2b3227.png]

The goal of Burnt Oak is a wider ownership distribution according to one’s contribution. 

Bleeding-edge technology requires certain effort in terms of testing. Burnt Oak invites  a wide set of individuals to test  for the rich set of different setups and combinations before the production phase.

This approach is inspired by the so called [Game of Stakes](https://medium.com/coinmonks/breaking-down-the-cosmos-game-of-stakes-5cbc538bcedb) - incentivized testnet period before launch of Cosmos network, an approach followed by other PoS protocols. 

As there is no concrete plan for allotment of outstanding 2.5% EDG tokens from genesis allocation (whitepaper states that these funds should be used as a reward for testnet/early participants and early contributors outlined also [here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/48) and [here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/49) ), we propose that the community defines concrete rules and mechanics for their distribution, laying the foundation for an ongoing bug bounty program, funding of Edgeware development etc..

This is a draft proposal, and a request for participation. All numbers are just tentative and we invite the community to propose more meaningful rewards. 

## Main Objectives

### Fixing Bugs

Identify and fix all possible hidden issues and establish an on-going bug bounty program funded from 50% of block rewards assigned to treasury. We draw inspiration from other bug bounties using owasp methodology (see picture below). 

[https://commonwealth-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/980de639-8474-4470-b934-c3f9b27d9f34.png]

Our proposal is to reward people from community who find: 

    1. Critical / high (priority) issue with 0.01% of EDG tokens from genesis allocation 
    2. Medium (priority) issue with 0.001% of EDG tokens from genesis allocation
    3. Minor (priority) issue with 0.0001% of EDG tokens from genesis allocation

If someone is able to write a fix then her reward is 3 times bigger e.g. in case of finding a critical bug and successfully fixing it she gets 0.01% + 0.03% of EDG tokens from genesis allocation. The final decision about the level of impact / risk should be considered by the Council, adhering  to the technical community. After the launch of the mainnet, reporting bugs should be in private communication with Commonwealth Labs or members of the Council to prevent abusing zero day exploits or causing any form of damage. 

## Rewards for early contribution

Edgeware’s proposition is to become the first smart contract parachain within the Polkadot network. The difference between an actively used platform and one that is obsolete lies in  variety of tooling for developers rather than number of transactions per second. Luckily, being part of the Polkadot universe allows to utilize a wide set of tooling developed by grantees for the whole Substrate ecosystem. We mean for example integration of test suites like MixBytes [Github](https://github.com/mixbytes/tank.ansible-polkadot/blob/master/tasks/blockchain/prod.yml) , Chainhammer [Github](https://github.com/mixbytes/tank.ansible-polkadot/blob/master/tasks/blockchain/prod.yml). Preferably, we would like to see more customized test scripts, to minimize the chance of potential issues. Integrator is expected to perform a thorough test run after integration.

### Rewards:
0.04 % of EDG gen. allocation in case of third party sw integration 
0.08 % of EDG gen. allocation in case of creating their own 

Integration of staking tooling making validating easier and is seen as the second highest priority. This includes manuals with best practices. 

### Potential rewards in this section: 

Manuals 0.002 % 
Integrations 0.02 % 

Third highest priority right now (but most important in the long run) should be integration or creation of smart contract tooling for writing smart contracts, their testing and deployment. We would also like to see the first templates of common smart contracts. For successful accomplishment of this section one can earn 0.05 % of EDG gen. allocation in case of third party sw integration. In case of creating their own tool a 0.1 % of EDG gen. allocation would be apt.  


## Validator Decentralization

### Familiarization of wider Edgeware community with Edgeware tech and staking.

Cartelization is one of the biggest challenges of PoS chains. Large set of validators is desirable in order to achieve a robust network. Edgeware did not have more than 45 validators. Commonwealth Labs was proposing 50 validators which indicates we find ourselves below the recommended number. In order to improve the situation we propose incentives for running a node during 2-3 weeks before the final launch. This period should be a custom version of Game of Stakes (Burnt Oak phase).     
 

Run a node two weeks prior with at least 95 % uptime could be rewarded by 0.007 % of EDG g.a. up to individual 150 nodes. 
We can also consider a reward for smaller actions such as transacting, voting, etc. for 0.0001% of EDG g.a.
