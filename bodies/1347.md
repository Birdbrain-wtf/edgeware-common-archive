## Collective Intelligence

We've been talking a lot lately about how we can better join the dots between people, projects and funding through increasingly transparent and accountable processes.

In simple terms this can be boiled down to a pursuit of getting the highest quality information, to the best qualified people, at the right time, with a community fund to accelerate things from then on. 

We can call this _collective intelligence_ and in an age of [DAOs, defi and infinite leverage](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1110-edgeware-grant-programme-structure-discussion) it is pretty much the most important and valuable resource a community can have.

## EDGmesh - an Edgeware philosophy

Following on in the grand tradition of EDG... monikers such as [@Thom Ivy](/edgeware/account/jKauGVPCQkteGTDKuuNwhYhMBrj5QuytgybEC4A5cS4Bki5)'s EDGpass and EDGports, it's time to coin another: _EDGmesh_.

EDGmesh isn't a _product_, it's a mindset initially and an evolving system after - our spin on the DeFi Lego meme, a way of thinking about, developing and building bridges between the people and projects that make Edgeware their home... now and in the future.

An **EDGmesh** mindset sees connections where others don't, it looks curiously at the jigsaw pieces of the evolving Edgeware community and steps on from the _[disagree but support](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/961-edgeware-as-an-incubator) _approach to community building which is predicated on wildly different values and motivations that cannot find common ground.

For me we can flip this into a _**question and connect **_philosophy that seeks to better build bridges (technical, cultural and financial) between our relative endeavours.

In the end, we are all building on the same [Substrate](https://substrate.dev), literally and figuratively and our collective success depends on us being able to not just tolerate each other, but actively collaborate as we share skills and solve problems across a vast array of challenges.

The delegation of our community treasury compounds this need to cooperate - more so than in Ethereum, but this also gives us a distinct advantage, because we can coordinate in a more focused way around a shared pool of funding. 

This will ensure we:

- Make our scarce resources (funding / talents / time) go further with greater impact
- Dog-food each others concepts, proposals and projects
- Be open-minded to the way we can all add value in different ways 
- Make novel connections between novel things. 
- Build a stronger culture that is more in line with the aim of our **Community Film**


_In a 1986 study, McMillan and Chavis identified four elements of "sense of community": _

1. _membership: feeling of belonging or of sharing a sense of personal relatedness,_
2. _influence: mattering, making a difference to a group and of the group mattering to its members_
3. _reinforcement: integration and fulfillment of needs,_
4. _shared emotional connection._

- The Commonwealth platform is effectively a tool for better incubating and coordinating the collective intelligence of the Edgeware community and my instinct is that we will continue to drive adoption of the platform and protocol via incubated projects. 


- Every time we hyperlink between posts, tag CW IDs or share a previous line of thinking we tighten the feedback loops and ensure relevant information is easier to find and the context easier to relate.

## Next steps:

It's vitally important we all stay open-minded, myself included , since it is very easy to bias towards your own interests and experience and become blinkered to the connections that might yield something novel - the success of the community relies on it.

So have a think about how we could collaborate better, where we could share information and insights in a way that means the pie gets bigger for everyone.
