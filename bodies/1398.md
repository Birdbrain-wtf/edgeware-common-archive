Building a strong community of core developers and tooling/apps developers is important for Edgeware. The root cause of most of our challenges goes back to lack of technical talent on the chain.

Building good on-ramps for technical community members should be a major priority. The bounty system provides a good structure for doing that.

New developers can apply to bounties, which lets them get engaged with the council and community platforms like Discord and Commonwealth. They can jump straight into building something Edgeware needs, rather than trying to come up with their own proposals.

**Kicking off this thread for ideas for bounties**, plus meta-discussion around the bounties system.** **It would be great to have a list of on-chain bounties for the council to fund.

As a matter of procedure, before every bounty goes on chain, it should have its own thread, where it gets discussed for at least a week. The exact phrasing of each bounty should be somewhat carefully considered, and we should ramp up using the bounty system cautiously.

We're targeting that new treasury proposals are not more than $50k, so we should also have a similar cap or rate schedule for treasury bounties (e.g. $5k or $10k). The value of EDG will fluctuate after the bounty is created, so that's just something to keep in mind.

## How the bounty system works

1. **The community agrees to fund a bounty** - for example, this could happen on a weekly community call.
2. **Anyone can propose the bounty**, by putting down a deposit, and specifying a title and amount of EDG.
3. **The council approves the bounty**. This can be done from within Commonwealth or polkadot-js. Approval requires the same number of supporting councillors as a treasury proposal.
4. **The bounty is funded. **At the next spend period, the bounty EDG is taken out of the treasury and reserved.
5. **The council proposes a curator for the bounty.** This also requires an approving council motion.
6. **The curator accepts the bounty.** They must put down a deposit to serve as curator.
7. **The curator works with whoever is completing the bounty. **The curator must send a transaction [every 30 days](https://github.com/hicommonwealth/edgeware-node/blob/135f3fe70cd8b979458a2c8aa74190c4fde4e9ee/node/runtime/src/lib.rs#L732) to keep the bounty active.
8. **When the recipient delivers the bounty, the curator releases funds to them. **There is a [delay of 7 days](https://github.com/hicommonwealth/edgeware-node/blob/135f3fe70cd8b979458a2c8aa74190c4fde4e9ee/node/runtime/src/lib.rs#L730) during which the council should review the deliverables, and can slash the curator if they determine the bounty was not delivered appropriately.
9. **The recipient claims the bounty.**
