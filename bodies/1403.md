# EDG on Uniswap


## Objective: Add EDG to Uniswap

**Treasury Proposal: 15m EDG + initial liquidity provision (LP).**

- Lock up 15m EDG from Treasury to be earmarked for 1:1 exchange with ERC20 EDG.
- ERC20 EDG claimable for EDG
- ~6 month delay for claiming "real" EDG: addresses can be added to the claims pallet and mapped to ERC20 addresses on the next runtime upgrade. 
- Provide liquidity to EDG on Uniswap with specified price range.
- Price should quickly be arbitraged to the price of EDG.
