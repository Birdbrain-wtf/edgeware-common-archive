Anybody looking for concrete examples of the current intersections of cutting-edge blockchain technology research and socio-political structures should take a peek at [“The Social Smart Contract”](https://github.com/DemocracyEarth/paper#ii_Contents) from Democracy Earth.

Democracy Earth is a group working to enabled token-based, open community participation. “The Social Smart Contract” is a three-part document with a manifesto for idealists, a paper for builders, and execution instructions for the pragmatists. In the authors’ words, their goal is for the document to be “a living roadmap for planetary governance.” Given the importance of the Internet in modern-day politics, Democracy Earth hopes “to continue paving the road of implementations that enable cryptographic open-audit voting.”

While using blockchain for voting isn’t exactly novel, the actual proposal is innovative in its breadth and depth of research. The manifesto is particularly interesting, diving into fundamental topics from network theory and globalization to modern geopolitics and AI governance. The paper itself elucidates how liquid democracy, Ethereum’s Solidity smart contracts, segregated witness, and the Lightning Protocol can be used to implement a democracy that provides everything from self-sovereign identity to Universal Basic Income.

Definitely an essential read for those interested in applied blockchain governance innovations and the intersection of blockchain and politics.
