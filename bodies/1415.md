## Description

**Kabocha - $KAB**
A Kusama parachain incubated through the efforts of EDG holders, who voted to support and fund the development of a canarynet in the roadmap, and instantiated by Edgeware.Agency on their behalf.

Kabocha, for EDG holders, is a research laboratory for interoperability and consensus tech in Substrate, governance and funding. It additionally shows that Edgeware teams are able and ready to deploy parachains with DOT auctions upcoming, offers a testing ground for teams on EDG, and provides the EDG treasury with it's first major asset: significant control of a new token.

For itself, Kabocha will, in the words of Rich, be for "research into areas that are outside of mainstream funding. Stuff on the edge, that requires long term funding, deep research, flexible timelines." and help align the technical offering of EDG to the unique community and audience of Kusama.

**Name Reasoning**
A kabocha is a japanese gourd - a PUMPkin. These pumpkins are a favorite icon of artist Yayoi Kusama, famous for her polkadot-focused artwork, and the namesake of the KSM network.

## Token Economics

**Distribution**
As requested, the distribution of KAB has been closely mapped 1:1, with the only exception being a 10% community tax on all addresses to fund sustainable development of the network. See the Snapshot date discussed below.

These revenues, along with the 1:1 amount in the EDG Treasury, will be diverted into an EDG-controlled Community Fund, for incentivizing maintainers, strategic partners, and other needs. Current estimates place this new community fund at roughly 20% of the networks supply, enough to offer KAB in the crowdloan, and incentivize entities in the network.

**Supply**
The supply of KAB will mimic the changes to KSM from the DOT supply, reducing the total KAB supply by 3 zeros, for example: 500,000 would become 500.

In order to maintain both the EVM and Wasm contracting modules (as that is what EDG offers and benefits from testing of,) we will not be altering the decimal places, and we will keep the 18 decimal places, equivalent to EDG. This is because the EVM functionality requires it.


**Community Fund**
As stated above, the value of 20% of the total supply will be mapped to a new, large multisig. The EDG network will use this to express the EDG political voice in KAB, incentivize maintainers and form partnerships.

This fund will be directed by a large set of active community, on behalf of the broader network until Bridge technology between KAB and EDG allow the treasury to hold it directly.

## A shift in strategy
The team that has proposed to manage the KAB chain once launched is Decent Partners. After understanding more about how they are able to do this with regards to maintaining a decentralized utility coin, they've requested more time to review the structure of the launch.

Additionally, we're seeing this chain less as a 'incentivized testnet' and more a product of Edgeware's core strengths: funding projects that will accrue value to the chain in a variety of ways. The 'chain factory' model, I've called it previously. As such, KAB is being given more freedom to pursue a concrete vision that will allow it to experiment with projects and technology that is more tailored for the KSM ecosystem, improving the results of it's crowdloan attempt.

**In short:**
- Edgeware still builds vital proof that we have the talent to launch parachains.
- Edgeware will still receive a massive amount of KAB tokens for funding this development, adding to our public wealth.
- EDG holders and KSM-contributors will still participate as planned in the distribution.
- We will continue to improve the community fund amount - originally slotted for 20% of network launch tokens to fund ongoing maint and to award EDG treasury. That breakdown is still under development, and will come in the form of a formal proposal from Decent to Edgeware - a test of our chain-DAOs ability to form a funding agreement: KAB gets EDG to fund it's launch, EDG takes equity in the new network.
