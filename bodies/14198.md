> **Reason for absence**
> Exactly at the beginning of the development of the second step in the ArtRate project, a social movement was formed in Iran. The use of the slogan ["Woman, Life, Freedom"](https://www.womanlifefreedom.today/) goes back to a series of protests following the [Death of Mahsa Amini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_Mahsa_Amini) in September 2022.
> After this, due to the presence of artists who were mainly the protesting section of the society and the activity model of ArtRate Campus in Iran, the government of Iran forced the closure of the campus.
> Although we continued the activity of ArtRate Academy in a limited way, the financial loss caused by the closure of the campus for this project caused us to not be able to continue paying the costs related to the development of the ArtRate platform, and the project remained suspended at the same level as reported.

# **Present Status:**
Moving forward, our team is fully committed to resuming the development of the ArtRate project. We have overcome the financial loss and are ready to continue the development of ArtRate with renewed energy and dedication. Our goal is to create a joint community with Edgeware for project management and ensure that ArtRate meets the highest standards and fulfills its intended purpose. We are excited about the opportunity to restart the project and are grateful for Edgeware's continued support.
Considering the change of strategy in Edgeware and the explanation of the new Roadmap, it should be acknowledged that we also tried to adapt the ArtRate project to the new conditions.
Currently, the ArtRate project has been created in a completely decentralized manner.
In order to manage the treasury, we created a DAO on the ethereum chain and we made governance portal in Commonwealth.im to de-silo our governance forum and share this with other Substrate-based governance interfaces, giving ArtRate users several ways to interact with the chain and propose their proposals for artwork creating.
In other words, we created the allocation of funds to projects and multi-sig management to withdraw from the treasury as a separate DAO on the ethereum chain, so that we can support all stable or unstable tokens in the treasury. In this way, we can maintain our cooperation process during the Edgeware development process. On the other hand, all artists or producers who are looking to define a project to create a work of art can vote their proposal or request through the DAO created in Commonwealth.im.

## NFT Multi-Chain Playground for Art Developers

## Executive Summary:
Artrate is envisioned as a cutting-edge NFT-focused artistic management platform that streamlines coordination, financing, and production of diverse NFTs. The platform comes with a cross-chain NFT marketplace, specifically designed for artists, creators, and developers to launch, distribute, and collaborate on creative projects. As a crowd-based NFT marketplace operating within the Edgeware network, Artrate offers a unique opportunity for creative minds to showcase their exceptional digital creations and engage with an avid community of art connoisseurs and admirers.

**Proposal Details:**

**1. Decentralized Artistic Governance:**
Artrate's decentralized artistic governance model aims to take power away from centralized institutions and place it in the hands of artists and the community. This approach fosters a more diverse range of voices and perspectives to be represented in the arts while encouraging increased transparency and accountability within the creative processes.

**2. Emotional Semantic Knowledge Graph (ESKG) Powered AI:**
Artrate will integrate ESKG-powered AI technology, introducing an innovative way of understanding and interpreting complex human emotions through artificial intelligence. This powerful tool will assist in curating personalized content for users, allowing them to discover new artists and creations that align with their emotional preferences.

**3. Cross-Chain Compatibility:**
In order to facilitate seamless transactions between different blockchain networks, Artrate will operate as a multi-chain playground that supports a wide range of cryptocurrencies. This flexibility allows both creators and collectors to utilize their preferred digital currencies when buying and selling artwork.

**4. Dedicated Platform for Artists, Creators & Developers:**
By providing a dedicated platform specifically designed for the art sector, Artrate will help promote creativity through enhanced collaboration opportunities among its users. The platform will also offer services such as financing options for up-and-coming artists, mentoring programs by experienced professionals, as well as customizable tools for NFT creation and distribution.

**5. Community Engagement:**
Artrate will encourage an active community of art enthusiasts and supporters to engage with each other, leaving feedback, participating in voting, and curating digital exhibitions. This builds a sense of collective identity and fosters a thriving ecosystem that nurtures collaborations and creative growth.

**Conclusion:**
Artrate's business proposal is an innovative solution that merges the worlds of blockchain technology, artificial intelligence, and artistic creativity. With its decentralized governance model, ESKG-powered AI, cross-chain compatibility, dedicated platform tailor-made for artists, creators and developers, as well as its focus on community engagement, Artrate has the potential to revolutionize the NFT landscape and redefine how artistic value is created and exchanged in the digital sphere.

## Artrate - NFT Multi-Chain Playground for Art Developers

**1. Introduction**

Artrate will feature an NFT-focused artistic management platform that streamlines coordination, financing, and production of diverse NFTs, along with a cross-chain marketplace. Artists, creators, and developers can use the platform as a launching pad to distribute their artworks, collaborate on creative projects, and engage in participatory governance.

**3. Artrate Marketplace**
The marketplace will be built within the Edgeware network to ensure stability, efficiency, and compatibility with Ethereum-based ecosystems. Users can explore exceptional digital creations from various artists and join an ever-growing community of art enthusiasts.

**4. Decentralized Artistic Governance**
Artrate's decentralized approach empowers artists and users, allowing them to make decisions regarding management and curation of content within the platform. Through this framework, they can foster a diverse range of voices that promote cultural exchange and creativity.

**5. Emotional Semantic Knowledge Graph (ESKG) Powered AI Integration**
To further augment the platform's capacities, Artrate will leverage ESKG-powered AI technology. ESKG allows machines to interpret complex human emotions, enhancing user experience when interacting with artworks. This novel development promises a new level of immersion and personalization for users within Artrate's innovative ecosystem.
