Hello everyone,

This post is a rundown of what we are looking to achieve with our YouTube initiative that will begin to unfold, and the long-term goal for this initiative as it relates to the developer community, and the Edgeware community as a whole. A YouTube initiative will begin to highlight core functionality and features of Edgeware, as well as providing detailed video walkthroughs on tutorials from our documentation. The main objective for this initiative is to educate and solidify Edgeware's narrative and provide another perspective and medium of content for the community to better understand and relate to what Edgeware means, and where we're headed.

Key points to consider:
- **Create videos that are relevant and generate interest**
- Videos need to be related to Edgeware's core principles and values and really nail the fundamentals of who we are as a smart contracts platform — this generates hype and interest by allowing our community to understand and relate to the messages we're trying convey
- Videos need to be interesting in the sense that they're not just cookie cutter tutorials, or make us come off as just another blockchain. Their needs to be some spirit and what I believe should be some level of entertainment factor when these videos are being created to show that we're not dull, and we're excited about the work we're putting out.
- **Create videos that explain and expand topics into greater depth**
- We need to have tutorials that are extensive and exhaustive on our core technologies like WASM and EVM ensuring that if our documentation is not clear, there is another reference that is very clear in expressing how to proceed to the next step. Furthermore, videos allow a deeper dive into what's happening with the ability to relate two separate pieces of information and how they interconnect.
- **Create videos that are able to provide a more cohesive narrative for Edgeware and want we stand for**
- Similar to the first point, we want to make sure that we are well understood for the identity that we want to be. In order to have a clear and cohesive roadmap, it's important that our narrative has a great deal of continuity and does not pivot every few months to simply fit a market trend. High quality videos resonate with viewers and can provide that needed clarity.
- Create videos that can allow for easy references
- When certain topics are brought up in discussion from time to time, references to a video that goes into a very descriptive explanation would probably be preferred over a thread in a forum. Links easy, comes in a nice package.
- **Create videos that encourage development and creativity in Edgeware by expressing our unique sets of tools and resources**
- We want to flex our muscles and show off what we have to our own community, and the rest of the blockchain ecosystem. We want to show partners and potential developers the benefits of building on Edgeware, and how we can take their product to that next level.

**Video content**
- <15 min
- High quality (full HD, quality microphone, quality infographics, quality intro / banners)
- Precise and to the point
- energetic and exciting
- informative and engaging
- Consistent and uniform in nature
- Premiering videos
