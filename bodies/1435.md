# Ecosystem Partner Grant 
**Structure Draft**

As part of the Agency's roadmap for this year, we targeted recruiting and supporting 3 'ecosystem partners' on EDG. 


Unlike a standard partner or grant recipient, our approach was to further decentralize the management and development of the EDG ecosystem through strategic and highly capable teams who could drive entire categories of use cases or otherwise serve as anchors for categories like the creator economy, defi, and orgtech. 


This document serves as the starting draft for the grant structure, which would offer very high rewards of EDG - up to percents of the network, in order to forge long term social contracts between the network and development and product teams. 

Note that while our traditional grant structure rewards deliverables and functions, this grant operates on performance metrics related to the growth and adoption of the network. As it is milestone based, teams would only be paid out as a result of reaching goals approved by the community on a per-milestone basis.

## Update July 26

Given the pushback from council members, I don't believe this will go forward for EDG at this time.

This leaves me with one reservation, which is that the Agency proposal is listed to provide three ecosystem partners. If there is to be no ecosystem partner structure, other than our usual grants -- 'traditional partners,' then we will be unable to fulfill and prioritize that item.

Instead, I will pivot instead to recruiting one core team, which I believe is essential to Edgeware's long term success, and treat that process as a one-off to be negotiated with the community ad-hoc.

We will of course continue the traditional grant structure, ongoing business development and launch support.
