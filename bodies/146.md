[Diverse Structures and Challenges for Oracles](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/113-diverse-structures-and-challenges-for-oracles)

## Privacy and Identity

[The Argument for Blockchain-based Privacy](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/119-the-argument-for-blockchainbased-privacy)

The culture of demanding true anonymity in the blockchain ecosystem goes all the way back to the 1970s and the subsequent birth of the Cypherpunks movement. But applying these foundational principles to the advanced technologies we have now can get a bit tricky.

[Decentralization and Self-Sovereignty for Identity on the Blockchain](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/123-decentralization-and-selfsovereignty-for-identity-on-the-blockchain)

Personal data is constantly harvested for use in ads and other psychology-based, profit-seeking campaigns. The solution? Decentralized, self-sovereign identities on the blockchain.

[The State of Over-the-Counter Crypto Trading](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/117-the-state-of-overthecounter-crypto-trading)

Over-the-Counter trading used to be for small businesses that couldn't get into a regulated exchange. Now, it's the preferred way to trade cryptocurrencies, for everybody from whales to hedge fund managers. 


## Protocol Updates 

[Potential Lessons from Multi-Collateral Dai](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/126-potential-lessons-from-multicollateral-dai)

MakerDAO Dai is now Multi-Collateral Dai. What could this experiment tell us about the intersection of DAOs and the traditional world?

[Diversifying Delegations & Stake Distribution: The Cosmos Example](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/118-diversifying-delegations-stake-distribution-the-cosmos-example) 

A recent analysis by Chorus One has led to questions about validator centralization on the Cosmos network. Community members have a solution that gives "diversification rewards" to delegating addresses with a Gini co-efficient proving sufficient validator diversity.

[Telegram Open Network: Innovative or Over-Ambitious?](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/120-telegram-open-network-innovative-or-overambitious)

Beyond backlash from the SEC, Telegram Open Network (TON) also faces criticism for its lack of transparency and ambitious claims of scalability.  Only time will tell whether TON will be able to offer a private chat and payment app rivaling David Chaum’s Elixxr, or even go as far as the network's initial goal of serving as an ecosystem builder.

[Why Did Poloniex Abandon US Traders?](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/124-why-did-poloniex-abandon-us-traders)

It was unexpected news this month when Poloniex announced that it would be leaving Circle. Should we care about Poloniex’s abandonment of American traders? Does it imply anything about the US-based blockchain market and the type of tech it wants? 

[Implications of the Tezos Babylon Upgrade](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/131-implications-of-the-tezos-babylon-upgrade)

The Tezos Babylon upgrade was a huge landmark for on-chain governed protocols. Despite its success, there were still lessons to be learned: mainly, the importance of documentation and community input.

[First Steps to Ethereum 2.0: Istanbul Hard Fork Review](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/143-first-steps-to-ethereum-20-istanbul-hard-fork-review)

## Future Tech

[Leveraging Reputation with Blockchain](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/129-leveraging-reputation-with-blockchain)

Reputation is powerful - and blockchain-based technology gives us unique new ways to explore how we might leverage that power to design better organizational structures.

[Outlook on Blockchain and Quantum Computing](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/114-outlook-on-blockchain-and-quantum-computing)

There's been lots of talk about how quantum computing could break PoW chains. But what if we could use quantum entanglement to create a "Qubit coin”? 

[A Reputation Bridge for Pseudonymous Identities](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/112-a-reputation-bridge-for-pseudonymous-identities)

Assume a spherical cow - or, instead, assume a crypto-Twitter. Balaji Srinivasan explained how followers could be used to quantify reputation and link credibility to a pseudonym, without giving up anonymity.

[How do stablecoins work and should we use them?](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/135-how-do-stablecoins-work-and-should-we-use-them)

Many stablecoins don’t embrace the dual promise of transparency and privacy that blockchain offers. Read about how they work, the Tether scandal, and the potential for an algorithmic stablecoin.

[Tests and Opportunities for Curation Markets](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/142-tests-and-opportunities-for-curation-markets)

The goal of a Curation Market is to enable community collaboration and fast growth on concepts or goals that are of importance to a specific group. Curation Markets have huge potential, but they also have serious design challenges. 

[Using an Iterative Comparison Algorithm to Rank Abstract Value](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/136-using-an-iterative-comparison-algorithm-to-rank-abstract-value)

Iterative Comparison Algorithms face the problem of ranking abstract value by judging based on a comparison, rather than trying to quantify an abstract worth. How could we apply ICAs?
