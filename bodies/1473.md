**1)** **Background**
Earlier this year we conducted a host of research interviews with various community members, these were used to inform the initial treatment ( [here. ](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1SCEV8VlzmomRWZR8LiDuP5WZSYUkJjVX/view?usp=sharing)) and to make a rough draft mood film [here](https://vimeo.com/509900486) (password edgeware) which aimed to explore how the communities voices would feel alongside narration and an exploration of visual style/tone.
The thread for which can be found here https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/989-edgeware-original-video-development-request-for-feedback

**2) Pre Production**
The team for this pre production consists of myself as Director and [@Olivia Sw](/edgeware/account/iz84fAEfR8xnmLpfVRiNHPFdyiycMYwZHEDHUbb5VwDaCks) as Researcher, a producer will be joining us as we ramp up the production process in the coming days. I have been focusing on bringing the creative approach up to date, as well as starting to work into a script the key takeaways of the project that need to be conveyed within the film with the help of the agency. This script is background work that will be used to inform a creative treatment which will be shared with the community (see 3) this will explain everything we will be seeing and hearing throughout the film . [@Olivia Sw](/edgeware/account/iz84fAEfR8xnmLpfVRiNHPFdyiycMYwZHEDHUbb5VwDaCks) has started to reach out to some members of the community who were not around at the time of the original research interviews, and speaking to funded projects and teams who are going through the proposal process to discover peoples thoughts and what they have available for us to film or any assets relating to the projects we are able access.

**3) Creative Treatment**
The creative treatment for the film is under way, whilst working on it it became apparent to me that although my first draft contains the relevant takeaways and technical aspects needed with regards to the project, it was lacking in a real relatable and emotional tone that would really bring the project and community to life. It's a real balancing act to get both right but I think the Edgeware community deserve a film that does more then merely impart information, i think if we're going to leave people with the call to action "What will you build?" we need to make sure we're inspiring people with a pioneering spirit as well a delivering a visual style that feels as exciting as the possibilities on offer.

**4) Community Involvement**
This week [@Olivia Sw](/edgeware/account/iz84fAEfR8xnmLpfVRiNHPFdyiycMYwZHEDHUbb5VwDaCks) putting out a brief to the community which will be a request
for archive material (photo's/phone footage/screen records) that we can use to build a picture of a global decentralised community. as well as getting people record answers to a handful of set questions which will put across a relatable human side of the community, a good reference for this style ( [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b2k4nIARvS8)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b2k4nIARvS8)) )
