This proposal seeks funding for the initial registration expenses of Edgeverse DAO LLC in the Marshall Islands.

As mentioned in [earlier posts](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/14795-edgeverse-dao-pioneering-a-subdao-for-enhanced-edgeware-dynamics), we are establishing a multichain DAO to enhance the Edgeware blockchain. We are partnering with MiDAO to register our DAO.

**Introduction**
The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) stands as the foremost jurisdiction for Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs) and Web3 projects globally.

**Legal Entities**
The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) provides options for both for-profit and non-profit DAO LLCs, as well as Series DAO LLCs. These legal entities are built upon the LLC framework and tailored to accommodate the distinctive requirements of DAOs and Web3 projects.

**Benefits of Legal Entities**
- Limited liability for members of the entity
- Corporate personhood for property ownership, contract signing, opening bank accounts, etc.
- Tax optimization


**Benefits of RMI DAO LLC**
- No trustees, directors, officers, or managers with additional liability or responsibility
- All documents, ownership, and records can be securely stored on the blockchain
- Most members have the option to maintain anonymity
- Governance by smart contracts, permitted by law


**For-profit DAO LLC**
For-profit DAO LLCs are liable to a 3% Gross Revenue Tax (GRT), applicable to all earned income and interest, excluding capital gains and dividends.

**Relationship with the United States**
The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) is an independent nation outside the jurisdiction of the United States. Numerous RMI laws, including corporate laws, draw inspiration from Delaware's legal framework. In the absence of specific statutes or case law within the RMI, its courts may refer to Delaware case law for guidance.

**Digital Asset Regulations**
At present, the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) lacks specific laws or regulations pertaining to digital assets, tokens, or protocols, with the exception of regulations for companies involved in the custody of digital assets on behalf of others. MIDAO is introducing progressive, technology-driven legislation and regulation specifically addressing digital assets, tokens, and protocols.

**Why the Marshall Islands?**
The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) has held a prominent position in shipping company jurisdictions for more than five decades. With over 40 public companies listed on NASDAQ and NYSE and entities constituting more than 20% of the world's shipping capacity, the RMI has demonstrated leadership. Building on this experience in the shipping industry, the RMI is leveraging its expertise to emerge as the foremost global jurisdiction for both DAOs and Web3 projects.

**Compliance**
The Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) is committed to adhering to international standards, particularly concerning beneficial ownership. Members holding 25% or more governance rights in DAO LLCs and any Managing Members are required to undergo Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures, providing authentic information such as their real name, address, and passport details. Furthermore, on-chain activities will be scrutinized for Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (CFT) purposes. Notably, the RMI takes pride in not being associated with any blacklists.

**Annual Reporting **
DAO LLCs report annually by sharing their updated company agreement and having beneficial members do KYC. For-profits must report their revenue for tax collection purposes.

**Banking for DAOs and Web3 Projects**
RMI DAO LLCs have established bank accounts with institutions, including Western Alliance Bank.

**Other RMI Corporate Entities**
RMI DAO LLCs are recognized as domestic entities. However, the RMI offers foreign corporations and foreign LLCs that are exempt from the DAO Act of 2022 and, consequently, do not enjoy its associated benefits.

**DAO/Web3 Project Legal & Governance Advice and Consulting**
MIDAO does not operate as a law firm and does not offer legal advice. However, through its partners, MIDAO can facilitate access to legal and tax advisory services. Additionally, MIDAO keeps a roster of RMI and global lawyers and can assist in making introductions on your behalf.
