## Background
Substrate is a bleeding-edge blockchain project. While Edgeware was one of the first chains to adopt and launch, we also faced some of the largest technical hurdles.  Additionally, due to our unique distribution that allocated a supermajority of tokens to community members through the lockdrop, we took risks on a non-standard strategy for maintaining development. 

The downstream effects of this have been to disalign values for powerful investors who could support core teams, as the upside was relatively limited compared to other projects launching over the past several years.  Chains who launched without this feature have had significantly more success in maintaining the development runway.

This matters, because EDG is solely dependent on its own token price for self-development., while other chains are able to deploy both traditional capital and treasury capital.  In a state where liquidity and market price are low, we are hobbled to continue to advance development.

More, the Ink!, the Wasm smart contracting language remains in infancy. EVM, thankfully is stabilizing rapidly and will be reliable after the Wako upgrade.

One large question has been the parachain future status, and this became especially unlikely recently with [the discovery of non-functioning smart contracting on parachains.](https://github.com/paritytech/substrate/issues/9354) This issue affects all Substrate chains, and will be an issue for 'the foreseeable future' in the words of Bruno from Parity. This implies that EDG, in order to maintain smart contracting, will need to remain a solochain. 

## Where We Stand

We have significant experience in organizing governance activities, written playbooks on community engagement, and milestone tracking. We have a set of experts in blockchain able to review proposals and grasp the worth of new endeavors. 

While we initially positioned EDG as a 'builders platform,' and incubator, we can tighten this use case up in light of the technical challenges before us, and ask** "What can we be right now, and very strongly so?" **

Enter ['Squad Wealth' ](https://otherinter.net/research/squad-wealth/) an article and term coined by Other Internet, which is a culture and process of pooling resources in ways facilitated by ever-decreasing firm costs, a trend initiated by the invention of blockchain, where transaction and coordination costs continue to reduce- fueled especially through the invention of smart contracting platforms. 

Most squad wealth experiments happen through web2 apps like Splitwise, or in Web3, DAOs /on/ L1 chains, but we have something fairly rare in the blockchain space: a chain-DAO with a public treasury. 

And so, we have gathered as a relatively small community to approve spends on projects that exemplify our communal values. - like Exit Theory, a book by Justin Murphy, and projects like LexBridge, a way of easily connecting DAOs to legal entities in the US, facilitating innovation and compliant business within Web3.

To this end, I encourage a shift in perspective on EDG:

**Edgeware is a sovereign-grade venture community.** We make investments using our pooled resources into public goods and cutting-edge, often software projects, and reap the benefits as a community. We allow participants via stake-in-the-game to accrue from all over the world, to vote on investments, negotiate terms, advise these projects, and exit their positions as necessary within the common project. 

Think Andresson-Horowitz but with hundreds of partners. Think Sand Hill road, fused with privacy mixers and pseudo-anonymity. Think Silicon Valley in the cloud, censorship-resistant, open, and with its own smart contracting functions that make its operations more feasible, automated, and reliable than ever before- and all built on a solid utility token. 

This we can do today, and have done for some time. We can help anyone in the world participate in the culture of investment into new and innovative endeavors; undergrads dreaming of business school, small business owners looking to diversify their risk, and TikTokers, Robinhood tweens, and Gamestop gurus to participate in the community wealth building that has exploded in the past year. 

Several months ago we allocated funds to BuilDAO, now termed Edgeware Alpha, as a for-profit venture wing that the chain as a whole would sit as a partner in, and the treasury would accrue profits for the benefit of the chain as a whole.

I now advocate leaning in and multiplying this effort, spinning out Edgeware Betas, Gammas and more, to target individual investment niches within the cutting-edge, not only blockchain but biotech, climate change, education, etc. Working with new advisors, providing value-adds using our collective intelligence and empowered delegates.

Targeting this case will shift our hiring priorities to focus more on financial management and analysis, build rapport with talented advisors to sit on the Treasury Sub-funds, and consider the technical UX of funding teams from a chain-interface perspective, and attract talent to the projects we support. 

## Conclusion
I think the future is bright for EDG as an evolution of the squadwealth thesis, where all of these functions are possible today, and where we diversify the treasury's holdings across many new assets and equities- and where EDG accrues value because of this fund model, rather than the traditional platform model of Ethereum.
