I’m curious - does anybody use crypto credit or debit cards? I don’t use one myself, so I’m wondering if people think they're actually secure, or why people use their particular crypto debit card. 

There seem to be a few debit cards available for Bitcoin and Ethereum, which you can use at any store. These cards are backed by credit providers that everyone recognizes: Visa and Mastercard. It would be huge for adoption if these trusted providers continue to support everyday use of cryptos - but in 2018, Visa started backing out of its relationships with many major crypto-card providers, including BitPay and WaveCrest (Irby). According to one report, as of last week there were only four major companies supplying crypto debit cards: Wirex, Coinbase, Bitpay, and Cryptopay (Beigel). 

Common coins supported besides BTC and ETH include Ripple (XRP) and Litecoin (LTC). Challenges seem to include high processing fees, dealing with exchange rates, and jurisdiction limitations. These are all in addition to major credit providers increasingly dropping support for crypto-debit cards. Anybody know why this shift is happening? 

Crypto credit cards, on the other hand, don’t really exist yet - though some are innovating in that space. For instance, Nexo is offering an insured, crypto-backed credit line to twenty-three cryptocurrencies, and claims to be the only crypto bank account that lets you borrow in more than forty-five fiat currencies. 

In the context of major credit providers backing out of relationships with crypto debit card suppliers, does it make sense to get a crypto debit card? Are there more benefits to using a crypto credit card like what Nexo is offering? Does it make sense to have a crypto card at all?
