Traditionally, blockchain ambassador programs have been made up of volunteer teams with the goal of spreading the word about the network. While the Commonwealth team has encouraged the formation of this sort of group for a long time, we think an ambassador program can be more than volunteers spreading the word. An Edgeware ambassador network has the potential to serve as a mechanism for our decentralized community to support its own coordination, guidance, and growth. 

Thom Ivy has previously suggested an [ambassador program for Edgware](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/43-funding-the-edgeware-ambassador-program), the development of which could be incentivized by funding from the treasury. Below, I’ve listed some current Ambassador Programs as a way to give the Edgeware community ideas about what our program could look like.

- **Blockchain.io**: [Ambassadors for this exchange](https://medium.com/blockchainio/blockchain-io-ambassador-program-is-open-to-applications-abb064f306f2) are meant to “spread the word about our platform, support all newcomers into our community, and enable people to join us at every step of the way.” The benefits of being an Ambassador include token rewards, as well as tons of networking opportunities, from the chance to host meet-ups to attending conferences with the Blockchain.io team. Their [ambassador program application form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe2RvGTzng5Uxnxog7M87F60xn1mRb2_Yh7ZP1pAQtmaf8cCQ/formResponse) requires information about your identity and trading history on Blockchain.io, and links to all social media profiles.
- **Enigma**: This privacy-focused dApp platform also has an ambassador program, which they have recently renamed [the Enigma Collective](https://blog.enigma.co/introducing-the-enigma-collective-3b5efdda167). These volunteers are responsible for “contributing to and leading dozens of projects.” Enigma Collective members work on community education projects, media outreach, organizing live events, and secret node operation, among other things. These ambassadors are offered unique access to the core dev team and the opportunity to provide real value to the Enigma protocol. The [Enigma Collective application](https://airtable.com/shry2jO40qy6e8CnS) emphasizes the chance to become an Enigma expert and use your skills to add value. 
- **Humaniq**: The [ambassadors at Humaniq](https://humaniq.com/ambassadors) are “helping to promote cryptocurrencies in the Global South, as well as explaining the Humaniq project and app to the people in their countries.” These Ambassadors are people who actually live in the countries and know the cultures of the unbanked groups that Humaniq is trying to help offer financial services to. 

Ambassador programs are part of the thriving heartbeat of the blockchain ecosystem, but the goals of each network's program are more targetted than the routine goal of pushing for exposure with good volunteers. Some programs emphasize building a community network, while others focus on the chance to learn about and genuinely contribute to new protocol innovations. Meanwhile, a network like Humaniq relies on ambassadors for unique cultural knowledge and lived experience that can help them in their mission to spread banking access to as many people as possible. These diverse goals of ambassador programs result in a variety of incentive methods, too.
