Hello everyone! I wanted to share stake.fish's validator campaign with the Edgeware community.

**1. What is your experience as a validator or node operator? What other protocols are you validating on right now, if any?**

Our founder, Wang Chun, has co-founded f2pool, one of the largest Bitcoin and Ethereum mining pools in 2013. He has been running and maintaining nodes on various Proof of Work chains for the mining pool. Our head of infrastructure, Kris Lucera, has 18 years of experience working on systems architecture and infrastructure security. He has been bringing cybersecurity best practices to the blockchain world.

stake.fish has been in operations for over a year. During that time we have run validators or nodes on: EOS mainnet, Cosmos Hub, Tezos, Chainlink, Algorand, The OAN (Aion), Kusama, Storj, Loom Network, IRIS Network, BOS, and Lino Network. We also have nodes on various testnets: Solana, NEAR Protocol, NuCypher, Oasis Labs, and Elrond.

**2. What's your technical setup?**

We make strategic use of both on-premise and cloud infrastructure. We try not to disclose too much on our infrastructure setup for security reasons. When asked about our capabilities, we like to point to our track record of running infrastructure on different networks for the past year without any issues. We will bring the same level of expertise to our Edgeware validator.

**3. How did you learn about Edgeware? Why are you interested in validating?**

While there are many (many) items on our checklist while conducting due diligence on projects, they can eventually be summarized in two questions. One, is the project working on something interesting? Two, is the community interested in building and staking on top of the project? To both these questions, Edgeware was a strong yes, and so therefore we want to support it as soon as possible.

**4. What are your positions on issues that governance will be expected to handle?**

We will have to review governance proposals case-by-case. However, so far we have supported proposals that we believe benefits each protocol long-term, which would translate to long-term benefits for our delegators.

**5. What proposals on the network are you most interested in seeing? (e.g. initial parachain offerings, DAOs, DeFi protocols, stablecoins, prediction markets, insurance)**

DeFi products. Financial instruments will be a key method to mitigate protocol level risk. It will be crucial to create markets that help hedge investors and developers their exposure to network level issues.

**6. What role do you think Edgeware should play within the larger cryptocurrency and Web3 space? Is there an overall long-term vision for the network which you are most excited about?**

Edgeware will provide a guideline to how parachain candidate chains should approach the Polkadot ecosystem. Additionally, we’re excited to see Edgeware evolved into the go-to smart contract chain for the Polkadot ecosystem.
