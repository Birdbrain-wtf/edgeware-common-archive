To expand on Thom's post: [Edgeware: An Open, Unstoppable Venture Community](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1504-edgeware-an-open-unstoppable-venture-community), I'll be giving light to what this might look for the developer community on Edgeware.

I think the analogy of a snowball works nicely for Edgeware. We work best when we have the most engagement and more active discussions, when we're rolling that snowball. We have the most engagement when we're actively advocating and promoting events and updates that sparks interest and gets new people introduced to the community, and this leads to more of the organic growth that we'd be looking for. Once the snowball (community) is big enough, less resistance is required to begin to reach more people, more people means more interaction and building that can take place, which will increase value. Ideally, we want the community to be self-sustainable which can be assisted with reputation / moderation systems. However, as we are beginning to shift our focus, we want to make a smooth transition into this more relevant paradigm shift, and this makes it more important than ever that we maintain continuity in our long term goals, by ensuring that we are identifying and carrying out operations in a coordinated and strategic way. The following is a first glance at how we'll begin to apply these strategies:

## **Actionable steps towards 'sovereign-grade venture community'**

What does this look like from a development standpoint — Looking at the end goal and understanding the objective for maximizing community engagement in governance and chat contributions.

**Making sure that everyone in the community is on the same page**

- **Technical content** needs to be produced that is doing a better job at informing community members on current status, how we got to this point, and a clear vision for the future. (articles, technical analysis, visionary prompts)
- Making sure **communication is clear and concise** as soon as new updates / innovations happen on chain or in documentation — communicated via community channels like telegram and discord as well as community forum pages for appropriate subcategories.
- Effective marketing and **amplification of governance proposals** that add to the functionality of developer tooling and or upgrades on chain that get community members and investors excited to interact and engage.
- Posts to **provoke discussion** about upcoming changes and trajectory for developers in the Edgeware ecosystem on the commonwealth forums. A more proactive effort will be made in expanding current topics, why we're doing this, and how this will enable growth.
- Making sure that **new members** who are brought into the community are vetted in a way that ensures they understand our processes, and our vision for the future. We want to maximize community engagement so that they're able to add meaningful discussion and insights that will bring the community up as a whole and not just individual power moves.
- **Listening to community members**, providing an opportunity for anyone and everyone to express public opinion on the matter of new changes in such a way that promotes constructive criticism to help agency members understand what we're doing right, and what we're doing wrong. We are looking to avoid in atmosphere in which there are perpetual conflicts of interest and begin to consider ideas carefully and thoroughly. The end goal will be to reach an equilibrium of sorts so that meaningful discussion can take place with the majority. This will work better if the previous steps are being executed upon.


**Focus points **
In trying to change our narrative, we also want to understand the demographic of people that will be interested in our mission, and how decisions made will begin to align our trajectory with our stated end goal. By creating and maintaining a consistent and dynamic message that appeals to our demographic of people/tribe, we'll be able to carve out the path of best action, which will not only allow us to reach this goal, but provide organic growth and sustainability for future goals as well.
Some things we may want to think about / ask in attempt to uncover this question:
- Who are we looking to attract?
- What solutions are they looking for?
- What solutions are already available?
- What are we doing right?
- What are we doing wrong?
- What specific problems are we trying to solve, how is Edgeware unique?
- Where do we see Edgeware in 1 month from now, 6 months from now, a year
- What can we begin to change now


**builders** and the **holders**
The main two groups of people that are currently engaged in Edgeware's development process. Builders directly influence holders, and the decisions of holders also in turn effect builders. Decisions that are made need to satisfy both groups in order to see quicker development, and maintain the harmonious balance that we'd like to perpetuate.

**Builders**

- Are interested in expanding Edgeware, exercising and utilizing Edgeware as a means of testing and creating and innovating using smart contract technology.
- Excited to help grow and build Edgeware by developing tools and resources that other builders and community members will also be able to use.


- Quality - When considering a platform, you're always looking for the best tools and resources that will be available to you, as as well as a guide that's easy to understand on how to best use those tools and resources. Highly successful companies always have a well designed, and exhaustive guide to well inform it's users or customers on the product or service at hand. There needs to be a consistent quality control within our website, wiki and repos to assure users that we are a reliable and trustworthy platform. in the tools and documentation we have available to use. Some benefits will include: issues will arise and resolve quicker, better options / solutions are found quicker, pressing issues are encouraged to be resolved at a faster pace to meet the demand of users, new mediums / solutions can be developed to satisfy the growth of a developer presence on the network.
- Interoperability - With our current structure, Edgeware is extremely interoperable, and can be used in majority of use cases for smart contracts. With that being said, we have a huge demographic of potential builders that would be able to enter the ecosystem and contribute.
- Auditability - All of our repositories are open, and all of our information is public.
- Cost - Edgeware is extremely cost effective to use in comparison to options like Ethereum. Not to mention extremely quick as well. These are ways we can garner support from individuals who are unsatisfied with the performance of other chains, and may be interested in learning about chains which are testing new ideas and looking towards what's next, like Edgeware.


**Holders**

- Holders of EDG have multiple different motives for holding the token: governance abilities, chain fees, appreciation in token value
- Holders have a much larger influence than holding stock in traditional companies / projects because in holding EDG they're able to directly influence decisions that happen on chain and see those decisions reflect in price fluctuations.
