## Takeaways

- Comprehensive decentralization is a key consideration for the development of Edgeware. An important part of that is transferring ownership of media channels to the community.
- How can we best shift ownership of communication channels to the community? Should Councillors play a key role in managing these channels, or perhaps a new “Admin” position?
- Are there content curation mechanisms the community wants to play with - like a curation market, or a TCR? 

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

As we think about the future of Edgeware, decentralizing all governance processes is weighing heavy on everybody’s mind. The sharing of information is one of those key processes. How can we plan to transfer the ownership of Edgeware media channels from Commonweath Labs to the community?

These channels include the HeyEdgeware Twitter account, the Discord space, the Telegram group, and the subReddit. In terms of establishing admins for the social media accounts, that could be as simple as nominations and then a community vote on who should serve as admins. This could also be a decision we leave up to Council members. Hand-in-hand with this issue is the question of whether these channels could be better integrated into the Commonwealth.im governance platform. 

But what about the actual sharing of content? Right now, Commonwealth Labs primarily shares content with these channels in hopes of providing useful information to the community. What if there was a way for our community to collectively decide what content gets posted to our social media channels? 

We could look at interesting ways to apply a [curation market](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/142-tests-and-opportunities-for-curation-markets), or [reputation-based mechanisms like a Token Curated Registry](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/129-leveraging-reputation-with-blockchain) to help the community decide what goes on our social media channels. For instance, we could build a curation market with an EdgTwitter Token - coins specifically designed to let people vote on what content they want to see on the Edgeware Twitter page. Or, we could have a daily TCR with the stipulation that content from that day that is ranked in the top five will be shared to media channels. Alternatively, we could use a Reputation-Curated Registry for the same purpose. 

## Application

There are certainly fun ways we could experiment with collective decision-making on sharing Edgeware content. After launch, making this change towards communal ownership of communication channels will be of key importance for the development of a healthy network. Does anybody have a proposal for concrete steps towards shifting ownership of media channels to the community?

- Is there a better way to integrate the many communication channels open now into the Commonwealth.im governance space?
- What challenges might we face in shifting media channels towards shared ownership?
