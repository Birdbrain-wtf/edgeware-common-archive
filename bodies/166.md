**It is a source of uncertainty for exchanges and coin holders that the supply of EDG could be inflated arbitrarily using on-chain governance.** It is in all EDG holder’s best interest to agree to prevent that. How can we design a proposal to limit arbitrary inflation? 

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Dillon Chen has suggested a proposal that seeks agreement among all yes-voters to pledge to:
- Never increase annual on-chain inflation to higher than 20% per year.
- Never conduct an irregular state transition that causes a discontinuous increase of on-chain supply. (This does not apply to funds that were locked).
- To vote against any instances of the above.

20% is the current inflation rate. As this is what we’re starting with, it feels like this should be the maximum inflation - but of course, community input on this is welcome. We can also consider the goal elucidated in [the Edgeware whitepaper](https://arena-attachments.s3.amazonaws.com/3850782/1928e31873075de95992d4987eb14a2e.pdf?1552432633): “tie inflation to a specific participation or security rate, with the inflation floating up or down to reach a desired total stake.”

We should seriously consider the amount of inflation we allow, and we should take this as a way to bake a rejection of arbitrary inflation right into our community culture. While it might look like an unenforceable social contract, the evidence supporting the disadvantages of inflation is stacked in a way that I believe we, as a network, could collectively and sustainably agree to uphold it. 

A good recent example: Stellar Protocol recently burned *half* of its token supply and eliminated inflation. The original idea of the Stellar inflation pool was to fund the development of the network, but this was unsuccessful as coin holders didn’t actually participate in the governance process to donate inflation funds to projects.

As a community, we can consider ways to incentivize sticking to a yes-pledge like the one proposed above. However, it would be a stronger motion if the proposal is not founded on using incentives or other means to manipulate the community into a commitment but instead focuses on getting consensus and cultural commitment to avoid the problem of arbitrary inflation. 

## Application

It is in everybody’s best interest to consider how best to limit arbitrary inflation of the EDG token. How can we intentionally design a proposal to limit arbitrary inflation? 

Remaining questions:

- Is there an optimal rate of annual on-chain inflation? 
- Are there state transitions that would cause a discontinuous increase in inflation that we should specify?
- Should we integrate incentives or penalties to help enforce the pledge to limit arbitrary inflation?
