- We all owe a lot to the ETH community and project for what it’s done to the smart contract space. But the delays in ETH2 and the transition of ETH1 to ETH2 show that there is an opportunity for Edgeware as a first smart contract chain in the Polkadot ecosystem to really offer something of value.

- We can capitalize and use Edgeware as the space to develop DAOs and dApps. Scalability and security ensured by Polkadot, plus a strong developer and user base. Sure you can argue that ETH is ETH and they’ve cornered the market on defi at least, but in Polkadot we can corner the market for actually scalable DAOs and dApps that ETH just isn’t ready for yet.
