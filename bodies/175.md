Check out what the Commonwealth team has been reading this week!

- [DAOs and Cooperatives](https://blog.autark.xyz/daos-and-cooperatives/): Autark shares legal wrappers for DAOs, taking a deep dive into the Liechtenstein Venture Cooperative framework. 
- [Identity and Quadratic Funding: standards to help fund projects and commons](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/identity-and-quadratic-funding-standards-to-help-fund-projects-and-commons/3835): Jamie Pitts introduces a proposal for standardized experiments on quadratic funding systems.
- [Crypto M&A: Barbarians on the Blockchain](https://research.tokendata.io/2019/11/24/barbarians-on-the-blockchain/): “M&A activity peaked in 2018 with more than 160 deals, and we estimate 90-100 deals for 2019.” The full research report from TokenData. 
- [How DeFi Cannibalizes PoS Security](https://medium.com/dragonfly-research/how-defi-cannibalizes-pos-security-84b146f00697): “On-chain lending has the potential to disrupt traditional secured lending. But it seems it may do more than that: it might also disrupt proof of stake consensus.” Haseeb Qureshi’s analysis.  
- [Quadratic Payments: A Primer](https://vitalik.ca/general/2019/12/07/quadratic.html): Vitalik Buterin gives an introduction to quadratic voting, quadratic funding and quadratic attention purchase (with pictures!). 
- [Cash is not disappearing. Your plastic card is.](https://eucrypto.substack.com/p/cash-is-not-disappearing-your-plastic): A curious comparison of cash-related facts and stats across global markets, in the context of the rise of cryptocurrency.
- [Exploring New Markets In The Arts #2: Generative Art Economies](https://blog.simondlr.com/new-markets-in-the-arts-generative-art-economies): “When you combine the ability to form economies around generative art, you can empower the art to descend from its adjacent possible space into our world.” Simon de la Rouviere shares generative art powered by crypto-economics.
- [The Scoop: A Conversation with Alex Gladstein](https://www.theblockcrypto.com/post/49524/alex-gladstein-cso-of-the-human-rights-foundation-discusses-the-increasing-importance-of-bitcoin-in-the-fight-for-global-human-rights): Listen or read the transcript of a talk with Alex Gladstein, CSO of Human Rights Foundation, on the intersection of cryptocurrency and human rights issues, from Virgil Griffith’s arrest to the IMF in Venezuela. 
- [Introducing ZK Sync: the missing link to mass adoption of Ethereum](https://medium.com/matter-labs/introducing-zk-sync-the-missing-link-to-mass-adoption-of-ethereum-14c9cea83f58): Matter Labs gives an extensive analysis on ZK Sync: “a trustless scaling and privacy solution for Ethereum based on ZK Rollup.”
- [Imagining A Multi-Payment Financial Network](https://medium.com/@michaelmsoussan/imagining-a-multi-payment-financial-network-d623bc4bb2f1): “A multi-payment financial network is one that will accept almost any type of financial payment instrument, regardless of the specific provider.” Michael Soussan explores the opportunities and caveats.
