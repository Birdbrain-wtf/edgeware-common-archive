We’re all excited about the arrival of EDG. But what will the Edgeware token actually be used for? Here are some of the possibilities: 
## Core Technology 
Implementing scaling technologies such as runtime improvements, sharding, and off-chain extensions. This might also include:

- Building an ecosystem bridge. 
- [Community-specific lockdrops](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/7-why-dont-we-make-the-lockdrop-multiasset).
- Creating a [stablecoin](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/135-how-do-stablecoins-work-and-should-we-use-them). 

## Governance Experiments 
Experimenting with governance systems, including on-chain identity as well as tools for organizing and coordinating the work of core developers and other participants. This might include: 

- Experimenting with crowdsourcing methods ([DACs, Quadratic Funding](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/101-crowdsourcing-the-public-good-dominant-assurance-contracts), or other [ways to fund open source](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/116-sustaining-open-source-funding)). 
- [Funding hackathons](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/163-funding-a-blockchain-hackathon-on-edgeware).
- Experimenting with other [governance mechanisms](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/140-organizational-governance-mechanisms-for-blockchains) (like [exit strategies](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/147-designing-dao-primitives-the-exit-mechanism) or a [Harberger tax system](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/160-how-blockchains-could-change-the-tax-system-harberger-and-daos)). 
- Creating [Curation Markets](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/142-tests-and-opportunities-for-curation-markets).
- Funding DAOs.
- Executing a [100,000 DAO experiment](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/158-seeding-100k-daos-on-edgeware).
- Supporting [co-operative organizations](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/144-the-promise-of-onchain-governance-for-cooperation-blockchain-and-coops).
- Implementing [shared ownership of social media channels](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/165-how-to-implement-shared-ownership-of-media-channels).

## Developer Experience
Building a mature toolchain for developing, debugging, and testing smart contracts. This might include:

- Supporting [the creation of development tools](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/95-how-to-fund-dev-tools-for-blockchains).
- Integration of existing development tools. 
- Establishing a grants program. 

## User Experience
Funding wallets and user experience primitives (e.g., JavaScript libraries) to make decentralized apps simple and easy to use. Edgeware dApps could be created for DeFi, developer support, gaming, NFT markets, or other purposes. 
## Ecosystem Support 
Engaging Edgeware validators, developers, users, and other stakeholders through global meet-ups. This might include:

- Establishing an [Edgeware Ambassador Network](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/154-creating-an-edgeware-community-ambassador-program)

We’ve seen the enthusiasm for some of these EDG use cases already. Building DAOs seems to have the strongest draw among community members. Framework Ventures has identified DAOs as the emergent use case for crypto that Edgeware is poised to offer the best space for. [In their words](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/30-validator-councillor-campaign-framework-ventures), “If we were to fund DAOs directly through the treasury, they would immediately be some of the largest DAOs in the space.” Meanwhile, [OurNode DAO](https://ourno.de/#one) is already building a Moloch-type investment DAO for EDG-holders. 

In a related area, the Commonwealth team has recently spoken with Start.Coop about how cooperative organizations might be able to leverage Edgeware’s tech and governance. We could look at the possibility of spinning up a DAO on Edgeware for a co-op and be among the first to offer these traditional organizations a crypto-based solution. This could be an opportunity for EDG to expand the limits of the crypto-sphere, sharing blockchain innovation with a whole new group of users. 

As we talk about the future of Edgeware, there is widespread agreement that what makes this software so special is the community’s commitment to staying at the bleeding edge of blockchain technology. That’s why we can consider something as exciting as a crypto-based co-op. That’s why there’s so much enthusiasm for building DAOs. We all share this sentiment: many of these things have never been done before, and we have a unique chance to experiment.
