“I owe $650 for SEO service for selling my books and some money to my parents for a share in the house and the food.” 

[Quote](https://forum.aragon.org/t/is-it-realistic-to-get-1000-dai-from-agp10-every-month/1519) the Aragon community member who requested 1,470 DAI from the AGP-10 Community Funding DAO - after they were awarded the money. They did not include what the money would be used for in their request proposal, yet the proposal passed - with a single vote. They went on to ask whether or not they could rely on the Community Funding DAO for 1,000 DAI every month. 

The Aragon community funding DAO is meant to encourage and support contribution to the Aragon community and projects - not ambiguous UBI payouts to individuals. While the community fund proposal template is not required, it is still highly recommended to avoid situations like this one.  

Responses from the Aragon community and leadership fell along a spectrum of confusion to hostility. The fact that the awarded DAI did not go towards bettering the Aragon ecosystem was cited as an abuse of the public fund and a clear reason for why the money should be returned (which it eventually was, at the consistent suggestion of Aragon leadership).  

While the core team did resolve the situation, this may be an indication that a public fund needs a formalized funding request process, perhaps including a minimum quorum for votes on funding proposals.
