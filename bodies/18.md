We launched Edgeware Testnet V0.5.0, but an issue with the genesis script that made it impossible for validators to join. Testnet V0.6.0 will be launching...within the next 24 hours, approximately.

**Block production is irregular on these latest testnets! We've left a large number of Aura slots open for new validators, so there will be delays of several minutes at a time where no blocks are produced.** If you are taking any actions like voting, signaling, staking, or creating proposals, they may take several minutes to complete.

Otherwise, we've been finishing a lot of work on the user interface. Here are the release notes:

- Discussions have been completely redesigned.
- Proposal pages have been redesigned.
- Responsive styles are greatly improved.
- You can upvote posts.
- You can now attach images to discussion threads, and they will show up inline.
- You can now preview posts.

There are still a lot of rough edges and unfinished workflows. But our focus is now on *usefulness and usability*:

- Organizing the homepage
- Making profiles more intuitive (the ability to set profile pictures is coming soon)
- General functionality: @-mention other users, subscribe to threads, get email notifications, etc.
- Bugfixes

The team is in Berlin for Blockchain Week. We'll be speaking at a few events, linking up with fellow developers, and continuing the push forward to mainnet.
