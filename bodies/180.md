When we soft-launched on September 15th, validators who had submitted a campaign proposal and validated on testnets over the summer were invited to join the genesis validator set.
Once the chain turned on, we noticed many validators were slashed due to incorrectly configured keys. This accounted for the great majority of slashing events at the time, more than any technical issues with Substrate. It also caused the chain to stall unnecessarily.
As a result, we’re now planning to launch mainnet with a much smaller validator set of only Commonwealth nodes. We think this will allow the upcoming network launch proceed much more smoothly, reducing uncertainty and allowing everyone to have a better experience.
Here is our plan:

- There will be 60 validator slots at the time of launch, with Commonwealth filling in 10.
- We expect other validators will quickly join that set, after which governance can raise the validator limit.
- For prospective validators who participated on testnet, we will send nominal balances to anyone who requested right after genesis, so they can boot up a validator and join the next-up queue. We will also use our EDG to nominate and support those validators so they can join the active set.

A few other considerations also inform this process:

- **[4 keys for validating](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/176-final-testnet-v099-updates-and-validating-cli-commands):** When we launched the lockdrop several months ago, only 3 keys were necessary to run a validator. After technical upgrades since the last community call, another key is now required. We had hoped to avoid this change, and we think it increases the likelihood of setting up a validator incorrectly.
- **60 initial validator slots:** At genesis, only 10 validator slots will be populated. History has shown that Edgeware usually has around 30-50 validators on a network, so we think anyone who wants to join will still be able to. Of course, governance can vote to change the number of slots afterwards!
- **Low initial inflation from NPoS:** Edgeware‘s inflation system targets a certain amount staked (initially 80%). However, if the amount of total stake bonded is lower than the target, inflation will be lower; it may be as low as 0.50%. This reduces the economic disparity of starting with a small validator set. And as more validators bond post-network launch, inflation will float upwards and rewards will be shared more equally.


At this point, we’d like to ask for the community’s feedback on this plan, and if that goes well, we will move forward with it! Please leave your comments in this thread.

While an official date has yet to be set, so far the testnet has been [running smoothly](https://telemetry.polkadot.io/#list/Edgeware%20Testnet%20v099) with no major reported errors.
