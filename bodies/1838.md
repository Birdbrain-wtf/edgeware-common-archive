So DAOs are pretty hot right now. 

_Why? _

Because DAOs are organising entities for humans.

Humans who pool resources and then embark on some grand endeavour together. 
That grand endeavour may be starting a cafe, building a pyramid or launching some cat JPGs.
You see grand is all about perspective and importance is a matter of opinion.

When you plug all these endeavours together you get a society.
That's composibility. Interoperability. Bridges. 
That's lego blocks. 
Crypto didn't invent this. 
And you know who invented lego blocks. Lego. 
Crypto just changed created a new canvas.
A new layer of abstraction. 

Abstraction. This is how our species has conjured _reality_ out of thin air. 

A company is a set of organising instructions committed (originally) to paper in ink.

DAOs are a set of organising instructions committed to networks by code.

Neither is more 'real' than the other. 

A blockchain is a communications technology.
Just like paper and the printing press.
They are different mediums to tell human stories.

And those stories are games. 
They're experiments to give us something to do. 
That help us figure out our world. 
That make us meaning making machines. 
That give us purpose. 
A reason to get up in the morning. 
To feel alive. 
To feel useful. 

A company or cooperative is just the ticket to play a part in a certain game.
What's important is the people you decide who to play with.
What values to be guided by.
What intentions to set.
What ambitions to shoot for.
What constitute 'success' and equally what constitutes 'failure', 
When to realise it's [just a ride](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lqv8Os-AdUo&t=4s).

All these things we worry about - linking the real world to crypto. 
The real world is no more real than the world of digital things. 
It's all magic and myth. 
Symbols and spells. 

Languages.
Towns.
Cities.
Nations. 
Religions. 
All the -isms. 
Every single one is a story. 
A hallucination. A waking dream. 

They're all just systems of belief. 
Belief systems. 
Art and science. 
Logic and intuition. 
Network and narrative.
Yin and Yang.  

So DAOs aren't new. 
They're just about people. 
Our hopes, dreams, fears and expectations. 

The question is what story you want to believe.
What game you want to play.

A layer 1 chain DAO.

Where anyone can propose the next step.
