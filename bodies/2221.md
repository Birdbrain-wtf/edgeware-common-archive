## Edgeware community film: Director's treatment
By [@Jamie_J](/edgeware/account/jqCACU9fQzGpzRzMkoZLyWKsCwtkG5zTpUgXkYvfPqwZ3Rt) 

_When I originally started to do the creative for the film it was very much in the mould of big cinematic shots and musical composition, with voiceover that spoke to world changing possibilities of a project like Edgeware. _

_However the longer I spent around the people and projects incubated by the community, the more I realised I was falling into the trap of trying to represent a new form of organisation (a DAO) in a old fashioned corporate way. _

_We’re going to get around this by breaking the 4th wall and letting the viewer into our team’s process and experience of making the film - making something interesting, experimental and captivating in the process. _

_We want to reflect the actual feeling of working within and being invited into the community, an approach which mirrors the experience of anyone else who wants to join and collaborate - after all, we need to encourage new people with a broad mix of talents into what can feel like an intimidating arena. _

_This overall change in approach was Inspired by this Young Thug music video:_


_To achieve this kind of feel the film will include Olivia’s and my own interactions with the community, screen records, Discord chats and/or Zooms. _

_We will utilise on screen text to quickly explain why certain decisions were made and also serve as a way to get key technical information about the project over quickly but with personality and honesty rather than fluffing it up, or hiding the challenges of building and governing a network of decentralised contributors._

_This style, delivered with a slightly a less comedic approach seemed to be a much more honest and original approach more befitting of a film itself funded through, and representing a decentralised community and still allowed us to get key information across to newcomers. _

## **The core interviews**
Active members will get across key information whilst also capturing their personal relatable journeys that brought them to the project:

- Where did Edgeware start?
- What is Substrate and what is Polkadot all about?
- How is the Edgeware community treasury generated and how is it spent?
- What are the problems Edgeware might address over the long term?
- Why do they choose to work for a DAO?


## **Style**
As previously mentioned, as a decentralised community we spend most of our lives of video calls and Discord voice chats so it makes sense to embrace this, utilising Riversidefm screen recording and high quality audio capture for our interviews with the active members. 

We want to see the human side of people so we will use personal archive to help us steer clear of the usual corporate talking head aesthetic.

## Community involvement
We will source archive from the community (videos/photo’s/screen records of projects they’re working on) these sections will feel intimate and help paint a picture of people trying to build and work within global community, 

Through voice notes gathered along with the footage we will hear the divergent reasons for people getting involved, from dev’s who want to build here, validators, creatives, people interested in governance and those who think it’s a smart investment.

## Funded projects (and those in development)

Our aim is to rep each funded project in a succinct 20- 30 second vignette. In these sections we want get across:

- Their journey to Edgeware and first impressions
- What it is attracted them to the project as people
- What are the benefits of being funded by Edgewares community treasury 
- The challenges of working in a decentralised way


Each project will be given int’s own modern visual style befitting of their particular arena and goals.
