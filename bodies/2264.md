## Edgeware council members

[@Thom Ivy](/edgeware/account/jKauGVPCQkteGTDKuuNwhYhMBrj5QuytgybEC4A5cS4Bki5) has recently pointed out that we currently have a number of council positions held by in-active members, who do not participate in governance, nor the community.

He suggested that people vote for a few more of his accounts, which solves the problem in one way, but is **definitely not ideal**. 

Though the council does not play a hugely active role in the project, whilst it exists it is still an important part of our governance.

Ideally the Edgeware council should occupied by people who are ongoing contributors to the chain, its culture and technical foundations and represent a broad set of stakeholders and who can be called upon to fulfil certain actions when necessary. 

Those representatives should offer experience and insights and cover off both the main stakeholder groups in Edgeware and the key elements required to push the project forward.

- Substrate development
- Validators
- Smart contracts (EVM / Ink!) 
- Governance / Proposals 
- Community development
- App development 
- Core community 
- Visual / design / media 


Right now we do have a pretty broad coverage of those areas, but we could add some expertise and given [Moonrocket's successful candidacy](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1510-rounding-up-the-numbers-moon-rocket-for-council) we should aim to add a few more. 

**Substrate development**
Much has been said about Edgeware's lack of technical / substrate expertise on the council and so I would like to nominate [@Ramsey](/edgeware/account/k44nJ5yAhFz3XHGeTPuKvegJ4GD1YCSVaPc7wj4miVL6epN) since he is both leading Kabocha and therefore has an increasingly strong grasp of Substrate fundamentals, but is also fresh blood with plenty of ideas around on-chain governance. 

**Visual / design **
I would also nominate [@Johan](/edgeware/account/jmoxayjwtZozJ6rr64ikaXhsZa7SZm7ihPwajvtodqXrmnj) since he has been key to the development of Edgeware's website, branding and is a founding member of the Edgeware Agency.

> There are difficult decisions to make, and there's no way we can resolve them using our current structure, which is coin voting + the council + community calls. We'll need a different political structure to be able to make this decisions, and it seems pretty clear to me that this should be a 1-person-1-vote entity open to anyone who has contributed to the Edgeware network since its inception. **I consider this the single highest priority/most key thing that we could do next; however, if we try to do it, we must do it well.**
