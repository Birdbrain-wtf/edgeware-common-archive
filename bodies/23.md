Here are a few questions that seem pertinent to gauge community / candidate thoughts on. As new campaigns roll in, we will aggregate them all in this thread.

### Questions for Validators

1. What is your experience as a validator or node operator?
2. What's your technical setup? (cloud vs. on-premise; software stack; key management)
3. Why are you interested in validating?

### Questions for Councillor Candidates

1. Please provide an overall summary of who you are and why you're interested in holding a councillor seat.
2. How do you propose the treasury budget should be spent?
3. What should Edgeware do to grow its community?
4. Which ecosystems beyond Polkadot should Edgeware bridge to, if any?
5. How do you think Edgeware governance system should evolve?

### Questions for Both

1. What are your positions on issues that governance will be expected to handle? For example:
    - Further lockdrop or airdrop to DOT holders, or holders of other assets
    - Genesis distribution (contract signalling, generalized lock)
    - In general, how should Edgeware handle forks? Are there other issues you believe EDG holders should be aware of, or which you believe governance should handle?
2. What proposals on the network are you most interested in seeing? (e.g. initial parachain offerings, DAOs, DeFi protocols, stablecoins, prediction markets, insurance)
3. Is there an overall long-term vision for the network you are most excited about?
