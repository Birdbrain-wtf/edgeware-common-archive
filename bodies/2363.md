# Overview
Kabocha is Edgeware's parachain candidate for Kusama. We're accelerating founders, creators and teams to build the subverse and metaverses for Web 3.0.

We will be delivering the Edgeware community into the Kusama ecosystem through an 100% undiluted distribution for Edgeware token holders. 

We’re doing it in Edgeware style, staying true to a fully community backed approach, while capturing the spirit of Kusama. 

Whereas Kusama is Polkadot’s experimental cousin, Edgeware is Kabocha’s mother and Kusama its technological father who houses an aria of canaries on its relay. Kabocha will inherit its father’s experimental and chaotic traits and will help realise its vision of being a nesting hub for the future of decentralised systems.

We propose a parachain that continues Edgeware’s governance and treasury experiment, community-driven mentality and applies to a multi-chain world, accelerating projects that further develop metaverses and the subverse.

Joining up with the Kusama network will enable seamless interoperability which will create new opportunities for real collaboration, co-operation and co-evolvement with other chains and communities. Be prepared for interesting, experimental and somewhat chaotic projects that push boundaries and inspire people to become immersed in Web 3.0 realms.

# Community mission
The Kabocha mission inherited from its mother Edgeware is to simply be the best place for teams to launch their projects from. Edgeware’s un-intimidating emergent leadership culture lets people build experience in all facets of decentralised organisations through participation. 

Kabocha will nurture and cultivate founders, creators and teams to build networks and tools of the future. We will give rise to new ways of designing social ecologies and interoperable network economies, working at the nexus of blockchain, art, media, science, and philosophy, for the betterment of all.  

By bootstrapping on Edgeware’s fair and wide distribution, active governance and treasury, we offer something no other Substrate project can - a truly decentralised community accelerator owned and operated by no central authority or offering preferential terms in private rounds that may later skew, confuse or constrain its public mission and assurances as to the project’s credible neutrality. 

## Roadmap

The Kabocha roadmap builds on Edgeware’s difficult-to-replicate advantages and is focused on evolving the incubator model into a multi-chain accelerator that advances tooling, parachains and relay networks that prioritise broad and fair community ownership. Our general approach is focused on improving the overall experience and capabilities for its users primarily around the everyday functions of DAOs.   

We will be sourcing talent and projects from Edgeware’s incubator, as a result, much of Kabocha’s ‘roadmap’, will be glimpsed in Edgeware first, allowing us to maintain a close relationship between the two projects.

**Will EDG holders be diluted by Crowdloan rewards?**

No. 100% of the EDG snapshot will be used as the Kabocha initial distribution, with no additional dilutions to EDG holders. 

**If no dilution then where will crowdloan rewards come from?**
The only balance in the distribution that will change control is the treasury, which will be reassigned to KAB governance. This account will hold 6.43m KAB and will be used to reward Crowdloan participants, collators and maintainers for the initial parachain lease.
 
This approach will add no taxation or dilution to individual EDG holders, a pure 1:1 distribution.

## Treasury Inflation 
After Kabocha’s initial treasury supply, Kabocha will not have fixed inflation going into its treasury account but instead successful treasury proposals will be funded through minting new KAB. 

Though there is no fixed inflation to accumulate in a treasury account, the treasury is not by contrast infinite. 

Treasury funding will be proportionate to the supply of proposals and the demand from the community to fund them. 

This will allow for more flexibility in distribution of tokens to relevant and valuable contributing teams, it will also remove negative psychological effects that may arise from a depleting treasury. 

Yearly inflation will be capped at 20%, though some years the aggregate of proposals will likely be much lower. 

On a second parachain lease a proposal can be made to fund the Treasury supply with the agreed upon rewards for crowdloans, collators, maintainers, etc.

## Network Services 
Network services is a role that removes the need for a network to launch with a centralised foundation. It introduces a 10% fee on top of a tender or proposal to be delivered by a specific builder, levying the chain with costs required to research, develop and deliver projects to completion. 

Decent Partners is Kabocha’s initial Network Service Provider, continuing its role from Edgeware, onboarding new projects and teams to each network, joining the dots between the people and ideas and maintaining community cohesion. 

## Community proposals 

To encourage an open and informed community, we will be launching the network with Kabocha Tenders which are developed and presented by Decent Partners. 

These Request for Proposals (RFPs) ensure transparency, accountability, and open access for proposals such that we set a brand new standard and level of fairness, which eradicates the notion of self-dealing.

The proposers will respond in separate threads in Kabocha governance and outline their proposals in finer detail.

On success the winning tenders will become master contractors that are welcome to sub-contract if necessary to deliver on the project.

The pool of proposers may start off being small, but will grow over time and will create more variation and therefore increase the overall quality of proposals, providing templates for others to follow. 

# Legal & Compliance

The chain will only become active after the community takes ownership. 

The service providers (Decentration of Decent Partners, or any other individuals) will only have sudo authority at test phase.

Kabocha will go through a method of handing over governance to the community. when a proposal to destroy sudo authority from anyone other than Kabocha governance, will be when the chain becomes active. 

The ability to send transactions will be turned on by Kabocha governance.

We are completing a full legal review that will be added to the overall document and template.
