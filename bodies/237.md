**Council **

_Dillon:_
Several parameters re: council. There is
- Lock staking, or bonding for voting or other forms of participating in gov.
- Proposals have voting periods, there is also an intiial launch period.
- Vetoing, fast-tracking, and a time delay after voting is done- even cancellations are possible using ⅔ of the council, essentially nullifying. 
- Phragmen system applies to councils.

 
_Ray:_
Councillors get elected on a pro-rata or a distibituion of the EDG that voted for them. For the first round, the chain startrs with one Councillor, and over 28 day periods, people can lock EDG to vote or run for Council. At the end of 28 days the council is repopulated to a number between 6-13, based on the Kusama experience.  We encourage validators to run, we expect 4 weeks for people to do multiple rounds of campaigning.

_Dillon:_
Bridges and Defi are exciting priorities, including making DAOs a core use case.

**Call Reviews December’s Top Points**

_Thom_ asks for updated ideas on the following issues:

- Inflation seems high
- Should we rebalance between treasury and vals? 
- _Jakub_ recommended 50/50, or 20/80 to fill treasury faster and build more.
- _Dillon_ describes NPOS, 50/50 is the split when Vals are fully bonded to target stake, 80% is the parameter. Assuming 80% of network is staked, then split is 50/50. Until then, the balance incentivizes more staking in the network.
- _Viktor:_ If staking is 30% then what would balance be? 
- _Dillon:_ We will create a modeling calculator. The least amount of the block reward that currently goes to vals is 3% under low staking conditions.
- _Viktor:_ We should have a separate call for economics. 

 
- Should we moratorium the treasury proposals?
- _Ray:_ Wait until meaningful participation to see really good proposals. We could also say no more than 5-10% of total minted in treasury, a microgrant system. We can also deploy personally some of the EDG allocated to Commonwealth.
- _ Viktor: _Gotta get to a stable blockchain, and then usage will tell us what good and bad areas are.
- _Thom_: Are we comfortable with erratic treasury actions without hard-coded caps or preventions?
- _Viktor_: Keep stakes and voting council small to avoid intially problems early on.

 
**_Jakub_: How many ambassadors are out there?**
_Thom_: Early on we brought on several folks but are planning to relaunch via the treasury, specifically community-led resourcing. 
_Jakub:_ Shouldn’t we have ambassadors before mainnet?
_Michael: _Ambassador programs advocate for an idea, we need to figure out core use cases to build an audience around. 
_Jakub:_ We should make a stable and well-documented platform first.
**Council Elections**
_Viktor: _3 months may be too long, don’t want to lock essentially unknown people in. Could also do three 1-month periods first, escalate after.

**Candidacy Bond**
_Dillon:_ We should increase the candidacy bond - currently it is set to 1000 EDG- we had many dust-to-low amounts of EDG allocated in the Lockdrop, however, we may want more ‘skin in the game’ for the purposes of running for council.

_Thom_: I support the higher bar.
