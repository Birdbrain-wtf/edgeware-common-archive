## **INTRODUCTION**
 Art Rate is a platform which is try to serve some services to navigate creation power. Art Rate uses a website (in progress) to manage artists' artwork for proposing in international markets, therefor this website is kind of vitrine of artworks which is categorized according to art formats and emotions. Also artists can define new categories of emotions in formats part to describe their art concepts better and expand emotion rout in platform. 
By assigning open sources semantic analysis tools and designing markets analyzing dashboards for every user we will show new routs of comprehension of subjects and persuading them to focus their creation power in those concepts which could be more noticeable in international markets. Art Rate try to support talented and artists' career by drawing their actions in platform and the routs which they took it.
Also it's considered to use Discord app to create team communities and expanding our social network there. As technical view it possible to use this app for making conversation rooms and being connected to Art Rate app in our development plan. As well as using Discord our mentors, admins and experts can manage teams and give them related services. Another side of Art Rate is about financial transactions which could be occurred in trading part. We will hold auction for master artworks and also manage some effective relations for artwork brokerage. We will handle these transactions by NFT technology and these parts could fit us in Edgeware ecosystem.

## **Background Information **
#Art_Rate platform is result of a brain storming which was holed in Tehran, Iran, between "Romina Art Gallery" and "Godar Thinkers Team". In that brain storming session, lack of suitable tools for analyzing subjects to art creations and lack of proper platform for proposing and trading artworks were discussed. Then integration ideas emergence Art Rate in our mind. We can consider Art Rate as kind of a cultural program which is try to serve suitable services to its own audience community who want take a part in art social network by their identities and making their careers. Even for supporting our local society we singed two agreements with "Science and Technology Paradise" and "Khane Sarhang cultural campus" to establishing campaigns management office and holding auctions and galleries there, although Romina Art Gallery supports art fields too and Godar Thinkers Team navigates the project.

## **Detail** 
Art Rate is not just a cybernetic community, art as an international language can gather people under a common roof of concept. We should consider that artworks include wide spectrum of formats, and most of them are kind of goods and are realistic. Therefor in first step of our development we focus on paintings and digital arts as our primary format which will be supported in Art Rat. Also according to strategic priorities for Edgeware include attracting creators, expanding and developing community and establishing commonwealth, we try to gather artists in Art Rate for proposing their artworks and supporting to trade them by in rolling in ecosystem.

Due to Art Rate is a kind of cultural program, we reportsome executive actions were occurred till now:
