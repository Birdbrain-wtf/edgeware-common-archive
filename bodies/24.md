I want to spill my thoughts on what a ClimateDAO can/should focus on and how it can operate. The goal of a ClimateDAO is to donate to non-profits or eventually act as one to do work that benefits the environment.

I think that any given ClimateDAO should have a specific area of focus (ocean/land/sea) as well as a type of action (cleanups/recycle programs/land purchasing). I'm happy to hear thoughts on whether there are alternatives that may be better.

Nonetheless, I want to focus on a land purchasing and rehabilitation ClimateDAO. If DAOs are bound to some legal framework, say LLCs in the United States, then this DAO can likely purchase land. A ClimateDAO that purchases land would focus on the following areas:
1. Finding endangered species and preserving/purchasing the surrounding landscapes for preservation.
2. Purchasing cheap land and growing an ecosystem.
3. Financing projects that involve rehabilitating land from disasters such as oil spills, etc.

The goal of my idea of a good ClimateDAO should be less political and less controversial than I'm sure others can be. The goal is to amass ownership of land resources to preserve and rehabilitate them, providing opportunities for new ecosystems to grow, and preventing industries from being able to extend into said regions.
