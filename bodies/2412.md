In community-owned projects and networks, no single authority can dictate where it goes, instead, the direction emerges over time through our funding and open-source developments.

 This section is intended to help track issues and features we'd like to see in the network. When items are popular, grant-writers and bounty-curators can fulfill them. Additionally, we can plan node upgrades and releases around hotly-demanded improvements.

 In short, a democratic eature request section is (I believe) essential for developing open-source and community-owned networks.  

To submit, post like normal, and add as many details as you'd like- being short on details. Be sure to vote on others ideas! In the future, it could also be interesting to stake towards feature requests, with backers of activated features winning treasury-spends or a lottery!
