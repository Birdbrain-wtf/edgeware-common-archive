# **Background:**

The diversification was an initiative to invest Edgeware treasury funds into other crypto assets as a part of diversifying the treasury.

The fund resulted in asset value preservation to an extent where it no longer requires active reallocation.

As active reallocation was no longer required the responsible team decided to retire the diversification fund A initiative.

Relevant threads:

- <https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/3809-funding-asset-diversification-proposal_fund-a>
- <https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/13201-edg-diversification-fund-funds-recoup-proposal>

## **Update:**

Recently, we have made a change in the management of this fund. We decided to transfer the assets from the initial MetaMask wallet to a new one due to concerns that the original wallet might have been compromised.

Details:

- **Reason for Transfer**: We suspected that the initial MetaMask wallet could have been compromised.
- **Action Taken**: To ensure the safety and integrity of the fund, We securely transferred all assets within the diversification fund to a new MetaMask wallet.
- **Future Steps**: We request the community to take a community mandate through a series of remark-based referenda to determine the further utility and custody of the retired diversification fund A.
