The idea is derived from war bonds, but in this case the bond only pays back the principal sum if a specified climate target is met. For example if the global temperature rise is kept below a certain threshold after 10 years or carbon in the atmosphere is kept below a certain ppm the smart contract will pay back the principal to the lender. The capital raised is then invested by an elected council - preferably a group of people with a mixed skill set covering venture capital, wealth management, technology, climate science etc. Just as the Edgeware treasury invests in blockchain projects this council would invest in a green future, and because the smart contracts only pay out if climate goals are met we can be certain that all the voting rights are held by people who actually care about the climate, so the council will only be beholden to them.

I think the world is more valuable when it's not a desert wasteland so I'm trying to think of why that's not already reflected in our economic systems. These bonds would flip the tragedy of the commons on its head as it gives a financial incentive to improve the commons.
