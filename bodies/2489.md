## A few quick reminders 

At nearly [9m blocks](https://polkadot.js.org/apps/?rpc=wss%3A%2F%2Fedgeware.api.onfinality.io%2Fpublic-ws#/explorer) processed, Edgeware is the second oldest Substrate mainnet chain after Kusama and predates even Polkadot, the two most valuable networks in the whole Substrate ecosystem. 

Both KSM and DOT are **relay chains**, therefore Edgeware is the **oldest and most experienced** Substrate solo-chain in existence. 

Edgeware launched via the lockdrop - there was no token sale, with over [2.2m ETH](https://twitter.com/zhusu/status/1167939840467324928?lang=ca) either locked by individual accounts or signalled by smart contracts holding ETH including Parity's locked multisig, Binance, Coinbase and Aragon. 

It is worth noting that Binance and Coinbase have still not listed Edgeware and so that large distribution is still in yet to be released - there is no reason to think that it will not happen as Edgeware becomes more established. 

The original plan from the launch team Commonwealth Labs was for Edgeware to become Polkadot's first parachain, with a value bearing test-net 'Hedgeware' on Kusama, with a primary focus on becoming an **[ecosystem incubator](https://blog.edgewa.re/edgeware-2020/)**. 

Due to the lockdrop distribution, which offered 90% of tokens to the public, these plans became the subject of public debate, as a community of tokenholders and also grant recipients emerged with their own ideas as to the best direction for the community, this had the effect of removing Commonwealth Labs' ability to make unilateral decisions with regard to funding and governance. 

**This is a feature of Edgeware, not a bug. **

## Commonwealth Labs move on

In the last few weeks, Commonwealth Labs have announced a move to offer core-development to [Astar](https://astar.network/) - formerly known as Plasm network, who also conducted a pair of lockdrops as part of their launch that saw [150k ETH locked](https://lockdrop.astar.network/#/lockdrop-stat).

Commonwealth have since announced that Picoswap, a fork of Sushiswap will no longer be coming to Edgeware, presumably as it will be launching on Plasm.

It seems clear that Commonwealth Labs see Astar as a better home for their projects than Edgeware, since Astar is already a Kusama parachain and the project is more clearly focused on being a home for dApps. 

## Edgeware is proving out the incubator model 

Initially this could be viewed as bad news - however under closer scrutiny it proves the opposite - it further **validates Edgeware's value proposition as an incubator of talent in Web3/Substrate.** The fact that Astar value Commonwealth Labs and Webb's experience is testament to that.
 
With Kabocha aiming to join Kusama it will become interoperable with Astar and so collaboration between Edgeware incubated teams and projects is inevitable and already accelerating. 

With Commonwealth Labs role in Edgeware diminishing, the  opportunity now rests entirely with community leadership, most directly through the proposals put forward by a decentralised network of individuals, teams and collectives. 

We are fulfilling a clear role in the Substrate ecosystem as a **Web3 focused incubator**, a place for people to learn their craft, to test their ideas and to develop their experience in on-chain governance, alongside a network of their peers. 

With [Kabocha](https://kabocha.network), we are taking things a step further, and aiming to offer teams who contribute to Edgeware's Incubator, a further stepping stone to an Web3 Accelerator, that can open up their ideas and interests to a multichain universe on Kusama. 

It's worth checking [@Ramsey](/edgeware/account/k44nJ5yAhFz3XHGeTPuKvegJ4GD1YCSVaPc7wj4miVL6epN)'s post on [Edgeware's multichain reality](https://medium.com/coinmonks/edgeware-a-multi-chain-reality-not-just-a-solo-chain-5769d95638c5). 

## Kabocha will be Edgeware's accelerator. 

Kabocha, in itself, is also a stepping stone - if we launch successfully, we will prove that we, the Edgeware community, can also conceive, launch and operate a community owned Substrate chain entirely in the wild, with no central intermediary or legal entity required. 

So now Edgeware's evolution as the foundation for **decentralised public networks** has three clear development phases:

1. **Edgeware - Web3 incubator **
2. People arrive, sweep the floors, bond with the community, figure out how to help others, test our their ideas in discussions - an ashram for founders as [@Ramsey](/edgeware/account/k44nJ5yAhFz3XHGeTPuKvegJ4GD1YCSVaPc7wj4miVL6epN) would say. 
3. Propose their own grants which are initially stewarded by more experienced members of the community (those who have proposed and delivered projects) who provide network services. 
4. Become active members of the Edgeware community hive-mind and are elevated to more evolved roles in line with their peers. 


1. **Kabocha - Web3 accelerator **
2. Edgeware incubated chain with 1:1 distribution that will bond to Kusama. 
3. Opens up interoperability to Edgeware incubated projects to Kusama's ecosystem of tools and chains. 
4. Demonstrates capability to build a chain farming model for publicly owned chains that never touch traditional legal entities and exists entirely on and through digital networks (some might call this metaverse native). 


1. **Relay network - Web3 ecosystem**
2. Founders and teams who make it through the ashram and learn the basics of decentralised organistion and creation acclerate their project through Kabocha.
3. They are doing this alongside peers who have also graduated from Edgeware's incubator - this shared experience will mean they are far more mindful of the challenges and opportunities that exist and also more likely to have fostered cooperative mindsets and projects that tightly mesh their relative initiatives.
4. This can evolve into a relay network incubated by Edgeware and accelerated by Kabocha, that onboards chains who all benefit not just from **shared security**, but from **shared values.**
5. These shared values are not just nice words or promises, they are explicitly designed into the foundations of each new chain's governance, distribution and indeed** social contract**.
6. This social contract will be powerful because it is enforced in relation to peers - people who have established mutual trust, respect fosted through a shared experience. 


In summary, the future for Edgeware is bright, it is unique and it is emerging just the way it should.
