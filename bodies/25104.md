[Referendum #149](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/14850-proposal-establishment-of-legal-entity-for-edgeverse-dao?comment=73862) gave the community mandate to liquidate 3000 USDT equivalent of ETH from 'Diversification Fund A', to be utilized by Bharathcoorg for the initial registration expenses of Edgeverse DAO LLC in Marshall Island.
However, since the approval of the remark-based referendum, due to the reduction in the price of ETH, the ETH holdings are worth less than 3000 USD equivalent. As a result, the legal entity registration has still not been initialized.

Meanwhile, Bharathcoorg has discovered a promising and cost-effective alternative called [OtoCo](https://otoco.io/). This not only presented a viable option for the Edgeverse SubDAO but also opened up a path for Edgeware DAO's legal entity registration. After a thorough evaluation, Bharathcoorg recommends registering the legal entities for Edgeware DAO and Edgeverse SubDAO in the Swiss Association and Marshal Islands respectively.

**Costs for the legal entities' registrations:**
- Edgeware DAO: 99 USD {Swiss Association}
- Edgeverse SubDAO: 149 USD {Marshal Islands}

Total cost: 248 USD

Total cost with ~5% refundable buffer: **260 USD** {includes buffer for tx fees}

After the 1550 USDT disbursement from '[Diversification Fund A](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/24651-clarification-on-the-diversification-fund-a-account-change)' corresponding to [Referendum #152](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/24944-reimbursements-of-infrastructure-hosting-costs-oct-2023-nov-2024), the remaining USDT holding is more than sufficient for both legal entity registrations.


### Proposing a remark-based referendum for requesting the following things:
- The community's mandate to spend 260 USDT from 'Diversification Fund A' for the mentioned legal entity registrations for Edgeware DAO and Edgeverse SubDAO by Bharathcoorg.
- Revoke the previous community's mandate received through Referendum #149 to liquidate 3000 USD equivalent ETH from 'Diversification Fund A' as it's no longer required.
