## **Project overview: **
[Kabocha (KAB): A Kusama Parachain Project by Edgeware ](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/2363-kabocha-kab-a-kusama-parachain-project-by-edgeware) 

Edgeware requested a parachain proposal and [Kabocha](https://kabocha.network) is a community response.

This is a proposal to the Edgeware community for EDG funding for technical development of the chain, parachain bonding to Kusama (subject to successful auction) and approval for genesis KAB stake in Kabocha. 

This thread contains two milestones for the development and launch of Kabocha that will be executed by **separate treasury proposals and simplemajority referendums **which exist to execute specific on-chain actions via the will of the community. 

The second milestone earmarks an initial 3% of Kabocha for the initial stewardship of Kabocha which will be drawn from the initial treasury funds.  

This 3% is spread across two Kusama parachain terms - 1.5% on both to incentivise long term commitment. 

There will be other related proposals that seek to fund other aspects of Kabocha's development, including website and media.

## Stake structure
Rather than paid as a mercenary, the stake will keep founders aligned to the success of the project, acting as voting shares and incentives in both chain networks. 
## 
## Reverse vesting 
Reverse vesting is best practice in the startup world. 

All funds in Treasury Proposal #2 will be reverse-vested for 96 weeks, meaning tokens provided upfront in a multisig, but the rights to funds will be unlocked to Decentration pro-rata. 

## EDG incubates KAB
EDG funds KAB maintenance for 2 lease periods in return for getting 100% snapshot of balances in KAB's distribution. 

## **Why get maintenance funding from EDG treasury? **
KAB is born from EDG, with 100% of EDG holders balances preserved in the launch of the token. 

For this service Edgeware treasury can support KAB maintenance for the first 2 x parachain leases.
