## **The challenge**

- Currently the intended 'snapshot' offers a path for EDG holders to receive KAB if we win an auction, however it does not present a solution to how we acquire the required KSM to make that a reality in the first place. 
- We could rely on newcomers to Edgeware/Kabocha locking KSM, but this doesn't make the most of our own community, who remain passive and should be at the heart of this crowdloan campaign and key to securing the KSM we require. 
- We want to steer clear of early bird and referral bonuses and rely on our community. 


## **Solution(s)**

This is our current best solution to this challenge, it is not definitive, so we are open to other ideas and welcome feedback and constructive criticism. 

Remember our key objective here the best strategy to WIN the auction so please frame your responses through that lens. 

## **Activating EDG balance** 

This approach is designed to incentivise EDG holders to lock KSM in the crowdloan alongside newcomers. 

- People activate their EDG amount in KAB by locking a proportional amount of KSM into the crowdloan.
- This amount is arbitrary e.g. for every 500k EDG you need to lock 1 KSM, so if you have 50m EDG you need to lock 100 KSM.
- We currently have no decision on rates: KSM per EDG.
- Direct KSM contribution gets 1x KAB whilst KSM contribution from EDG holders gets 2x KAB 
- Potential for additional multiplier - may make things more complex, we'd rather keep things simple. 


- Contributors - community members with approved proposals to get an NFT pass which will activate their EDG balance and therefore bypassing the need to activate, this would reward active community members and encourage new proposals / contribution.


## **Upsides**

- A simple incentive mechanic to encourage EDG holders to contribute KSM 
- Edgeware's community will literally be crowdfunding this project - making it happen. 
- It will clean unused EDG balances, making Kabocha a more immediately engaged network.
- People will take it seriously as it’s not a free meal ticket for all 🎫  we're in this together.


## **Downsides**

- Changing the plan can cause confusion - why we're presenting this now. 
- People who bought EDG for this reason may not be so happy 
- Binance balances may well be left behind (unless we allow basic balance for all)


## **Our recommendation **

- We recommend creating a bonus / multiplier incentive for EDG holders who also commit KSM. We can fine tune the exact details but this seems to be the best way to align the goal and our community through active engagement. 
- If there is no stronger idea after 1 week from this post, we will move forward with this strategy.
