## Background

We recently had interest from a buyer, (and this has happened before,) for a large purchase of 500k-1m USD EDG. Typically these buyers request an aggressive discount off market price. Currently, there is no holder able to authorize such a sale on behalf of the community. 

In a discussion on Jan 14 morning, the following points were made by various community, I will restate them:

1. The buyer should disclose their identity, their intended purpose of the funds in order to reduce the likelihood that our markets may be gamed by the offer.

2. Time locks would help align these buyers to our long-term success, and this is common with these types of deals. They sometimes bring advisory or marketing support.

3. Buyers should be required to participate in an open auction round, rather than the community entertaining single offers from any third-party.

4. Purchases of any treasury-origin funds by a party should be open, conform to a set standard or be individually approved by democracy process.

5. Discounts should be within a community-approved range or come with certain qualifications, such as logistical support, time locks, or other features that benefit the chain proportionally.
