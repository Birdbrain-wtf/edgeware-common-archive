## Possible Use-Cases for EDG Scheduling & Automation

- **Future Payments**: Whenever someone wants to simply send a transaction at a future point in time, Automate is a solid and tested solution that will do the work for you, even if you forget about it in the future. Set it and forget it. This is extremely valuable if you also introduce the governance aspect of Edgeware into play, which at a time could also benefit users from scheduling.
- **Recurring payments**: With the arrival of Edgeware dApps, a recurring payment solution could potentially be applied to pay monthly subscriptions and other types of fees that may incur the users.
- **Conditional Scheduling**: As Automate’s “flagship” capability, conditional scheduling has been used quite a bit on Ethereum for all sorts of goals and setups, and we are certain that this will also give Edgeware users an edge whenever dealing with DEX and orders of all kinds. With conditional scheduling, you could even watch specific traders or whales’ addresses and match and mirror their trades.
