- **Background information**

We've been operating since 2016 as a decentralized non-automated organization, giving a solid platform to upcoming writers for sharing their knowledge and opinion related to football (soccer). It started as a Twitter Profile and a website where everyone could join and participate. Since then we've seen how our writers have been hired as writers by big media companies like The Athletic, Opta or scouted as Data Analysts for important sports clubs in Europe.

This past summer, BTL released its first digital magazine focused on the Football European Nations Cup. 
I helped structure the roadmap, supervised the writer's work progress, managed all the content's pre & post-production phases, directed the website development hosted on our server, and led the launch of the digital magazine released in July 2021.
The outcome was very positive due to the magazine's great feedback from the sports community, important former players, journalists, and influencers. 

Develop a DAO platform focused on creating one of the biggest and most reliable sports newsrooms in Web3. 
This would be the cornerstone for building a Metaverse where either content creators or engaged readers can have a platform where they might share their sports knowledge, watch games, assist meetings, and create new projects aligned to BTL's core values.
