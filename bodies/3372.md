## Terms
1. Every 2 weeks, with a submission period lasting two weeks.
2. Do rounds have themes?
3. Yes
4. Time commitment: 1.5hr every contest.
5. Logistics:
6. Announce + Explain
7. Open Rounds
8. Close, tally and pay rounds.
9. Manage contest payout funds.
10. eg Thom paid out of Agency funds.
11. Convenient model trusted community proposes to the treasury for the amounts needed for 3 Months.
12. Deposit/Reward — 2 payments to coordinator, one at the beginning and the end.
13. Deposit includes the 3 months of reward funds.
14. What is this worth to us?
15. $100 in EDG per Contest (200 per month x3 months =600 for the bounty duration.
16. Split into 300 on deposit, 300 on final.
17. Who will pick the winner?
18. Discord channel and reactions to select the winner. Coordinator will present at end of round.
19. Rewards (Top 3)
20. 1st: 30$ ~4524 EDG
21. 2nd: 15$ ~2262 EDG
22. 3rd: 6$ ~904 EDG
23. Plus: ECEPs
24. Same reward for all participants?
25. 100,000 ECEP/ divided across all participants
26. Pool increases with more participants?
27. Shankar to provide algo.
