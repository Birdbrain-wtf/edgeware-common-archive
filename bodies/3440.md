## **Project Summary **
This proposal seeks funding for the further development of Kabocha identity, visuals, teaser video and NFT assets ‘Kabocha seeds’ that will all be components of the upcoming Kabocha crowdloan, auction and launch. 

Kabocha seeds will be issued to EDG holders and KSM contributors in the Kabocha crowdloan the terms of which are to be finalised in the next week or so - there will be a separate post on this.  

The Kabocha seeds will offer new twists on Kanaria’s hatching and will have emerging utility in the emerging worlds created by Kabocha and its future offspring. 

There will never be an opportunity to buy these seeds after the crowdloan. 

The work is being delivered by Beej, one of the co-founders of [New-Futures](https://new-futures.co), the RMRK based Web3 media project funded by Edgeware and taking signups ahead of a launch on EDG/KAB in Q1 2022.

Check his personal portfolio out [here](https://www.beejandthem.com/)
