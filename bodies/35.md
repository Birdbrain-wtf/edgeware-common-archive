2. What's your technical setup? (cloud vs. on-premise; software stack; key management)
Mixed deployment of multiple public clouds and physical machine rooms
Public cloud services are now very easy to use and can provide multiple data centers to choose from. In addition to selecting several high-quality public Cloud service providers (such as AWS and Google Cloud), we have also set up our own physical machine rooms all over the world.

多公有云及物理机房混合部署
现在公有云服务已经非常方便使用，并且能够提供多个数据中心供我们自由挑选。除了选择多个优质的公有云服务商（例如 AWS 和 Google Cloud）之外，我们也在全球多个地区建立了自己的物理机房。

Globalization deployment
In order to ensure sufficient decentralization of nodes in physical areas and data centers, our full nodes and validators are globally distributed.

全球化部署
为了保证区块链节点在物理地域以及数据中心上足够去中心化，我们会将全节点和验证节点进行全球化部署。
