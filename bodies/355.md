On the last community call, we discussed the importance of building an active validator ecosystem. We're excited to see the progress towards that goal — right now, 17% of EDG has already been staked, with the least nominated validator having ~10m EDG or 0.2% of the overall network staked towards them.

Today we’d like to announce Commonwealth's Community Validator Program. To build a larger active validator ecosystem, we’ll use Commonwealth’s genesis allocation to nominate smaller community validators to join the active set.

To participate, validators will need to fulfill some initial criteria:

1. Validate on Edgeware Berlin Testnet for a week without issues ([steps to get started on Github](https://github.com/hicommonwealth/edgeware-node) and on [docs.edgewa.re](https://docs.edgewa.re/understanding-edgeware/validating-on-edgeware-ui/validation/setting-up-a-node))
2. Create a [validator campaign here on Commonwealth](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/23-how-to-create-validator-councillor-campaigns).
3. Participate in our monthly Edgeware Community Call ([third Wednesday every month, at 2PM ET, link to calendar invite](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r/eventedit/copy/YnQzMDFxNnFibGVoZ2Z0OHJ1cjVsbW03aGNfMjAyMDAyMTlUMTkwMDAwWiBjb21tb253ZWFsdGguaW1faWhiczg5MnBxdWJ2b2d0b2ltNmMxaGY3Y2tAZw/ZGlsbG9uY2hlbjEzQGdtYWlsLmNvbQ?scp=ALL&sf=true)).
4. Take a commission of no more than 10%.
5. Comment below on this thread :)


**Seeding New Validators**
If a new validating entity is starting out without EDG, we may seed individual validators with a nominal amount (100 EDG) to create stash / controller accounts, at our discretion.

**Ongoing Nominations**
On a rolling basis, we’ll now start nominating validators who’ve done the above. Validators will still have to play a role in recruiting non-Commonwealth nominations by being an active community member.

Once we’ve nominated a validator, we’ll periodically shift our nominations. We’ll be biasing towards making sure smaller community participants are able to become stakeholders, but things like ongoing validator performance (aka slashing) as well as community participation will impact how our stake gets shifted around. We’ll aim to do this regularly and make sure we communicate changes individually as well as Commonwealth if necessary.

We hope this is one way in which we can continue to bootstrap the larger Edgeware community.
