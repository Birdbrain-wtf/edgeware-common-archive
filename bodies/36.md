Nodeasy.com is an  Staking Economy Portal in China, we provide precisely statistics info about POS and MN projects. Questions for Councillor Candidates
1. Please provide an overall summary of who you are and why you're interested in holding a councillor seat.

Nodeasy.com是中国的Staking经济门户网站，我们想帮助国内的粉丝更好的理解Staking经济，更好的参与到网络治理的过程当中。同时在对于Edgeware，我们通过文章科普的方式吸引了一大批社群的粉丝参与到Lockdrop当中，我们希望成为议员候选人为中国的社群粉丝提供服务，更好地帮助他们传达自己的心愿。

Nodeasy.com is a Chinese staking economy portal. We want help Chinese fans to understand Staking economy better and participate in the process of network governance. For Edgeware, we have attracted a large number of community fans to participate in Lockdrop through the article. We hope to become a councillor candidate and helping Chinese community fans to speak out their aspiration.

2. How do you propose the treasury budget should be spent?

除了团队的开发经费之外，更多的是致力于Edgeware的生态网络建设，如全球社区的组建，验证节点的激励计划与合作方式，Edgeware的节点锁仓奖励分配，以及在未来EDG主网上线后的交易所上线计划。因为更好的流通性是投资者们更关心的一点。

In addition to the team's development funding, more is committed to Edgeware's ecological network construction, such as the formation of global communities, validators of incentive plans and cooperation, Edgeware node lock reward distribution, and Exchange plan after the EDG mainnet launch. Because better liquidity is a point that investors are more concerned about.

3. What should Edgeware do to grow its community?

Edgeware应该与Polkadot，Ethereum这两者保持联系，例如通过空投的方式吸引不同社群的其他粉丝。同时对于参与整个网络的各个节点验证者予以相应的激励，鼓励其在全球各个地区建立Edgeware的粉丝社群，让更多的人参与到网络治理当中。

Edgeware should keep in touch with both Polkadot and Ethereum,For example, by using airdrop to attract other fans from different communities. At the same time, corresponding incentives are given to each validator participating in the whole network.. Encourage them to establish Edgeware's fan community in all regions of the world, let more people participate in network governance.

4. Which ecosystems beyond Polkadot should Edgeware bridge to, if any?

比特币和以太坊。比特币是目前市值最大的加密货币，而以太坊拥有巨大生态如DeFi，Dapp等，与这两者建立生态系统的联系将更加有利于Edgeware生态的发展。

Bitcoin and Ethereum. Bitcoin is currently the largest cryptocurrency market value, and Ethereum has a huge ecosystem such as DeFi, Dapp, etc. And the establishment of ecosystem links with the two will be more conducive to the development of Edgeware ecology.

5. How do you think Edgeware governance system should evolve?

就像最初的lockdrop设计的分散性投放，如何使Edgeware的网络更加去中心化、如何设计更去中心化的投票模型对于治理模型尤为重要。

Just like the distributive delivery of the lockdrop , how to make Edgeware's network more decentralized, how to design a more decentralized voting model is especially important for the governance model.
