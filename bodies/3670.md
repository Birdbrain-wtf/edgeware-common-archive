## The Problem: 
- In the chain factory model, until the incubated chain/protocol gets live, the contributors of the incubated chain/protocol can’t get incentivised from the future treasury of that chain/protocol.
- Price momentum risk: Given the uncertainty of market conditions, the contributors also need to consider the timing of the market rather than just focusing on their deliverables.
- If all the supplementary grants* of the incubated chains are treated just like normal grants, over time altogether they could imply too much pressure on Edgeware’s treasury and sustainability of the ecosystem.


* [The grants focusing on incentivisation of the contributor(s) of the incubated chain/protocol apart from the primary grant are referred to in this thread as ‘Supplementary Grants’.]

## Solution: Introducing Convertible Note Grants [🥁](https://emojipedia.org/drum/) 
- Convertible Note Grants aims to provide the incubated chain/protocol flexibility without any compromise on the incentivisation of their current & future contributors.
- Utilizing Convertible Note Grant, the contributor(s) of the incubated chain/protocol can get incentivised in $EDG and after the full-fledged launch of the incubated chain/protocol and it’s/their treasury, the Edgeware treasury can be reimbursed^ in form of the token(s) of the incubated chain/protocol. This reimbursement is supposed to be done on the chain/protocol level and not directly by the contributors. 


^ [The reimbursement can be preferably done directly to the Edgeware’s on-chain treasury or to a 4/7 multisig with known Edgeware contributors as the signers.]

## Unintended benefits: 
- Multi-assets public treasury: Convertible Note Grants could play an important role in the diversification of the public treasury and thus reduction in the risk of Edgeware the community’s holdings. In future, community grants can also become multi assets’ grants subject to the choice of the proposer to diversify the grants’ risk.
- The incubated chain/protocol can take the credits of funding their own contributors through their treasury indirectly. (Matters a lot if the incubated chain/protocol aims to become a DAO in future.)


## Eligibility Criteria:
- Contributors of the Edgeware Treasury incubated chain/protocol can apply for the Convertible Note Grants for the multiple supplementary grants* as long as the USD-equivalent value of each proposed grant is less than 30% of the USD-equivalent value of the main funding grant.
- The Convertible Note Grants should not influence/change the parameters of the main funding grant.
- For contributors with no public presence in Edgeverse or across social media, they will need to go through a KYC verification process.


## Terms: 
- **Financial terms: **
- Amount: USD-equivalent value of single Convertible Note Grant should be less than 30% of USD-equivalent value of the main funding grant. 
- Maturity Period: 12 months or 1.5x the ETA of the full-fledged launch of the incubated chain/protocol as mentioned in the main funding proposal (Choosable by the proposer.) Reimbursement of Convertible Note Grants can be done before or at the conclusion of the maturity period.


- **Conversion terms: **
- For community-oriented chain/protocol that aims to do a 1:1 token sharedrop to EDG holders [i.e. if a person holds X% of the total supply of EDG, the same person will receive X% of the total supply of token of the incubated chain/protocol]: While the reimbursement of Convertible Note Grants in form of the token(s) of the incubated chain/protocol, the 30-days average of USD-equivalent value should be considered with no discounts.
- For chain/protocol with no 1:1 sharedrop to EDG holders but having no tokensales of any kind: While the reimbursement of Convertible Note Grants in form of the token(s) of the incubated chain/protocol, the 30-days average of USD-equivalent value should be considered with 7% discounts.
- For chain/protocol with conventional fundraising/tokensale structure: While the reimbursement of Convertible Note Grants in form of the token(s) of the incubated chain/protocol, the price of any of the two lowest-priced rounds should be considered.
- Maturity Period Extension Penalty: 14% annually (approx 2x of the inflation of EDG) 
- In case of the acquisition/merger of the incubated chain/protocol by an entity/DAO, the Edgeware community should be provided with the two choices of 2x refund in USD-equivalent value of the Convertible Note or multiplier on the tokenswap (if there is any).


- **Rights/Perks:**
- NFT(s) as a receipt of Convertible Note Grant(s) 
- Any chain/protocol specific perks {Optional}
- Any perks by the recipient contributor(s) {Optional}


- **Others: **
- The network service provider fee should not be considered in the reimbursement  


## Example:
Let’s demonstrate how Convertible Note Grants could work in the case of the proposal for ‘[Kabocha visuals and crowdloan NFTs](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/3440-proposal-kabocha-visuals-and-crowdloan-nfts)’:

The main funding proposal(s): (Total approx ~200k USD)
1. [Edgeware Road: parachains and bridges *core development*](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/1327-edgeware-road-parachains-and-bridges-core-development) (20k USD-equivalent EDG)
2. [Kabocha Technical Steward - Milestones, Treasury Proposals and Referenda](https://gov.edgewa.re/proposal/discussion/2515-kabocha-technical-steward-milestones-treasury-proposals-and-referenda/) (Milestone 1: ~ 50k USD, Milestone 2: TBC, approx total cost based on the price at the time of Milestone 1: ~180k USD)


30% of 200k USD = 60k USD is the max cap for a single supplementary grant eligible under the Convertible Note Grant structure. 

- Requested amount in the proposal for ‘Kabocha visuals and crowdloan NFTs’: 10k GBP = 13.4k USD (which is less than 60k USD and fulfils the first Eligibility Criteria.) ✅
- This proposal is also not influencing/changing any parameters from the main funding proposal(s) ✅
- The contributor Beej aka James Harford had a significant presence in the Edgeverse so far. (He was one of the guests for the New-Futures’ AMA). ✅

Thus this supplementary grant is eligible to become a Convertible Note Grant. {The on-chain governance process remains the same.}

Let’s assume that the contributor/proposer opt for the max maturity period. In this case, it is most likely to be 12 months. 
Also, Kabocha is a community-oriented chain that aims to do a 1:1 token sharedrop/paradrop to EDG holders. 
Thus, the Convertible Note Grant reimbursement from future Kabocha Treasury to Edgeware Treasury in the form of KAB can be proposed/executed any time within 12 months as per the 30-days average price in 13.4k USD-equivalent of KAB with no discounts.
i.e. If the 30-days average price of KAB is ‘A’ USD, the reimbursement amount = (13400/A) KAB
