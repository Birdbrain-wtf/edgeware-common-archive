**Background Information**
**Problem Statement**
Edgeware price is dropping community is depressed, we are lacking in meaningful fun marketing & community engagement. Edgeware needs leadership Moon is here to help.

**Proposed Solution**
Edgeware is going run a marketing campaign to buy eligible holders (with 100k $EDG or more) a free pizza.

**Work to date**
Concept open to community discussion and brainstorming.

**How does the solution work**
First we set a block number for the airdrop to open and close in most likely a Friday to Sunday morning to account for different time zones.

Community members wishing to participate then tweet at Edgeware with the tag 
"Thanks for Pizza Air Drop $EDG Edgeware!" 
including a photo of the pizza you are enjoying then DM your holding address and link to your tweet for your free pizza ($EDG tokens to be dropped).

Of course you will have to buy your pizza first and get refunded for it in $EDG tokens but essentially the chain is shouting you a free pizza or you have an excuse to buy yourself one and hodl some extra $EDG tokens.

Conditions
- One airdrop per community member.
- Account must have at least 100k $EDG in it to receive air drop.
- Must tweet "Thanks for Pizza Air Drop $EDG Edgeware!" with photo of pizza.
- 100 participants max on first come first serve basis to DM tweet and EDG address.


**How does this align with Edgeware values**
This is a community driven marketing program that will allow Edgeware to get exposure while giving something back to the community participating and paying attention to Edgeware.

**How have you meaningfully engaged with community so far:**
I have been involved with Edgeware for over 1 year now participated on council, I am also staking and vote in most proposals.
I have a long term outlook on Edgeware and not here for the short term.

**Deliverables**
Air drop each participating and eligible community member approx 2000 $EDG when $EDG is valued at $0.005 usd.

**Funding request**
200,000 EDG
This is based on a max cap of 100 active community members receiving the 2000 EDG drop each with the remaining funds at the closing block being returned to treasury.

**What does success look like?**
I would like to see this become a annual event.
