# **Kabocha crowdloan campaign parameters - discussion summary and next steps**

This is a roundup of the various community responses to the [Kabocha crowdloan campaign strategy discussion](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/2903-kabocha-crowdloan-campaign-strategy-discussion) and associated poll. 

The key question was related to what incentives we should create for EDG holders to also contribute KSM in the crowdloan in return for receiving their KAB. An example idea was for people to activate their EDG amount in KAB by locking a proportional amount of KSM into the crowdloan.

## Key points:

- The general consensus (and poll) suggests people are up for us incentivising EDG holders to also contribute KSM, but this should be an option, not something that is enforced - a carrot rather than stick approach.  


- As a result EDG holders will not be forced to contribute KSM to activate their KSM which feels in line with the overall openness and spirit of the project. 


- To encourage KSM contributions we are introducing **Kabocha Seeds**


- Kabocha Seeds are NFTs that will only ever be offered in this crowdloan and will be central the the governance, organisation, identity, reputation and value capture of Kabocha going forward. 


- There will be a few categories of Seeds available that are designed specifically to:


- Reward work to date from key community members
- Incentivise KSM crowdloan contributions 
- Encourage ongoing participation to the project


- A separate post will follow take a deeper dive into Kabocha Seeds and you can check out the [Kabocha Visuals and Crowdloan NFTs post here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/).

## Crowdloan terms 

Beyond Kabocha seeds, we are continuing to debate our Crowdloan cap as we take into consideration:

- The relatively [low number of KSM](https://parachains.info/auctions/kusama) required to secure a slot
- The existing market conditions
- The overall interest in Kabocha - we currently have over **2200 signups**
- Keeping the project focused on organic and sustainable growth 


## Crowdloan analysis 

The below analysis is fro[@Advait Rane](/edgeware/account/mW9wgCp5XVmg4and5gEdhBiLLR3wxZpTXgExUhfjBLwk1kt) and the Edgeware financials team, you can view the f[ull data and analysis here](you can view an analysis from the Edgeware financials team here.)

- From the data we can observe that, the KSM required for winning the auction was about 50% of the max crowdloan cap. The mathematical avg is ~55% for all the participants.


- For the last 3-4 rounds, participants have won the auction around 20k KSM. The latest crowdloan slots from Litmus and Crab, have won with 10k and 7k KSM respectively. 


- For Kabocha we can consider the bracket to be from 20k to 25k KSM, which means each **Crowdloan-contributor **will receive approx ~160 KAB per KSM contributed, which is approx 0.000245% of the total supply. Additionally, an **EDG holder** will get extra KAB proportional to his/her holdings from the 90.2% pool of funds, which drastically drives up the network share that the individual gets.


- The number of contributors have decreased over the auction rounds, except for a few projects which have generated extra interest. Mathematically, avg is around 7000, but realistically we can expect around 4000-4500 contributors for Kabocha, based on the latest rounds.


- The crowd loan reward pool allocated by previous winners on an average is **15%**, For Kabocha, its 4.9% of the total supply, which is lower as most of the supply is reserved for the EDG holders.


- With regards to the vesting period, most of the participants have decided to award** 30%** of the amount on launching and the rest **70%** would be linearly unlocked over the period of 48 weeks.

[@remz](/edgeware/account/kWgq9PZzqF6jYKSnZXd2tP8AiKEmm1NcQNWiwCXgfNFsWA1) introduced some thoughts around ways to encourage new EDG holders to [not dump post snapshot](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/2903-kabocha-crowdloan-campaign-strategy-discussion?comment=12550):

- 1. Start at beginning of locking period and monitor all balances above dust threshold. 
- 2. Look at every block (or once a day, depending on how heavy that script can be) and take the min of the balance m0 till the end of the locking period. This is the standard distribution method. To this we add the following: 
- 3. For each incoming transactions to contract X of the defined insignificant amount a0. 
- Record the balance b0 just before the transaction sending a0.
- Look whether the next transaction of the sender is of amount a1 and if so, lookup the EDG address and translate to a KSM one. 
- Lookup the KSM chain for crowdloan information and record the crowdloaned amount c0 in KSM committed to KAB.
- Use the oracle for KSM/EDG at the time of a0 (we can have some tolerance, since the timing of a0 is the the one of the amount exchange, and the oracle is not the effective exchange price everywhere), and adjust m0 by m0=min(b0,m0+c0*rate). 
- This is basically telling people that if they exchange EDG for something during the monitoring period, they will not get KAB for that amount (since we take the minimum), UNLESS they exchanged EDG for KSM and crowdloaned that amount for KAB, they would then get KAB on the full EDG amount, and get the additional KAB from the crowdloan reward. It still creates some selling pressure, but only from the ones willing/able to optimize their EDG/KSM holdings to maximize their KAB distribution.
