## **Project Summary **
This proposal seeks funding for the ongoing development and delivery of the Kabocha crowdloan interface and app (front and backend) located at[ Kabocha.network](https://kabocha.network), which will include a simplified user experience for the community to navigate and participate in the various functions of the network through the signing of transactions via the Polkadotjs plugin. 

- Crowdloan
- Calculator
- Contribute
- Seeds 
- EDG snapshot
- Redeem 
- Assets
- Proposals
- Governance
- Documentation 
- FAQ
- Settings


When Kabocha has launched, it is expected that Jan and Johan will continue to be funded via a new KAB proposal.
