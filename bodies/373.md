**Intro**
To better organize the community for the first year of Edgeware, (and to launch a conversation on the refinement of these programs,) I’m introducing the Edgeware Working Group Assembly. While EDGWGA sounds more like a new screen size, instead it’s an evolution of the ambassador program we initially launched almost a year ago. That program had no mainnet to coalesce around at the time, but introduced several Edgeware fanatics and decentralized governance fans to each other - we invite them back now that the mainnet is launched.

Overall, it is my position that the traditional ambassador program common in cryptoeconomics is outdated- specifically for chains with on-chain governance enabled. Validators, formal governance leaders like Council members, tokenholders and developers now come together naturally to fulfill complementary ecosystem roles, laying the groundwork for collaboration that represents the diverse interests of these players. 

Finally, whereas ambassador programs had a barrier to entry, usually an application - the Edgeware Working Groups will be totally open to the public, enabling casual participants as well as the more intensive. I hope this brings even more diverse perspectives and involvement.

I will continue to be extremely active in organizing these groups and the broader community initiatives - but these groups will advance decentralization and democratic ownership of Edgeware significantly, and I believe it important to begin this work sooner rather than later, to avoid entrenched political or economic interests and hasten the rate of progress. 


**The Ask**
I am looking for existing community leaders to step up and express interest in leading these groups - I expect that treasury proposals will inevitably arise to resource these programs- including the notion that group leads may be remunerated.  I am also looking for Edgeware participants of any kind to lend their expertise in these groups. 





## Proposed Working Groups: 


**Governance**
Develop standards for proposal descriptions and researches/advocates for advances in chain governance.
- Formats /Templates of Proposals 
- Research and track, report on other chains innovations in gov.
- Social and Economic EDGIPs
- Norm and ethics development for council. 



**Brand and Marketing**
Handling marketing, brand, comms channels, profiles/social.
- Outreach and partnership building
- Leads some treasury-based grants related to group responsibilities
- Monitors and crafts narrative
- Translations and localization
- Assists in onboarding, community
- Meetups, events



**Builders**
Developers, core, and application.
- Developer relations
- Bug Bountys
- Development Grants
- Develops security practices
- Leads major runtime upgrade proposals
- Technical EDGIPs



**Validation**
Advocates and discovers the needs and issues re: validation.
- Monitors centralization and considers solutions.
- Develops body of standards for validator behavior and technology.
- Develops campaign templates.




Those interested in stepping into these groups should post in the respective dedicated thread - should multiple leaders emerge for these groups, I see no reason to avoid co-chairs per se, but this can be up for determination by the groups themselves.
