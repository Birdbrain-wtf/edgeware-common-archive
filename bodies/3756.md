## **Proposal summary**

Collab.Land leverages the power of identity through crypto currency to create a social space unique to a specific network of humans. 

Once you add the bot to your Telegram group or Discord guild, they will manage your people for you. Depending on their token holdings (fungible/non-fungible) they will be allowed to join the community. If they sell, the bot boots them. 

It has become one of the most widely adopted Web3 tools, providing a vital service for communities to organise their operations. 

This proposal seeks funding from the community to bring Collab-Land to Substrate via native chain integrations in [Edgeware](https://edgewa.re) and [Kabocha](https://kabocha.network) via a 2 year vesting retainer. 

## Rationale

- Building on its broad and fair distribution, Edgeware has pushed the boundaries of community led on-chain governance in the Substrate ecosystem, pushing forward decentralised organisation and funding for a wide range of teams. 
- The core challenge for blockchains with treasuries and on-chain governance systems is no longer capital formation, but how to scale and credit the creative contributions of a decentralised network of humans. 


- Currently our community is organised generically and there is no way to utilise on-chain actions or holdings to add nuance to access permissions.


- Many projects are focused on developing on-chain identity and reputation systems, however we believe that rather than implementing old world (passport/ID cards/bank accounts) and Web2 identities into Web3 systems, we need to build native Web3 identities, that are verifiable through the assignment of NFTs representing creative contributions.


- Over time, accounts that are assigned NFTs relating to their contributions will build not just status, but access permissions that offer them utility in Web3 networks and beyond, offering a path to the development of true digital and peer to peer meritocracy that is not reliant on centralised intermediaries.


- You can read more about how a contributor journey might evolve on Edgeware/Kabocha [here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/705-edg-governance-treasury-community-waterfall).


## Implementation 

We are not focused on porting Collab-Land to Edgeware’s EVM using existing Solidity contracts, but on developing a Substrate specific implementation using the same NFT pallets and [RMRK](https://rmrk.app) technology that will form the foundations of [Kabocha Seeds](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/3440-proposal-kabocha-visuals-and-crowdloan-nfts) and [New-Futures](https://new-futures.co) projects. 

By utilising Substrate's on-chain governance pallets alongside RMRK we can offer advanced NFT functionality that will enable Collab-Land to offer more advanced and upgradable capabilities than with ETH based DAOs, integrating the tool into the runtime of Substrate projects and providing a key organisational component within our emerging chain factory model. 

This approach will enable the development of a more nuanced and productive environment for creative contributions, linked to an emerging system for Web3 reputation and identity baked into the core community. 

## **How does the solution work?**

You can view [this Discord walkthrough](https://collabland.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/70000036689-discord-bot-walkthrough) and this [Telegram walkthrough](https://collabland.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/70000081544-telegram-bot-walkthrough) to get an idea of how the Collab-Land bot and commands work. 
There will soon be other Collab-Land integrations with other well known Web2 platforms. 

In these examples the bot interfaces with a Metamask, with Edgeware/Kabocha it will be integrated with the Polkadotjs signer. 

- The Collab-Land bot connects with the signer/wallet (Polkadotjs) 
- Using an RPC provider the Collab-Land bot scans the blockchain checking the status of addresses. 
- In Edgeware/Kabocha's case we are aiming to also monitor more complex actions such as Extrinsics, Voting and other Substrate native functions. 
- The bot checks a users wallet and the permissions associated with their tokens - NFTs/ fungible and performs actions in Discord/Telegram or other relevant platforms / locations.
