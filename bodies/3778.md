**Creator**

Edgeware's values resonates with me and I feel connected to what's being built, tapping into the energy and philosophy of DAO and governance.  I've been starting to have conversations with certain members, and plan to integrate myself more into the working part of the community. 

I have skills in design and visual communication and I am motivated to add value to the community. 

How we learn and build better systems is motivating force that I am curious and driven towards. The community has built a different branch to the idea of a crypto currency and the potential to have value as a community. 

The type of design work that inspires me is characteristically simple and elegant, containing clean lines, patterns and geometric shapes. My aesthetic typically communicates fine and meticulous details. I am proficient in the Adobe suite, as well as being a photographer and videographer.

**Summary**
Edgeware is a revolutionary mechanism. Being a multi-chain proposal system that builds out rather than on, like many other chains. 

We are missing a solidified visual narrative. To existing members and anyone familiar with anything cryptographic, this understanding is already granted. The unfamiliar and often unknowing wider public, fall short of interacting with this ‘complex’ narrative. 

Reduce the complexities and intricacies down to clearly communicated core principles. Invite and entice new members to Edgeware. A platform and community that inspires change needs to fly this banner proudly and simply. 

Brands and beliefs to be showcased. What better way to communicate this through a visual language that everyone will come to know. Edgewares optics is underpinned by 3D block forms. I propose to expand this in a number of fashions.

•	Expand Edgewares visual style

•	Create a visual style guide for Edgeware (Staying true to what already exists)
	- Propose dos and dont’s. (Establish and agree on)

• 	Create a visual alphabet of block like forms that can be accessed by anyone who needs to create assets or contribute towards any future marketing.

• 	Visual templates that can be accessed at any time.

• 	Visual glossary - establish key terminology. For example:
	- Multi chain
	- Crowdloan
	- Token
• 	A visual explainer page (A new tab feature for the home page) for anyone who would like to get under the hood and stimulated through images and not just words. 

• 	Future idea: Creation of Edgeware NFTs that follow and continue its block like style. This could also be used to support the EdgePass concept.
