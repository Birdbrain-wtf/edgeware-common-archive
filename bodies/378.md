After our first month of Edgeware mainnet live, we're excited to share our highlights and development updates- quite a bit due to all the excitement.

**Stats**
- Over 5000 active Edgeware accounts.
- Averaging 92 validators with a current limit of 60 active (and going up soon!)
- Over [1[3 public campaigns](https://edgewa.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=04521a838ac5cb3bb4012c002&id=a8bcdf36e0&e=b5cf810348)](https://edgewa.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=04521a838ac5cb3bb4012c002&id=eeeba21b53&e=b5cf810348) from Validators courting Nominators.
- 18.33% of all EDG currently staked and rising daily, a total of 925.147M EDG.
- 341 Nominators and growing.


**Technical and Validation**
- Commonwealth Lab's engineer Drew Stone has a comprehensive technical update at Commonwealth.im, including [the plans for the first runtime upgrade and other node-running and staking information.](https://edgewa.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=04521a838ac5cb3bb4012c002&id=467a747615&e=b5cf810348)


**Community**
- We announced [a new working group initiative](https://edgewa.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=04521a838ac5cb3bb4012c002&id=6101496cd6&e=b5cf810348) open to anyone ready to be experts and leaders in Edgeware.
- We also launched [the Community Validator Program over here at Commonwealth](https://edgewa.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=04521a838ac5cb3bb4012c002&id=8ce801838a&e=b5cf810348). We'll nominate and support validators who are active in the community- a good way to boost your nominations.
- [Community calls continue monthly!](https://edgewa.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=04521a838ac5cb3bb4012c002&id=b195f7f90e&e=b5cf810348) We're also exploring a second validator specific monthly call. Let us know if that's of interest.


**Governance**
- In the first-ever successful governance action, Commonwealth Lab's [Thom Ivy](https://edgewa.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=04521a838ac5cb3bb4012c002&id=3d9f521494&e=b5cf810348) has been assigned Registrar status. In 7 days when that vote becomes effective, you can petition for the Edgeware 'blue checkmark.' 
- A proposal to increase the validator count from 60 to 85 failed to pass the last referendum round and is [back on the referendum table](https://edgewa.us19.list-manage.com/track/click?u=04521a838ac5cb3bb4012c002&id=7c98d4cf49&e=b5cf810348), but it needs a supermajority vote to succeed.
