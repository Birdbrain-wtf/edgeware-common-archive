# Kabocha: Edgeware grants and recoup

This is a follow up to [@Shankar Warang](/edgeware/account/jA7pLH65zgRNA4UgwqHEWWRxTgg51wuAUgfzmZoYWM2dMVS)'s '[Convertible Note Grants](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/3670-convertible-note-grants-a-new-funding-type-idea-for-the-chainprotocol-factory-model)' suggestion in which he outlined the following challenge:

> In the chain factory model, until the incubated chain/protocol gets live, the contributors of the incubated chain/protocol can’t get incentivised from the future treasury of that chain/protocol.
> Price momentum risk: Given the uncertainty of market conditions, the contributors also need to consider the timing of the market rather than just focusing on their deliverables.
> If all the supplementary grants* of the incubated chains are treated just like normal grants, over time altogether they could imply too much pressure on Edgeware’s treasury and sustainability of the ecosystem.

And the solution presented:

> **Convertible Note Grants** aims to provide the incubated chain/protocol flexibility without any compromise on the incentivisation of their current & future contributors.
> Utilizing Convertible Note Grant, the contributor(s) of the incubated chain/protocol can get incentivised in $EDG and after the full-fledged launch of the incubated chain/protocol and it’s/their treasury, the Edgeware treasury can be reimbursed^ in form of the token(s) of the incubated chain/protocol. 
> This reimbursement is supposed to be done on the chain/protocol level and not directly by the contributors. 

In principle the concept is sound and does address a key issue, however the example that followed felt overly complex and not in keeping with our keep it simple approach to Kabocha. 

## Back to basics

The undiluted snapshot will copy the Edgeware treasury to the Kabocha treasury, which will then hold around 4.9% of the total supply - which will will be around **66.8 million** after the 1:100 redenomination.

The Kabocha treasury will therefore hold around 3,273,200 KAB (or 327m EDG equivalent).

3% of this reverse-vests to Decentration as the project's technical steward, whilst the remaining 1.9% or **1,269,200** KAB remains available for collators / staking and other basic costs. 

Going forward all of Kabocha's proposals will mint new KAB, rather than relying on the treasury.

## A simple 1:1 recoup

To keep things as simple as possible the best approach is to repay the EDG grants 1:1 with KAB. 

So for example the [Kabocha visuals and crowdloan NFTs](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/3440-proposal-kabocha-visuals-and-crowdloan-nfts) grant was for:

**2,894,736 EDG**

Once Kabocha is up and running, the Edgeware community can put a proposal to the Kabocha treasury to recoup the initial grant, but this time denominated in KAB.

2,894,736 EDG =** 28,947.36 KAB **

## A good faith agreement

The Edgeware and Kabocha communities will have the same major stakeholders and community members, so we can assume this as an agreement to be executed in good faith and can cover all of the Edgeware treasury funded Kabocha grants.   

This approach keeps things simple, easy to understand and steers away from speculating on the ultimate value of Kabocha which is impossible to know and should therefore be kept at arm's length from the simple focus of enabling Edgeware's treasury to recoup its initial costs.

This approach also ensures we continue the 1:1 relationship between EDG and KAB within our own emerging public economies, rather than relying on external market pricing.
