**PURPOSE:**
The purpose of this proposal is to found the financial working group consisting of Advait, Vismay and Ash to request $11,000 in EDG (market equivalent) for investment purposes on behalf of Edgeware’s community Treasury into other blockchain assets (tokens). The funds are to be used to create a diversified portfolio of blockchain assets (tokens) that appreciate in value (monetary, utility) overtime that Edgeware can use for capital heavy opex (operating expense) or funding future growth.

- Requesting: $11,000 in EDG equivalent (as per 7 day average)

- Timeline: 2-12-22 to 2-12-23


	                                                      **–TLDR–**

**PROBLEM:**

We believe the problem is twofold:

1.   The Edgeware token(s) monetary value is volatile.
2.   The Edgeware token(s) monetary value is diminishing.

The reasons for these problems are due to an exceedingly competitive marketplace of blockchain projects, a lack of awareness to retail investors and an unknown/confusing use-case. It is to be noted that there are other factors beyond the project such as industry trends in project types, capital and regulation that have also added to the volatility and dampened price.

**SOLUTION:**
Our solution is to seek an allocation of tokens from Edgeware’s Treasury and invest it into a diverse set of blockchain assets; this will result in limiting our exposure to a single volatile asset/ecosystem. This approach also allows the Treasury to have a basket of assets that are diverse and appreciate in value across a period of time w/ sound investment strategies.
The investment approach also allows for the Treasury at a future point to utilize the yield generated from the assets through staking / lending and other profit-making activities to help fund capital intensive operational expenses and new ecosystem growth.

**STRATEGY:**
The fund has a value investing strategy at its core, backed by strong quantitative analysis and supplemental data research that allows us to create a balance between risk on & off assets. Basically, our goal is to start with creating investments around core layered assets across ecosystems that generate both active & passive yield. Our preliminary weighting of the fund would be towards: 
1. 35% - High risk model
2. 35% - Low risk model 
3. 20% - Defi specific - yield 
4. 10% - Day trading 


**NOTE:** Preliminary details of the assets in these baskets are shown in the model links shared above.
It is to be noted however that as the overall market changes the fund strategy/ allocation thus allowing us to better adapt and ensure profit while managing risk

**FEES**
Our fees are: 10%-20%
- 10%- fees for management 

We will charge a 10% flat fee from the amount invested (for this Fund, $11,000), where we will immediately take $1000.00 and split it amongst the Fund Managers (Advait, Vismay, Ash). For simplicity’s sake we are forgoing the $100.00 (10% of $11,000 = $1,100.00) and only accepting $1000.00 as an upfront management fee. 
- 20% - profit allocation to fund working group members

Any profits that are made atop of the $10,000 we will take a 20% of as payment for the Fund Managers and be split accordingly. This framework incentives us as managers of the fund to create additional yield and profits such that we are profiting on a personal level as the fund profits. The fees along with profits will be remitted in the asset choice of the Fund Managers, to allow for flexibility in maintaining value in both monetary and utilitarian terms. 
The allocation of fees & profits between Fund Managers are as such: 
- Advait: 35%
- Vismay: 35%
- Ash: 29%
- Shankar: 1%


**Exit Strategies - Fund Managers **
Fund managers have the option to take out their share of the profits from the fund (20% - allocation above) at End of the Year if the fund has generated a positive return through either asset appreciation or yield generation. These funds as indicated above can be paid out in tokens of the fund manager’s choosing and they are in no obligation to keep their profits within the fund. 

**Exit Strategies - Treasury**
If the Fund generates profits by the end of the year, a poll can be raised on-chain such that the community can decide whether the profits can be transferred into a stable coin or back to the EDG token and be used to fund operational expenses and other ecosystem development growth. The percentage of divesting / liquidation from the fund profits can be determined by the poll; the Fund managers will furnish the community with a recommended divesting amount; however, this is purely to be considered as a recommendation.
