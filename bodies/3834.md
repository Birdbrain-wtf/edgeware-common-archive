## Statement

The Edgeware Agency was formed from a set of community volunteers interested in earning EDG to tackle high-priority network issues. We are now at the end of the funds provided by the first proposal, posted 10 months ago in May. In order to continue operations, we require more funds to be allocated asap. 

The agency is at an awkward point: We are developing new patterns and tools for permissionless contribution, testing bounties, and our volunteers have grown in their expertise so as to become councilmembers of EDG, or are otherwise self-sufficient. If Edgeware is an ashram, the Agency has been the daily practice hall, and I’ve been honored to support and test the growth and character of our most core contributors. 

However, one of our goals was to decentralize ourselves using tools and that individual growth. I have asked that the top performers proceed with drafts of their own funding proposals, reflecting my belief that they no longer need my intermediary oversight and can benefit from directly interfacing with the community.

I believe that we should proceed with more intense focus on marketing, staking participation, and governance participation, however, with decentralization comes a loss of execution speed. That said, we basically estimated our progress successfully according to Proposal 1’s deliverables (at top.) For the Agency to grow in an operational capacity, it would need much higher funding- amounts required for a traditional working experience. 

So, this is the awkward stage: I think we are doing great and as-estimated at current funding patterns, and this can essentially be decentralized. To do more or do faster, however, would require a decently capitalized organization that can provide a stable working environment and a traditional hierarchy. If the community is interested in this, I am happy to explore, but would require less funding and more of a network share that would - like a core team - justify investors backing the org. 

**To keep our operations going while staff designs their own proposals over 4 months, I am proposing a funding request for:**

**25m EDG**
 
Breakdown:
Currently, the Agency averages around 30k USD in spending per our finances. (See link above.)

At today’s admittedly low price, this works out to ~6,157,635 EDG per month for 4 months. The above amount would give us confidence that we can execute the next 4 months and either continue with a modified plan, or that agency members can see to their own designs.

The work would focus on _continuing_ to deliver our daily progress, community operational capacity, marketing, reporting, training, and strategy, as well as unfulfilled items that remain prioritized in the deliverables link (see the google doc.)
