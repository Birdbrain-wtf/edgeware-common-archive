## Edgeware community priorities

From a [recent discussion](https://discord.com/channels/537281916567814144/747673085108551701/944709224687091712) in Discord:

![](https://commonwealth-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/41c5eae0-06d8-4f05-a9ee-e99c29e362c8.1645354743124)

Given the open and ongoing debate on [the new agency proposal ](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/3834-agency-funding-feb-22)and future proposals put forward by team members it might be worth getting a wider perspective from across the community on basic issues that are making simple things harder.

The agency was established to focus on the basic operations of the community and are now moving to advance the UX/UI of voting and delegation tools. 

From the agency proposal:

> I believe that we should proceed with more intense focus on marketing, staking participation, and governance participation...

However it still seems that some basic things are still falling through the cracks. This might be because:

- They are not seen as a priority
- They are deemed good enough
- They are not in the remit of the agency
- The agency are not aware of the issues


By bringing issues to the surface we can discuss and refine them then ensure they are folded into either:

- Agency responsibilities
- Follow on proposals either by agency staff 
- Created as bounties
- Addressed by Decent Partners 


## A few starters... 

- **Edgeware website subdomains**


- We have [https://gov.edgewa.re](https://gov.edgewa.re) but then we have
- [https://edgeware.app](https://edgeware.app) (why not [https://app.edgewa.re)](https://app.edgewa.re))
- [https://docs.edgeware.wiki/](https://docs.edgeware.wiki/) (why not [https://docs.edgewa.re)](https://docs.edgewa.re))
- We now have [https://edg.dotreasury.com](https://edg.dotreasury.com) as an interface for our treasury / proposals / spending, this can have [https://treasury.edgewa.re](https://treasury.edgewa.re) 


- **Documentation**


- Honestly, it might be worth starting from an entirely new base and building up from there, rather than trying to adapt the existing docs. 
- This would mean we'd be working from first principles and could make them simpler, easier to follow and focused on what is possible now, rather than what  will be possible soon... currently the docs feel more like marketing, than basic documentation. 


- **Governance**
- Commonwealth is ok, but it could be improved
- We have two choices: 
- Make Commonwealth better given our experience and needs and have a working group focused on making iterative improvements in conversation with the community.
- Shift to [Subsquare](https://subsquare.io) which is a Substrate focused governance interface created by the Open Square team who delivered [EDG Treasury](https://edg.dotreasury.com) 
- Build something from scratch...


Please share thoughts / issues... what basic things are you stuggling with that we might not be aware of, where could small improvements make big differences?
