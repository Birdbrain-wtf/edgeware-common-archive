Hi everyone,

I am opening this thread in order to be as transparent as possible about the approach we at Staking Facilities want to pursue with regards to the the slashing event from February 25th, which was due to a bug-induced downtime.

We wrote a post mortem about what specifically has happened which you can find here:
[https://medium.com/@stakingfac/postmortem-2020-02-25-edgeware-validator-outage-645a728dbd2f](https://medium.com/@stakingfac/postmortem-2020-02-25-edgeware-validator-outage-645a728dbd2f)

## **A. Staking Facilities Validators:**
After we went back online, we quickly regained 10% of the validator slots, but decided to shut down 5 of our 10 validators because the bug has not been fixed yet. Once we regain confidence that Edgeware can be run reliably, we will reevaluate our decision.

## **B. Reimbursement of slashed funds:**
For the reimbursement of our nominators funds, we chose the following process:
1.) We will publish a list with the affected accounts and the associated number of slashed token.
2.) Two weeks after publishing the list, we will send out the reimbursements. This will give everyone the chance to review the list in order to avoid mistakes in the distribution.
3.) Once all tokens are refunded, we will inject a treasury spend proposal to refund our upfront reimbursement
We chose this process so that the outcome of the proposal and the reimbursement of our nominators funds can be separated. Should we get funds allocated by the treasury before reimbursing our nominators, people would need to trust Staking Facilities to redistribute the funds, because Substrate only allows to submit one address as a beneficiary. Reimbursing our nominators in advance allows us to avoid any trust related issues.

## C. **Application for the council:**
From the very beginning of the Edgeware network, we had the plan to support the network and community by applying for a place in the Edgeware council. After the slashing incident, we started questioning this decision because of a potentially biased position regarding the planned proposal. Here is why I, as a representative of Staking Facilities, will still run for a seat in the council:

- As mentioned above, it was our plan from the beginning to contribute to the community as a representative in the council and that hasn't changed.
- Most importantly: we think it is critical to get more people with a public ID in the council. In times of the Steem take over, transparency in governance is key for the healthy and secure development of the network. Right now, we mostly see anonymous applications for the council and that needs to change
- We talked to members of the community and the Edgware team about our situation, and the feedback was that we should still run for a position in the council.


I want to point out again that we take this matter very seriously. Nevertheless, slashing caused by a bug in the software was never an intended behavior and should never be one!

We will continue our support and engagement for the Edgeware network and community. We believe in this project and are here for the long run! Updates will be posted in this thread.
