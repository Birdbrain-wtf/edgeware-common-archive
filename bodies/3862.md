## Problem

1. In order to complete our listing process for Exchange B, we need a combination of BTC, USDT and EDG to address marketing campaigns and market-maker deposits. 
2. The promotional campaign costs are part of the agreement with Exchange B, and the MM deposits are the means to conform to Exchange B's requirements on liquidity performance. 
3. You can review the docs around these blockers here: [https://edgeware-a.slab.com/posts/exchange-b-blockers-7dyh2dok](https://edgeware-a.slab.com/posts/exchange-b-blockers-7dyh2dok)
4. Additionally, our market-maker has informed me that our reserves of the BTC side of existing market-making is lower than optimal per their trading team- due to a combination of market price of BTC dipping and the trading activity balancing us to be heavier in EDG. They are requesting we deposit 0.6 BTC as soon as possible in order to quote properly. 
5.  Consequently, I would like to include 0.6 BTC worth of EDG in this proposal. Normally I would separate this out into a separate proposal, but_ since the same liquidation plan would be run for the whole set of funds,_ I'm not sure it makes sense to split. 
6. If the community is adamant that this should be a separate proposal I'm happy to organize.



# Proposals and Cost Breakdown
We propose to fund and partially liquidate EDG from the treasury to support:


**(1) Market Making Deposits for Exchange B**
To be clear: The amounts in this section are not going to be liquidated, they are a market-making deposit, and the chain will own the profit and loss, as it does current MM deposits.

- **$37.5k** in additional EDG for EDG-sides of pairs.
- Ultimately we need to provide 100k$ in EDG that will be split into two piles, 50k for the EDG side of the BTC pair, and 50k for USDT, both on the unlisted Exchange B.  
- **Note, however:** We have funds remaining from the original Exchange B Proposal, you can[ observe these in a multisignature wallet](https://edgeware.subscan.io/account/nXwJYrYhWMj99FrrGzKxmjpjjnkzpKSqfTDp75Fxz1JkgkP), totaling 16,909,095 EDG ($~75k) the intention is that these amounts will be used to provide the EDG-denominated MM Deposits, leaving a remainder of $37.5k (25k*1.5) EDG that will be in this proposal. 
- **~$150k EDG.** Enough EDG to purchase $50k in both BTC and USDT. We will calculate the** 7-day TWAP * 1.5 (Volatility buffer) on the proposal launch, as we have with previous exchanges.**


**(2) Promotional Campaign Cost for Exchange B**
- Our total promotional obligation is $100k in EDG including an advance deposit of 5m EDG. This will be transferred to Exchange B for traditional listing campaign use. I believe we can use a simple 7-day TWAP here. 


**(3) Integration Costs for Exchange B**
- **None. **We only have to fulfill promotions (we don't get these back,) and our market-making deposits. (We control these.)



**(4) BTC Top-up for Kucoin MM **
- **$35k EDG for the purchase of 0.6 BTC.** (Roughly $23.4k 1.5x =35k )The price of BTC has decreased since we funded these accounts,  Efficient Frontiers has requested 0.6 BTC additionally to the account on Kucoin in order to quote properly.  These funds continue to be owned by the chain.
