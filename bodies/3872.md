**Highlights**
- Edgeware’s solochain mainnet became 2 years old on 17th Feb 2022. The self-sustainability experiment turned stability-oriented public ecosystem has entered into the third year with several teams and solo individuals as contributors!
- The new [runtime developer, Filip Kalebo has been onboarded](https://twitter.com/EdgewareDAO/status/1494295708303859720?s=20&t=jynyPY9STGrUjOIqu5aIOA) by the community.
- [The crowdloan waitlist](https://kabocha.network/) is open for Kabocha.
- All validators are requested to upgrade their nodes to the [release v3.3.3](https://github.com/hicommonwealth/edgeware-node/releases) to proceed further with the ERUP5/Wako upgrade through the on-chain governance. The core community dev Ramsey recently shared [the docker image for v3.3.3 aka ERUP5/Wako upgrade](https://twitter.com/HeyEdgeware/status/1438226365833175048?s=20) to save the compiling hustle for node/validator owners.
