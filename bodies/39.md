### 1. What is your experience as a validator or node operator?

I'm an individual running validators for Polkadot (Alexander testnet), Kava, Regen Network, Althea, and Harmony.

### 2. What's your technical setup? (cloud vs. on-premise; software stack; key management)

For my vaidators I use VPS servers (8GB/2CPU) at various cloud providers (Digital Ocean, Vultr, Linode). Nodes are configured with Ubuntu 18.04.

### 3. Why are you interested in validating?

I believe POS represents a better solution for network growth. It ensures diversity and also provides a lower entry barrier so virtually anyone can participate. Edgeware seem to offer a new blockchain governance model, and it would be particularly interesting to see how experiments with DAOs and DeFi will evolve, even though I believe your distribution model was far from fair.
