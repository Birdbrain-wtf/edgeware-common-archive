Following up on my proposal in the March Community Call, I've authored this draft of a developer relationship lead- looking for feedback on all of this before we plan a treasury proposal.

---

An ideal candidate has a background in development paired with proven experience in effectively reaching developers and people management experience. Further, the candidate will be expected to serve as an ambassador for the platform.

**Evangelization**
Bring new developers to create solutions on Edgeware via direct engagement of the developer community through online and offline meetups, hackathons, lectures, training and other channels of education.

**Activation**
Facilitate a great experience with Edgeware for developers and take them from awareness to being effective. Achieve this at scale with broad reach channels such as chat and discussion platforms, video conferences, and technical support platforms.

**Community development**
Foster a healthy community globally through user group support.

**Content**
Create content to engage the developer audience through social media, videos, blog posts and documentation.

**Open-source**
Developer relations are the champions of open source. The team is responsible for gathering requirements that are essential for adoption. This includes encouraging and reviewing open-source contributions.

**Samples and demos**
Help showcase Edgeware's capabilities with samples and demos that the community at large can leverage.


**Qualifications:**

- Demonstrated leadership skills to help build trust and authority with the developer community.
- Prior experience in the management of budgets and work programs of a similar nature.
- Development skills. A Computer Science degree, or equivalent experience, with a track record of development in Blockchain and related languages.
- Ability to travel.
