**Intro**
We are Rusty Crewmates, a company that aims at creating a strong community of Rust and blockchain developers. We love the language and we love the decentralized world that we see emerging. Our objective is to make it as easy as possible for web2 developers to migrate to web3. To do so we hire them, teach them Substrate and find some interesting project for them to start on. We want them to become active members of the ecosystem as fast as possible and with the best support they can find. In this mind we are constantly looking for blockchain features to develop and train our newcomers on.

We’ve been talking to Ramsey regarding structuring a plan that is suitable for Edgeware and Kabocha’s processes. 

**Summary**
What we can offer to the Edgeware and Kabocha community is a mix of junior and experienced developers to carry on the development of their project. We would like to build a small team to work on some features and proposals.

**Milestones**
We ordered this set of features by increasing difficulty. Developing them in this order would allow for our team to gradually build a better understanding of your codebase and a mutual trust with the Edgeware community.
The two first ones are self-contained features that require no more milestones. The next ones will require more exchange with the community in order to co-construct and validate relevant milestones.

**Funding**
For the easier ones would like to work on a “bounty” model, being paid when delivering the actual feature. 

Dynamic bounties through picking up trello tickets
1. keep view of trello,
2. pick up tickets that are unassigned.
3. Get an agreement from a steward on the ticket. Agree a fixed price, or provide how many hours it took to complete.
4. Deliver task
5. Get it validated
6. Get funded through tipping pallet.

For the more complex ones we may require a grant, with progressive installment as the milestones are met.
