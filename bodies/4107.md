## **Summary**
This is a proposal that continues the initial work on Kabocha’s runtime and overall support with a new focus on on Kabocha “Kitchen Node” / with [Substrate Recipes](https://substrate.recipes/) docs that I have taken the responsibility over from Parity’s Josh Orndorff. 

## **Introduction**
From the Substrate Recipes documentation: 
Substrate Recipes is a cookbook of working examples that demonstrate best practices when building blockchains with Substrate. Each recipe contains complete working code as well as a detailed writeup explaining the code.
Substrate recipes aka the “Kitchen Node” is one of the most valuable learning resources in the developers journey, here is [a link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KVJIWxZSNHQ&ab_channel=Polkadot) of Josh Orndorff presenting which gives a good overview. 

This helped me on my Substrate and Rust journey, so I chose to dedicate a bunch of time to upgrading from Substrate V1 to Substrate V2 which took 5-6 months working in my spare time. 

Old Substrate was structured around declarative macros (decl storage! (), decl events!, etc), but in 2021 changed to attribute function macros (#[pallet::storage], #[pallet::event], etc.) 

I decided to contribute because it is a win-win situation. I can learn by contributing and I give something to the community. 

Pull request link: [https://github.com/JoshOrndorff/recipes/pull/471](https://github.com/JoshOrndorff/recipes/pull/471)

Surprisingly, Substrate Developer Hub at Parity was no longer going to maintain the Substrate Recipe and Kitchen Node, even though it's arguably one of the most useful resources to new Substrate builders so they gave me the opportunity to become the product owner and maintain the repo. 

We have a great opportunity to inherit the most valuable learning resource in Substrate and to maintain and update it with maintenance delivered by Kabocha. 

Link to repo: [https://kabocha-network.github.io/recipes/](https://kabocha-network.github.io/recipes/)
