Hello Edgeware community,

Just a heads up to everyone that this week, we'll be running the first upgrade on the Berlin Testnet. We plan to run this for 1-2 weeks before deciding to put a vote through on the mainnet or proceed with more debugging and testing.

The upgrade pegs against a fork of Substrate since it deals with migrations that have been removed from the upstream Substrate master branch. It is pegged to the PR #5197 that introduces an upgrade hook to activate the necessary migrations.

For a list of things fixed in this PR, refer to: [https://github.com/hicommonwealth/edgeware-node/pull/158](https://github.com/hicommonwealth/edgeware-node/pull/158)
