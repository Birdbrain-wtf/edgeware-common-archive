Hey all, posting on behalf of Commonwealth Labs. Here’s the write up to the specified councillor campaign questions.

__Please provide an overall summary of who you are and why you're interested in holding a councillor seat.__

Outside of helping launch Edgeware, we want to play a role in growing the community beyond the original crypto userbase. It seems that a fair portion of EDG holders still believe in the original vision that was posed last year—governance experimentation. As we’ve gone through the process of engaging with the community, we’ve gotten to know many folks on an individual level. We feel that we are in a good position to channel both the ire and goodwill into fulfilling the vision. Of course, we can't take this for granted. We hope the community continues to entrust us with helping steward Edgeware!

__How do you propose the treasury budget should be spent?__

1. Tooling to make Edgeware easy to build on :

- Developer tools for WASM. It would be awesome if Edgeware was the first network where it's easy to build and deploy simple WASM smart contracts, through Parity Ink's CLI and/or an online IDE. Some proprietary chains are developing online IDEs on their own, but it would be better for the community and for Edgeware if there were one very high-quality IDE that connected to multiple chains.
- The contracts module. For example, optimizing contracts for fast execution, or development of an off-chain oracles module.
- User interfaces to work make the governance process streamlined.
- Clear and concise documentation is a must.

2. Seeding high quality/impact teams with the Edgeware Treasury - initially focused on DAOs. A few ideas:

- The treasury can be used as a "collateral of last resort" to Maker-like Stablecoin
- DAOs that run validators.
- Political or other grant-giving DAOs
- A DAICO to fund crypto research focused on ZKPs.
- We discuss more in the answer to the next question.

3. Growing the community beyond crypto:

- Future locks. This has been discussed previously on [Commonwealth](https://commonwealth.im/#!/edgeware-testnet/proposal/discussion/7)
- Bridge to Ethereum. Onboard DeFi users who want to play with DAOs.
- Build non-crypto native developer ecosystem and user base. The experience has to be at a level where it blends into the normal web experience.
- Hosting a worldwide Edgeware Conference, run by the ambassador community. [RadicalxChange](https://radicalxchange.org/) did a really great job of attracting a fertile set of experimenters. If done right, we could have a similar atmosphere of economic and political cross-pollination.

__What should Edgeware do to grow its community?__

We think the community can be grown by funding innovators, whether they are working on blockchain infrastructure or outside of the industry. Individuals who are pushing their subfield forward (and may already be underfunded), usually have passionate collaborators. By seeding these individuals, we think Edgeware stands to attract equally ambitious follow-on experimenters. Possible non-crypto areas of interest might be:

- Ambitious proof-of-concepts and leaps forward in technology, e.g. zero-knowledge proofs or an anonymous signaling system based on ring signatures.
- Focus on DAOs and governance experimentation as a core use case. This is in line with the original thesis behind Edgeware.
- Funding scientific research. Alexey Guzey has written about the [state of biology research](https://twitter.com/alexeyguzey/status/1162017187919478784). In this review, he gave concrete examples of changes, which could be seeded by us! If these programs prove successful, they can ideally become self-sustaining, community-run DAOs.

__Which ecosystems beyond Polkadot should Edgeware bridge to? (e.g. Ethereum, Bitcoin, etc.)__

We're most interested in bridging to Ethereum and allowing users to build on top of existing DeFi infrastructure. This also allows us to benefit from existing ETH-BTC bridges. We should also be open to other networks interested in bridging to Edgeware. Web3 Foundation has started a set of RFPs for bridges to Ethereum, Bitcoin, and ZCash.

On the more fun side, it would be interesting to bridge to our sister chain Straightedge, provided they play nicely with us 🙂

__What is your position on Edgeware's governance system? How do you think Edgeware governance should evolve?__

It is extremely early to say. We think after even just one month, more can and will be able to be said. However, it seems very important to quickly decentralize ecosystem funding so that a variety of ideas can arise. We can point to Ethereum—EF, ECF, Moloch, Metacartel--as an example which is starting to decentralize funding of initiatives. We could do this by having the treasury subdivide so that different decisionmakers are responsible for "subtreasuries”.
