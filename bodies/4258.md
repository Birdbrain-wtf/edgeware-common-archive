## ARWEAVE

- Archival Service
- AR Drive Launched 
- Bundler Payments 





_Main Benefit: _**Blockchain** **Integrations **

Solana stores their blockchain data using Arweave. The Graph using AR to allow developers to analyze and incorporate all data stored on the AR Chain. Candidate for archiving ETH upcoming (EIP-4444) data burn.

_Main Concern:_  **Data Integrity** and **Performance**
 
Their IWF support and stated future compliancy with requests for data removal may impact your availability or retrieval of archived data.
Multiple repots from users regarding slowness. Actual upload and retrieval speeds still unknown.

## **STORJ**

- Cost Basics 
- Performance & Availability
- Data Integrity & Immutability


_Main Benefit:_ **Cost **
![](//:0)




_Main Concern:_  **Decentralization**

Satellites, is the term used to describe their centralized storage nodes. Satellites authorize all accesses to pieces, and can therefore meter and limit access to popular files. 

The following quote highlights the roadblocks for decentralization 
“Any user can run their own Satellite, but we expect many users to elect to avoid the operational complexity..”

I have yet to locate any resources intended in lowering this complexity.
