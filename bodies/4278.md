**Council Candidate and Delegate Thom Ivy**

Hi all! Longtime EDG community manager turned protocol steward, around since before the lockdrop. I've been the main architect of our growth and community strategies for several years, and over the past year have directed the Agency, a core group of community members paid to help develop and maintain the DAO and chain, including managing legal agreements offline. 

I'm the most experienced proposal writer alongside MrBulb, negotiate and manage our exchanges, and write often on EDG strategy. My vision for EDG is a vibe-heavy sovereign DAO that makes fantastic investments into new tech and culture, incentivizes bounty hunters and grant fulfillment, ensures validators are rewarded for securing the chain, and operates like a co-operative. 

This means bringing value for it's members in several ways. I've written previously about EDG as an 'auxiliary network state,' as a source of 'EDGPass' Citizens benefits like education, entertainment and health tools, and a funder of cutting-edge ideas that seeks to onboard some of cryptos most interesting thinkers.
