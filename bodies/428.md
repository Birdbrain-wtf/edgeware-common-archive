**1) Date of the proposal? **

**2) Who is proposing?**
     Staking Facilities GmbH

     a) What is their motivation?

	     validators and their customers. The proposal is intended to reimburse Staking 
	     Facilities for the advanced repayment of slashed funds.
 
     b) Do they have any conflicts of interest?
         We have a potentially biased position on the Proposal since I am a council member:
	     I discussed this with the Edgeware team and the known members of the council.                     In consideration of their feedback, I decided that I will still cast my vote.

         1) Unknown members in the council
There are still a lot of unknown members in the council and there is no way to                      discuss the proposal and understand their position. That means that every vote counts.

         2) Position as a representative of the community:
The intended behavior of slashing is not to punish validators and nominators for                      bugs in the software! As a representative of the community, this is a standpoint that I will always support. Regardless of my position.
