**Summary:**
**	**Solo Contributors’ Union aims to reduce the friction from the solo contributors’ side as well as the on-chain governance side for the monthly incentives and to disburse them promptly and under an equal scenario.

**Problems: **
- Limited bandwidth for proposals: With the decentralisation of the agency (one of the major contributing teams) in the coming months combined with the limitation of the external queue, the referenda stage could be bottlenecked leaving near to no bandwidth available for other kinds of proposals.
- On-chain governance can’t process the incentives at once and thus creating differences due to price/market momentum.
- Funding for multiple months is not reliable at this (growth) stage and if the market goes down, the contributors would need to propose a top-up proposal.


**Solution:**
- Introducing 'Solo Contributors' Union (aka SCU)'. SCU will allow the contributors to acquire the community mandate through their initial/first month's proposal and then be a part of SCU from next month.
- Clubbing multiple proposals together: The total amount in EDG will be calculated based on the 7-day average of the total monthly incentives of all SCU members.
- A new multisig will be set up on the 24th of every month if there are any new members added to the SCU. This could be replaced with the multi-sender EdgeWASM smart contract in future. But until then, the multisig threshold ratio will be determined as follows:
- If n = Total number of solo contributors in the SCU,
- And ⌊⌋ represents ‘[Greatest Integer Function](https://www.cuemath.com/algebra/greatest-integer-function/)’,
- Then, the multisig threshold ratio = ⌊(n/2)⌋+1 : n
- To avoid manipulation using multiple accounts, the _primary threads_ with the topic “Solo Contributors' Union” will need a minimum of 100000 EDG holding to post/comment.


**Criteria:**
- Only solo individuals and smaller groups with no hierarchy (ex: Community Mods) will become members of the Solo Contributors' Union.
- The contributor(s) will have to go through the initial solo proposal phase again in the following cases:
- If they want to increase or decrease their commitment.
- If any other community member/contributor raises concern about their contributions on the _primary thread_.


**Guidelines for the contributors:**
- Post your proposal draft as a discussion thread on the forum and set the topic to “Solo Contributors' Union”. (If you have a ‘Network Service Provider’, ask them to do so.) This will be referred to as the ‘_primary thread_’ of the solo contributor(s).
- Just like other treasury proposals, this will go through pre-governance discussion & feedback stages and then on-chain governance stages. (The proposal can be pushed as any type of referendum. {SuperMajority or SimpleMajority bias})
- Upon approval of the proposal, they will be added to the list below and then they no longer need to propose on a solo basis as long as they fulfil the ‘Criteria’ mentioned above.
- The SCU’s collective proposal will be proposed around the 25th day of every month. After the disbursement of the SCU’s monthly collective proposal, all the SCU members have the responsibility to coordinate further distribution to their accounts by queuing up and signing the multisig transactions.
