## Summary 

This proposal seeks funding to recoup costs for the acquisition of a Kusama 'para thread' by [@Ramsey](/edgeware/account/k43sLKozrmQq7d7aqpYCzqiUM7DJgMaMrVsrY1uPQvpZLWT) which is secured through a refundable KSM registration fee and follows the acquisition of the [Kusama Para-ID](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/3759-proposal-kabochas-paraid-deposit-for-kusama) deposit. 

This is one of the last stages before the launch of a crowd loan, acting as an on-ramp for new parachains into the relay. 

> Parathreads are an idea for parachains to temporarily participate (on a block by block basis) in Kusama security without needing to lease a dedicated parachain slot.

You can read more about para-threads [here](https://guide.kusama.network/docs/learn-parathreads/).

## Funding terms

The 60 KSM will be returned to Edgeware if Kabocha does not secure a slot and at the end of 48 weeks if Kabocha does not secure a second year as a parachain. 

If Kabocha retains the slot for a second year, the 60 KSM will remain locked. 

The 60 KSM includes 1 KSM to cover miscellaneous extrinsic calls / fees.
