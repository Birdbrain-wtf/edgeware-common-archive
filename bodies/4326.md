## **A sandbox for a sovereign future**

Kabocha is a holistic self-evolving grass-roots parachain project, funding and incubating teams to build new public infrastructure. 

It is an experiment in crafting new realities through direct participation, collaborative creation and shared values. Its ownership and governance is fair, transparent and decentralised at launch thanks to its genesis in the Edgeware community. 

The project introduces a _Network Public _template and novel _Store of Values _currency and is built with the Substrate framework.

## **Network Publics**

A _Network Public_ is a sovereign digital institution designed to interface financially, culturally and lawfully with established jurisdictions. 

Network Publics enable the sustainable funding of creative contributions from borderless work forces, enabled through on-chain governance, curated proposals and an aligned value capture mechanism.

We will be funding new public utilities, services and spaces that offer foundations to develop, educate and inspire bright new futures.  

Network Publics all share an opinionated Substrate design template known as _Publik_. 

Kabocha is a product of three years of experience contributing to the on-chain governance of Decred and Edgeware and the strengths and challenges of each.

## **Fair Distribution: _Edgeware Mainnet_ _Snapshot _  **

At over 11m blocks Edgeware is one of the longest living self-governing Substrate chains alongside Kusama and Polkadot and certainly its most decentralised. 

It has one of the broadest and fairest distributions of any network, with over 90% of the network in the hands of the public after 2019’s lockdrop. 

Kabocha will benefit from Edgeware’s broad and fair distribution and mainnet status via a launch that takes a snapshot of the Edgeware chain, minting KAB 1:1 to EDG holders. 

The only balance in the distribution that will change control is the treasury, which will be reassigned to KAB governance. 
This account will hold 6.43m KAB and will be used to reward Crowdloan participants, collators and maintainers for the initial parachain lease.

This approach will add no taxation or dilution to individual EDG holders, a pure 1:1 distribution.

The snapshot will be taken 14 days after a crowdloan win. 

## **Funding: _Project Mint_   **

Rather than inflating a supply and directing a proportion into a treasury, new money will only be minted to fund projects that the community decides are relevant and valuable.  

As a result at launch Kabocha’s supply is fixed, but in practice it can be infinite - the only limitation is the imagination of proposing teams and the benevolence of the community. 

In year 1 we are aiming to cap proposal inflation at around 20% of circulating supply through curation by the founding Network Service Provider - Decent Partners.   

## **Coordination: _Network Service Providers_**

A Network Service Provider (NSP) onboards external talent, teams and projects to Kabocha - curating, communicating and formalising proposals that are then voted on by token holders. 

The NSP ensures the careful deployment and management of approved funds, the successful delivery of agreed milestones and the timely reporting of issues, progress and lessons learned through the project.  

The founding NSP in Kabocha is Decent Partners, whose co-operative membership is elected via successfully delivered proposals of supported project teams.  

Decent Partners acts as a bridge between Kabocha’s sovereign jurisdiction and existing legal entities and will act as a steward of assets, shares and any rights that are not originated on-chain. 

A NSP receives a 10% Network Services Fee on top of the proposal funds. 

## **Value Capture:_ Cultural Collateral _**

Kabocha is backed by _cultural collateral_ - the creative contributions of its community. 

The funding of projects directly accrues value to a reserve through the assignment of genesis rights by project teams that represent their project concept. 

This cultural collateral will give access, rights, permissions and future utility to those holding KAB and will be upgraded as the funded projects develop over time.

Successful projects seeded by Kabocha will evolve into their own Publiks that join a _Publik Relay_.  

Cultural collateral will be interoperable and composable within the _Publiks _template, opening up opportunities for permissionless collaboration between teams. 

## **Governance: _Seeds_**

Seeds are central to the governance of Kabocha, representing evolving roles, rights and reputation in the network and future Publiks.  

Seeds are unique, non-transferable assets that grow based on continuing contributions to the project.

They will define how you build identity, your voting power and access within future Publik domains.  

There is no limit to the number of Seeds and uniqueness will not be defined by a predefined algorithm, but by the continuing contributions of the owner. 

## **Liquidity: _Notes _**

A _Note_ is a network native financing instrument similar to a convertible note.

Notes enable a new Publik to raise development funding from a parent Publik through an innovative swapping and conversion mechanism, with the call option on the swap mediated between the governance of the two Publiks. 

Notes also offer funded project teams a path to liquidating tokens into working capital in a seamless and predictable fashion without creating downward price pressure.

Notes can be purchased by regulated entities including listed companies who are incentivised to buy tokens at a consistent discount. 

Somewhat similar to liquidity providers, these entities (or compliant persons) provide long term funding in a controlled and transparent fashion with incentives aligned to the long term goals of the Publik.

## **Roadmap**

**Phase 1: **
Kabocha will launch as a _Parachain Publik_ on Kusama, with proposals curated by Decent Partners as the founding Network Service Provider. 

**Phase 2: **
Kabocha will launch a _Publik_ relay-chain, with a grass development approach to launching parachains (more to come on this later in the year), becoming the first network to bond to the relay and bridging to Kusama and Polkadot in the process. 

**Phase 3: **
Kabocha will launch a range of _Parachain_ _Publiks, into the new Publik Relay, _retaining stake in the networks it develops. 

## **Demand narrative**
Kabocha’s demand narrative is rooted in a rework of the well known ‘Store of Value’ meme, the major narrative hook for Bitcoin.
However rather than being backed by machines and sunk electricity costs of miners, Kabocha is backed by the time, effort and insight of its contributors. 

Those investing in Kabocha are essentially betting on the future collective cultural value and access afforded by the assigned assets.

We call this ‘Store of Values’. 

## **Vision: Another Way**
Our grass-roots development approach and decentralised contributor model will advance ambitious on-chain public ventures and bring their stories to life, demonstrating in the process that there is _Another Way_.

With the _Publik Relay_ as their network nursery, these projects could then onramp to Kusama and then to Polkadot. 

A more optimistic stance is that Kabocha’s Network Public vision, _Publik_ template and _Store of Values_ narrative could stand apart, yielding a more sustainable, scalable and values aligned ecosystem. 

## **Genesis**
Kabocha has been developed in Good Faith by the Edgeware community through [a series of proposals](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussions/Kabocha) from independent contributors, each developed and approved by the community.

Edgeware is owned, operated and secured by token holders who work together to steward the entire economic, organisational and cultural resources of the project through open, meritocratic and democratic processes that are all executed on-chain on the project's own blockchain.

It is the second oldest mainnet chain in the Substrate ecosystem - younger than Kusama, but older than Polkadot with over[ 10M blocks finalised.](https://edgeware.app/#/explorer)

The project did not conduct an ICO or fundraising even, instead it was bootstrapped using a[ lockdrop](https://medium.com/commonwealth-labs/whats-in-a-lockdrop-194218a180ca), where holders of ETH could lock their tokens for 3, 6, 9 or 12 months in return for receiving EDG.

Binance recently distributed the lockdrop rewards.

## **Kusama Crowdloan terms**

4.9% (half of the treasury) of KAB is reserved for KSM lenders. 

Reward per KSM will be dependent on the amount of KAB that is locked up in the Crowdloan. 

Contributed KSM will be returned at the end of Kabocha parachain lease.

KAB will be distributed after the mainnet launch of Kabocha.

Vesting: 30% upfront, 70% vested over term of crowdloan.

## **Eligibility**
