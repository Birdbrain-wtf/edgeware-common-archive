**Questions for Validators**

**1. What is your experience as a validator or node operator?**
 

We were running nodes on the first version of testnet and helped to write the first community manual about it.
 https://medium.com/@ambroidcrypto/running-an-edgeware-testnet-validator-node-80fee462fd9b

**2. What's your technical setup? (cloud vs. on-premise; software stack; key management)**

Vultr VPS with 2048 MB RAM 55 GB SSD and 1 CPU core.

**3. Why are you interested in validating?**

We knew Edgeware before it was a thing. We were excited about the vision of governance experimentation so it makes total sense that we want to be a part of the network infrastructure and security from day 1 and utilize our lockdrop reward in a productive way. 

**Questions for Councillor Candidates**

**1. Please provide an overall summary of who you are and why you're interested in holding a councillor seat.**

Zee Prime Capital is a crypto investment firm that takes an activist role in their investments. As an early investor behind the team that built Edgeware, we are naturally inclined to take part in stewarding the network development.

**2. How do you propose the treasury budget should be spent?**

To name a few: bug bounty program, tooling for developers, well documented showcases of interesting smart contracts, incentivization of integrations, closer cooperations with other parachains/projects

**3. What should Edgeware do to grow its community?** 

Better communicate its unique value proposition (e.g. i. vs other parachains and ii. other smartcontract platforms). Help newcomers understand Substrate / Polkadot / Edgeware primitives as fast as possible.
 

**4. Which ecosystems beyond Polkadot should Edgeware bridge to, if any?** 

Ethereum ecosystem. Lockdrop was a successful endeavor, we should built on top of it. 

**5. How do you think Edgeware governance system should evolve?** 

We are believers in minimalism so we expect that the best governance model will converge to a simple set of procedures. After all, ‘the most important and hardest part of trust minimization is governance minimization’.

**Questions for Both**

**1. What are your positions on issues that governance will be expected to handle? For example: Further lockdrop or airdrop to DOT holders, or holders of other assets**

DOT holders as they contributed to the creation of Substrate, then for the asset from important integration (e.g. Chainlink)

**2. What proposals on the network are you most interested in seeing? (e.g. initial parachain offerings, DAOs, DeFi protocols, stablecoins, prediction markets, insurance)**

TCRs, DAOs and DeFi. IPOs should be fun too.

**3. Is there an overall long-term vision for the network you are most excited about?**

Reliable and scalable base protocol for hot DAOs acting also as a relay chain for smaller dapp chains. Perhaps a testbed for some yet unseen and unbuilt DeFi concepts.
