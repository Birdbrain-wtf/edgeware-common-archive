**Incentivizing Contract Development through Rights to a Portion of Transaction Fees --specifically rebate.  **

In the new Riot channel, Councilperson Jam, Legend and Ninsis (various pseudonyms) discussed the best way to leverage transaction fees to encourage better contract development - simply allowing a claim on transaction fees for busy contracts opened the potential for traffic to be faked, but they brought in the case of NEAR, in which  only the contract owner can claim a portion of transaction fees -- and since the owner pays the fees in the first place, as long as fees rebated are less than fees paid, there is no incentive to fake traffic. 
Some discussion was had about the use of treasury proposals to deply ETH contracts once the EVM pallet is in-- and that they expect poorly ported projects to be an issue to some level.
