**Drew Stone, core developer of Edgeware, has been working on a proposed feature for the Treasury.**
- "Treasury Reward" is a streamed payment - a percentage of the block reward that goes to the treasury that can be allocated to addresses. 
- The allocation is automatic and occurs with each block reward.
- Multiple accounts can receive an allocation.
- When over a limit and new accounts are added to the allocation list, it dillutes the reward of other assignees. 
- Initiation and termination can be conducted though a Treasury Motion.
