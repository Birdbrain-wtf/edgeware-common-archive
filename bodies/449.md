Recently community members have discussed reducing the Ideal Staking Rate, a chain parameter, from 80% to 50%. 

>  "This value should probably lie between 0.3 and 0.6, and we originally set it at \chi_{ideal}=0.546"
[Web3F Research](https://research.web3.foundation/en/latest/polkadot/Token%20Economics.html)

The consequences of adjusting this down are not totally clear- so I'm attaching two modeling tools I've worked up, to help us explore the option. Both have a parameter for the Ideal Staking Rate that can be modified.

These tools have not been fully vetted for mathematical accuracy, but they SHOULD -  a team with a specialty would be an amazing grant recipient to develop these models into more usable and verified tools.

After some playing around, one item of note is that both models display a lower reward rate for stakers if we shift the ideal staking rate down.  That makes sense to me - we are using strong incentives to get it up to 80 - and rewards are a way to do that. Drop the goal post, and you may not need as much incentive to reach it.
