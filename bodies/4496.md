**Introduction**

This post is about a proposal to modify an existing governance parameter related to the bounty process, i.e. the Bounty Payout Delay which is currently set at 7 days. In short, we’re proposing this delay be reduced to 5 days.

We’ve discussed the complete process about finalizing the new delay (5 days) below.


**Problem Statement**

As we know that in Edgeware the bounty process is designed as follows: 

a. First, there is a council motion to finalize the bounty (1-14 days)
b. Then, another council motion to assign the curator (1-14 days)
c. The Bounty Update Period(1-30 days) - this is when contributors shall work on the bounty task, and the curator can close the bounty if the task is successfully completed or can update the bounty if the goal is not achieved in the given period
d. Then comes the Bounty Payout Delay (7 days). This is when any community member or a council member can come up with objections if any and can reverse the bounty.
e. The treasury spends cycles(3 days). This is a predefined period for treasury spends and the bounty will be paid according to the period.


Now, in step ‘c’, when a contributor completes the bounty task and the curator has played his part by verifying the work, it is not appropriate for the contributor to wait for another 7 days + (0-3) days depending on the spend cycle, to receive the rewards he’s entitled to.This is a bit unfair to the recipient of the rewards as his work, would have already undergone a certain level of scrutiny by the curator.

Also, in those 7 days the price of EDG may undergo some upward/downward movements, and this may cause some losses to the contributor because of the payout delay. 

Now, on the other hand, any community member should have the right to raise objections (if any), regarding the successful completion of the task. Thus, we cannot completely do away with this Payout Delay. 

So, an optimal solution to this would be to set the payout delay to such a duration where, 
(i) The contributor loses the least, due to price fluctuations and
(ii) Community members get sufficient duration to verify and raise objects 

And so, we did some mathematics based on the price of EDG. The data considered is from Jan’21 to April’22. 


**Solution and Calculations**

The important factor that we’re focusing on in this model is the price volatility of EDG, and thus this model is completely based on Standard Deviation, which truly represents the volatility in mathematical terms.

Now, for this exercise, the data samples which we considered were 10 day, 9 day, 8 day, 7 day and 6 day data samples. 
For example, in the 10 day data sample we took the Std dev of the price of EDG in every 10 day period starting from Jan’21 to Apr’22. An additional point here is that, in order to consider all the fluctuations in the price of EDG, we considered multiple samples such that their starting points were unique compared to any of the other samples.

Also if we consider the sample duration to be ‘n’ days, we will have exactly ‘n’ unique datasets, so 10 data sets for a 10 day model, 9 data sets for a 9 day model and so on.

Thus, we can conclude that the 8 day period (5 days bounty payout delay + 3 days of treasury spend cycle), would be the most appropriate combination based on the above calculations. More importantly this satisfies both the requirements related to the bounty process, which was discussed above.
