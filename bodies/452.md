Commonwealth Labs believes that the Edgeware network would benefit from a Community Manager to focus on the network full-time, and particularly on Asia and the Chinese speaking community.

We propose that unlike a traditional hire, this chain-contractor role is paid from the network's treasury. The role would be structured like a Request For Proposal (RFP) and this role will be responsible for their full plan - strategy and execution.

Day-to-day, we anticipate that this person would respond to community support questions, coordinate online and offline events, update documentation and issues, and manage a global community with a specific focus on Asia and the Chinese-speaking community.

We imagine that this role will work closely with core developers while having the agency to fulfill milestones and KPIs as approved by the network's community. Given that structure is a bit new, we anticipate that the person taking on this network will need to be flexible and open to experimentation on how the community perceives performance.


**An ideal candidate is:**
- Familiar with the Substrate and Polkadot ecosystem.
- Experienced in social media and technical customer support.
- Mandarin-speaking optional.
- Exceptional written and oral communication skills.
- Able to research complex topics in Blockchain and to stay up to date on improvements.
- Demonstrable results in the field of communications.



**Responsibilities:**

- Set and implement social media and communication campaigns that support the success of Edgeware users, product awareness, and engagement.
- Coordinate and moderate in-person and on-line events.
- Coordinate with development and validator community to stay on top of protocol changes.
- Managing community, communications and translations of English language content into other languages.
- Providing engaging text, image and video content for social media accounts.
- Responding to comments and customer queries on Twitter, Discord, Telegram, and on Commonwealth in a timely manner.
- Providing bottom-up content and needs of our community.
- Serving as the network's voice for China and Chinese speaking regions.
- Develop relationships with fellow community managers, journalists and other organizations.
