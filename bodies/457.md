We've got a sizeable treasury and I'd like to get some discussion going about how we can best allocate it to best support the long-term grown of Edgeware and the Polkadot ecosystem broadly. 

Here are the top 3 priorities currently as I see it:

**1) Smart contract support **
Edgeware first appealed to me early on because of the promise to support smart contracts. This is an important service for the Polkadot ecosystem that won't be supported natively by the Polkadot relay chain. I haven't heard much talk of this since launch - is this being worked on? Could an allocation from the treasury help get this done quicker?

**2) Ethereum bridge **
Another Edgeware initiative that appealed to me early on but has been quiet since launch is the Ethereum bridge. Obviously a lot of EDG holders are also ETH holders and if we can be the first to build a bridge between Ethereum and the Polkadot ecosystem, there is a lot of value there. Is there anyone working on this? I think this is an initiative that we should be committing treasury funds to. 

**2) Exchange support**
As one of the first substrate-based chains that allows users to utilize nominated proof-of-stake, it's in the interest of the community to broaden our community base by getting supported on exchanges. Some exchanges like Binance and Huobi charge a listing fee. It could make sense to pursue this to be the first substrate-based chain to get listed on a major exchange. This initiative is a distant third for me though.

What do others think? Where should we focus on allocating treasury funding to?
