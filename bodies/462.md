**Referencing Signal Event:[ EDG SIG 8](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/signalingproposal/0xe22b8fb78a0f099d294696532a8d809169989b135fbdc9b0d7728cebfb667e54-use-signaling-before-proposing-in-validator-set-changes)**


I think I am favor of a deliberative population obtaining a comprehensive signal about the views of others on a matter before they take it to binding referendum. 

For instance, you may approach a vote normally with the idea that 'my vote doesn't matter' but if you knew before that it was actually quite 50/50, you might vote differently or avoid 'protest votes.' I would take this a step further and say that our democratic process should FORCE a signaling event prior to referendum. 

Would love to hear views about why we wouldn't want this to be required at least some actions - speed is one, but if the tools the community is building to enhance gov (including this one!) work at compelling robust and consistent engagement,  I am all for shortening cycles.
