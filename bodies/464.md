The Edgeware network is looking for a new developer to support active projects in the Edgeware and Substrate ecosystem. The role of the developer is to track active projects in the Substrate ecosystem, recommend additions to the Edgeware blockchain, and implement and test these additions in conjunction with the rest of the core developer community.

We frame this role as a chain-contractor opportunity, not a traditional hire, submitted as an request-for-proposal (RFP) response, and paid according to milestone or KPI achievement from the network's on-chain treasury.

The ideal candidate possesses the following skillset and is eager to join a growing community to push new technical proposals as well as projects in the Edgeware ecosystem forward.

### Skillset
- Demonstrably familiar with OSS Development
- Knowledge of Substrate and experience building and/or testing runtime modules in the Substrate ecosystem.
- Experience with Rust, Typescript, and shell scripting and/or an ability to learn new concepts.
- Understanding of blockchain concepts such as forks and upgrades.
- Great communication skills, fluency in English is a must and any other languages is a plus.

### Responsibilities
- Implement and test new runtime and node updates from upstream Substrate on Edgeware and any necessary forks.
- Run through upgrades against various runtime versions
- Ability to implement and test migrations from upstream Substrate.
- Implement and/or test any runtime modules in development.
- Designing runtime module specs, sharing with the community, and engaging feedback to update specs and their respective runtime logic.
- Track progress on a variety of Substrate modules and be able to provide bi-weekly updates on feasibility and use of these modules for external developers.
- Contracts
- EVM
- Staking functionality
- Democracy functionality
- Implementation and testing of sample contracts on both contract based modules.

### **Compensation**
We recommend an initial 30-day period at 10 hours per week (42 hours total) at the EDG equivalent of $51/hr. ($51/$0.0025 per EDG = 21,000 EDG per hour)
For the whole period, we recommend a grant of 882,000 EDG (21,000 EDG/hr*42hr) from the Edgeware Treasury. After this, the role would be renewed every 3 months and milestone grants given accordingly.
