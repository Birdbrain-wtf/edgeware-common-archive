I understand that a proportion of the block reward goes to the treasury to achieve active decentralised governance on the following proposed areas:

Onboarding
* Community voting on prioritisation of documentation and to incentivise the development of tooling.

Upgrades
* Inter-chain bridges for assets and communication (i.e. Ethereum)
* DAO designs
* Parachain design
* Forks
* Parathreads usage
* Innovation (i.e. product management onboarding process where governance takes users through a process from initial ideation, to specification and design)

Tooling
* Recipes of different incentive mechanisms
* Upgrades are selected based on community votes to try different incentive mechanisms (i.e. using Sourcecred to attest to and allocate rewards) to encourage participation from validators, nominators, DApp developers, advocates, other ecosystem users.
* Monitor recipe behaviour
    * Build tooling for monitoring the different incentive recipes that are deployed
    * Community selection and opinion votes across different metrics based on comparison across different behavioural patterns to determine changes in community sentiment
* Monitor recipe usage

Infrastructure
* Reducing node build time

UX
* Design competitions for different DApp boilerplates

9. What are your positions on issues that governance will be expected to handle?
* Automated voting dashboards
    * Allow community members to dynamically adjust the proportion of locked tokens across different range and thresholds (i.e. DApp with a UI that lists key referendum categories with a slider that allows them to adjust the proportion of their holding that they want to lock for voting on different issues to improve participation) in order to automate itemised voting across different categories and broad preferences, whilst giving them the option to overriding their pre-set vote and manually vote when desired. Analogy is subscription (rather than ad-hoc) voting with pre-defined preferences that users may adjust across the issues that they care about.
