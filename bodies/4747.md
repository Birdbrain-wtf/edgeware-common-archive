## **Structure**
The following document constitutes the project scope, roadmap and vision, hereon referred to as **“The Project”**

Individual treasury proposals will be presented to EDG / KAB communities via a series of [simple majority referendums](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1563-simplemajority-a-proposed-edgeware-governance-standard), hereon referred to as “**Proposals”** that scale in ambition over time. 

Each proposal will have clear deliverables, from hereon referred to as **“Milestones”**.

Edgeware will seed the initial project and will recoup from Kabocha’s treasury via the [established mechanism](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/3806-kabocha-funding-and-edgeware-recoup), continuing to build the network's stake of KAB, from hereon referred to as **"Recoup"**.

Upon successful completion of the initial proposal to Edgeware, Kabocha will continue funding, establishing a clear relationship in the lifecycle funding of the two networks, with Edgeware operating as seed stage and Kabocha as follow on.

## **Project Summary **
Auto Fabrica will be launching a Web3 design community that leverages Edgeware’s emerging DAO functionality alongside [RMRK’s](https://rmrk.app) native upgradeable and equippable multi-resource NFTs to experiment with the development, creation and production of digital asset based concepts that also exist as **unique real world vehicles**. 

## **Cultural Collateral **
Auto Fabrica will be assigning cultural collateral, hereon referred to as **“CC” **in the form of genesis NFTs based on the [RMRK standard](https://rmrk.app) to the accounts of the three key stakeholders involved in developing, funding and delivering the project. 

1. **The communit**y: Edgeware/Kabocha treasury inc snapshot of EDG addresses
2. **The creator**: Auto Fabrica design studio and their 137,000 member community    
3. **The curator**: Network Services provided by Decent Partners


This approach to assigning CCs rewards holders of EDG and KAB and drives fundamental demand due to expectation of airdropped assets and more sustainably as the underlying network currencies become backed by reserves of CC.  

## **CC Snapshot **
The snapshot is taken of accounts when the first Auto Fabrica proposal is approved. 

Auto Fabrica CC will be distributed to Seed holders after Kabocha's launch. 

From then on it will not be possible to acquire the CC other than to buy on a marketplace.  

## **Advancing RMRK **
The assigned CC utilises and advances RMRK’s upgradeable, equippable and eternally liquid design philosophy which enables use cases beyond Ethereum NFTs. 

- CC is entirely on-chain and compatible across all Substrate chains, apps and market places due to the fundamental interoperability of the Polkadot ecosystem. 


- CC are your keys to the EDG/KAB verse, unlocking emerging story worlds built on shared, interoperable and open public infrastructure.


- CC is multi-resource, meaning a single NFT can trigger different media to load in different interfaces and experiences. For example a 2D visual in one setting, streaming video in another, audio book in another, or even an experience in Unreal Engine. Over the longer term we see all of the above consolidating as a unified experience inside Unreal Engine. 


- CC will be assigned to Seeds issued via Kabocha, which are non-transferable NFTs - a characteristic hereon referred to as **“Roots”**, another concept only possible with RMRK. Seed NFTs will own other NFTs and become your identity.  


- CC enables native Substrate royalties that are unenforceable in Ethereum or via cross-chain transfers, since there are no consensus guarantees enabling value to be shared more favourably and consistently with creators and contributors. 


- CCs can be compatible with Open Sea or other exchanges, but the best experience moving forward will be enabled solely via Substrate’s relay chain design principles. 


- CC is on-chain IP that will develop its own legal title established in a new network public jurisdiction that can interface with established legal systems. This will allow Kabocha’s CC to return value to project contributors through licensing agreements to distribution partners. 


## **Store of Values**
As EDG/KAB funded CC becomes more well known and established as valuable with the floor price of on sale assets rising, so  the underlying reserves of EDG/KAB, allowing sophisticated investors to calculate fair value to the tokens. 

This in turn enables a new kind of rights based value accrual mechanism for on-chain governed crypto-currencies that we call Store of Values, hereon referred to as “**SoVs”**

## **Governance**
Governance process, standards and technical infrastructure to manage assigned CC to be decided through community discussion in addition to the technical development work being undertaken by the Kabocha team to iterate existing pallets and create new ones.

## **Our vision **
We want to create a bold and adventurous ‘[Pininfarina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pininfarina) of the metaverse’, discovering and unleashing untapped creative talent from around the world, fueling a global, decentralised design renaissance that is entirely open to contributions from anyone, anywhere. 

In previous eras, value has flowed to the individual designers and manufacturers who were best positioned to protect, license and defend their designs and processes. 

With the emergence of internet media, alongside NFTs as mechanisms for establishing credit and context, we see for the first time a chance to share value across an emerging design _movement_, an approach that recognises ‘genius’ as a collective rather than individual endeavour.

## **Building Web3 identity through emerging reputation **
Contributions during the design stage will be credited to Seeds, hereon known as“**Shoots”**, enabling participants to build their on-chain reputation and identity. This emergent on-chain reputation will begin to define the roles, responsibilities and capabilities of the holder within Kabocha’s network.

A Seed’s credit will also dictate future distributions of tokens in Kabocha’s planned multi-chain incubator. 

An example of how this works would be as follows - we post on social media that we require input in a certain part of the project - for example engineering the frame of a motorcycle - we open this room for each person who applies to work on it. 

This enables us to harness talent globally without any restrictions which can in turn scale our creative output and share value in a way not possible in existing social networks. 

## **So is this a metaverse project?**
Much of the conversation around ‘the metaverse’ is distracting. 

We believe the metaverse should be read as a metaphor, not literally. It is a process, not a place, an emerging playground of possibilities, a way to connect, collaborate and create with a borderless network of talent - but where real meaning, comes when the digital bits are made real back in the world of atoms as **fully engineered concept vehicles back in the ‘real world’**.
