On the latest [governance call](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/461-may-community-call-summary-and-notes), there was a healthy discussion around how the treasury should be used. Hopefully, this thread can crystalize that conversation around “budgets" and further the conversation by proposing a governance process for approving such a budget.
## **Areas of Focus**

One of the core features of Edgeware outlined in the original whitepaper is the on-chain treasury. The goal for the treasury is to incentivize the development of network goods that users can provide.

Currently, the chain specifies a ~500m EDG to be minted directly to the treasury—a 10% increase from the initial supply in the first year. However, with unclaimed validator rewards going directly to the treasury, the treasury is running a “surplus”. This surplus arises when the percentage of the network staked is lower than the target (80%). In this case, an additional +5% of yearly inflation is going to the treasury. The budget discusses how both the 10% and additional surplus could be used.

**30% of treasury inflation - Core Development**: Making the chain attractive for developers by chain feature set. With many different pallets being developed by independent teams within the Substrate Ecosystem, it seems this will take the shape of Edgeware continuing to allocate grants to teams to help integrate their runtime and improvements to those pallets, or ground-up development.
- Projects include:
- [Integrating Runtime Bridges](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges#runtime-module-bridges)
- Additional Runtime Audits
- Bug Bounty Program
- Social Key Recovery Pallet
- Cumulus Runtime Integration
- EVM and Contracts Module Support
- Alternative client implementations


**20% of treasury inflation - DOT Bond**: For Edgeware to bid for a parachain slot, it will need to acquire DOTs. While the mechanics of this are still a bit unclear, it would coincide with Polkadot network launch. This is likely a non-reoccurring pool. There are parties who hold EDG that also hold DOTs and might altruistically bond on Edgeware’s behalf.

**20% of treasury inflation - Network Effects, Access, and Community**: Currently, there are around [6k holders of EDG](https://edgeware.subscan.io/account) growing +50% YoY. As the number of active holders of EDG increases, and more token value is stored on-chain through novel applications, Edgeware utility will continue to grow. Some portion of funds should be set aside to help this process:
- Adding an incentive to individuals to add their on-chain identity to allow for the possibility of quadratic funding / voting
- Providing liquidity to on-chain projects (for example being a funder on an EDG backed stablecoin or "underwriting" a dominant assurance contract)
- [Exchange marketing (mentioned by NNT)](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/457-edgeware-priorities)
- General Community Support for individuals whom want to host (virtual) Edgeware meetups.


**20% - Commonwealth / Governance Dev (distributed through streaming treasury reward)**: There is a separate **[thread to discuss this more at more length](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/476-moving-towards-an-commonwealth-streaming-reward)**, some grant from the treasury would help Commonwealth achieve long-term sustainability. Some things that we are particularly excited about working on:
- Further funding and governance decentralization: Right now, the council has a lot of power, and governance is weighted towards larger token holders. We want to continue building governance infrastructure to allow power to gracefully rest in network users hands:
- Continuing on BD and network coordination: for example, establishing a parachain that acts on the Kusama Canary Network, and organizing community calls.
- Protocol Development using Edgeware Smart Contracts.
- Node maintenance, and core development: keeping Edgeware upgraded and caught up to master


**10% - Developer Tools and Documentation:** Projects like a GraphQL library will enhance the developer experience on Edgeware. We have a list of proposals that are up for grabs in grants. As suggested by Remz, we should also try to fund other projects within the larger Substrate Ecosystem, like [paritytech/frontier](https://github.com/paritytech/frontier)—an Ethereum compatibility layer.

**Surplus - On-chain incubator: **Incubators like YCombinator or Entrepreneur's First have created tremendous value for both investors and the world in a very capital-efficient manner. For YC, [$12m dollars](https://techcrunch.com/2014/07/16/y-combinator-1-billion/) has created an estimated $1b for direct investor returns and then [+$155b in total company value](https://www.ycombinator.com/topcompanies). Edgeware can leverage the treasury and EDG-holding community to help bootstrap newly created projects—**funding 10 projects over the course of the year, with a standard package of $50k per project ($500k total). ** For the initial proposal, we could look to target 5 projects so the proposal would be something like ~$250k for 5 projects. These projects would be differentiated from core runtime improvements in that they may also launch their own token. Projects like on-chain oracles, sustainable public goods funding, and a stablecoin, and automated market makers would all be fair game. Of course, there is much more to be discussed for this including legal, operational, and community decision making process, but would like to propose this now.

**Surplus - Supporting blockchain research: **Funding research is core public good. If we imagine Edgeware's role into the future. It scope can grow to more than funding Edgeware's public goods, but the larger ecosystem's. To test this capacity, we should use some portion of the treasury to fund these public goods. It will help stay true to the Edgeware mission of improving public goods funding through governance mechanisms for the world at large.

A test run of this program would be like a "Developer UBI". Where peers select who gets to join the group and selected members are given some research stipend per month. Initially, **a program like this might be $100 per developer in EDG at a max of 100 researchers per month,** which could scale as the community sees results. There are existing precedents for this such as [Emergent Ventures](https://www.mercatus.org/emergent-ventures) or [K99 grants](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/research-funding/process/apply/funding-mechanisms/k-awards/k99-r00) (~$70k per year per research). The idea being that funding individuals rather than projects can increase overall research productivity. We would try to seed the initial set with undergraduate, graduate students, and research projects around Edgeware and general blockchain topics.

Supporting open-ended chain research endeavors could have large secondary brand and community building effects by associating top schools, research labs, and conferences with Edgeware. This should have far-reaching benefits for discussion around innovative ideas and projects to implement on Edgeware, albiet on a longer timescale.

**Treasury Burn**

Beyond these two portions from the surplus, the remainder of the treasury could be burned in network-wide votes at the end of the first year of the chain.

## **Governance Process**
It’s been great to see a number of treasury proposals organically develop, and get pushed through council voting. Before we go through an overall budget approval process, it seems okay to continue with funding grants in the current manner.

I’m proposing a yearly set of signaling polls to set the roadmap and budget. It's intended to be lighter on-chain voting and heavier on conversation. The council will have more legitimacy to approve treasury spends as long as they fall with the budget that has been proposed. Future changes to on-chain governance by setting a max approval per era, or max treasury spend size, and shortening the council length from one month could further limit council power.

**Use a signaling proposal to approve a budget till end of 2020**
Currently, the above pools are sized in order of importance—leaving enough room for items outlined on the roadmap as well as new ideas. Hopefully once the roadmap is agreed upon we can move through a signaling poll relatively quickly. I think that a two week comment period might be appropriate before putting together some ranked-choice signaling polls. At this point, nothing will have moved from the treasury. For the signal, it’ll be important that the vote has good voter participation to signify legitimacy—something on the order of magnitude of the ~25% of the network currently staked.

**Monthly Treasury Payouts and Checkins**
As proposals and grants roll in, the council will evaluate them approving or denying after community signaling polls and conversation on community calls. [See Staking UI as a recent example](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/454-staking-ui-grant-proposal-draft). During this time, the council should continue to weigh the guidelines set to make sure that the network is not “overspending."

**Use Working Groups to generate more project and roadmaps for next year.**
The working groups are some existing community infrastructure. It would be helpful to utilize them, and as a reminder they are open for people to join. The working groups can continue to get feedback from the community on an adhoc basis.

**2021 Planning Cycle (at the year end)**
Discuss Roadmaps pulled together by the open working groups + create signals to approve Discuss and approve budget guidelines.

I’m curious to hear what folks think and where there are weaknesses. I will want to discuss this on the next community / governance calls. Hopefully the year long cycles are infrequent enough so that we can gather wider participation. It feels like Edgeware governance will evolve quite substantially as we continue to decentralize power away from the council.
