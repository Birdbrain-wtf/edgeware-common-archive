In the _[Treasury Budget](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/475-treasury-budget)_ post, I proposed a potential budget for Edgeware as well as governance process for gaining social consensus around it. Of the 10% of network inflation due to the treasury, there was one particular request for Commonwealth Labs to earn 20% of the 10%. This would be allocated in in the form of a streaming block reward. This has separately been mentioned across past community calls, but I want to present this here in a bit more detail. I’ll discuss how we think we’ll grow our role on Edgeware and the proposed implementation of this reward.
## **Commonwealth Continuing Role in the Edgeware Ecosystem**
While we played a role in helping get Edgeware launched, the majority of the network’s growth is still in front of it. We want to make sure that Commonwealth Labs can continue to grow its involvement, while still allowing us to work sustainably, and stay aligned into the long term. We think this streaming reward is an ideal solution. We’ll continue to work in four key areas.

**Governance Decentralization**

- One of the first things that we did on Edgeware was to implement the signaling module as a light touch way for the network to get feedback on ideas. We want to continue working to decentralize Edgeware governance. In the long term, enabling a quadratic voting and funding process that is native to chain. Although non-trivial, it’s something like [raymondz](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/408-edg_ref_3-discussion-prop-to-increase-validator-set-limit-to-100-from-85) has already outlined. "A response to that might be for a large stakeholder to assign some stake to be decided by a democratic allocation mechanism, perhaps a HumanityDAO-like 1p1v vote or quadratic voting by verified identities.”


**Community Coordination**

- This comes in two flavors, product-driven and community driven. In terms of product driven, we’re posting this on the Commonwealth UI, which we hope can continue to grow into an important piece of social infrastructure for the community. We’d like to continue adding in native support for all governance and chain actions. For example, integrating tipping directly into the site or as a bot in Riot to make this interaction feel more organic. Beyond this, Drew has already started working on an anonymous voting module through a Web3 Grant, which hopefully will allow the community express their opinion without being tied to a specific address.
- On the community driven side, we’ve started hosting community calls, but we want to scale the set of community events into something like an EdgeCon over the coming years. An event like this could be used like political conventions have been used in the past, where the network votes to set roadmaps and budgets over the coming years.


**Developer Focal Point**

- A key component in Ethereum’s growth was the role that Consensys played—incubating and acting as a focal point for developer talent in the Ethereum ecosystem. We’d like to play a similar role for Edgeware. As we expand Commonwealth, we will be able to make open ended contributions to runtime and tool development. Some of these modules, can then turn into protocols themselves.
- While we discussed rolling out a chain-contractor position for an engineer, it may be difficult to attract top candidates. For example, we had previously identified several candidates which we believed would have been great additions to the chain. However, as we tried to bring them through the process, other projects that were able to act more quickly were able to hire these developers. A Commonwealth discretionary portion would have enabled us to move more quickly. In the same vein, Ethereum core developers have seen some churn, for individuals who did not participate in the Ethereum crowdsale (and for us the lockdrop), it’s a tough financial decision to make, where they don’t already have an economic interest. If Edgeware continues to be successful, then we hope that many new developers will come into the ecosystem.


**Core Dev and Infrastructure**

- We’ve in the midst of the first of many network upgrades, focused on stabilizing finality as well as enabling EVM contract functionality. We think there will be at least one more feature-rich network upgrade, mostly focused on bridging to other chains. Beyond core development, we want to contribute in two ways. First, we would like to plan a Kusama Canary Network, so that we can understand the dynamics of bonding to become a parachain. Second, we want to improve the infrastructure for the Berlin Testnet into the future.


## **Implementing the Streaming Payment: Initial 3-Month Trial to Referenda**

The module has been implemented and is potentially ready to be added into the first network upgrade. Of course, community discussion should continue on both the implementation as well as Commonwealth's role in the ecosystem before going live.

There has been conversation (mostly in Riot) around the pros and cons of implementing a streaming reward module. Where the _pro_ is more flexibility and certainty from the developer's side, and the _con_ is the potential risk from the chain side around the outcomes of a grant not being delivered. In this case, there aren't many paths for recourse that the chain can take. 

A preliminary solution was proposed where a team or set of developers would first get a 3 month trial period grant from the treasury, thereafter they would be added to the streaming reward module as their performance allows. As the streaming treasury module is undergoing testing, Commonwealth can serve as an example for this process. Although this could potentially be sidestepped if the community chooses to.

For an initial 3 month grant of **25m EDG**, (June-August 2020 or July-September 2020), we're going to continue pushing forward with the areas above. Specifically, we are anticipating working on:

**Core**
- Upgrade from pre-alpha Substrate to the latest alpha
- EVM pallet tuning, working towards a second upgrade with full Ethereum compatibility
- Launching a Edgeware Canary <> Kusama Canary Parachain
- Becoming a parachain on Polkadot

**Governance**
- Treasury Streaming Reward Module

**Developer Coordination**
- Leading / launching the on chain incubator program (more on this soon) - finding 10 teams to build on Edgeware in the first year. Beyond core upgrades, this is the largest area for value creation on Edgeware.
- Leading / launching the [grant process](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/466-creating-edgeware-grants) on Edgeware - including finding teams to fulfill grants and leading them through the process. With an eye towards turning them into long term contributors.

**Commonwealth UI**
- Phragmen council calculation module
- Offchain signaling with signed receipts & instructions for verifying them
- Getting up to par with Github for chain issue tracking
- Email notifications, subscriptions, subscription management to increase governance participations
- Notifications in-app and through email for chain events (such as upcoming votes and new validator rewards to your address)
- Design update to discussion threads, to enable better threaded conversation
- Redesign (profiles, voting history)


After the 3 month trial period and pending a network upgrade to include the treasury module, Commonwealth would submit a referenda to the chain to include the Commonwealth address and specified amount in the treasury module.
## **Streaming Rewards Provide Long Term Alignment**
Edgeware launched with a streaming treasury module that allocated 95 EDG per block directly to the treasury. Drew has worked on augmenting this module to allow for new destination addresses to be added. We are proposing a Commonwealth address this amount to the treasury.

We think it’s easier to process this proposal than creating periodic treasury proposals because of two main reasons—longer term incentive alignment and lowering conflict of interest with council voting.

- **Long Term Incentive Alignment:** The streaming payment essentially acts as a vesting schedule for Commonwealth Labs. And If the network feels that we aren’t executing well, then we can be removed from the streaming reward. A prominent example of a reward like this is the [ZCash Founder’s Reward](https://electriccoin.co/blog/funding/). Yet, knowing what the vesting schedule looks like gives Commonwealth more fiscal certainty, allowing us to plan with a longer term view. In the future, we don’t anticipate that Commonwealth will be the only entity receiving the streaming EDG. For teams that have a demonstrated interest in continuing to develop and support Edgeware for the long haul, this provides an option to them, that isn’t based on treasury milestone grants.
- **Lowers conflict of interest with council voting:** The streaming payment would be voted in by a referendum, not by the council. Given that community members have already voiced their concern on council power, this is something that is actually voted on by a referendum so everyone will be able to weigh in. Additionally, Commonwealth as an entity would not request any additional EDG from the treasury, but may help bond for treasury requests for other individuals (like SeedCode) and specific programs, like funding the incubator program. This should further clear up any questions that may have arisen in the eyes of the community due to Commonwealth employee's individual participation on the council. Furthermore, if there is a budget surplus from unclaimed validator transactions, this streaming payment would not use any of that. In other words, any surplus would be distributed to other projects working on the chain.
- **Automatically Phased Out Over Time:** Edgeware is disinflationary, that is, overall inflation is always trending downwards—from 20% to 16% in the second year. The treasury reward that would be included for Commonwealth decreases over time as a percentage of the total network over time. Additionally, if the overall treasury reward gets decreased, then the proportion of the treasury that goes to Commonwealth.


We don't anticipate that we will be the only development team that would pull from the streaming reward. In the future, streaming rewards may directly go towards funding popular treasury "programs" such as the on-chain incubator as well. We invite any and all comments on this potential proposal, we’re excited to continue earning the community’s trust.
