We're excited to open a conversation on release naming for Edgeware. As many networks have conveniently named releases that help users understand what versions they are interacting with, we want to take the opportunity to create a process- and a game, for selecting these. To begin, we're advocating for the use of international city names to track Edgeware's progress.

_**Why are we choosing cities?**_

- Cities are bottom-up, resilient and emergent structures.
- They are also cooperative collectives and economies.
- Cities grow over time.
- They welcome all new-comers.
- City names are memorable, accessible and inspiring.

## The Naming Tournament
We're proposing that we hold 2 rounds of signaling polls where 1-account-1-vote applies, starting with 3 4-choice polls of the cities found below. The top winner of each set continues onto the next until the order according to most winning is discovered. The higher the # of accounts voting for that release, the later the named release, ensuring that our favorite names represent the most advanced versions of Edgeware.  While this does open the door for sybil attacks via 1-a-1-v, the matter is thankfully more fun than serious. 

Barring any issues, we aim to launch this process this coming **Tuesday,  June 23. **
