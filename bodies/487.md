OK! So, as I promised in the Edgeware Brand Working Group, here is a little text summarizing my thoughts regarding what should be done in the near future, in regards with the Edgeware brand.

## Website

The website really needs to be refreshed. And the reason why I think so is mainly because if you currently go on the website, you will just think of Edgeware as again, another smart contract blockchain. 

The problem is that there are tens, if not hundreds of smart contract blockchains out there, which all failed. So how is Edgeware different. How is it not going to fail?

You can't convince people that Edgeware is not going to fail, if you bring the arguments to the table our competitors use. 

And the arguments our competitors bring to the table are pretty simple: scalability, interoperability, decentralization, security,...

But guess what? People don't care about that. What people care about is how this product is going to change their lives for the better. How is Edgeware going to improve your life? How is it different from all the other blockchains out there?

So, how should the website look like?

## How the website should look like

In my opinion, the main page should be very minimal. A bit like [Ethereum](https://ethereum.org)'s website. It needs to be very minimal, and people need to quickly understand what Edgeware is about.

So in my opinion, the "key ideas", "lockdrop", "edgeware stack", and even "about Commonwealth labs" parts should be removed. 

The only thing on the page, should be a little paragraph, ideally just a phrase, summarizing what Edgeware is about. Like on Ethereum's website. 

Just under, there should be 4 links. 

### Use Edgeware

The first link should bring users to a page where they can learn more about using Edgeware, which projects are currently building Edgeware,... Essentially, the Edgeware ecosystem. While this will likely be very little currently, it will hopefully grow as time goes. [MakerDAO](https://https://makerdao.com/en/ecosystem)'s website does a really good job at this, explaining where you can use DAI.

On top of that, there needs to be a list of reasons why Edgeware is better from a user perspective than other blockchains. **And these reasons are not because it has great performances, or because it uses WASM, or because it's based on top of Polkadot**. People don't care about that. What people care about, is how it's actually going to improve their lives. 

This is the reason why if you going on the [iPhone SE](https://https://www.apple.com/iphone-se/)'s website, all the features Apple lists, are actionnable things, which are actually going to improve the lives of their users. 

Regarding the chip for example, Apple explains it like: "So everything feels fluid, whether you’re launching apps, playing the latest games, or exploring new ways to work and play with augmented reality."

Then regarding the portrait mode: "The advanced camera system with A13 Bionic brings you Portrait mode, which artfully blurs the background to put the focus on your subject — even when you’re taking selfies."

And finally a last example regaring depth control: "Depth Control.A simple slider lets you blur the background as much or as little as you like — before or after you take the photo."

Like you can see in the three examples above, Apple definitely understands that people don't care what software they used to make for example this depth control possible. They know that people don't care how they made their chip more powerful. No, what people care about, is that thanks to the depth control, they can easily improve their selfie skills, and that thanks to the new chip with a fancy name, they will be able to do even more cool things on their iPhone. That's all they need to know. 

Well it's exactly the same for blockchain. People don't care about WASM, they don't care about the Edgeware stack, and they don't care about Polkadot (it should be mentioned that Edgeware is built on top of Polkadot though, more on that later). 

What they care about in this case, is that Edgeware has a top-notch stablecoin, an active DeFi ecosystem whith **LOW** gas fees, and dApps they can use in their daily lives. [Blockstack](https://https://app.co/)'s ecosystem website does a really good job at briefly showcasing the best apps built on top of their system, which you can use by creating a Blockstack account.

### Develop on Edgeware

I'm not a developer, so I don't know precisely what is needed to develop on Edgeware, and what makes Edgeware interesting to use from a developer-perspective, however, it's likely exactly the same thing as for people wanting to use Edgeware.

If you go on [Apple's developer website](https://https://developer.apple.com/discover/) (I'm not an Apple fanboy by the way), it's just that they do a really good job at marketing. There is a reason why they are on of the most profitable companies in the world.) you will notice that again, they list actual advantages for developers choosing building on top of Apple's platforms. 

Again, you don't want to say "We are using WASM!", no, instead you want to say "Thanks to our WASM integration, you can build extraordinary things on our revolutionary blockchain, while using the language you are the most comfortable with". 

There should also be a link to a page which briefly explains everything needed for a developer to start building a project on top of Edgeware. A kind of "From 0 to 1" course.

### Govern on Edgeware

Governance is **incredibly** important for Edgeware. In fact, it's what makes Edgeware so interesting, as everyone can participate, and everyone has a voice.

What people care about in this case, is that **with Edgeware, people truly have a voice**. **Edgeware is the blockchain where you matter, where YOU, yes you, can actually improve the world by sharing your ideas with others, and participating in governance**. 

**Edgeware is the internet, reinvented**. **Edgeware is the internet as it was supposed to be, an internet where your voice matters, where you can change the world, wherever you are, and whoever you are.**

That's something which is actually going to improve people's live. Why? Mainly because with Bitcoin, or Ethereum, people have nothing to say. There is no governance. Ethereum is made the way the Ethereum Foundation wants it to be made. That's it. While with Edgeware, you can actually participate, and you may literally make history by proposing a change which will make Edgeware the new go-to blockchain.

### Validate on Edgeware

The final link on the main page, should bring people to a page explaining why they should be creating validators, and running nodes.

Again, don't list why the Edgeware stack, nor how Edgeware is great because it uses WASM. People don't care about that, remember?

What people care about, is if, and how much rewards they will earn. It's how light-weigh the infrastructure required to run a validator/node is required,....

I haven't found any project doing this well currently, so I can't give examples here unfortunately...
