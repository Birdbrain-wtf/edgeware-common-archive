**Fund updates + strategy: **

After receiving the EDG, the advisors have converted the EDG into stablecoin (USDT) to shield the fund from any negative market related fluctuations of the initial capital, we chose USDT opposed to other stablecoins due to universal accessibility across CEX’s and DEX’s. 
We have determined that the fund will seek exposure across 4 ecosystems w/ specific sectors: 
1. Ethereum
2. Defi 
3. Money market
4. Synthetics
5. Dex’s
6. Metaverse / Gaming. 
7. Polkadot
8. Cosmos
9. TBD


![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/i_o1_UQuJ8QOo2avcrCn5OFkhUgGcz2KyZ_QmogGJHGZICKvq1CJUU_eHHwOb-QPoIM5SGc62Kvd2gZ9H2-pKGam9x5YLHb8-KYSvbkzpYzlxfBD1VBKA3ZWQGGT4NICUcxVTYMM7M5FfBKzPw)

Each ecosystem will have specific projects that are to be allocated to each sector; our preliminary allocation across ecosystems is: Ethereum (40%), Polkadot (20%), Cosmos (30%), TBD (10%).

The allocation into these particular sectors is due to the overall reasoning by the advisors that these sectors demonstrate the greatest possibility of growth within the time frame of the fund and are in line with the value / network effect investing strategy of the fund. Specifically our approach in the Ethereum ecosystem is to allocate into leading projects within the respective sectors (money market, synthetics, dex’s), and create a networked effect investing approach, the easiest of which is a cross-protocol token farming. Polkadot is longer term allocations, in that due to the nascent nature of DeFi, allocation to the native system token is to see the token price increase from the current price and seek value appreciation. This approach can be stacked upon by studying the DeFi projects that are being developed and pursuing yield farming strategies. Cosmos having more of a DeFi sector would qualify for yield farming along w/ cross protocol token farming but done so with the purpose to qualify for airdrops that can be farmed. 


_Note: The Cosmos approach is still being formulated, however the advisors have identified the preliminary allocation of token positions to be taken to qualify; however the ideal optimization across assets will be determined as this strategy is employed. _
