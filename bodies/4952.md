# Edgeware & Kabocha locations for contributor events, meetups and hackathons

## **Introduction**
Edgeware first launched its mainnet in February 2020 after the [2019 lockdrop](https://medium.com/commonwealth-labs/whats-in-a-lockdrop-194218a180ca) that created a broad and fair distribution that put EDG into the hands of a decentralised network of members. 

Back then, holding EDG was a bet on the network becoming a parachain and perhaps even more so, as a hedge to Ethereum's scaling challenges. 

Fast forward over two years and Edgeware is slowly and steadily evolving into a grass-roots ecosystem, funding public projects through innovation grants and providing an open door to talented contributors from around the world to learn 'applied Substrate' in an active community setting. 

As the project has evolved, there has been a near constant request for 'events' and ''marketing', however we need to stay true to our core strengths and values, making the best use of our ever expanding network of contributors to create something unique and differentiated that builds value from the ground up. 

## Our approach

As detailed in our recent [network strategy overview,](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/4872-decent-partners-edgeware-kabocha-structure-revenue-and-growth-strategy) and following [Twitter thread](https://twitter.com/decentpartners/status/1526895852400295936), we are positioning Edgeware as a _public innovation fund (PIF)_ stewarded by a decentralised association of members and Kabocha as the follow on, advancing interesting ideas, concepts and technologies, taking a share of any future revenues via service and licensing agreements that go on to deliver commercial success. 

As part of our expanding this strategy and our [Network Services offering](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/3650-decent-partners-an-introduction-to-network-services), Decent Partners has been investing in and building out an emerging and globally distributed network of locations that will form the initial basis for a growing calendar of Edgeware and Kabocha's IRL events for contributors who continue to advance the network. 

The three locations below will bring together contributors from across a wide range of disciplines, but they are just the start, we hope that other locations will emerge as the community grows, allowing the network to offer more and more benefits and opportunities to allow people to do their best work. 

In time your Kabocha Seed will become your profile and access key to these events, continuing our ambitions to build out a network of products and services that can have sustained impact in the real world and in a real economy and connect back into our emerging accounting, financial and organisational systems. 

## **UK - Portico Gallery in London**

A 10,000 square foot studio and events space in London that is at the heart of the local community, providing classes, practice space and cultural events across the year. 

This is the location we are shooting much of the upcoming community film im and will be the space we begin to scale out the project's content production and has been home to many music projects for emerging artists as well as large commercial shoots with the likes of Channel 4, Nike and Spotify.

## **France - Chalet Belin in St Jean D'Aulps**

Chalet Belin is located in the small alpine village of St Jean D'Aulps in the French Alps and benefits from its own water supply, alongside a soon to be completed outdoor gym and yoga/viewing platform. 

Rebuilt over the last three years, the space has been designed to cater to small groups of around 12 people, with a focus on bringing together distributed teams for meetups, project development or hackathons in a healthy, energising and peacef environment. That does not mean the [EDG Pizzadrop](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/3679-pizza-airdrop) cannot manifest for real up in the mountains... 

Our aim from the start has been to use the space and the surrounding activities - some of the world's best hiking, mountain biking, running, cycling, rafting and climbing to give an exciting creative space for creative teams.

## **Brazil - Under development, Garopaba**

Still under development, this location is situated in Garopaba, one of the most beautiful parts of southern Brazil and will be another space for Edgeware and Kabocha's contributors to visit, work and collaborate. 

Set on the hillside, looking out over the Atlantic and near some of Brazil's best surf beaches it has been in active development since 2016. The first house has been developed using environmentally sustainable practices and the growing area is now providing many of Garopaba's restaurants and suppliers with regeneratively grown produce as well as its own Kombucha (Kabocha Kombucha anyone) :) 

We are continuing to develop the location and will be creating a larger space for teams to visit, collaborate and learn about agro-forestry, renewable energy and of course surf, as well as a small festival of ideas '_Everywhen_' which is due to launch this year.

## Proposals 

This document is our project overeview, from here we will bring individual proposals to the network. 

Beginning with our location in the French Alps, we will put a series of proposals to the community, funding meetups, events and hackathons for contributors and EDG and KAB members, giving a focal point to the communities activities and ambitions and advancing our shared interests through a unique, grass roots and values aligned approach that will continue to set us apart in the Substrate ecosystem and beyond. 

These proposals will also be documented through audio and video, providing foundations for an emerging EDG / KAB content ecosystem of educational, inspiring and entertaining material.
