# Decent Partners - 2021/22 roadmap, deliverables and funding

## **Intro**

This document summarises Decent Partners' key contributions and developments in Edgeware's ecosystem and a brief overview of the strategy going forward which seeks to double down on Edgeware's decentralised incubator model and position in the emerging DotSama ecosystem. 

## Background

Decent Partners' is a UK based limited company focused on developing a '[Network Services](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/3650-decent-partners-an-introduction-to-network-services)' model within the [Substrate](https://substrate.dev) ecosystem of solo-chains, parachains and relays.

Network Services iterates the existing incubation and partnership model such as that employed by the [Web3 Foundation](https://web3.foundation/) or more traditional startup incubators and corporate innovation studios such as [Prehype](https://prehype.com), towards a democratic, borderless and public on-chain structure. 

Our core focus is the creation, retention and exploitation of novel IP, to drive recurring real world services and licensing revenues that can be shared more transparently, fluidly and equitably to contributors across the full lifecycle of a project. 

We call Substrate based projects that institute this design philosophy _Network Publics_. 

Our mission is to unlock creative potential in anyone, anywhere. 

## Edgeware, Kusama and Polkadot

Although not immediately obvious the three oldest Substrate chains are organically evolving from their shared codebases and genesis distributions into a curious family that each offer different pros/cons, cultures and interests. 

**Polkadot** 
The most valuable, the slowest moving, with the largest public profile, but the most centralised when it comes to governance and contributions.

**Kusama** 
More decentralised and risk taking than Polkadot, but inheriting the same genesis distribution. 

**Edgeware**
The most decentralised at launch owing to 2019's lockdrop distribution, but with a large share still held by Parity/W3F.

## Adding Kabocha to the mix

As and when [Kabocha](https://kabocha.network) becomes [a community incubated](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussions/Kabocha) parachain bonded to Kusama's relay, it will add a final step in the development lifecycle, demonstrating that public chains beginning in Edgeware can migrate upstream.   

Understanding this emerging hierarchy we see an opportunity to develop a grass-roots incubator of fairly distributed, sustainably funded and transparently governed Network Publics that aim to prioritise and scale contributions over speculation.

This can in turn create a nursery of decentralised parachain candidates that can begin their lifecyle as R&D projects in Edgeware, before progressing to pilots in Kabocha before onboarding as fully fledged networks in Kusama, before reaching Polkadot as they economically mature. 

## Network Public vs For Profit vs Common Good Parachains

Parachains onboarding to Kusama and Polkadot are currently divided into a few categories...![](https://commonwealth-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/76aaef44-882f-4162-a983-833da4991694.1653051374086)
For the sake of simplification, we can distill the above into two more general categories. 

- **For Profit** such as Acala/Karura, Astar/Shiden, Moonbeam/Moonriver
- [Common Good](https://polkadot.network/blog/common-good-parachains-an-introduction-to-governance-allocated-parachain-slots/) such as Statemint/Statemine and Encointer


We propose a third category: _Network Publics _that offer a third balancing category between purely profit driven, and purely common good.

Network Publics all share broad and fair distributions and include a range of iterations to the basic Substrate template in their treasury and governance architectures, alongside a value accrual and commercial model based on funding and retaining original IP, whose recurring revenues ensure long term alignment of communities and contributors even as the networks scale. 

## Network Services -> Network Publics

Our original definition of Network Services: 

_A network service provider onboards external talent, teams and projects to community networks - curating, communicating and formalising proposals that are then voted on by a decentralised network of token holders, before ensuring the careful deployment and management of approved funds, the successful delivery of agreed milestones alongside the timely reporting of issues, progress and lessons learned through the project. - [An introduction to Network Services](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/3650-decent-partners-an-introduction-to-network-services)_

- Network Services is now performing an ever expanding role across decentralised networks such as Edgeware:  


- Governance standards and processes
- Distributions and snapshots
- Incentive and mechanism design
- Identity and reputation 
- Communications
- Media production and distribution
- Events, meetups and hack weeks
- Listings & Partnerships
- Rights management 
- Legal & Compliance
- Regulatory engagement 


The result of this experience is the _Network Public_ parachain concept and the _Publik_ parachain template that seek to address three key problems:

**Sustainable funding**

- How can we sustainably fund creative projects that ensures teams and communities remain values aligned over the long term?


**Independent media**

- How can we create internet native public service media that provides a vital balancing force alongside advertising and subscription models?


**Network jurisdictions **

- How can we develop network native legal jurisdictions that can interface with the legacy system?


Each challenge is independent, but they are inter-related. 

By solving these problems we can create foundations for Network Publics - truly parallel and independent systems that can serve the people, drive real and recurring revenues from real world business cases and in turn address many of humanities greatest challenges, at the largest and smallest scales imaginable. 

## How Decent Partners develops Network Public in practice

Our incubation approach is entirely network native - onboarding external entities into Network Publics. 

- We use income from treasury proposals to fund operational overhead and to source new network talent.


- We develop proposals from a geographically distributed network of contributors.


- We steward them through the community process, on-chain governance and ensure projects are delivered.


- We bring funded projects to life through independent media projects akin to a (network) public service broadcaster. 


- We develop original IP based value accrual models that ensure long term alignment between networks and contributors.


- We return value to Network Publics through the creation, negotiation and collection of licensing and service revenues.
