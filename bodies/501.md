## Staking Rewards: EDG Integration and Marketing
Proposal for Edgeware Grant Program - June 24 2020

**About**
Staking Rewards, represented by Mirko Schmeidl, brings this proposal to fund the integration of the Edgeware staking environment into our platform at [https://www.stakingrewards.com/](https://www.stakingrewards.com/providers), an interactive guide to over 200 staking providers that lets users find staking opportunities and compare nomination/delegation targets. We are planning to integrate EDG in order to expand the functionality of the platform as well as to offer promotion to the Edgeware ecosystem that will ultimately grow it’s userbase and improve the staking situation, which has real security benefits. 

Edgeware currently has roughly 27% staked, far below it’s coded target of 80%, and also below the KSM total-staked of >60%. This makes the costs of attacks more possible by making them [cheaper and easier to execute, as this estimator tool shows.](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VlzTUDESbbfOggMRz3GyE9-VqR9MlOhNuoekBboKvLw/edit?usp=sharing)  Given the complexity of Substrate’s staking and economic functionality, our interfaces and guidance allow users to better understand the reasons to stake on Edgeware particularly. 


**Offer**
 
The integration of EDG will be completed presumably within 7 days of grant-approval, with the following content.

1. Full-featured Asset Profile for Edgeware with dynamic data fetching for all relevant metrics, FAQs and Charts (similar to https://www.stakingrewards.com/asset/tezos).
2. Profile being featured on top of the Main page (https://www.stakingrewards.com/) for at least 1 day.
3. Integration into the Staking Calculator under https://www.stakingrewards.com/calculator including individual reward calculations based on the setup and certain network assumptions.
4. Spotlight Article written by our Research Team (Breaking down Edgeware Core Principles and everything nominating, validating, inflation, slashing, reinvesting, dynamics and general guidance for stakers)
5. Article being featured on our Journal Frontpage (https://www.stakingrewards.com/journal) for 7 days
6. Listing of all Staking Providers / Validators that offer services for Edgeware with their own (verified) profile on Staking Rewards
7. Validators / Users can submit neutral Step-by-Step Staking Tutorials that will be published in our Journal
8. Details around how the rewards are calculated. (Negotiable)
