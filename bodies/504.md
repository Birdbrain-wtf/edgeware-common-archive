In a recent call with the Gantree team, I learned about their monitoring tool and future work extending it to a robust system for validators. In short, Gantree is building a node monitoring system that integrates with Substrate blockchains and provides individual and network level node statistics around node health, uptime, and more. I think it's a great project to onboard into our ecosystem and have an interesting idea how we do so.

## Service Partners
Gantree's service will likely be a monthly subscription service paid out based on the number of nodes you integrate. They may offer unlimited node service for a fixed monthly cost and small additional cost per node. This makes it quite appealing to fund entirely through the Treasury for ALL Edgeware node operators. In this sense, we could fund operations of Gantree for every Edgeware node operator, funded by the Treasury.

I see this potential partnership as a network-wide service partner as we could potentially negotiate for a network-wide agreement. This would benefit all current and future Edgeware node operators and validators by providing them tools, paid for by the Treasury, to help them manage and understand both their current node infrastructure as well how to improve their infrastructure.

One potential way we do this, is by utilising the streaming reward or scheduled payments to Gantree for the course of 6 months or a year. Defining a service agreement would allow us to pay once and for all potentially for a service that any node operator of Edgeware will benefit from. This model sounds super appealing for a blossoming chain like Edgeware and is a novel way to both support and integrate service providers like Gantree into the Edgeware ecosystem.

Would love to hear if people find this interesting and if we would like to continue the discussion further with Gantree.
