## EDG_TP_10
Commonwealth Labs and Shankar Warang are proposing to fund Shankar in the role of Community Manager for a series of 3 1-month milestones for a total of the EDG equivalent of 2400 USD/month,  7200 USD total. 

## About This Proposal
Commonwealth Labs in collaboration with the proposed hire, Shankar Warang, bring this proposal to fund a contractorship for Shankar. This proposal follows weeks of interviews, scopes of work, and engagement with the community. Commonwealth Labs is confident that Shankar is a good fit for this role, and proposes a 3-month, 3-milestone grant for this work. 

## Overview
Apart from just moderation and assistance, Community Management plays an important role in the growth in engagement, product/brand awareness, developer relations, event planning, and a little bit in operations. In order to achieve long term sustainable engagement, we need to allocate our focus on multiple major community-building aspects.
