Hi all. So had brief chat with Dillon in the EDG design / brand telegram group. I'm a participant in the lockdrop and am fascinated by the opportunity around DAOs with sustainable funding that can help coordinate global talent pools across a wide range of disciplines.

I am also a long time member of the Decred community and have been focused most recently on solving many of the issues around brand building, narrative development and community culture that Edgeware are currently discussing. Something that Decred has been testing ideas around since 2018. 

For those not aware, Decred was inspired by issues the core development team saw with Bitcoin.

1. Centralisation of miners and overall security (solved with a hybrid PoW/PoS system) (60% of block rewards go to miners, 30% to those staking who also validate blocks)
2. Sustainable funding - the remaining 10% pool to a [treasury](https://explorer.dcrdata.org/address/Dcur2mcGjmENx4DhNqDctW5wJCVyT3Qeqkx). This will soon be fully [decentralised](https://proposals.decred.org/proposals/c96290a2478d0a1916284438ea2c59a1215fe768a87648d04d45f6b7ecb82c3f). 
3. Governance - they created the Politeia proposal system and [contractor management system](https://github.com/decred/contractor-mgmt). 


I wrote this [Forks In The Road](https://github.com/monsieurbulb/forksintheroad/blob/master/Decred_forks_in_the_road.md) paper about the challenges the project has faced and thought it might be useful to share here. Chris Burniske (an investor in both Decred and Polkadot projects) shared his thoughts [here](https://twitter.com/cburniske/status/1265104775550570498?s=20). 

My belief is that coordination of sustainable on-chain treasuries and cross-chain governance is a necessary precursor to tacking the tribalism present in crypto - enabling communities to work together for the common good, rather than engaging in endless (and pointless) spats. 

Would love any thoughts.
