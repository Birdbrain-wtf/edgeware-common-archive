In short, we think we can quickly bootstrap network adoption on the developer side by creating an on chain incubator. We think this incubator can should be focused on two key use case cases--DeFi and crypto social. As a community we have potential distribution to over 13m users, as well as people that want to experiment with different new projects. There is an existing playbook for distribution and building demand in DeFi that we can also learn from. On the legal side, Edgeware could hold tokens as well as equity in different "real world" entities by creating corporate form (a Delaware LLC).

We think this could be seeded by the Edgeware Treasury ($250k - $500k) to create a legally linked entity. We have also chatted with the Polkdot Treasury members, and think that this something that could see wide spread Polkadot ecosystem adoption.

A future form of builDAO could allow people to add assets along a bonding curve, creating a "permanent capital" incubator or fund.
