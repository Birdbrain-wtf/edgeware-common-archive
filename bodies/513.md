There is a phrase:

> [A rising tide lifts all boats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_rising_tide_lifts_all_boats)

It is a term associated with the idea that an improved economy will benefit all participants.

Right now there is an opportunity for a certain group of projects to benefit from the (crypto) mainstreaming of DAOs with **sustainable funding**, **governance via proposal systems** and **decentralised contributions.**

This narrative is well underway as evidenced by the last couple of months hype/speculation around 'governance tokens' and more specifically the arrival of [Substrate](https://substrate.dev/) based chains, all of which have their own treasuries, governance and multi-million $ treasuries.

For most people, the first generation of DAOs primarily consisted of DAOstack, Moloch and Aragon - an area that has failed to live up to adoption expectations.

All have all been donation / individual based and therefore are not sustainable in nature since there is no perpetual funding mechanism of the sort pioneered by [Decred](https://decred.org) that gives the ability to invest consistently over the long term.

The number of projects with self-sustaining on-chain treasuries will increase exponentially over the next 12 months with [Cosmos SDK](https://cosmos.network/sdk) being the base for Aragon's [own chain](https://aragon.org/chain).

This also doesn't include existing projects with treasuries / founder rewards such as DASH and ZCash, whose issues with transparency and impact have been noted [elsewhere.](https://medium.com/@richardred/observations-of-the-dash-treasury-dao-c94231b2b5c4)

## **The tribalism dilemma**
Tokens naturally inspire tribalism - since greed / price discussion dominates the conversation and actually distracts from much of the progress made in the space.

However, the broader shared goal for credible project teams is in building a fairer, more decentralised future that offers users better control and coordination of their information, money and communities.

Put simply, crypto teams by and large share more in common than the crypto-twitter flame wars and trolling would have you believe.

It is also obvious that there is already a lot of duplication of effort as each team attempts to solve the same awareness and adoption issues, especially around community building, governance engagement, proposal templates and visions. This issue will continue to compound as the tech matures.

Decred has the longest running formalised governance in crypto, however it has struggled to build bridges to other projects and has become somewhat isolated, something that I discussed in the [Forks in The Road](https://github.com/monsieurbulb/forksintheroad/blob/master/Decred_forks_in_the_road.md) paper.

The reasons are numerous and you can also regard this as simply the result of developers being focused on other priorities and the way the treasury is guarded as well as it has not been in Decred's DNA to chase the partnership announcement gravy train.

So is there **another way** to think about this issue - one that turns a systemic problem into an opportunity for the space and for the wider world that also stays true to principles of transparency and decentralisation?

## **The On-Chain Alliance**
Ok so the name is a bit of a nod to the Enterprise Ethereum Alliance, but in principle the idea would be to research a range of core issues facing fully on-chain economies that that are setting out to escape centralisation vectors and to create a 'Meta DAO' to address them that benefits from ongoing investment from a range of projects.

## **How it works**
Chains commit some funding (say 2%) of funds as some sort of opt-in **public duty** and others are also invited to contribute.

By decentralising on chain treasuries you begin to get something akin to a global public good fund...

## **Type of projects**
On a small scale, the open source research of [cryptocommons.cc](https://cryptocommons.cc) by social scientist richardred is a good example of a net positive for the whole space, but since it was totally funded by DCR, many other projects might have been suspicious or it didn't get the exposure it deserved.

## **Why do it?**
- It drives the narrative towards working for DAOs rather than speculating on tokens. 
- It focuses public attention on, on-chain economies and provides clear differentiators. 
- It has compelling public good narrative that will resonate with many in the space and beyond.
- There is the chance to raise awareness both individually and as a group.
- Projects save money and don't have lots of duplicated effort across a range of categories.
- There would be the chance to bridge communities and create deeper governance engagement. 
- Project collaborations - there is a natural gravity created to bring different teams and communities together in service of a common goal which will naturally foster new ideas and projects.


## **Style / Tone**
Monero parodied the Ethereum Enterprise Alliance in [this short clip](https://mea.business/) - it's funny, I think we can do much better in terms of OCA (On-Chain Alliance). 

Right now I think crypto has lost an edge when it comes to cultural connection - too much financial engineering and technical chat, not enough genuine creation/cultivation of popular culture that builds bridges through entertainment, but has an educational message that can be dialed up over time.  

For those of you old enough to remember, the fictional corporations created in [Robocop](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3LKQ3eX9pEg) (and [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8oJzfmWO3CU)), [The Running Man](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST_TUpblYz4) and [Starship Troopers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S-s4lEk91ng&t=17s) are interesting references. 

More recently [The Big Short](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=epb98OcFLZE) and [here ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A25EUhZGBws)took very complex issues with the existing financial system and made them entertaining.

Self-sustaining DAOs have the chance to solve many problems facing existing corporations - but there needs to be a smart way to tell this story.

I haven't touched on the technical implementation (e.g. how a cross-chain fund would be managed), other than high level so that's all for discussion. 

## **Notes.**
This is designed as a thought-starter but I think Edgeware is well positioned to help push something like this forward alongside Decred. Two generations of crypto working together is a story in of itself and plays well to the advantages of good governance. 

In general this sort of idea will generate momentum fairly quickly once a few are committed, since to not contribute will become something of a reputational risk, not to mention the benefits of being early to the party from an awareness perspective.

Getting it started is the hardest thing, but I think it can be done (barring technical / governance detail) and this would not be seen as competitive to each project's individual ambitions.

Looking forward to any thoughts / pushbacks.
