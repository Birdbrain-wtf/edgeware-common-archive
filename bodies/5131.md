## Deliverables 

**Minimal Edgeware Apps**
Users, both new and old, struggle to interact with the current Polkadot Apps interface due to it's focus on technical functionality; I would like to make a website that simplifies the UI and UX experience for Edgeware and potentially other substrate-based chains like Kabocha in the future. This new website would be a more minimal set of essential functions with a focus on user onboarding into the ‘Edgizen,’ the governing and staking community, and a standard set of holder functions.
- Manage and add Accounts 
- Send and receive tokens; 
- The ability to vote on proposals.
- The ability to delegate; 
- The ability to stake. 

These are the features that most people use when interacting with the edgeware blockchain. This website would be bare-bones MVP and not much styling and CSS; this will come later. The goal is to get the must-have functionality working first, then style it later when we are happy with it in the future. Edgeware Apps would remain for ‘power users’ of the chain, proposal-creation, and other more advanced and involved functions.

Though I have not validated before, I would take this time to set one up and contribute to the Edgeware chain in this way. In addition, I would seek to achieve the bronze level tier outlined in the [Infrastructure Provider Strategy.](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16Yau4dPJBEPYaJwO8qXWVt9YNmvlRaPOfTDyFmTx2XM/edit#) ([https://docs.google.com/document/d/16Yau4dPJBEPYaJwO8qXWVt9YNmvlRaPOfTDyFmTx2XM/edit#](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16Yau4dPJBEPYaJwO8qXWVt9YNmvlRaPOfTDyFmTx2XM/edit#)) and explore the treasury staking model discussed by Thom Ivy, where qualified Community Validators can pursue Treasury support in nominations.

**Ongoing Research into Hummingbot Market Maker**
This is a bot that conducts market-making services on exchanges and was recommended to us by our current Market Maker. Ideally, this would replace market makers that we already pay to provide the service on exchanges or can be documented to help any community member run this bot for EDG. This bot could potentially be good enough strategy wise to both decentralize our market-making efforts and has the potential to save the DAO funds, preventing large liquidations to pay Market Maker services. At the very least, Humminbot can be a backup if we lose a market maker adding to the DAO's self-sufficiency.
