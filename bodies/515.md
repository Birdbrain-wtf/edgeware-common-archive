[@Thom Ivy](/edgeware/account/iJFChQEmukA7CNckr7TLZBjp4QxMxugEnzUVjceaJbPK9Sp) has done a really great kickstarting the [Brand](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/372-brand-working-group) and [Governance Working Groups](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/370-governance-working-group). They both feature a regular contributors in both chat and on calls. By being anchored by a two or three community champions and a few part time contributing members, these help Edgeware move focus on specific area—the Brand Working Group on _making Edgeware a meme_ and Governance Working Group on _improving Edgeware governance._

There are many ways that Edgeware still can grow. And creating working groups feels like a good bottoms-up way to do so. Any community member can create a chat room / creating a thread on CW to start a conversation with a topic. Hopefully the two ideas below are able to create enough social and financial incentives to also create _new_ working groups.

**Using a monthly treasury tip for “chairing' a working group and helping members. **

Ideally, working groups are able to follow up on ideas that they think are valuable. Sometimes this isn’t that glamorous. It takes time and effort to fostering conversation with a group, as well as empowering working group members to follow up on their ideas. This usually is in the form of a small project / grant. A chair or helpful member finds a team to fulfill the grant (or completes project), and getting funds from the treasury if necessary, and keeping track of progress.

I’d like to propose that each working group nominates a one or two member to receive a standard tip from the Edgeware Treasury, something like **15000 EDG (~$300) **for recognizing the effort that the member has put in. Hopefully this is a light touch process that councillors will just be able to approve quickly.

**Recognizing working group contributors with on-chain roles. **

It’s also important to socially recognize these community contributors. It would be great to set on-chain roles / identities for WG creators and members. Hopefully this is a community driven version of verified accounts on Twitter. This would be really cool as the data gets integrated into a UI like Commonwealth or Polkadot Apps, and could even lead to some special admin privileges on forums or TG groups.

In terms of implementation. This could take a few forms, but probably could be a society or collective, or a new judgement / remark on the identity pallet. 

**New Working Groups**

As Edgeware matures it’s important to think about both user and developer adoption. Some ideas for different working groups.

Some other ideas for developer focused working groups. If anyone is interested in chairing one of these working groups definitely respond on this thread!

1. _Location Specific Working Groups: _Ethereum did a really great job in helping foster meet-up groups. It would be great to see a _New York or Asia Working Group._ These could help host meetups, talk to teams and users in those areas.
2. DeFi: Getting DeFi on Edgeware. What liquidity incentives to provide? What applications can we support really well?
3. Social: Getting social apps on Edgeware. Ethereum is a bit too expensive. How can we natively support interesting use cases.
