**The following is an overview of our active service roles, functions and responsibilities.. **


**Community Moderator:** Decentralized community building
- Community management in social network (i.e., Discord, Elements, Telegram)
- Meeting engagement (Presentations, Project Updates, Perspectives)


**Content Moderator: **Decentralized community service
- Content Contributor (Research, Learning, Quality Assurance, Financials, Business Intelligence) 
- Meeting engagement (Presentations, Project Updates, Perspectives)


**Production Moderator:** Decentralized community productions 
- Production management (Preproduction, Post Production)
- Meeting engagement (Presentations, Project Updates, Perspectives)


**Network Service Provider: **Decentralized community recruitment
- Recruiter for chain personnel (Service Roles, Partnerships, Affiliates)   
- Meeting engagement (Presentations, Project Updates, Perspectives)


**Product Steward:** Decentralized Project Auditing
- Product management (Drive Milestones, Overcome Roadblocks)   
- Meeting engagement (Presentations, Project Updates, Perspectives)


**Proposal Curator: **Decentralized community project funding 
- Proposal process support (Scope, Audit,, Pre-Image )
- Meeting engagement (Presentations, Project Updates, Perspectives)


**Operations Moderator:**
- Processes and services administer (Registry, Frameworks, Business Development)
- Meeting engagement (Presentations, Project Updates, Perspectives)


**Technical Moderator: **Decentralized upgrades to our smart contract platforms.  
- Technical upgrades, Testing/Debug, Audits and maintenance (EVM, WASM, Pallets, Websites, Forum, Interface)  
- Meeting engagement (Presentations, Project Updates, Perspectives)




*This list of decentralized service roles is an active list is dynamically updated by a citizen assigned a Community Moderator role. The service roles listed are established on the weekly community calls. Commitments for each responsibility is set by the Contributor and agreed upon by the EDG & KAB community through democracy when onboarding.


A detailed breakdown of each service role and the community expectations will be available in the frameworks section of our forum and under the community section in our wiki.
