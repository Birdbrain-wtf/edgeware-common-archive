## **Summary**

This proposal is to cover completed and ongoing research and development (R&D) contributions by Richard Welsh (RW), managing partner at Decent Partners (DP), the current lead [Network Services](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/3650-decent-partners-an-introduction-to-network-services) provider to Edgeware and soon Kabocha.

## **Background**

RW joined the Edgeware community over 2 years ago after contributing to the lockdrop. 

He arrived at a point when the community had reached a deadlock, unable to move forward with governance decisions, owing to disagreements between the founding team and large holders. 

He began contributing by mediating between the two main groups, before helping to advance the project as a founding member of the [Brand Working Group](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/643-brand-working-group-proposal-updatelive-council-motion) (BWG) which later evolved into the [Edgeware Agency](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1112-proposal-edgeware-agency-funding) (EA). 

From October 2020 to March 2021 he/DP was paid fees for specific grants within the BWG which evolved to a monthly salary from the EA of $9000 per month for four months from April 1st until 31st July 2021. 

As with all Edgeware contributors these were self-created and directed roles covering a wide range of work across the emerging ecosystem within two different remuneration structures; project grants and monthly salary. 

In general the work could be classed as a mix of research, development and operational support, covering economic, organisational and cultural domains within Edgeware’s leaderless public network. 

After leaving EA, DP established the [Network Services](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/3650-decent-partners-an-introduction-to-network-services) model, a network native organisational structure designed to solve a clear challenge within on-chain governed networks such as Edgeware, namely incentivising the sourcing, development and management of new proposals and associated contributors. 

Since the first project incubated in this manner around 10 months ago, RW / DP have received network income solely through the 10% Network Service Fee (NSF) that is paid on approval of successful proposals. 

You can view these at the bottom of the document including each specific NSF.  

The total income from NSF in this period was **4,805,301 EDG**, or 0.007% of the network.

Around 50% of this income has been used to cover minimal operational overhead, the remains have been used to further develop new talent and projects, who are offered 0% loans, recoupable against future commercial revenues within the emerging Edgeware and Kabocha, R&D incubator and parachain accelerator model. 

## **Network Research & Development**

NSF constitutes one clear strand of work - namely acting as a specialist grass-roots talent recruiter or _Network Agent_, filtering, recruiting, onboarding and managing values aligned network talent from initial introductions, through the structuring and communication of projects and on-chain governance procedures, and then onto the management of funds and assuring successful completion of community initiatives. 

Parallel to this work, RW also delivers R&D within the Edgeware ecosystem, covering almost every aspect of the network, including governance standards, templates, processes, organisational structures, value capture mechanisms, emerging technology, partnerships, narrative and community development, and soon events, hackathons and exchange listings. 

This R&D has direct and continued application and value within Edgeware and its emerging ecosystem of chains, creating a platform for an evolving network communication strategy that in turn informs the recruitment of talent through an ever expanding Network Service model that seeks to offer the most exciting proposition and support structures to grass-roots talent focused on missionary, rather than mercenary work in truly decentralised public networks.

## **Remuneration**

Rather than seeking a salary, it feels better aligned to stay consistent with the current model of Edgeware’s £25k R&D grants - itself [a product of network R&D](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/1110-edgeware-grant-programme-structure-discussion), which will ensure a continuing output of work structured around quarterly grants, each producing research, analysis and strategy with applications within the ecosystem.

This approach enables a more predictable earning schedule, matched to clear outcomes and regular summaries that can develop into a valuable publishing operation in its own right.

## _**Edgeware, Kusama and Polkadot**_

_Although not immediately obvious, the three oldest Substrate chains are organically evolving from their shared codebases and genesis distributions into a curious family that each offer different pros/cons, cultures and interests._

_**Polkadot**_
_The most valuable, the slowest moving, with the largest public profile, but the most centralised when it comes to governance and contributions._

_**Kusama**_
_More decentralised and risk taking than Polkadot, but inheriting the same genesis distribution._

_**Edgeware**_
_The least valuable but most decentralised at launch owing to 2019's lockdrop distribution, and with a large share still held by Parity/W3F._

## _**Adding Kabocha to the mix**_
_As and when[ Kabocha](https://kabocha.network) becomes[ a community incubated](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussions/Kabocha) parachain bonded to Kusama's relay, it will add a final step in the development lifecycle, demonstrating that public chains first incubated in Edgeware can migrate upstream._

_Understanding this emerging hierarchy we see an opportunity to develop a grass-roots incubator of fairly distributed, sustainably funded and transparently governed Network Publics that aim to prioritise and scale contributions over speculation._

_This can in turn create a nursery of decentralised parachain candidates that can begin their lifecycle as R&D projects in Edgeware, before progressing to para-pilots in Kabocha before onboarding as fully fledged Network Publics in Kusama, before reaching Polkadot as they economically mature._

## **Next phase of Network R&D: Network Publics**

The following is in the early stages of R&D: 

- Network Publics offer a different set of assumptions, compromises, opportunities and incentives as compared to 'for profit' and 'common good' parachains. 
- Parachains must have a broad public distribution to qualify as a NP.
- NPs are offered longer parachain slots of 3-5 years, which differs to the 1 year of a for profit and the perpetual slots of common good. 
- The slots may have some sort of annual public review process that acts as a check on delivering against a network’s public mission/charter.
- NPs can seek funding from DOT/KSM treasuries like common good chains but unlike for profit chains, but under certain conditions that do not allow for the creation of casinos / thousands of L2 tokens.
- NPs can have their own network currencies, unlike common good chains, which use the relay tokens but like for profit tokens and have simple and consistent value accrual mechanisms that align funding to creative contributions with rights assigned from the project team.

## **Long term alignment**

The hope of this proposal is to receive good faith approval for an ongoing R&D role within Edgeware, subject to quarterly network updates and review. 

It is not a given that each quarter’s proposal should be approved, it is up to the community to continue to mandate the spend, however it is hoped that barring major issues with performance, this should be a long term deal which benefits both sides and offers some stability for the contributor. 

Profits from Network Services are reinvested in the sourcing and development of new projects and talent, ahead of R&D requests and are not used to further applications or projects not associated to the further development of the core networks. 

In general this takes the form of 0% interest loans that are recoupable later from the commercial revenues of a project that reaches Kabocha’s pilot stage.

## **Recoup**

As with other proposals, specific R&D that is funded initially by Edgeware but that is utilised or commercialised by Kabocha can be later recouped fully or partially through the existing current [good faith agreemen](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/3806-kabocha-funding-and-edgeware-recoup)t that allows for negotiation between the two networks.
