# Network Plan Proposal

Tagline: Edgeware can be the third relay chain in Substrate ecosystem. 

After the light and useful discussion we had with Shankar and Thom last week in the developer meeting, and discussing with filip today, here’s a summary network plan for Edgeware which can be achieved in 6 to 12 months: 


## **Edgeware Relay Chain with EdgeEVM parachain.** 

Edgeware Relay is much more potent in the long term as a multichain framework that has evm capability through its first parachain, than just a smart contract solo chain. 

We break down the most challenging upgrade in substrate, into 3 pieces: 

1. **Edgeware Core Upgrade** “Edgeware-v0.9.18 - upgrade of the core system pallets we use day to day; primitives, types and storage migrations. Switching off evm from the main chain, to be same as Polkadot and Kusama. 

2. **Relay Flip** “relayflip-v0.9.18” - add relay functionality; crowdloan switched off, and all parachains installed through governance (details to follow). 

3. **EdgeEVM parachain** “paraevm-v0.9.19” - the first of Edgeware’s parachains, EdgeEVM. (details to follow). 

**FAQ:** - **Edgeware has too low market cap to be a relay and secure parachains.** - Yes, if we were running outside projects parachains then this would be a concern. However adding relay and running our own parachain(s) makes no difference to Edgeware’s security. 

- **will that mean EdgeEVM has its own cryptocurrency?** Not necessarily, EdgeEVM would use EDG. 


- **What about become a parachain on Polkadot?** we can still do this, but the EdgewareRelay can stay a relay and preserve its historical blocks. 


- **why be a relay chain?** the short answer is why not? multichain essentialism is a better design than single chain maximalism, aka an all-you-can-eat smart contract blockchain with DApps. This is why a lot of us are here in substrate world to be freed from smart contracts and build custom chains rather than DApps. 


- **what about the current evm history?** as mentioned by Shankar last week, we can take a snapshot of ECEP and transfer it to the parachain.





**### Resolution** 
There are also people divided in Edgeware whether it should be a smart contract evm chain or focus more on multichain, with this design it resolves all camps.

### What are the other benefits? 

- The projects we fund and incubate can be become parachains in our relay network. 
- We can integrate parachains through governance rather than make chains go through the rigmarole of crowdloan process. These ideas can be explored and exprimented with, becoming the focus of discussion. 
- We have an opportunity to be more successful as an interoperable multichain network. As it stands, Polkadot and Kusama have parachains that are each separate communities, with different goals, cultures and values. Thus communication between chains are limited.
