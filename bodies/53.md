Sept 25, 2019 Written by Meghan Michel and Thom Ivy

Berlin Blockchain Week just happened, and SF Blockchain Week is coming up. These are exciting opportunities for the blockchain tech community to come together, collaborate, and learn. We want to propose a similar opportunity for the Edgeware and Commonwealth.im community: EdgeCon 2019. EdgeCon would be an open, inclusive conference to connect in-person with others in this group, as well as other individuals with any sort of stake or interest in learning about on-chain governance, Edgeware, and the people involved in the community.

In the spirit of collaborative planning, we've assembled a 'menu' that we'd like your opinions on for priorities for Edgeware's first big gathering.

The conference schedule could include:

- Breakfast networking events
- Workshops and
- Roundtable discussions on different governance issues
- Guest speakers
- Q&A’s with the founders of Commonwealth Labs
- A happy hour,
- ..and more.

With more detailed proposals and votes of agreement, we could, as a community, provide treasury funding for different parts of EdgeCon. While bigger conferences, like Etherium’s DevCon or their annual hackathon in Denver, gather crowds from around 2,000 to more than 5,000 participants, because of how new Edgeware is, and the geographically diverse population of the Polkadot ecosystem, EdgeCon would likely begin more manageable; maybe around 200-300 attendees with strong planning. 

We could take submissions (RFP!) or gather quotes on a space to host the Con, consider conference themes, and panel or workshop topics. Community members could submit proposals for guest speakers, or to be speakers themselves - we encourage any and all ideas or contributions.


Supporting and Sourcing Organizers

We could source organizers from within the Edgeware community channels, including [Commonwealth.im](http://commonwealth.im) would be key to the success of this project, which is more likely to succeed wth a financial incentive to help build the event. This might also be a good use-case for a no-loss lottery-like PoolTogether to fund initial planning (we all fund and the interest is given to the winner, in this case, the planner.)


Scholarships

We could also explore funding “scholarships,” similar to GitCoin’s SKALE scholarship for Devcon5 this year - ie, if there is a particular party who wants to attend EdgeCon but has some financial barrier, they could make their case and be funded by the community to come to the conference. These accessibility features go a long way in a highly technical field, they help build new ladders into the industry!
Sponsorships


Sponsorships

As a group, we could also discuss, propose, and vote on the possibility of the sponsorship for the event. For other conferences of a similar nature, like the Blockchain Connect Conference held this past January in SF, sponsorship bought a package that might include everything from WeChat mentions to VIP tickets to a Keynote Speech. While our community probably wouldn’t benefit from sponsorship that results in programming being influenced by the sponsor's desires rather than those of the group, we could explore sponsorship options that would be more community-driven and would not result in control given to any single donor. 

This should be an event owned, run, and enjoyed by the Edgeware community. Sponsorship shouldn’t get in the way of that, and to that end, if we agree as a group on accepting sponsorship, we should keep the net amount contributed below 50% - or much lower. Another possibility for offsetting treasury costs could be a participation fee, yet one more topic we can decide on as a community.

SF Blockchain Week could be a good space to meet with potential organizers or otherwise hear your thoughts on what EdgeCon 2019 could look like- look forward to an Edgeware meetup where we define this opportunity further and hear comments in person.

 Let us know if you want to connect on this if you will be there! This would be a first-of-its-kind event - a DAO hosting it's own conference! It would be great to pull something together that the whole community is excited about. There is a lot of room for discussion here, and we would love to get your feedback.
