# Summary 

Placeholder DOrg is collective that aim to experiment in immersive experiences for the music industry, leveraging Kabocha and Edgeware blockchain.

# Partners 

**Mahogany** - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kr0vuvEyrtE One of the leading global platforms for music content and live activations, with operations in the UK, US and Australia - they have created original content with the likes of Billie Eilish, Florence + Machine, Rag n Bone Man, George Ezra and hundreds more to a global audience of over 1.5M fans.

Key member from Mahogany: Mark

**New Futures** - A creative collective focussed on immersive experiences and blockchain Technology. New-Futures focuses on attention catching content and the places to experience it. We are stepping into a new paradigm of content immersion. Placeholder labs will be the first of its kind, a Web 3 music (consumption) experience - Living memorabilia. Utilising the unique properties/characteristics/utility of NFTs - not as verified JPeg art, but as tickets that contain verified music memorabilia. 

Beej - > "We strongly believe the future of the metaverse will be built in Unreal Engine, we aim to onboard  artists and musicians into this space via a familiar pathway. The performances will be a virtual world, projected in a real world. Available to be experienced IRL. Filmed for Youtube. This approach leaves us with the option of expanding this performance into the metaverse. (We are metaverse ready)."

_Key member from New-futures: Beej_

Decentration - Realising web3 through engineering and product development. Decentration are founding stewards of Kabocha and member of the Edgeware council web3 foundation grant funded. Working on making useful and usable experiences in web3.

_Key member from Decentration: Ramsey_
