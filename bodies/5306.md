**     4. Bounty Parameters Optimization**
**         [https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/4496-bounty-parameter-optimization   ](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/4496-bounty-parameter-optimization)    **
**	**
**	As a part of this contribution, we studied the existing bounty incentivisation mechanism and noticed that there was a scope to reduce the payout delay which is currently set to 7 days. We used some of the data sampling techniques and prepared a model based on the standard deviation of EDG prices to arrive at the conclusion that a 5 day bounty payout delay was sufficient for the optimal functioning of the bounty mechanism. We think this is one of the major contributions as this is a modification related to a governance parameter of the Edgeware ecosystem. **
**	This change is to be implemented on a technical governance level in the upcoming ERUP upgrade.**

**     6. Categorization of the treasury proposals**

**	[Proposal analysis](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1_AugoZrSXK-WtAm2_Wa63t-1ekOsnlvvDgCm66ofoEM/edit?usp=sharing)**

**	This was our very first deliverable after we started contributing to the Edgeware ecosystem. As a part of this exercise we listed down all the data related to the various treasury proposals, democracy proposals and the council motions which had occurred till the as a part of the governance process. Then we prepared a dynamic model to sort the proposals based on the method of passing as well as the category to which they belong.**
**	This list will definitely help any new community member to very easily sort through the proposals in a short span of time as well as to understand the categories in which the passed proposals belong to, such that the contributor can align his contributions along the same lines to get funded through the onchain treasury.       **
