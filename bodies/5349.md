Given that Edgeware is working on developing pallets and open source tools that will benefit wider chains, it makes clear sense to partner up with other funds that have mutual aims. 

For greater cooperation between Edgeware and [Web3 Foundation](https://web3.foundation/) "Web3", [Decentration](https://decentration.org) has been working on creating a joint funding programme for the purpose of delivering value to the substrate ecosystem, and using Edgeware (and Kabocha) as a "playground of possibilities" to launch, test and incubate new pallets, tools and utilities. 

The aim is for our grassroots development ethos to  permeate into the the wider ecosystem, and our growing community of chains (Edgeware, Kabocha, Beresheet, Pop-Art, etc.) will set an example and standard for how to design, build, launch and nurture projects. 

The benefits to Edgeware in this are manifold. A key benefit is that it alleviates some of the burden from Edgeware and Kabocha for funding things that may benefit many stakeholders in Substrate. The open-source funding model is useful but can be improved, as there is essentially a stakeholder that bears the brunt of expense, coordination and ongoing maintenance. Edgeware can explore this avenue and help to create even better funding structures for open-source (or hybrid open source) structures. 

## How will it work?

It can work in various ways:

- **Web3 approved** - Web3 can approve and fund a project grant application, which validates its need to be worked on, Edgeware will then joint fund it and help to incubate the project through testing it on its testnets, and be the first to launch it on its chain. Edgeware can be selective about the projects as not all projects would be suited to Edgeware's goals and values. 

- **Match Funding** - Edgeware and Web3 go 50/50 on the grant funding of a project from the beginning. 

- **Edgeware Lead** - Edgeware can fund the first milestone of a proposal, Web3 foundation for later milestones. Or Edgeware funds first milestones and joint funding on later milestones. 

- **Investment opportunities:** Edgeware can have the opportunity to capture shares in the projects it provided grants to. 

- Multi-grants: Edgeware can lead the way gathering grants from various chain stakeholders (moonbeam, acala, polkadot, kusama, and many others) to fund work that has mutual benefit. (as mentioned by Thom Ivy)
