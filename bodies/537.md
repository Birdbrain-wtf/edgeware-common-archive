## Growing a Polyculture: Artist Residencies

My work has largely been to grow the community of Edgeware - we've tried many different strategies over time, and now that Edgeware has launched we're approaching new tools that we can test. The strongest of these thus far has been the Treasury.

My strategy for bootstrapping the community recently has been the deployment and nurturing of a set of working groups and support the resourcing and coordination of these groups until they are self-sustaining. 

At the same time, I also watch and listen carefully to the explicated values of the users in order to find the 'ground floor' of Edgeware; the core tenants that unite the community. We've recently begun work at the BWG on drafting a constitution of sorts - which we are currently terming the 'Architecture.'

Whereas many chains begin with a monoculture, I am explicitly rejecting that path in favor of a robust polyculture: that Edgeware should have a diverse field of groups supporting different values, experimenting in different ways, and proving their needs and results to better access resourcing- for example through treasury grants.

Working groups are permissionless. Anyone may start one- and I would like to see many more: NFTs, Gaming, Token Engineering, DAOs are some that come to mind. In brief, I believe they allow others to collaborate on common goals, immediately find like-minded peers within our ecosystem, and support the growth of specific industries through co-support within Edgeware.

To build onto this infrastructure of a polyculture, I am proposing that we fund several Residencies or (should these be successful, longer term fellowships) native to the chain. Classically, residencies are endowed with the mission to explore and produce work which represents and investigates the core values of a project or entity.

 I expect that when the core values and architecture are _stable enough,_ we can convey them to those selected and their work will help present the brand, enliven our community, and engender a broader sense of purpose to our work on what is currently- at the core, a technology project.

I believe 5-10 short term residencies, lasting 3 months each with rotations where EDG tokens are granted from Treasury will be sufficient. At the end of the cohort, it may be helpful to use signaling to establish Fellows- longer term residencies granted to artists who are _exceptional,_ and convey the values of the community in a _clearly impactful manner._

While I believe we should begin with artists, I would also like to see student interns within comp science or other engineering leaders granted residencies to explore the functions of the chain. I also envision Working Groups opting to deploy their own residencies- for instance, the Governance Working Group may reach out to the DGov Foundation and community to encourage their members to apply and produce work related to their interests intersecting with Edgeware.

The goal here is to broaden the stakeholders in our community, enliven and give form to our values, and massively boost awareness and commitment to the community.
