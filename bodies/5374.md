# Pallet Supersig 

## Network Steward
Ramsey(Decentration)

## Team
Decentration (https://decentration.org) an Edgeware team.

**tl;dr**
_We have got work subsidised by Web3 foundation by at least 60% for a pallet which Edgeware would have funded fully. We hope this is the first of much more to come of work subsidised by other funds, which will help to alleviate the burden on Edgeware treasury, especially during the downtimes._

This is the first proposal connected to the Edgeware X Web3 funding cooperation, which you can find [here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/5349-edgeware-x-web3-foundation-joint-funding-programme).

**Preamble**<br>
Decentration has stewarded the mission to forge a working relationship with Web3 foundation. We are in the process of designing and building products that fulfill our philosophy which is to build products people really use, and not to over-engineer untested assumptions. We are a team containing product developers, product managers, engineers and UX developers. One of our partners are Rusty Crewmates (sub-contracted by Decentration), who are doing a great job providing a great standard of work. 

The first proposal in the Edgeware X Web3 foundation joint funding programme, is the greatly anticipated Supersig, <br><br>

> “Like a multisig but with superpowers 💥”, <br><br>

Supersig may replace (or be more used than) Multisig across all chains, including Polkadot and Kusama, and it will be tested first on Kabocha and maybe Edgeware (if the major upgrade is unblocked).<br><br>

This application has been approved by Web3 foundation as a Level 2 application which required 3/5 approvals.<br><br>

## Funding
**EDG 10m**<br><br>
The funding request will be less than 50% of the total funding, the rest will be covered by Web3 foundation ($27k). The extra funding provides an additional funding buffer for over-budget milestones and adds an extra milestone (milestone 4) to partially fund a period of ongoing maintenance. <br><br>
