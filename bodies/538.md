**Intention:** Recently there was confusion about the longevity of discussions around proposal - where that conversation was happening, and how to understand their current status. To alleviate this, I offer a single post linking to threads with brief descriptions and status on every active proposal in Edgeware. Long term, this will likely be automated by some tooling (hopefully Commonwealth.im!) but this is very helpful in the meantime.
