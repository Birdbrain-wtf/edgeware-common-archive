### Technical
- **The [first major upgrade (Codename Naples) is ready](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/519-release-naples-hicommonwealthedgewarenode-github),** but some Validators still need to update their nodes. You can view Telemetry to see which Vals are not running the latest version. Commonwealth Labs expects to push the upgrade proposal within 1.5 weeks but is deeply interested in getting as many validators to upgrade first in order to prevent potential slashing events. Due to quirks in the use of Rust in Substrate, the binary is not able to be verified using the usual SRTool, and [Drew Stone talks about that in the Riot channel.](https://matrix.to/#/!dQIXacXSBDQsPsWEYR:matrix.org/$89umDtR2OHAZ55pNamR_e85Eq9nX09GbTf8-g0JsrL8?via=matrix.org&via=t2bot.io&via=matrix.parity.io)
- In preparation for the Naples release of Edgeware, the testnet has upgraded to the same codebase, (Edgeware Node 3.0.8,) and renamed [Beresheet, and is live on Github](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/517-bereshet-testnet-launched) and currently the operating testnet.
- The Blockx Labs team reports that they are approaching the completion of their integration of EDG into their [Enzyme browser wallet.](https://getenzyme.dev/) You can [see their Github here.](https://github.com/blockxlabs/enzyme/)
- Right now, there isn't an easy way to see the progress of Treasury Tips, [but Thom Ivy shows how you can get the raw data at Subscan.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/533-view-the-data-behind-treasury-tips-to-see-amounts-and-tippers)
