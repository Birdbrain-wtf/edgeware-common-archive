# tl;dr

Kabocha Music is the arm of Kabocha community that focuses on the music industry.

# Summary 
Given the tools we now have as a creative engineering community, what can we create and transform in the realm of music?

The legacy music industry has always been amongst the first things that get impacted by new technolgoies, through discoveries in electrical theory, experimental music instruments, P2P music sharing, which made pirating music a norm, and thus which lead to  the syntehis of music streaming we have today. This though is a placeholder for what capacities for new paradigms we have available for web3 technologies.

There is a burgeoning opportunity to create new ways for music artists to interact with their fans. For fans to become collaborators, royalty distribution and IP ownership, music collectives, fractional ownership of music, etc. 

There is a demand from legacy music companies to get into web3 by collaborating with in-the-know decentralised communities to develop web3 music platforms and products. Currently we are seeing prototypes for music projects in the cryptosphere, a prototyping stage. With experiments in live concert ticketing, royalty NFTs. However there is a lot more interesting things that can be done. 

## Limitations on smart contract chains
NFTs on legacy smart contract blockchains such as Ethereum are limited in what can be designed, this is because "music DApps" have no control over transaction fees, underlying technology and governance of the blockchain they are building on. Royalties built into smart contracts are not guaranteed, though RMRK are way ahead in this regard. Soon these projects will need have better tools and their own blockchain infrastructure. Kabocha community can incubate these kinds of projects with expertise in creative and engineering. 

Kabocha already has within it various stakeholders in music industry wanting to work on interesting things: artists, music channels, music companies. 

## Mission
To deliver events and experiences through experiments to build out novel platforms and Web3 products. 

## Events
Working with creative teams we will form collaborations to fund experiments that will work with experiences production teams, artists to unlock the capabilities of Kabocha's playground of possibilities and tools. 

We build verticals not horizontals.
Instead of assuming what people might like to use and spending too much time and too much resources on over engineering such assumptions, we will create mininal viable products MVPs to deliver the required tools for the experiments. Such as QR based ticketing services,
