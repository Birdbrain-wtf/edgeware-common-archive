## **Summary**

This proposal seeks funding for an Edgeware research and development (R&D) project delivered over two milestones that aims to build a meaningful and long term connection between the project and the [Amazonian Huni Kuin people](https://vimeo.com/481759993) who are on the frontlines of the defence of the Amazon rainforest. 

This proposal advances Edgeware’s real world event plans, alongside the [EDGpass concept for citizen benefits](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/1101-concept-edgpass-citizen-benefits) and expands [Kabocha’s Seeds](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/3440-proposal-kabocha-visuals-and-crowdloan-nfts) with a novel use of [RMRK NFTs](https://rmrk.app) as assigned assets that leverages a slight hack to enable the unlocking of token gated EDG/KAB related digital experiences.

## **Introduction**

Our planet is magnificent. Life has evolved for millions of years, creating a rich and diverse ecosystem, an intricate web where all life can thrive. The climate crisis is the result of a deep imbalance on the Earth. 

Our species has been extracting, producing, and polluting without much consideration for other forms of life, even our own. This imbalance is a symptom of our alienation from natural rhythms, cycles, and systems and our lack of caring relationship to our world. 

This also manifests in ever-increasing crises in mental health and crucially; the breakdown of communities. 

The Huni Kuin people live deep in the Brazilian Amazon Rainforest, yet they are on the front line of this imbalance as more and more of their forest is destroyed. 

They come to the Western world to create awareness of their existence and the reality of the threat to the forest and their sacred medicines. They have also come to create alliances with new communities to strengthen their work and connections.

## Questions this R&D aims to ask

Can we stitch together an integrated media and physical event experience that is token gated for EDG/KAB holders with RMRK / and Kabocha's non-transferable Seeds at the core?

Can we better organise our communication within emerging cross-chain Substrate tools such as [Collab-Land](https://collab.land/) or [Sumi](https://www.sumi.network/)? 

Can Edgeware's emerging ecosystem be useful for bringing alive the stories of grass-roots communities and as a way to onboard new users not familiar with crypto?

Can we amplify this kind of message and in the process re-awaken a deeper connection to nature and ancient wisdom that runs counter to crypto's current profit maximising culture?

## How EDGpass can work to give access to digital experiences 

There currently two routes to making EDGpass a reality within digital experiences:

- [Collab-Land on Kusama](https://mirror.xyz/iamnick.eth/j5eXu_xgZBQeFI8l_m96uvnVEK3NTv6zwqbJbRhKTOc) via Talisman signer or with...
- [Sumi](https://www.sumi.network/) a new Substrate project that is focused on NFT gated communications to simplify collaboration and messaging across Substrate chains.


Here's how the Collab-Land/KSM/RMRK/Talisman flow looks:

- Currently Collab-land discord bots only support KSM and RMRK NFTs minted on the chain via the [Talisman browser extension](https://app.talisman.xyz/).
- However we can hack this together for a proof of concept since every EDG address also has a corresponding KSM address that a user can derive if they set their wallet to be available across all networks.
- This means we can whitelist EDG addresses and mint and send RMRK NFTs to an EDG derived KSM address.
- Then we leverage the Kusama Collab-Land integration with Talisman's browser signer - you can view [setup process here](https://mirror.xyz/iamnick.eth/j5eXu_xgZBQeFI8l_m96uvnVEK3NTv6zwqbJbRhKTOc).
- This can then be used to login to a token gated channel in the EDG discord.
- From here we can setup an EDG/NFT gated channel and then [stream a feed of the live event to Discord](https://www.streamscheme.com/discord-streaming/) exclusively to holders of specific RMRK NFTs/tickets.


The Sumi approach will be updated here as we learn more.

## R&D milestone 2

In milestone 2 we will be collaborating with the EDG funded [New-Futures](https://www.new-futures.co/) as part of their platform launch to create NFTs with meaning, and real world benefit. 

Visuals and original music created for the live event will be transformed into unique RMRK NFTs that will be assigned to a contributor’s non-transferable Seed. These NFTs will utilise RMRK’s ‘unboxing’ capability, enabling the owner to ‘break open’ the NFT to reveal a Huni Kuni prayer from the forest. 

Using the EDG/KSM derivation function, we can send these NFTs to all EDG holders who attend the digital event.

For those who attended in real life who may not be EDG or indeed crypto holders we need a simple way to onboard them and currently we are looking to utilise [Kusama's gifting functionality](https://gifts.kusama.network/#/generate) to retrospectively send the attendees a wallet containing their own Huni Kuni NFT prayer. 

We are working with the RMRK founding team alongside some of their project’s most innovative developers and experimenters to advance the adoption and understanding of the technology.

The Huni Kuin have received their knowledge and wisdom over many thousands of years from the forest, the rivers, the plants, the elements, trees and animals. They are the true caretakers, guardians and protectors of the Amazon rainforest, medicines and spiritual traditions. 

Their people have been contacted for about a century now and have gone through various phases of suffering, narrowly escaping extinction. Some of them who fled to Peru and to the farthest lands in Acre were capable of maintaining a community life and ritual knowledge completely interwoven with the rainforest and its wildlife without interruption. The groups who stayed in Brazil were obliged to live in small shattered settlements working for pittance on rubber plantations. 

Today, it is mainly racism, poverty and human greed that threaten their lives and destroy their habitat. More and more pieces of forest are becoming victims of fire or are being cut down to make room for cattle and soy plantations.
One solution to save themselves and the forest is to acquire their own land. This is unusual, because indigenous peoples do not normally have private land ownership. The Huni Kuin do: they are one of the first indigenous Amazon peoples to have already purchased tracts of their own land. The Huni Kuin want to continue with this. By acquiring it, they are strengthening the association of indigenous territories that exist in the region.
