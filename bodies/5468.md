# Milestone 1

**The Pilot Experiment**

A one-day music event in a unique location in London featuring an
established headline artist.

The event is free, however consumers must sign-up via the Kabocha Music sign up portal before a deadline.

Following the deadline, the invitation is sent for participants to
claim their free ticket to the event.

Once the participants have attended the event they can claim their Verified Experience NFT. 

The NFT contains a digital ticket in the form of unique art, as well
as priority access to future events in the series, and private groups using Collab.Land or equivalent service. 


## Event outline

The event is split into three identical performances across the day to
maximise quality of experience keeping it into cohorts.

The headline artist will be surrounded by LED screens projecting a
custom-built Unreal Engine/Cinema4D environment.

Across each performance, the audience will be immersed by the rich visual
experience, the unique location and the intimate proximity to the headline
artist.

A designated number of tickets will be allocated to partners, press
and VIPs are who invited to experience the product offering.
