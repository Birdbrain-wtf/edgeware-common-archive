## Summary
Pallet subscription will be a way for a person, multisig (or supersig) to get recurring payments from a subscribing account on the Edgeware or Kabocha blockchains. Think Patreon, or Netflix or other types of monthly membership services. Pallet_Subscription will enable this functionality. <br><br>

**Use case:** People can subscribe to your blogging service and pay you monthly in EDG or KAB. The payments will be withdrawn monthly from the subscribers account, automatically.<br><br>

## Milestone 1 
**Pallet minimal viable version (work already underway)**

The first pallet will enable the subscriptions to be taken in EDG (on Edgeware) and KAB (on Kabocha). <br><br>

Limitations:<br>
The pallet will not have a pricing oracle though this could be included in later editions.
