# Contributor Bill of Rights



Below is a listing of the available Rights to each contributor. Each Right is willfully applicable by the Contributor. Participation is a choice retained in Citizenship. 


The Spirit of Law is the inherent goal.
The intent of each provision is provided under each Right. 



##                                                                                       Contribution is open for all citizens.



Understanding that the foundation of support for our citizens is the key to our success.
Below is an overview of the service rights for any contributor that is approved by the community through democracy: 


Contributor Right: **Declared Presence **
Action: _On-Chain ID_
Spirit of Law: Each contributor retains the right to declare their own name for contributor services. The on chain-ID works as an Introduction to citizens. Providing transparency into the support for our community.



Contributor Right: **Commitments**  
Action: _Forum Post_
Spirit of Law: Each contributor retains the right to define their own commitments when on-boarding. Declaration of your service parameters, project details and known milestones are clearly defined on the referenda post in the community forum. A Contributor retains the right to declare future modification to existing commitments. Acceptance of the additional commitments are enacted through Community Democracy. 


Contributor Right: **Stability **
Action: _Top Up _
Spirit of Law: To increase enrollment by providing life security. Contribution is voluntary, awards are distributed in post to services provided. The 30 Day exchange rate proposal (Top Up), accounts for the variance occurred through awarding in Blockchain based democracy.  Providing the average difference, creates consistency in the funding for our service supporters.



Contributor Right: **Accountability **
Action: _Democracy _
Spirit of Law: Each Contributor retains the right to value citizen feedback. Contribution is performed in servitude to every community member. Citizens, through democracy accept and retains the right to vote in accordance to efficacy of the contributor’s commitments. Contributors are measured on their declared commitments on the approved referenda. Continuous transparent growth is feature of our DAO. 



Contributor Right: **Acknowledgement**
Action: _Reputation_
Spirit of Law: Collaborative effort to highlight the successes of our Contributors. Community service is always appreciated, exceptional performance can be showcased within (_democracy when applicable, but not limited to_) Community meetings, Featured Blog Posts, Film Segments, distribution of additional Grants or awards from our Contributor Benefit programs. 




Feedback is always welcomed and appreciated..
