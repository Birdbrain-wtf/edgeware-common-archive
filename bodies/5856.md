# _Event scenario_

1. First with the arrival of guests and the width of the welcome; Guests are invited to visit the entire complex. At the entrance to the first floor, each guest is presented with a package list and a brochure. In each floor, one person is responsible for welcoming and guiding the guests up to the third floor, and explains the spaces of the complex to the guests and presents their use.
2. Then, by announcing through the loudspeaker of the complex, the guests are invited to attend the courtyard to have a drink and watch an artistic performance.
3. Next time after eating finger food in the same area; Live music will begin and during this time will be a good time for friendly conversations and answering possible questions.
4. In the next stage, by inviting the guests to visit the painting exhibition on the second floor, the cinema hall will be ready to show a short film.
5. At the end of the film; In the presence of a facilitator, the guests will be thanked and a commendation ceremony will be held.
6. Finally, when the guests leave, discount coupons and class registration forms will be provided to the guests and the invitation link will be given to talented artists.


**_Notice:_ At all stages, it tries to make the guests move and search freely in the space of the complex, and trying to create a good feeling and experience will be a fun day.**
