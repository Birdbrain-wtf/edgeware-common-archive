With the first week of September down, I'd like to update the community on what has happened so far in the last couple weeks of Edgeware development. It's certainly been an exciting past few weeks. A couple notable achievements:
- We have pushed the first runtime upgrade democracy proposal to upgrade to Substrate 2.0 rc3.
- We have coordinated a network wide node update to the v3.0.8 Edgeware node.
- We have deployed our first few EVM contracts to our edgeware-frontier node in a local network.


## WASM Contracts
- We have onboarded Matej Nemcek to work on developer relations and WASM tutorials.
- We identified working versions for WASM contracts with useful features at Substrate 2.0 rc4 and will plan to upgrade past this in the 2nd network upgrade.
- Balance transfers in Ink! only work on rc4 and on so we will have them ready by the 2nd upgrade.
- We created a variety of tutorials that will be spun out in the future for WASM development. Once we achieve the next couple upgrades and a more consistent feature set of the WASM contracting system on Edgeware, we will launch a developer portal with tutorials and an FAQ.


## EVM Contracts + Frontier
- Jake Naviasky, Dillon Chen, and myself are working on EVM integrations, testing and documenting compatibilities in our frontier-tester repo: [https://github.com/hicommonwealth/frontier-tester](https://github.com/hicommonwealth/frontier-tester)
- We have working versions of the Edgeware lockdrop on an Edgeware local network and have been working hard to deploy Uniswap.
- Deployment works but interactions are not 100% stable. For example, adding liquidity to uniswap markets fails.
- I have submitted issues as well as upstream PRs to integrate more precompiles for developers to choose from in pallet-evm.
- Existing precompiles in Ethereum have been added, such as modexp
- Newer precompiles not in Ethereum have been added, such as bn128
- Plans to integrate bls381 precompiles and more are planned.


## Parachain bonding
With a variety of teams integrating testnets as parachains on Rococo, we also want to follow suit and have onboarded
- Alex Miao, a student at the University of Pennsylvania, has joined and begun investigating and integrating cumulus into an Edgeware runtime.


## Edgeware's 2nd Runtime Upgrade Proposal - ERUP 2.0
With the first upgrade out of the way and ongoing work integrating the EVM system, Edgeware's 2nd runtime upgrade plans to work off the work described above. We hope to continue contributing upstream to Frontier and the EVM module to prepare a 2nd upgrade proposal that integrates EVM logic to the Edgeware runtime.
To that end, we have begun testing:
- Upgrades from v1.0.1redo → master → edgeware-frontier and it works after preliminary testing (transfers and creating treasury/concil/democracy proposals).
