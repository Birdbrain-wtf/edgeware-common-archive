Welcome back! Another exciting week in Edgeware:

## Technical

- The _proposal_ [to upgrade the runtime was succesful and is now a tabled _Referendum_](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/541-edgeware-runtime-upgrade-proposal-1)_. _Assuming it succeeds here, and it seems like to with many millions of EDG in support, the upgrade will go live in 2 weeks, bringing Edgeware to Substrate 2. Several more upgrades are already in design.


- [OnFinality announced Node-as-a-Service Support for Edgeware,](https://medium.com/@OnFinality/run-public-edgeware-nodes-on-onfinality-2ea656262446) allowing quick infrastructure spin-up.


## Governance

- Thom Ivy proposed that Edgeware chain-hire Matej Nemcek for the role of Developer Advocate. [See and comment on this proposal.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/593-proposal-to-chainhire-matej-nemcek-as-developer-advocate) Next, it will go on-chain for a council vote, where the treasury will either pay for Matej's work or decline to do so, pushing the proposal back to drafting. 


- Ryan Zurrer [proposed on-chain a proposal to fund the creation of BuilDAO](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/534-treasury-proposal-12-buildao-treasury-request), a DAO-based startup incubator that seeks to be funded with EDG tokens, and the treasury/chain would benefit from the success. [Learn more about BuilDAO or discuss.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/510-buildao-incubator-discussion-thread)


## Community

- Johan, a power member of the Brand Working Group, [showcased his prototype of the new Edgeware site.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/595-johan-presents-his-prototype-of-a-new-edgeware-site-comments-welcome) The BWG has been workshopping  the copy, design, and brand for over a month. Feedback welcome!


- Several Commonwealth Labs members [responded to a prompt from Mr. Bulb on the cultural values and goals of Edgeware. ](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/536-why-are-you-here-edgeware-founding-values-culture-and-focus)Everyone is welcome to contribute to the thread, which is helping the Brand Working Group plan.


- Thom Ivy [raises the pre-proposal of creating Artist Residencies for Edgeware,](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/537-artist-residencies-and-more-on-edgeware) to develop and explore the cultural values of the chain.


- [A twitter poll ran to determine what Edgeware users would like to call themselves, given the unique culture of the chain.](https://twitter.com/HeyEdgeware/status/1303283053683445765) At 40%, they selected Citizen.
