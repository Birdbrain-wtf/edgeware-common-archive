**Summary:**  A followup to my [vision post of Artist Residencies, ](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/537-artist-residencies-and-more-on-edgeware) this is a list of artists, art communities and curators to support those artist residencies.

---

In considering the structure of the Artist Residency program, we can have a pure-application based process-- and therefore need to plan and review those inbound applications, _or _we can tap our networks to discover higher quality fits through their curatorship. If this program is successful, we will want consistent access to residents who move our vision forward. 
## 
Mr.Bulb and I have several contacts that may be of interest, and we should share them here - anyone is welcome to add an entity or artist they would like to see involved. We may also want to think about the role of a curator entity to recommend or advocate for specific residents, and whether that role needs to be incentivized or not, or whether we simply fund these curator orgs directly and they sub-designate to artists in their collective.  Given their expertise in these matters and the limited organization capacity of the chain itself at this time, we can take any of these approaches or hybridize. 

** Personally, **I think tighter relationships with art-communities on the whole is a wise move, and empowering them to empower artists to dedicate time to Edgeware's vision makes sense to me.  Long term we might envision an Edgeware Art Curator to maintain our own proprietary network and body of artists and art.**  **

**I would love to see Public Art Works in the form of NFTs or analogues, held by the Chain itself to the benefit of the citizens.**

## Museum of Contemporary Digital Art
> The Museum of Contemporary Digital Art provides digital art education and technology to artists, collectors, institutions and art lovers. First and foremost, MoCDA is a museum that exhibits digital artworks for the purpose of documenting, collecting and advancing the position of digital art.
> MoCDA provides a foundation for understanding digital art in its own context, rather than as a mere by-product of the larger art world. The MoCDA platform offers a space for everyone to connect, discover and learn more about digital art. Our focus is on education and the endorsement of contemporary digital art in a way which is inclusive and future-oriented, encouraging engagement from a diverse audience with an artist-first approach.
