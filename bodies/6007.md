## **KAB Treasury**

The Kabocha treasury is created from the KAB issued to the Edgeware treasury at this snapshot block.

KAB treasury = 6,437,924.018517766

This treasury has been set aside for crowdloan rewards and for Decentration's founder's reward and other setup costs, including[ Edgeware's recoup](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/3806-kabocha-funding-and-edgeware-recoup) which we are currently calculating, collators and general network contingency. 

Moving forward, Kabocha will not direct inflation into the treasury, but instead use our new[ Pallet_Mint](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/4852-rusty-crewmates-pallet_mint-development) which mints coins associated to approved proposals. 

Those new coins go into the beneficiary’s “Supersig” account, (enabled by [Pallet_Supersig](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/5374-pallet_supersig-joint-funding-proposal-with-w3f), which is another Kabocha innovation), Minting via Pallet_Mint is capped at a max inflation of 20% in year 1.

The first proposal to use the Pallet_Mint function will be the Decent Partner's founders year 1 crowdloan reward of 1.5% which is the same as Decentration Technical Steward). 

## **Crowdloan rewards ambiguity**

There is an ambiguity of 4.9% of total supply or 50% of treasury on snapshot date choices - we had previously communicated both since the Edgeware distribution and treasury was always a moving target as we didn't know what the treasury was going to be before the snapshot date. 

By the snapshot date, the treasury was a bit less in proportion to the entire supply.

Though the difference is not major we can perhaps lean on the side of giving a larger bucket for crowdloan participants, given that we generally communicated it to be 4.9% of genesis supply and it is mentioned in the [summary of Kabocha’s Initial proposal](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/2363-kabocha-kab-a-kusama-parachain-project-by-edgeware).

## **How to work out your own KAB allocation**

There were a total of 478 contributors to the Kabocha crowdloan.

If X = your KSM contribution

Let’s take X = **120 KSM**

120** **/ 12,644 KSM * 120 = 0.949

You will receive = 0.949% of the total crowd loan allocation

The genesis supply = 3,386,232.56 (4.9% of total supply)

0.949% / 100 * 3,386,232.56 = **32,137.60 KAB **which is your allocation. 

Alternately a shorter route:

X / 12644 * 3.386m = Y KAB
(X * 3.386m ) / 12,644 = Y KAB
