We've used treasury funds for grants, proposed funding VC-like BuilDAO launches, funding residencies for cultural minds, and more.

The treasury is already showing strength as a game-changer among blockchains when the funds are deployed strategically. The use of funds for the public good is the mandate-  however this does not preclude their application towards private ends, so long as the Treasury gains /more/ funds in the future for continual deployment.  Jam recently discussed Flash Loans, and I believe they meant from the Treasury and this is genius because the treasury is unaffected at worst, and if deployed for the right ecosystem reasons, these loans could increase the value of EDG, spur development and ecosystem building.

At a glance, here is the stack I am seeing unfold:

- Project/Business launches (Grants or BuildDAO- equity, tokens)
- Single-purpose Transactions or Refi/Arbitrage (Treasury Flash Loans)
- Project/Business Exit (Exit-to-Community: Treasury pays, Edgeware citizens benefit.)
- Various others (Tips, Gitcoin-like Matching from Treasury)


The one use case I am not seeing well-served is that of growth. R[ead a nice article from Zima Red's NFT newsletter on Pipe](https://andrewsteinwold.substack.com/p/new-funding-methods-and-markets-for), which basically provides funding to projects ready to grow by letting projects:
- Sell annualized future revenues instead of equity at a high APR
- Investors can also use Pipe as a platform, making financing terms competitive.


The treasury can mimic this by taking ownership of a time-limited future revenues, and also lower the costs of other investors hopping in -- leading rounds of this kind of investment. Zima Red also points at the capacity to tokenize these debt contracts,** allowing the treasury itself to sell these.**

The programatic nature of on-chain activity makes some of this much easier than the due-diligence involved with analogous activity on chain, imagine 1-click button to trade a duration of revenue for funding.
