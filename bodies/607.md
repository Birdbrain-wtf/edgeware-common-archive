## Norms
- Regarding the recent vote to fund Commonwealth, several council objected to the process.
- On this call, Jam mentioned that it is normally the case that individuals who benefit from a proposal recuse themselves.
- However, Jam also pointed out that **if all members of Commonwealth were to recuse, it would be impossible to reach a threshold required to pass the motion, likely.**
- Thom and Jam discuss technical solutions to this issue, including the ability for a council member to delegate their motion-vote to either individuals or multisigs, and Jam affirms that vote proxying any gov action will be possible after the next upgrade.
- Jam also says that the Prime Councillor (selected using a process similar to Phragmen) would command the votes for any unvoting members. This ability to activate the prime councillor, a moving role among council members, will be possible after the coming upgrade.

## Norms around On-chain ID
- Commonwealth supports broad use of on-chain ID. 
- Registrars can set their own conditions for verification, which can lead to differences in the /meaning/ behind the verif., which displays little granular info in the UI of Polkadot Apps. 
- We should consider what it means to prioritize on-chain IDs, and whether we inadvertently create a kind of 1st class citizen.
