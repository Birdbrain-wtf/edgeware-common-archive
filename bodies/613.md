_Authors: Thom Ivy, Dillon Chen, Drew Stone, Raymond Zhong_

The first upgrade has been passed! That's not all. Overall, we're on track with the [Edgeware 2020 Roadmap](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/509-edgeware-2020-roadmap-discussion) that we outlined. We're pushing forward with the overall vision of making Edgeware the best place for new projects and communities to be incubated within the Polkadot Ecosystem. We wanted to highlight progress here:

### **Developers**
- A community stablecoin project has approached us for developer support. They're interested in working on something similar to Maker using EDG as a collateral type, using the EVM pallet. Right now, the team behind the project is working on deploying contracts to an Edgeware Test chain, and we're working with them to integrate the Edgeware Treasury and Council as part of the protocol.
- A team working on a novel undercollateralized lending protocol also intends to launch on Edgeware. We've reviewed the whitepaper, and once the team is ready to post an announcement we'll share in our community channels!
- A synthetic asset protocol is being built on Edgeware. We're really excited about the work they're doing. They've shared a preliminary website and economic model, and have prior experience working on the Edgeware stack.
- We worked with an outside designer on a spec for a social network built on Edgeware identities. We're still working through several angles this could take but we're interested in product-forward teams and developers to work on various versions of this project.
- Other conversations with 5+ teams working on DeFi and social applications. While the conversations are still early, we're excited to see what can come.
- [OnFinality](https://onfinality.io/) announced a node-as-a-service provider. [EverStake](https://twitter.com/everstake_pool/status/1306992885070548993) joined as a validator. We have continuing conversations with three bridge providers in the Substrate ecosystem.


### Technical
- Edgeware's first upgrade was approved by referenda and entered the dispatch queue. [See the full list of changes and features in the Release Notes.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/612-edgeware-upgrade-1-release-notes) In roughly 5.5 days, the upgrade will execute on nodes throughout the network. If you haven't updated your nodes, now is the time to do so - [validators risk being slashed if they fail to update](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/519-release-naples-hicommonwealthedgewarenode-github).
- Edgeware's second upgrade is coming soon, taking us to the latest version of Ink! smart contract compatibility.


### Governance
- The [proposal to hire Matej Nemcek as Developer Advocate](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/593-proposal-to-chainhire-matej-nemcek-as-developer-advocate) has been sent [to the Council](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/councilmotion/0x5304a57de2d2d58bdec1d6edf2b4419419b26f404e68eabec54ca83f8e0a8706).
- Discussion on the [BuilDAO proposal continues in a thread](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/534-treasury-proposal-12-buildao-treasury-request), [while 7/8 councillors have voted to approve](https://polkadot.js.org/apps/#/council/motions) the funding of the DAO's establishment.

Here is a preview of the prototype, [which you can view and comment on here.](https://johan317017.invisionapp.com/console/share/Y81J6A8ES4/480651492)![](https://commonwealth-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/43b99088-7703-492f-99b6-4ccdceb9f688.png)

- Thom Ivy [posted an outline of a growth tool](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/610-citizenled-immigration-to-edgeware-or-sponsoring-a-new-edgholder) that could become a fun and community-centric way to onboard new EDG community members, by allowing EDG holders to sponsor them through donations and a support channel. Applicants would complete small tasks or join up with working groups to pay it forward.

Also discussed was the path to parachaining and the DOT raise required, the culture around on-chain ID, and how that might impact community growth and reputation.
- Thom Ivy posted ["Extending the Power of Public Funds"](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/605-extending-the-power-of-public-funds) which discusses tools and uses for the public funds held in the treasury to maximize Edgeware's ability to publicly support development both on-network and in the ecosystem, using existing plans like BuilDAO, but also futuristic tools like tokenized contracts based on the future revenues on-chain entities might accrue.
- The BWG is looking for artists, curators, and art organizations that may want to partner or participate in [a concepted Artist Residency program.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/600-candidate-list-for-artist-residencies-and-orgspersons-of-interest)
