The bounties pallet by Emiel from Polkascan and Bryan from Acala/Laminar has just been merged into the [master branch of Substrate](https://github.com/paritytech/substrate/issues/5713).

I think it would be great for us to get it merged into Edgeware in one of the upcoming runtime upgrades that [@drewstone](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/account/hwR8hAatmmdupBLXQSxLUPBa8GhRomLD9hf6iRtFeXs8fcY) is working on.

The pallet lets councillors propose and fund bounties through a fairly well-thought-out-system (see section 5 in the issue above for a full description of how it works):

- Bounties are proposed with a curator, a description, and amount.
- Bounties must be approved by a simple majority of councillors, its funds are earmarked and held from the treasury.
- The council cannot alter the bounty once it has been approved. The curator is responsible for delegating the work associated with the bounty and releasing it once that's been completed, but there is a delay that allows the release of funds to be canceled through democracy (i.e. through a coin-holder vote).
- If enough time passes without the bounty being completed, the bounty expires.


Once this is integrated into the runtime, it would be great for councillors to get together over a call, ideally one open to the public, to come up with a few solid bounties for Edgeware. For example:

- An implementation of Thom's [citizen sponsorship](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/610-citizenled-immigration-to-edgeware-or-sponsoring-a-new-edgholder) system for new Edgeware members
- [Artists' residencies](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/600-candidate-list-for-artist-residencies-and-orgspersons-of-interest)
- Other projects in line with our [values](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/536-why-are-you-here-edgeware-founding-values-culture-and-focus)
