This post pools key insights, areas of interest and concepts from the community into something approaching an ordered structure - working from high level to deep dive. 

It is aimed at those familiar with the work so far and also newcomers - it is a long one and is currently hosted as a Google doc [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/14CTc_mCN--4YWs4dU_JNjnZSPLZCeiQzPvPTAHAQP2Q/edit) so it can be easily updated and commented on. 

A following post will place these key insights into a tighter narrative structure that can be discussed, further refined and implemented into Edgeware's website, social pages and visual content production materials.

This will onboard a wider community of makers, builders, creators and communities who can further scale the reach, impact and legitimacy of the network.

## Why do it this way? 

This approach aims to drive a core principle of transparency - effectively 'showing the working' involved in creating, developing and launching 'Edgeware as a meme'. 

By documenting key steps through an open and interactive process the aim is to drive increasing engagement, de-mystify key concepts and enable those from different backgrounds to find common language. 

Ultimately this will build the project's culture on strong foundations that can act as a shared reference going forward.

As ever it is worth reiterating this is a constant work in progress and ideas, suggestions and push backs are encouraged - the only request is for criticism to be constructive.
