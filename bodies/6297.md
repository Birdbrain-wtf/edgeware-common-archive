Hi all, 

A few months back Decent Partners were asked if we would consider helping with the [Exchange B listing](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/3862-proposal-market-makerfaciliatated-liquidation-and-exchange-b-final-items), which has been slow, difficult and complex for all involved. 

The original agreement to deliver the listing was taken up by Commonwealth Labs as the responsible party. 

When Commonwealth Labs left, the project was taken up by the Edgeware Agency under the management of Thom Ivy, however for a number of reasons this has stalled - the major issue is around the US regulatory situation.

Upon receiving the listing agreements we reviewed with our UK lawyers and assessed the issues.

The primary issue is that these documents were originally to be signed by Commonwealth Labs as the 'issuer' of EDG. 

Owing to standardised legal agreements, any organisation wishing to engage with a centralised exchange takes on the responsibility of 'the issuer'. 

What this would mean in practice is that were Decent Partners, or any other organisation outside of the US to take on the listing of EDG, then it also takes on the legal liability of being the 'issuer' of EDG.

From our lawyers:
 
> My first more general observation is that under the agreement you would be the issuer. Being an issuer comes with very different and significantly greater risks compared to simply advising on an issuance. My second more specific observation is that, assuming you are doing this in the UK in the name of your UK registered entity, you would likely be considered to be acting as a cryptoasset exchange provider. It is a criminal offence to act as such in the UK without being registered with the UK FCA.
 
**Assessment**

The listing process cannot be fulfilled by the Edgeware Agency due to legal restrictions on US corporations, and nor would we recommend that it is done by this entity, since all liability will then pass to this organisation, and away from Commonwealth Labs. 

There is all of the downside, and none of the upside that CW Labs enjoyed. 

Decent Partners cannot take on this project for the same reasons. 

## Assessment

We recommend that the Edgeware Agency return the agreed funds to the treasury and just as with BuilDAO we reset our positions given a greater understanding of the situation.

This might feel to many like a failure, but it is simply the challenge of operating Edgeware without a central authority. In the short term it is a speed bump, but in the long run it just reminds us that we will have to think differently as a collective given the project's relative strengths. 

## Options moving forward

The options are as follows:

- Commonwealth Labs conclude the listing as the legal issuer. 


This is probably unlikely given the lack of engagement. Chance 1/10

- Decent Partners registers with the FCA as an exchange provider. 


Very unlikely to happen, and certainly not in the near term. Chance 1/10

- We review the options open to establishing an off shore vehicle to manage the listing, which would be owned and operated by trusted EDG members - this is not a small undertaking both in terms of work and the responsibility this entails, so the risk would need to warrant a reward.


Possible, but will need in depth research and working group to weigh up relative merits and learn lessons from other projects. Chance 5/10

- Edgeware itself becomes a legal entity that can sign documents etc, with its entire membership ultimately taking responsibility.  


This is an area of discussion that has been discussed in the Decoding Law series - its possible, but again, the timelines are likely on the order of a few years, if not more. Chance 2/10

- Focus on listing EDG on[ DEXes rather than CEXs](https://komodoplatform.com/en/academy/decentralized-exchange/). 


Long term this is vital for the resilience of the system, we can also seek to learn lessons from other decentralised projects who do not have the benefit of a centralised team. Chance 2/10

IMO the DecredDEX would be the first project I would focus attention on and would need us to fund a team to do a Substrate implementation - [their docs](https://github.com/decred/dcrdex). 

## **Summary** 

In the immediate term I would consider Exchange B to be on hold pending a full reset. 

Edgeware Agency should refund the original payments as best as they can.

As a community we assess the options open to us and see this once more as a chance to learn together and play to our strengths.
