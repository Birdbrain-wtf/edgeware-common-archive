The first big milestone of the Edgeware 2020 has concluded, bringing 6 months of improvements online without major issues! This paves the way for a much simpler upgrade, which will bring us up to the latest version of Substrate, expected within the next 30 days.

**Developers**
- The [BuilDAO Funding Proposal](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/councilmotion/0xc017fa86a38955d6b8ed1ed5f330a9e2dc9c9de628ddb5fb1df63b23efb65fa1-treasuryapproveproposal12) has received council approval. The next steps are two-fold: Making a proposal to the Polkadot Treasury for a contribution of DOTs, and starting up [discussions on what the DAO should fund](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussions/updates-builDAO).


**Technical**
- Edgeware's first upgrade was executed to the Naples release. [See the full list of changes and features in the Release Notes.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/612-edgeware-upgrade-1-release-notes) The next upgrade is expected to be proposed within the next 30 days, and will bring us up to the latest version of Substrate 2.0.
- The Naples release migrated many users' account balances to a new pallet in Substrate, but accounts created within the past 30 days before the update may need to be migrated manually. These accounts will show a zero balance in [a block explorer](https://edgeware.subscan.io/) until they are migrated, you can [resolve this issue on your own with another account with EDG, or use our form to let the Commonwealth team migrate your account for you](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/625-edgeware-postupgrade-account-migration). Migration takes under a minute.
- Alternatively, you can use the PolkadotJS Apps UI to handle the migration yourself. Find the Extrinsics page under the Developer section and create a system.migrateAccounts transaction to handle it. If you need some EDG, please hop into our Element channel to have it done for you.

**Community and Working Groups**
- The Brand Working Group continues to refine the motivations and narratives of the Edgeware community into the brand. Richard, aka MrBulb, [has collected this material into a post on Commonwealth](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/623-edgeware-positioning-update). Next, the group intends to propose a budget for the next 2 quarters to fund the development costs of a new Edgeware site ([which you can view and comment on here](https://johan317017.invisionapp.com/console/share/Y81J6A8ES4/480651492)) as well as programs [like the Artist Residency proposed by Thom Ivy](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/537-artist-residencies-and-more-on-edgeware). This group is growing and hosts weekly discussions with fascinating thinkers in Edgeware leading the path to broad awareness. It also contains talent from creative studios, art and research outfits, and other protocols like Decred. To get involved, join the [Telegram](https://t.me/joinchat/E5sXQxpO99lCCeX4btHplw).
- Many Edgeware community members and Commonwealth team have responded to an inquiry on the motivations for developing Edgeware, and [the remarks are comprehensive and interesting.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/536-why-are-you-here-edgeware-founding-values-culture-and-focus)
- The BWG is looking for artists, curators, and art organizations that may want to partner or participate in [a concepted Artist Residency program.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/600-candidate-list-for-artist-residencies-and-orgspersons-of-interest) Currently, the Brand Working Group is facilitating discussion with MOCDA, [the Museum of Contemporary Digital Art](https://www.mocda.org/) in order to partner on the project.
- The Governance Working Group is becoming more active - they will focus on issues like ensuring participation, metagovernance norms, and governance techniques like voting and tally types. The [Commonwealth subforum is a good place to check their current work -](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussions/wg-governance) and they also have [a Telegram group for interested members.](https://t.me/EdgewareGWG)
