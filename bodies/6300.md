Hello EdgVerse.

Every so often I post / repost / reshare the original Edgeware "[Why are you here?](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/536-why-are-you-here-edgeware-founding-values-culture-and-focus)" post which was my initial attempt at getting to know the existing Edgeware community - its members, their values and their hopes and dreams.

From the original post:

> With the project in the very earliest stages, asynchronous conversations the norm, and decentralised governance a work in progress, it is important to utilise the tools available to drive increasing community participation to aid decision making, transparency and to better plot a path forward together.

Given the community has evolved a long way since that first post nearly 3 years ago and we now have many new members, projects and perspectives and have learned a huge amount together, it makes sense to break the post back open with a few small additions to give us all a renewed perspective on the aspirations and intentions the assembled contributors.  

In the end, we're building this weird organism together, as independent contributors, so the better we can understand each other, the greater our collective intelligence.

Here are a few prompts to help things along - feel free to ignore entirely... its up to you how you answer, there are literally no right or wrong answers. Even if you don't have a strong view, share your thoughts - or your feelings, every comment is valuable. 

- What first attracted you about the project?
- What problems do you see Edgeware addressing?
- What is Edgeware **not** for?
- What other projects do you follow or think Edgeware could improve upon? 
- What lessons do you think Edgeware can learn from other projects?
- What founding values or principles do you think Edgeware should have? 
- What areas in the wider crypto-sphere interest you?
- What areas or communities outside of crypto interest you?
- What does success look like to you? (what kind of a world do you see?)
- What does failure look like to you?


Go :)
