## Campaigns’ moto: 
Welcoming citizenships through a sustainable approach.

## **Problem: **
**	**In current scenarios, crypto communities are very diversified and so are the influencers’ networks. The most engaging activity for crypto projects across any influencer community is AMA! The current AMA culture has the following negative points:
- One time reward-motivated mindset (of both AMA participants and Influencers)
- Short-term yield for projects
- Reward oriented mindset and generic questions
- Time bounded structure and thus only a few questions getting answered
- Very few people get incentivized


## Solution:
- In order to harness sustainable reach from AMA, we aim to divide it into two subtypes: 
1. (conventional) AMA: team members will answer questions
2. reverse-AMA: team members will ask questions


- The sole purpose of reverse-AMA is to motivate AMA participants to research/learn about Edgeware and thus having a better understanding will yield more detail-oriented questions in the earlier conventional AMA round.


- Two dedicated campaigns are proposed as follows to incentivize AMA participants from both rounds and the Influencer of the respective community:


**	_Citizenship 101 Walkthrough Campaign:_**
**	**This campaign aims to incentivize the path which will be guided by the walkthrough series tutorials. The process flow of the campaign will be as follows:
1. Taking telegram id and EDG address inputs of the AMA participants pre-AMA using a google form.
2. AMA participants will get a low amount of EDG (approx 2 USD worth) as a token of citizenship. 
3. Then they will try out to-do tasks mentioned corresponding to every walkthrough video. (Tasks will be simple and based on the corresponding walkthrough video. Examples: setting on-chain identity, staking the smallest amount, quality comment on commonwealth.im portal, etc.)
4. There will be another google form to submit all their walkthrough tasks submissions. After submitting this form, they will be rewarded with some airdrop level amount (approx 8 USD worth EDG).


**	_Influencer 101 Validator Walkthrough Campaign:_**
**	**Rather than paying an upfront huge amount, we will pitch the influencers the sustainable benefits(commission) they will be getting by setting up a validator. The influencer will be getting the following exclusive perks:
1. Complete assistance apart from the validator walkthrough video(s).
2. Shout out to their validator on Edgeware Validators’ channel.
3. Technical assistance for future node upgrades.
