# ## Kabocha Seeds - Understanding the concept and the emerging design thinking

_Note: the following is a work in progress, everything we are doing is emerging organically. Please ask questions, poke at holes, tell me what is dumb and present your own ideas..._

Kabocha 'Seeds' was one of the central narrative themes in the lead up to and during the successful crowdloan, but so far we have only shared a little bit of information about them. That isn't because we had some sort of incredible masterplan we were slowly drip feeding , but simply because the concept was emerging organically.

From the start, Kabocha has evolved as a response to the challenges we faced and are facing in Edgeware - it is the result of the day to day experience of governing, coordinating and advancing a decentralised network of contributors. 

Currently Edgeware's citizens primarily rely on 'identity' as provided by an EDG address linked to a Commonwealth account - [here is mine](https://gov.edgewa.re/account/jXC7ghviUzVcVySg3eD8prB7C9m6VzK6cw2MTyMTDTUye5q/). 

Rather than developing an 'identity protocol' and then onboarding 'identities', Edgeware is going about things the other way around. We are creating trusted relationships, and building credibility through good old fashioned contribution - it is a meritocracy in the classical sense.

However I don't own that identity. My 'ID' sits on a Commonwealth Labs server.

What is more, I don't have any way to backup any of those posts, or comments, or all the backlinks should the forum disappear - and if it did, there would be no fingerprint on the Edgeware blockchain as a testament to the information having existed.   

If CW decided to shut down their support for Substrate chains, or they changed focus, or shut down, we would all lose the very essence of Edgeware, the hundreds of posts and the thousands of comments that constitute our emerging collective intelligence.

Right now data on CW is not portable, we have asked for months for it to be made open, which seems in direct contravention of all Web3/crypto principles and introduces the sort of platform risk not dissimilar to building a community on an existing social network.

Irrespective of whether CW do open up the data, the bigger point is that to ensure the persistence and durability of identity in the EdgVerse, namely the emerging ecosystem of projects and chains that are spawned from the Edgeware network. we need our own sovereign identity solution.

## (Re)Introducing Seeds

Seeds is a novel take on 'self-sovereign identity' that rethinks the whole concept of identity from a first principles perspective. It starts by posing the question what exactly is 'identity'?

_You_ is not a fixed position. _You_ are not coordinates on a map. We understand this intutively, and yet every aspect of society, work and digital life is built around the idea of identity as being fixed, simply because nicely packaged humans who behave predictably makes for a great business model. The most insidious aspect of social networks is not the fixed identity, but it is how the algoritmns create prisons of choice, that not only restrict identity, but shape its evolution to ends that a user is not fully aware of.

There are three key insights that form the basis of Seeds.

- Identity is non-transferable
- Identity is not fixed, but emergent 
- Identity is context dependent 

Working through them: 

## Identity is non-transferable

It might be obvious, but your identity is yours. It is not something you can transfer, nor sell, nor transform or trade or copy or paste. You are you. By utilising RMRK's non-transferable NFTs we ensure that each Seed sticks with its owner and evolves based on their contributions.

## Identity is not fixed, but emergent

Your identity is not your Twitter handle, it is not your Facebook account, your LinkedIn resume, it is not your name, your passport, Your identity is who you are, your being, the myriad inputs that evolve to become your personality and the context that is stitched together around you in a fantastically complex web of social relationships. 

Seeds (real seeds) don't just grow into flourishing plants, they need the right conditions. They need good soil - (a substrate), root systems, nutrients, helpful bacteria, mycelia, water - a whole companion ecosystem of other life that enables the whole thing to thrive. 

So if there is a masterplan, you can see we're just borrowing it from mother nature. 

Seeds append contributions to a contributor’s Seed as either Roots, or as Shoots in the form of NFTs. 

Roots are things that are impossible or very hard to change or move, you know, like actual roots. 

One example of a Root is if you contributed KSM in the Kabocha crowdloan. 

Other examples will be contribution to the network by proposing and delivering a project. 

Shoots are also NFTs that append to a contributor's Seed, and these are likely to be easier to transfer and may have financial value, but likely in terms of a particular skill or service your identity offers and that others can recruit you for. 

Based on your ever evolving Roots and Shoots, each Seed will become its own generative algorithm, which continually updates and evolves your Seeds visual appearance. Which brings us to the final part of the identity trinity... 

## Identity is context dependent

No man is an island, as the famous phrase goes. We do not exist in a vacuum, carefully constructing our own identities in a safe space, insulated from all the other identities, only to pop out, to meet the other IDs, like some sterile lab rats raised in isolation.

Our identities are a result of our interactions with others. The relationships we have are the web that gives us a sense of place, of time, of family. This is what community is. 

So your Seed will not just develop individually based on your contributions, but it will also do so in relation to other Seeds that you connect and collaborate with. 

If you're part of a project team (a multisig), then these relationships contribute to the evolution of your Roots. 

The projects you create together contribute to the evolution of your Shoots and will gradually expand to include Rights and Access permissions within the EDGverse of projects and chains.

## You are the generative algorithm

Some of the most valuable NFT projects in Ethereum are generative in nature. The value is ascribed to the fact that the logic for the creation of the art, is held entirely on-chain with the code contained in a smart contract enough to generate the image/art as long as Ethereum continues to produce blocks. There is no 'off-chain' media and it is this persistence and resilience that lends this design of art its potential value. Check [this post by FingerprintsDAO](https://fingerprintsdao.medium.com/launching-prints-dao-why-on-chain-art-matters-27b289565a56) to understand this thesis more. 

However these projects use pre-determined algorithms, that are a reflection of the creator or designer, with each minted artwork the result of the same functions. So we can say a 10,000 mint of generative pieces ensures each one is unique, but only within the confines of the programming established by the launch team.

As we've already established, identity is emergent and identity is context dependent and therefore the algorithm that makes you, You, is down to your contributions and connections not some programmer you'll never meet, from a company you have no connection to or control over. 

The final layer of Seeds comes from the way we will utilise the inputs of your Roots and Shoots - your ongoing contributions and connections - to create smart contract contained generative algorithms on Edgeware that continually updates and evolves your Seeds visual appearance and sets the scene for a whole new design space, as the foundation to our own metaverse.
