Sublime is a new lending protocol aimed at providing a more diversified and robust lending experience by:

1. Tokenizing borrow positions to provide users exposure to price volatility of the underlying collateral and to self-liquidate.
2. Creating fixed size reserve pools to enable instant liquidations, provide cover in case of adverse events, and enable LPs to onboard new collaterals.
3. Enabling undercollateralized loans against future tokens earned through collateral staking, yield-enhanced tokens, or locked with Sublime to earn SUBL governance tokens.
