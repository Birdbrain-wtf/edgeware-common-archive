**Summary: **A weekly meme tournament with a finale at the end of every month to encourage users to participate and create viral content to spread awareness.

---


This isn't much of a governance proposal as it is one to the Brand Working Group and just an idea to help engage the community, spread awareness, and bring us all together once a week for something fun. Looking for feedback on this, specifically around award amounts and timing. The goal was to be as inclusive as possible and use amounts that would actually generate great memes. This can be modified over time to alter the talent and results, hopefully.

**Timing:**
- The window for submission lasts 1 week, ending Wednesday 00:00 UTC, when judging happens.



**Instructions:**
1. Create a meme that best conveys something about Edgeware. You can make a static image or GIF, or MP4. 
2. [Add this meme to our Collaborate Gallery ](https://www.are.na/edgeware-community/edgeware-memes)
3. Post it here to be included.


**Topics:**
- Values
- Use Cases / Solutions
- Technology
- Inside Jokes (Edg-Lords!)
- Community
- Market / Price Action


**Judging:**
Thom Ivy, myself, as the provider of the prizes, will judge entries as ranked choice, with prize winners being 1-3rd.

**Awards:**
1st: 1000 EDG
2nd: 500 EDG
3rd: 200 EDG
_If there is a tie, the award is split._

**Delivery:**
Thom will cover rewards.


**🤯Monthly Bonus Round🎉**
The top scorer of the months 4-weeks of entries will receive a bonus prize of 3000 EDG, judged on the last day of the month by the meme with the highest points. If there is a tie, the award is split.

**🎏Marketing Opportunities for Other Projects**
Meme Duets: Other protocols and projects can sponsor a week of our Meme contests, and users can try and create comparative or contrasting memes for higher prizes. Contact Thom to run one of these.
