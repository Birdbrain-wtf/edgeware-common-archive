Out of nothing comes something. And out of something comes everything.

This is the first formalised proposal and request for funding from the emerging Edgeware Brand Working Group (BWG) which has been hard at work collating, structuring and organising initiatives that will leverage the EDG treasury, governance and emerging infrastructure to scale out the networks awareness, use and impact.

The full draft document can be found [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1DPodqUajc9yzTG4iVGUSMekOtj3BTLMUwYWUUIuZI4M/edit#) - comments are encouraged.

## **Summary**
A request for development funding for the Brand Working Group that will cover ongoing work on key Phase 1 proposals. 

This will pay for both time spent and the creation of materials, products and assets that will be signed off in stages with clear deliverables and reporting for each. 

Each contributor to the group will invoice the BWG Fund for work done at a pre-agreed hourly rate and outline clearly their contributions and how time has been spent. 

There will be a monthly strategic, delivery and budgetary review and the aim is to make this process as simple, flexible and transparent as possible. 

## **Key projects include:**

- Project 1 - Website redevelopment
- Project 2 - Brand review, design, guide
- Project 3 - Video / original content series planning
- Project 4 - MetaDAO
