### Development
- The first public announcement of a protocol launching on Edgeware happened this week. [Sublime Finance](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/636-announcing-sublime), a DeFi protocol who brings undercollateralized loans and other novel primitives to the network:
1. A reserve pool to enable instant liquidations, provide cover in case of adverse events, and enable LPs to onboard new collaterals.
2. Tokenizing borrow positions to provide users exposure to price volatility of the underlying collateral and to self-liquidate.
3. Enabling undercollateralized loans against future tokens earned through collateral staking, yield-enhanced tokens, or locked with Sublime to earn SUBL governance tokens.
- [Read the post on Commonwealth.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/636-announcing-sublime)


### Technical
- Reminder: The Naples release migrated many users' account balances to a new pallet in Substrate, but accounts created within the past 30 days before the update may need to be migrated manually. These accounts will show a zero balance in [a block explorer](https://edgeware.subscan.io/) until they are migrated, you can [resolve this issue on your own with another account with EDG, or use our form to let the Commonwealth team migrate your account for you](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/625-edgeware-postupgrade-account-migration). Migration takes under a minute. Otherwise, we are working on an automated process for migrating any remaining accounts that have not been updated, which we expect to finish in another week.


- **Reminder: Commonwealth will not DM to ask for your private key/mnemonic.**


- Finality on the chain is stalling and there is no fallback mechanism at this moment. We are working to handle finalisation issues through two methods: a hardcoded future block to restart the finality mechanism and a runtime upgrade that exposes functionality to unstick the finality mechanism through the runtime. You can [track updates and code](https://github.com/hicommonwealth/edgeware-node) with respect to this work in the develop branch of the edgeware-node.


### Working Groups

**Builders Working Group:**
-  Matej, aka Yangwao, Edgeware's newly approved Developer Advocate, is now leading developer community building and advocacy! He will be sharing tutorials, updating docs, and planning events via the new Edgeware Developer Twitter at [@EDG_Developers](https://twitter.com/EDG_Developers).


**Brand Working Group:** 
- Mr.Bulb posted [the group's drafted proposal for funding a budget](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/643-brand-working-group-proposal-draft) for the next year or so. It includes funding for the work already conducted by early contributors, website development costs, artist residency funding, and much more. After a discussion period, it will go on-chain as development on the site is underway, and estimated to be delivered within 2 months.


- The [notes from the opening planning call of the Artist Residency program was shared,](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/639-artistic-residencies-community-call-w-mocda-haute-to-death) where the Museum of Contemporary Digital Art agrees to be a partner in the program and support both artists and technological partnerships in the process.


**Artist Working Group:**
-  The Brand Working Group is leading the creation of the artist residency program, but we are also coordinating the launch of a new artist working group. This program will resemble an [Arts Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_council), but will also work to design new solutions and systems to challenges faced by the artistic community. Currently, [the best way to get involved here is to join the Brand Working Group and stay in the loop.](https://t.me/joinchat/E5sXQxpO99lCCeX4btHplw) The group is starting to take shape, with Thom Ivy, Mr.Bulb, the Museum of Contemporary Digital Art, and Detroit-based [art collective Haute to Death](https://www.instagram.com/hautetodeath/) leader Jon Dones launching the efforts.
