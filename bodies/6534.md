_**Challenges and learnings**_

_Our main challenge has been twofold - firstly, figuring out how to integrate RMRK NFTs into New-Futures given we are working with Webflow rather than react as the main site - this includes the login and other interesting functionality like Collab-Land. _

_The second challenge has been considering how best to connect Edgeware and Kusama/Kabocha without specific chain bridges. Edgeware as a solo chain is not able to send assets natively to Kusama or Kabocha, but we have figured out a nifty workaround that leverages the wonderful native interoperability of Substrate. _

_It is a solution we can use for all our future projects and enables Edgeware to maintain its solo-chain independence, but benefit in turn with cross-chain interoperability. _

_When you generate a Substrate public/private key pair, this account is what generates chain specific addresses, which is something you might have noticed with the option to ‘allow on all chains’ within the Polkadot.js browser extension…_

_**![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/3FqP4HIRwpLJcOmnomQD0Dapcej1oqTLF0UQHwPlz3nyJTICym49BotvUmQf98IRPRJ22FTViaPa6jc_l8nXonbmxEtUBscIMuACgLd1fEtb7oaJaiziNnEVsEmXAut4k6qU7H9aVfQaGQA0NpQLdfo)**_

_What this means in practice is that your Edgeware address can also generate a related Kusama address, or a Kabocha address for that matter. _

_In fact this is the way that crowdloans work- you send KSM from a Kusama address, and then receive your tokens in the address of the new chain._

_In practice this opens up fun ways for us to utilise cross-chain utility, without needing bridges. _

_We can mint RMRK NFTs on Kusama and give them directly to EDG holders, the same for KAB, by sending them to an address you, and only you can access. _

_In the short term, this solves many problems for us and indeed for Edgeware: unlocking cross-chain communication by using the most fluid part of the tech stack: you!_

_But this doesn’t mean we don’t want to mint on Edgeware - and as soon as this functionality becomes available - we will be the first to jump on this. Edge-native NFTs? Yes please!!_

**Challenges and learnings**

_Building out the network of artists to feature and collaborate with. _

_We are currently curating our community - the focus is on quality members, rather than numbers, using personal meetups and in-real-life gatherings to build a stronger foundation.  _

_We are learning from the crypto community, that huge groups of people with mixed opinions and incentives for involvement can be ineffective. So ‘grassroots’ has most definitely been our approach. Evidently, we have learnt that this approach is a lot slower - but is definitely creating a stronger foundation, and real sense of shared direction. _

_As part of the ongoing research and development of our creative platform and the process of being a funded member of the Edgeware community, it is essential to our collective development and understanding to record and communicate the challenges and learnings we have experienced throughout the process._

_As we are now at the halfway point in our project we thought this would be a good time to evaluate the process and illustrate the challenges of building a creative network from a grassroots level._

**The Creative Process**

_For any project that is building in unchartered territory and exploring ideas that implement new technology in innovative ways, the process is inherently more fluid and the development of the project through each milestone is far less linear than in a traditional, tried and tested workflow._

_Instead of hitting each objective in our first milestone before moving onto the next, we have had to adapt the workflow to accommodate all of the moving parts. This is an experience that has been mirrored across every project funded by Edgeware so far._

_We have instead hit percentages of each objective in both our first and second milestone. As a result of this, we have reordered the milestones to explain where we are in the process so we can release the second part of the funding which will allow us to complete the project._

_We do however suggest that future funding applications with a staggered release of funding should be evaluated on a percentage basis as opposed to with linear milestones. For example, if there are 10 milestones and milestone 1-4 are 100% complete, milestone 7 + 8 are 50% complete that the project overall would have successfully completed half or its development and the next stage of funding can be released._

_Alongside this, we should more clearly articulate and understand these funded projects as highly experimental research and development - with an emphasis on an enormous amount of research. _

_**Onboarding Artists**_

_A core focus of New–Futures is to bridge the gap between the real world and the metaverse. We are exploring this objective both in this initial stage and in our plans for the next phases of the project. An integral part of this ideology is bringing together artists and musicians and facilitating their exploration into this new space. We have been growing an exciting community of creatives who we have successfully onboarded as part of the New-Futures launch but it has definitely been a more complicated conversation to have when compared to a traditional collaboration. For many, the decision to get involved has been far more complicated due to fragmented understanding of the current industry, technology, environmental impact etc._
