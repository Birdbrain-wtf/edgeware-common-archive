# Decent Partners Network Services: Project status report August 2022

## Summary

This status report seeks to update the community with the status of all curated projects, their status, their challenges and path to completion.

## What are Network Services (NS), Network Service Fees (NSF), and Network Service Providers? (NSP)

Decent Partners introduced the Network Services concept to Edgeware around 7 months ago - read more [here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/3650-decent-partners-an-introduction-to-network-services). 

Network Services sought to address a major bottleneck in the project by creating a clear structure to bridge the existing community with its emerging culture governance structures, onchain processes and the sourcing, development and stewarding of new or emerging contributors. 

In this model, the curator would receive 10% of the proposal on top of the team's budget as a fee paid by the network. 

This 10% covers the risk/reward of a Network Service Provider putting up a 5% bond and developing and managing a proposal to approval and a successful delivery.

In some ways it is an approach analogous to Kusama's 'curator' bounties, except it does not use the bounty pallet and instead focuses more on the off-chain elements of curation. 

Its development has aligned with proposed funding for ongoing network research and development that provides insights and strategies for new waves of projects. 

![](https://commonwealth-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/16f5cf49-5950-4144-bf0f-e16ed0f870d9.1661076162755)

Recently the model has been critiqued by community members with regard to the funding structure, especially in regard to capping funding, the representation of the best interests of the network and other areas of remuneration. 

Given Network Services is, like everything, an emergent and organic process, it feels like a good time for the community to assess the positives and negatives and to evolve Network Services 1.0, to Network Services 2.0 which can become a better understood and implemented framework alongside other initiatives such as the[ Solo Contributors Union ](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/4289-solo-contributors-union-scu)(SCU). 

This will also tie into the larger strategic development of _Network Publics_, _Network Public Service Media_ and the unlocking of Kusama and Polkadot as aligned treasuries within a new category of parachain development for Edgeware's emerging chain farm.

[Community film](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussion/2221-edgeware-community-film-funding) 

This project has been in funded development for a year and production for 10 months and has been perhaps the most difficult of all curated projects.

The core challenge has been this is a film about Edgeware's contributors and the overall vision of the project.

In the time we have been working on it, we have had three versions:

1. Version 1 had Commonwealth involvement and contribution then they left the project.
2. Version 2 was around the time of the Edgeware Agency and had Thom central to the vision and narrative.
3. Version 3 is currently where we sit, which exists at a point where the Edgeware Agency has been dissolved and the project is fully decentralising its contributor model.


In addition to this, we began the project in a bull market, with Edgeware's token price and treasury at least 10-20x higher and a completely different market sentiment. 

Now we are in the bottom of a bear market, with many theories disproven and a completely different sentiment that is far better for Edgeware and indeed for us to tell a more interesting story with the community film.

In this time, we have also gained a large number of new contributors, each of whom can add their own thinking and perspectives and also are reaching the conclusion of product launches such as Kabocha, New-Futures, GMI in Crypto Media among others.

Grass-roots community moderation, Pizza Drops and other initiatives are also adding colour to the community.

We are 60% complete on the film with many of the most difficult aspects complete, James and Jamie are now focused on the contributions of the current and longer term focused contributors.

## Summary 

Network Services has focused on addressing core issues that Commonwealth and the Edgeware Agency were unable to during their tenures:

- Core development: Recruitment of Flipchan/ERUP5)
- Parachain development: Various contributors/Kabocha
- Liquidity: Exchange B listing and others
- Awareness: Pet/Joon, GMI in Crypto Media
- Arts and Culture: New-Futures creator platform
- Funding: Unlocking Kusama and Polkadot treasuries for EDG incubated teams
- Vision: Network Publics, a new category of Substrate parachain 


In the case of liquidity, this work is still ongoing with both Edgeware and Kabocha and we hope to resolve the challenges for each in the next phase of work.
