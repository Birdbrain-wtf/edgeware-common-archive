## **Problem Statement **

- People want to participate in Edgeware governance but that can only happen if they are aware of the basic steps. We all know Edgeware governance is a bit complex and tricky to understand.
- Edgeware DAO is complex for new users, in fact even for those already involved in crypto
- The community needs to be well-informed and confident in participating in projects and investing their time and capital
- Community participation in governance
- Understanding the proposals that go through voting by governance 
- Understand technical updates that are published by the Edgeware technical team
- Understand the Substrate portal and its tools
- Due to the growth of the Edgeware ecosystem and its subsystems, there is a humongous increase in the amount of information generated. It’s important to create content that quantifies them in digestible chunks. As I suggest in my proposal, we aim to bring compiled information to the community in a consistent timely manner.


## **Solution**

- Our goal is to create video tutorials that’ll guide beginners to participate in the DAO ecosystem by explaining each topic step by step. 


- The videos will solely focus on explaining everything in a clear and concise manner and make sure that all the information comes from trustworthy sources.


- Our goal is to create a complete walkthrough of the EDgeware DAO where all of its components are explained in a simple way making sure the Hindi-speaking community gets started in the ecosystem.



## **Proposal’s Objective and Goals**

- **What is our objective?**

As a team, our goal is to educate users quickly and efficiently so they can participate in the governance and update themselves around the ecosystem.

- **How the work is done? **

Our goal is to produce more quality videos and write content in both languages that resonate with the masses. Our general process is to create a video that includes the following steps:

- **Topic Research**
1. This field includes a lot of research based on the most frequently asked questions on youtube and websites i.e. answerthepublic.com etc. This also includes the questions that people ask in each video based on its content
2. Finding topics that are relevant and will help viewers understand this ecosystem


- **References**
1. Official Websites i.e. edgewa.re, [https://gov.edgewa.re/etc.](https://gov.edgewa.re/etc.)
2. Third-Party Websites i.e. Investopedia, exchanges, google searches, etc.


- **Content Writing**
1. Collecting all the facts around a topic
2. Finding quality third-party content and making the content relatable
3. Writing a factual piece around the topic


- **Video**
1. Finding relatable news pieces to add as props
2. Video shortening or removing unnecessary content
3. We manage the video theme and icon color to make the video attractive


- **Video editing**
1. Logo creations, adding animations and layout adjustments, etc
2. Arranging on-screen items to make the video attractive


- **Video uploading**
1. Research and insert topic-specific hashtags in the about and description sections of these videos
2. Uploading and verifying the youtube video for content and final round checks


- **Community Feedback**
1. Ingesting and appropriately responding to public comments
2. Sending responses to community questions asked in [support](support@describedot.com).



## **Generally, all episodes will follow the same content structure:**
- **Intro**
- **verify that the information and the process shown are completely true through Constants and different sources;**
- **step-by-step guide on how to perform the corresponding actions;**
- **show the most frequent errors that we can find when performing these actions;**
- **invite to watch related videos and goodbye.**
