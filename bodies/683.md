Generationally influential brands of recent memory have been those that have understood that building long term trust is not about marketing, it is about riding the wave of cultural movements and then seeding that story through influential networks that share common values.

These brands were born into and made famous in an era when TV was dominant and a cultural narrative could be defined and controlled from the top down. 

There is a mistake made by most product companies, especially in a social era, that marketing is analogous to building a community, when in fact the latter is far more about cooperation and collaboration, taking a role closer to the facilitator of a community’s intrinsic ambitions, rather than authors of a marketing message.

## Community Networks
Blockchains are evolving into coordination systems for _borderless community networks_, where cultural narratives are the key driver in the winner takes all tendency of network effects. 

Assuming technical competence, it is likely that over time a cultural narrative also known as a _movement_, will be a far better indicator of crypto currency valuation than any direct comparison of the monetary policy or capabilities of the underlying technology.

## How do you create a movement?
The development, production and distribution of compelling original content that can cut through the noise online to reach and engage newcomers is vital to kickstarting a movement. 

Existing crypto-media has historically been of poor quality, often retaining a speculative focus, especially with YouTubers and podcasters who have realised they can make a quick buck through affiliate revenue and kickbacks by shilling projects in easy to understand ways. 

Whilst effective at hyping projects, this approach is very much short-termist and does not build any fundamental cultural value that can attract and retain talent motivated by a compelling mission, strong narrative and original aesthetic. 

Existing crypto-news sites such as CoinDesk and Decrypt can be valuable for announcements but do not build strong relationships with core communities and they ultimately control the messaging and route to the audience. 

One of Ethereum's most successful approaches was to onboard a creative community, rather than just hardcore developers. [The Ethereal](https://www.etherealsummit.com/about) event series shows how effective this approach can be and provided foundations for the NFT movement, however they have never created long running and consistently engaging media content. 

Right now there is a gaping hole in crypto-media, to create standout media brands that elevate the standards in the whole space. 

## Foundations for a movement
The Brand Working Group is currently [distilling key insights](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/650-edgeware-narrative-development) into the project's foundational messaging, identity, ethos and values that will form the basis of the website and also provide a platform for the development and production of a range of audio visual content.

This content will be distributed across the existing website, social channels and emerging Web3 media spaces 

With the funding of original media that connects with audiences around the world, there is the potential to build Edgeware into a leading crypto-project that will attract the next generation of developers, artists and entrepreneurs. 

## Where this can go
Further down the line we imagine a Web3 enabled [streaming platform](https://livepeer.org) that would use Edgeware's on-chain identity to return users control of their data and offer exciting new ways to engage with, contribute to and share in the value created by crypto native media. 

When considering the [BuildDAO project](https://www.notion.so/hicommonwealth/BuilDAO-Incubator-Paper-35d451f814014f9cbff96b6c8ab443a4) that bridges to the real world, there is also the potential for secondary revenues to come from streaming platforms (an Edgeware funded documentary about systems change that is then sold to Netflix for example) that could flow back from the_ 'real world' _to the project treasury, making this a cash-flow positive and regenerative initiative in the long run, whilst also scaling reach and awareness.
