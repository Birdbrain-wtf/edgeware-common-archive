### Development
- The [latest release of Edgeware,](https://github.com/hicommonwealth/edgeware-node/releases/tag/v3.1.0) 'New York', has been advanced as a [democracy proposal](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/democracyproposal/12-0x249623e9ec28e752a0ce6355f7d5527956799a50e28228958497f3a9bcc0bd1f). This upgrade includes:
- [Assets pallet integration](https://substrate.dev/rustdocs/v2.0.0/pallet_assets/index.html)
- [Bounties pallet integration](https://github.com/paritytech/substrate/issues/5713)
- [WASM contracts pallet upgraded to Ink! 3.0](https://github.com/paritytech/ink)
- New GRANDPA extrinsics for noting finality stalling
- Pallet weights consistent with Substrate's default weights
- Identity migration to recover identities at improperly hashed keys

**                           Parameter changes:**
- Max locks per account set to 50
- Max council members set to 100
- Bounty deposit base set to 10 EDG
- Bounty deposit payout delay set to 7 days
- Bounty curator deposit set to 50% of bounty
- Bounty minimum value set to 100 EDG


- Reminder: The Naples release migrated many users' account balances to a new pallet in Substrate, but accounts created within the past 30 days before the update may need to be migrated manually. These accounts will show a zero balance in [a block explorer](https://edgeware.subscan.io/) until they are migrated, you can [resolve this issue on your own with another account with EDG, or use our form to let the Commonwealth team migrate your account for you](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/625-edgeware-postupgrade-account-migration). Migration takes under a minute. Otherwise, we are working on an automated process for migrating any remaining accounts that have not been updated, which we expect to finish in another week.
- **Reminder: Commonwealth will not DM to ask for your private key/mnemonic.**


- Finality on the chain is stalling and there is no fallback mechanism at this moment. We are working to handle finalization issues through two methods: a hardcoded future block to restart the finality mechanism and the above-mentioned New York runtime upgrade that exposes functionality to unstick the finality mechanism through the runtime. You can [track updates and code](https://github.com/hicommonwealth/edgeware-node) with respect to this work in the develop branch of the edgeware-node. Commonwealth Labs expects finality to resume within 2-3 weeks. Some users may wish to avoid exchanges at this time, as exchange logic may prevent withdrawal during a finality stall.

**Brand Working Group**
- The Brand Working Group [published a draft of its budget proposal](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/643-brand-working-group-proposal-draft) and plans for the next year or so of work. Council has asked to have it broken down into bundles for funding as the previous works are completed successfully. The group, while ambitious, has experienced workers in design, media, banking and regularly hosts weekly planning and ideation sessions that you can join, all are welcome. Commentary is requested in the proposal draft.


- The Brand Working Group [elaborates on their promotional video project](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/683-edgeware-original-audio-and-video-development), to be performed by UK Media production venue [https://lemonademoney.tv/](https://lemonademoney.tv/), and would create several media including a headlining video for use on the new Edgeware website. The proposal requests 12.5k USD in EDG over the next several months.
