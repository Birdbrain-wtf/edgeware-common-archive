Edgeware community gave a significant response to the Moderators' Recruitment Quiz v1 and many of the community members wanted to start their contribution journey as mods. But with the limited number of community mods' roles for Edgeware, the community came to the consensus of onboarding mods for Kabocha. Some community members also requested hosting this Mods' Onboarding Quiz after Kabocha's Tech AMA to have a better understanding of Kabocha.
As we hosted the Kabocha Tech AMA recently, here we are with this draft for the community moderation of Kabocha's TG, Discord and Element groups/server/rooms. 

Quoting the role description from the v1 quiz draft:
> "Community Moderator is not just a role but one of the best learning opportunities if you want to be an Edgeverse expert! This role could help anyone to build an ecosystem reputation and to guide them for further career enhancements as Edgeware contributors in several domains."

You’re a perfect fit if you have:
- Intermediate knowledge of Edgeverse (especially Kabocha)
- Good communication skills
- Social skills
- Calm mindset and patience 
- Creative thinking
- Flexibility with work timings


Initially, we can start with 4 mods and increase them in future as per the demand.
The community-wide recruitment quiz will be hosted on the Discord server in the #quiz-arena channel. This quiz will have any questions related to Kabocha and basic questions from the whole Edgeverse.
There will be 2-day training sessions for the new community moderators before they take over the responsibility.

Quiz time: 6 pm UTC on September 11th, 2022
Quiz hosting place: [https://discord.gg/FMZJxmqsKm](https://discord.gg/FMZJxmqsKm)
Time per question: 30 sec
Total number of questions: 30
{There will be a 100000 ECEP pool for all quiz participants (to be distributed equally).}

Number of moderators to be selected: 4
Trial period: 1 month
Incentives per moderator for the trial period: 300 USD equivalent in $EDG per month until the democracy launch on Kabocha [with 1:1 proportional network share recoup]
(The drafted amount is low considering the current engagement across Kabocha. However, this can be scaled based on the increase in community engagement.)
Total treasury expenditure per month: 1200 USD equivalent in $EDG  
Opted for SCU: Will be collectively decided by the mods.

**Responsibilities:**
- Answer/assist/engage across TG, Discord and Element
- Remove any kind of spam


**Optional deliverables:**
- Increase community-wide engagement
- Draft potential tweets
- Any community events
- {The sky's the limit for ideas}


Anytime after the end of the trial period, the mods can scale their incentives based on their contributions using 'Role Upgrade Proposals'. 
{Example of 'Role Upgrade Proposal': [https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/6311-role-upgrade-proposal-senior-community-moderators](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/6311-role-upgrade-proposal-senior-community-moderators)}

**Reference: **[Community Mods' Recruitment Quiz (v1) for Edgeware](https://gov.edgewa.re/discussion/2938-introducing-moderators-recruitment-quiz-decentralising-community-moderation)
