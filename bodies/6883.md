**Problem**
**Blockchain technology is developing extremely fast. New approaches, blockchains, frameworks, tools and applications appear in the space every day. Effective knowledge sharing is highly important for the crypto community and overall space development. However, the current approach for knowledge sharing in Web3 is proven to be very inefficient.**

**Messengers such as Discord, Telegram, and Slack are the most widely used tools for community knowledge sharing. There are several significant disadvantages of using these tools for community knowledge sharing, particularly Q&A:**
- **Questions have a short window of visibility to the community and disappear in the flow of the new messages in communication threads; **
- **Existing questions are hard to find causing the same questions to be asked repeatedly;**
- **Questions and answers are not indexed by SE engines as Google;**
- **Questions and answers are not curated;**
- **Communities in different messengers and different languages exist in silos.**


**There are asynchronous Q&A tools such as Stack Exchange (Stack Overflow) and Discourse. However, for various reasons these tools are being underutilized. We see several reasons for that.**

**Stack Exchange:**

- **High barrier to entry for the communities. Only the biggest communities have the capacity to start their community on Stack Exchange;**
- **By the time the Stack Exchange community is started, the community is already large enough and used to communicate in Discord, Telegram, or Slack;**
- **There is a chance that Stack Exchange will not allow crypto communities at all.**


**Discourse:**
- **Lack of incentives for users (e.g. such as reputation on Stack Exchange);**
- **Communities are used to communicate in Discord, Telegram, or Slack.**


**There are also philosophical issues with Stack Exchange and Discord where a centralized entity owns collective knowledge of the decentralized communities.**

**Below are short descriptions of the main components of the system.**

**Community knowledge base - the core of the Peeranha protocol implemented on blockchain and Filecoin/IPFS. The knowledge base is publicly available and owned by the community. Users are incentivized to contribute and earn reputation and token rewards.**

**Discord bot - bots for Discord that allows interacting with Peeranha protocol from Discord.**

**Telegram bot - bot for Telegram that allows interacting with Peeranha protocol directly from Telegram.**

**Auto-translation bot - process that instantly translates Peeranha content to various languages and posts translations on Peeranha.**

**Auto-answer AI + NLP - AI that can answer questions from users based knowledge base on Peeranha.**

**As you can see, Peeranha brings all of the channels together.**

2. **Telegram bot checks with Auto-Answer AI if there is an answer to John’s question and suggests to John already available answers;**
3. **If the answer from Auto-Answer AI does not help John then Telegram bot posts that question on Peeranha protocol;**
4. **Auto-translation bot translates the question to other languages (e.g. Spanish, Turkish, Chinese, etc);**
5. **Discord bot sees that there is a new question in English and its translations in other languages and posts notifications in appropriate channels in appropriate languages;**

7. **Discord bots picks up the answer from Chen and posts it on Peeranha;**
8. **Auto-translation bot translates the answer to other languages including English;**
9. **John is notified about an answer to his question by Telegram bot;**
10. **Question and answer are documented on Peeranha and can be found by Google SE in all supported languages.**

**Proposal Specification**

**The diagram below shows a high-level overview of the core components of the Peeranha protocol.**

**![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/F9PwcSq-zLMGZB3yUMdEvHQJ2vVBmqivYx35U9ReVTIqvNDCNfnC9hH1h3z9fC6LXLCGGrldWZHyaF_hxnayy3SdXhiLMmn6iY0wyAd2mfPOmAZNalTV9diFXAINl0nAF82dXO-vud7JpEQHY-NFoac)**

**Components descriptions:**
- **Smart contracts - core business logic of the protocol including posts attributes, rules, rewards, and history;**
- **Filecoin / IPFS - it is not feasible to store the text content of the posts on the blockchain as it is expensive and the text itself is not important for the business logic in the smart contracts. For that reason, all text content is stored on Filecoin / IPFS and its hash is recorded on the smart contracts;**
- **Subgraph on the Graph protocol - decentralized search index of aggregated information stored on Peeranha smart contracts and Filecoin/IPFS files for effective querying by the web interface and other end-user applications;**
- **React JS app - web interface allowing to interact with Peeranha protocol ([https://peeranha.io](https://peeranha.io)). **


**The current implementation of Peeranha uses smart contracts on the Polygon blockchain for tracking transaction history, rules, and rewards.**

**It is not a preferred approach for the Edgeware community to store its collective knowledge on another blockchain. The Edgeware blockchain provides fast and cheap transactions and is a good fit for running Peeranha’s smart contracts.**

**This proposal recommends deploying Peeranha smart contracts on the Edgeware blockchain and using them for any Edgeware-related communities instead of Polygon.**
