We currently use Telegram for Working Groups primarily which is convenient:

- Many users have TG
- Many of our community members are most active in Telegram it seems.
- The software is easy to use and quick to load.

It also suffers from bots, not unlike Discord but Discord may have more tools to prevent abuse.

Discord on the other hand, has one MAJOR advantage: Channel management.  We can quickly spin up sub-units of communities whereas Telegram doesn't nest groups.  We also heard from other protocols which had begun using Discord voice chat channels for meetings, rather than Zoom. On the other hand, we have a councilmember who is against Discord for privacy reasons and these also seem salient to a cryptocurrency project.

An aside: It would be amazing to have the 'send to whole channel' function of Slack built into subchannels -- imagining a Working group being able to 'bubble' up an especially important conclusion to decisionmaking to a parent channel. I suppose Telegram uses Forward for this.

We can continue to bridge channels, of course - but opening a discussion on what our native platform should be and also how we use Zoom right now. Some projects prefer the open source Jitsi.
