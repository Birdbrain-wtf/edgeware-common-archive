# **Edgeware Proposal template**

## Why use a template?

You might have a great concept, team and plan but if you can't communicate the key points into clearly defined proposal then all of your hard work will be in vain.

This template outlines the key requirements you should consider when submitting a proposal.

Each numbered section can be used to create a template and give you a checklist to consider.

It's worth noting that every proposal and every team is different - so treat this as a guide, rather than a bureaucratic nightmare.

--------

1. **Date of proposal**
2. If/as the proposal is updated this should be noted
3. Over time we hope to have a form of version control
4. **Author/s of proposal **
5. Name
6. Contact details
7. Telegram / Discord / Element
8. **Proposal summary**
9. TL:DR of key aspects that ensures people have all the key information up top.
10. Paragraph outline
11. Work completed to date
12. Community participation
13. Member/Partner support
14. Funding request
15. **Member/Partner support* **(note this will be a subject of a separate post)
16. Which established community members support this proposal?
17. Are they providing the 5% bonding fees required to propose?
18. Are they also staking (risking) reputation / funds to assure milestones are met?
19. What support are they delivering the proposal?
20. Is this support part of the funding request?
21. What is their relationship to the project and/or team?
22. **Background information**
23. Set the context to the proposal
24. Help us understand where you are coming from
25. **Problem statement**
26. What is the challenge your proposal addresses?
27. **Proposed solution**
28. How does your proposal address the challenge?
29. **Work to date**
30. What stage is the proposal:
31. discussion
32. concept
33. research & development
34. proof of concept
35. emerging project
36. established project
37. **How does the solution work? **
38. Explain things step by step
39. You can use bullet points
40. You can use imagery
41. **What does success look like to you?**
42. Define metrics
43. Define outputs
44. What is possible after you have completed the work that wasn't before?
45. **How does this proposal align Edgeware's vision and values?***
46. Note this is a work in progress
47. **Is this proposal a response to existing work, issues or bounties?**
48. Detail how this relates and link to relevant resources
49. **How have you meaningfully engaged with the community so far?**
50. Examples include:
51. Attended calls
52. Posted to Commonwealth
53. Solved issues or contributed in some other way
54. Delivered work successfully as part of an earlier project or team
55. **How does this involve the community?**
56. Can they input ideas?
57. Will they be able to do new things?
58. How can this be collaborative and participatory?
59. How does the proposal build on the initiatives of others?
60. **Deliverables or Scope of Work (SoW)**
61. What are your key milestones?
62. What do you want to achieve?
63. When do you want to achieve it?
64. **Are you assigning rights or licenses as part of the funding?**
65. Examples can include
66. Access (via token permissioned gateways)
67. Licenses
68. Cashflows
69. Taxes
70. Equity
71. Patents
72. Voting / participation rights
73. **Validating and voting**
74. Large funding requests enable the holders to vote and validate
75. Will your funds be used to vote in the network?
76. Does this create a conflict of interest?
77. **Funding request**
78. What are the costs?
79. How are they broken down?
80. Calculate the exchange rate using the [7-day trailing average tool.](https://repl.it/@thomivy1/7-Day-Trailing-Avg-dollarEDG)
81. **Payment details**
82. How do you want to be paid?
83. What is the address or account?
84. Is it a verified address?
85. **Payment terms**
86. Is it a one off payment?
87. Is it milestone based?
88. It it ongoing funding?
89. **Milestones**
90. What are the key milestones?
91. **Reporting**
92. How will you report on progress?
93. How often will you report?
94. Who is responsible for reporting?
95. **Team**
96. Who is delivering the proposal?
97. What is their background?
98. What are their roles?
99. Do you need help building your team?
100. **Team structure**
101. Who are the key contacts?
102. How can they be reached?
103. **Conflicts of interest**
104. Are there any conflicts we should we aware of?
105. **Any other relevant information or attachments**
106. Links to previous work
107. Links to community discussions
108. Links to relevant documents
109. Links to relevant media
