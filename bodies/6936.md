## **Ecosystem Updates**

- [OnFinality published the August 2022 usage report](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1tZJqAC7pHptjbMmZnHcpAEkiKdpG6QUdn6Sx_YNIylY/edit?usp=sharing) of their load-balancing RPC infrastructure (now featuring origins/domains as well) for Edgeware solochain mainnet.
