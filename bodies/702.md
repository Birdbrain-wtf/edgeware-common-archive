In Edgeware, working groups function as mission-based development orgs that are composed of members who share interests, needs, and passions. Where an Ambassador team is largely formed to promote a network in a variety of ways, Working Groups are intended to solve issues specific to a community while also growing community. 

**This post will detail the existing working groups, and also describe why you might want to create one and how they can operate.**


**What is a working group?**
[**"**A **working group**, or **working party**, is a group of experts working together to achieve specified goals. The groups are domain-specific and focus on discussion or activity around a specific subject area. The term can sometimes refer to an interdisciplinary collaboration of researchers working on new activities that would be difficult to sustain under traditional funding mechanisms (e.g., federal agencies)."](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_group)

**What Working Groups can be created?**
Many types of WG can be valuable:
- Regional groups that serve to promote and use Edgeware and improve problems within certain regions, Asia, Africa, and South America are all great places to open a WG. 
- Specific technology applications, Privacy for instance.
- Identity or Community-based solution groups: Minorities, Social Justice, or others.
- Many others! Share ideas, none are bad.



**What can I do with a Working Group?**
- Solve problems using the new world of blockchain and currency and collaborate with other Edgeware experts.
- [Request funds from the Treasury to execute your mission.](Request funds from the Treasury to execute your mission.)
- Organize your community into a political power within the network.
- Whatever you want, really: Your community is self-determining.
- Gain and use a free forum that interfaces with your favorite chains to organize your group.
- Advance the community of EDG citizens.
