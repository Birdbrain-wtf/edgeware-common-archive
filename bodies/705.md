## **Edgeware - Governance, Treasury & Community Waterfall **

This follows on from [@Thom Ivy](/edgeware/account/iJFChQEmukA7CNckr7TLZBjp4QxMxugEnzUVjceaJbPK9Sp) primer about [EDG Working Groups](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/702-edg-working-groups-a-primer) and outlines a flexible but logical flow that creates a (draft) formalised structure for community development through stream funded community groups. 

This ensures an organisational approach that gradually devolves debate, power and funding away and out from **EDG** **General Discussion**, **Council** and the **Treasury **but has the benefit of also ensuring collective intelligence, accountability and reputation flows back upstream.

It is an approach that retains individual/collective autonomy at all levels, is oriented around a network strategy and ensures a bias for action that can generate and sustain momentum inside and outside the project.

The** Edgeware Governance Waterfall:**

- Edgeware **Website/General/Watercooler - the journey begins. **
- Welcome!
- Where do you want to E**xplore**?
- How do you want to **Contribute**?
- Where can you add **Value**?
- Signposting to **phase 1 development aka** **Working Groups**
- High level overview of **Activities** going on in **Working Groups**.
- Each **Working Group** has its own **Funding Power**.
- Each Working Group has a **Multi-Sig Funding Address**
- A **Multi-Sig** enables a shared responsibility of **Funds**.
- The **Edgeware Treasury Fund** seeds this **Multi-Sig**.
- A **Working Group** seeds a **Project Team**
- A **Project Team** uses seed funding to develop and propose a **Project Proposal **
- **Project Teams **can/should have their own** Names **
- **Teams **may be formed **Online** or **IRL**
- **Teams **can operate under **Real Names** or be **Anon**.
- Teams are made up of **Members**.
- Teams gain a **Verified Team ID**
- Teams offer **Members** a **Membership NFT **
- These NFTs may later give access** **or unlock new value if a Team develops its own **Native Token** or even **Blockchain / Parachain. **
- **Membership NFTs** are assigned to your individual **Edgeware ID**.
- Teams may be a** Multi-Sig **or a **Multi-Sig linked LLC. **
- Ultimately** Names **develop **Community** **Reputation & Trust **
- These** Teams **could ultimately govern the** Edgeware Council **
- **Commonwealth** is a **Project Team**
- **Lemonade Money **is a **Project Team**
- **MoCDA **is a **Project Team**
- A **Project Proposal** assigns **Deliverables**, **Timelines**, **Responsibilities** + **Executes. **
- Each **Project Proposal** should also include a media / editorial budget line and plan for documenting the process.
- Each **Project Proposal** should also include a analytics / reporting and value capture budget line. Over time the community may develop our own methodologies for **Valuing Spend**.
- Successful **Project Proposal** funds the **Project Team** multi-sig.
- **A Project Proposal NFT** is sent to the Project Team multi-sig.
- The **Project Team **reports progress on the **Project Proposal** back up the funnel.
- A **Completed** Project Proposal means the** Project NFT **can be assigned to the **Project Team ID.**
- Over time Project Teams build reputation.
- This becomes a** Publishing Cycle** that ensures all levels of the community are kept up to date with all projects underway.
- Edgeware's funded **Audio and Video** **Working Group **should play the role of highlighting this work with media that can be easily disemminated and expand out from the existing community to bring in new community members.
- The cycle becomes self reinforcing - media ensures education and inspiration in equal parts.
