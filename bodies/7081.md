## **Parachain incentives **

- Development of new parachain / relay incentive model
- Sets out definitions and incentives for existing parachain/relay models


- **Free Market Parachains (FMP)**
- Generally product of well capitalised startup teams
- Auction and crowdloan based
- Marketed directly to retail investors
- No direct value accrual to relay
- Boom and bust cycles of hype
- **Common Good Parachains (CGP)**
- Public utilities that offer functions for assets, NFTs etc
- Historically incubated by Parity/W3F
- CGP team now keen to partner with outside communities
- Tension between FMP and CGP
- Concerns from FMP that CGP encroach on their functions
- Ultimate aim of moving all functionality from relay into CGP
- Treasury to move from Relay into CGP to allow relay to hold other assets such as those from Parachains.
- Discussion around Common Good DEX as alternative way to release liquidity for KAB and other grass roots parachains.
- See thread [here](https://kusama.subsquare.io/polkassembly/post/1788).
- **Introducing 'Grass Roots' parachains **
- Utilises _ParaNotes (PN) - _originally mentioned in Kabocha's announcement post here
- PNs similar to treasury notes/bonds
- Aim to unlock KSM network liquidity for illiquid networks such as KAB.
- Focuses on solving major headaches for three parties
- _Contributors_ needing working capital
- _Kabocha_ being illiquid and slower burn project
- _Kusama_ not accruing value from grantmaking
- In this model contributors assign cultural collateral to KAB in return for funding.
- KAB is minted to an anonymous proxy pallet enabling time delayed transactions with community oversight
- KAB issues PN to Kusama, which then advances KSM to contributor.
