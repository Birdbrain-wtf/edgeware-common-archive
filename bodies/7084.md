1. **MARKET MAKING | WHAT?(in brief)**


	The term market maker refers to a firm or individual who actively quotes two-sided markets in a particular security, providing bids and offers (known as asks) along with the market size of each. Market makers provide liquidity and depth to markets and profit from the difference in the bid-ask spread.

	A market maker participates in the securities market by providing trading services for investors and boosting liquidity in the market. They specifically provide bids and offers for a particular security in addition to its market size.Market makers must also quote the volume in which they're willing to trade along with the frequency of time they will quote at the best bid and best offer prices. Market makers typically profit off of the difference between the bid and ask spread.

	This Market Making activity is a very time and resource intensive task, due to which it has traditionally been done by huge corporations having considerable financial resources and capable personnel at their disposal. Moreover, due to this very reason these corporations charge a significant amount for their services as continuous monitoring of the market is required for keeping the capital intact.

1. CONNECTING THE DOTS | STRATEGIES


1. Strategy 1: ATR Strategy


1. Reasoning/Hypothesis:

	ATR represents the Average True Range. Basically, it represents the average length of the candles formed in the specified period. The length of the candle represents the difference between the bid-ask prices. The no. of data points taken were 50 on 2hr/4hr data. From observations, we noticed that in the current market scenario ATR is almost always 0.001 which is taken in the % format and used to set the bid-spread and ask-spread parameters.
	From our research and experimentation up until now, what we noticed is that Market Making is primarily a game of ATR’s of the spreads. So almost all the strategies will have the ATR as a base parameter, and other configurable parameters will be built upon ATR in order to add liquidity efficiently. 

1. Metrics for Success: Increase No. of trades
2. Parameters:
3. Order_refresh_time: 900 secs/ 600 secs
4. Order_amount: 100$
5. Order_levels: 2-4
6. Order_level_amount: 50$-100$
7. Order_level_spread: 0.5-1%
8. Add_transaction_fees: true/false
9. Time / Scenario deployment 
10. Results/Conclusions:

	This strategy works well with EDG as well as with other higher activity tokens like BTC, ETH, DOT etc. We are successfully able to generate required volumes but the overall P&L needs to be optimized using other HummingBot parameters.

1. Strategy 2: ATR/Moving average


1. Reasoning/Hypothesis:

	In this strategy, we use the ATR(Average True Range) of EDG as a % of the Moving Average Prices of EDG. The % value obtained is used to set the bid-ask spread parameter. From observations, we noticed that, for EDG this value varies between 3-3.5%, which is relatively on a very higher side from the perspective of Market Making. On the contrary, for BTC, ETH this value falls between 0.1-1%, which represents the ideal value for Market Making.

1. Metrics for Success: Increase No. of trades
2. Parameters:
3. Order_refresh_time: 900 secs/ 600 secs
4. Order_amount: 100$
5. Order_levels: 2-4
6. Order_level_amount: 50$-100$
7. Order_level_spread: 0.5-1%
8. Add_transaction_fees: true/false
9. Time / Scenario deployment 
10. Results/Conclusions:

	As briefly mentioned above, we used this strategy to experiment with EDG as well as some very active tokens like BTC, ETH, DOT. What we observed was that the final % i.e. the ATR as a % of the moving average for the active tokens was a considerably lower range which allowed us to set a lower %bid-ask spread parameter, which in turn results in a higher number of orders. On the other hand, with EDG, the % value was almost always greater than 3%, which results in considerably less buy and sell orders getting filled, due to the very large delta from the mid-price.

1. Strategy 3: Hanging Orders


	I. Reasoning/Hypothesis:
	As mentioned above, this Strategy also has the ATR parameter as the base component, and is complemented by the functionality of hanging orders provided by Humming Bot. The exact purpose of using these additional parameters is to make the existing ATR based trades more profitable in case of a sharp turn in the prices of EDG.
	Now, a brief about the hanging order parameters. Consider, you have placed two order levels on either side(buy/sell). Now assume one of the orders, let's consider the level1 sell order is triggered. Now, the remaining orders are level 2 sell, level 1 buy, level 2 buy. In normal circumstances when ‘hanging_orders_enabled’ is false, the level 1 buy will be canceled when the order_refresh_time is reached and new orders will be placed wrt the new mid-price of EDG. But, in the case where the ‘hanging_orders_enabled’ is true the level 1 buy order remains active with the previous mid-price(for which level 1 sell was triggered). This is known as the ‘hanging order’ as it continues to exist even after the refresh time is reached. Further, this hanging order will continue to exist till the spread for this order doesn’t breach the ‘hanging_order_cancel_pct’, i.e. the order will get canceled only when the spread percentage is exceeded and independent of the ‘order_refresh_time’. 

1. Metrics: Increase No. of trades, Total P&L	
2. Parameters:
3. Order_refresh_time: 900 secs/ 600 secs
4. Order_amount: 1000$
5. Order_levels: 3
6. Order_level_amount: 100$
7. Order_level_spread: 1%
8. Add_transaction_fees: true
9. hanging_order	_enabled: true
10. Hanging_order_cancel_pct: 2%
11. Time / Scenario deployment 
12. Results/Conclusion:

	We noted that deploying this parameter helps us increase the trading profits as it buy lower and sells higher in case of a sharp reversal in prices thus allowing to capture higher value through the hanging orders as compared to the newer mid-prices.

1. Strategy 4: Inventory Skew


1. Reasoning/Hypothesis: 

	The inception of this strategy was due to an issue we faced during our experimentations. During one of the experiments, we were faced with a situation in which we completely drained our USDT(base asset) and were left with 100% EDG in our inventory. This is not an ideal situation for a Market Maker, as in this case only sell orders will be placed until a substantial amount of the base asset is recovered back. This severely affects the performance of the Market Maker.
	Now, as a precaution to avoid getting into a similar situation we use the ‘inventory skew’ functionality of the Humming Bot. Inventory Skew allows us to set a target inventory distribution percentage, i.e what % of the inventory should be the actual asset and base asset(generally it's 50-50 on either side). Further it also allows to set up a permissible range with which both the assets can exist. If at all, the permissible range is breached, the bot places only buy/sale orders based on whether the actual or base asset has breached the range. This is how the inventory can retain assets on both the sides and not completely drain either of the assets.
1. Metrics: Increase No. of trades, Total P&L and preserve inventory balance
2. Parameters:
3. Order_refresh_time: 900 secs/ 600 secs
4. Order_amount: 1000$
5. Order_levels: 3
6. Order_level_amount: 100$
7. Order_level_spread: 1%
8. Add_transaction_fees: true
9. hanging_order	_enabled: true
10. Hanging_order_cancel_pct: 2%
11. Inventory_skew_enabled: true
12. Inventory_target_base_pct:50%
13. inventory_range _multiplier: 1
14. Time / Scenario deployment 
15. Results/Conclusion:

	This strategy combined with the hanging orders functionality helps to increase the liquidity optimizing the overall P&L and maintaining the inventory on both sides.
