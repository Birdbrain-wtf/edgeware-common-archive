The upcoming upgrade of Edgeware - which is not only a runtime upgrade, otherwise there would be no need for validators to upgrade their node - brings us in line with substrate 4. There are littke changes that are specific to Edgeware, but some of those are functional changes that people should keep in mind. Here are the ones I see as very important for everybody to know about. I am trying to be exaustive, but may extend the list if I think of more later.

1. The confidence voting delay has been corrected back from 1 day to 1 week. It was always supposed to be 1 week, but had been set to 1 day only by mistake since the last releases. It is meant to be long to really allow people with personal conviction to leverage their funds more than others. It affects how long tokens are locked when someone votes with conviction. There is an exponential multiplier that goes from 0 to 32 times the locking period. Votes with no conviction count for only 10% (0.1x) but don't lock tokens, votes with 1x lock with multiplier 1, votes with 2x lock with multiplier 2, votes with 3x lock with multiplier 4, etc. till votes with 6x that lock with multiplier 32.
Currently voting 6x is locking the tokens for 32 days. With the release it is going to be 32 WEEKS so please, bear this in mind when voting! Now people with high conviction can really leverage their votes vs others as it will require a much stronger belief to vote with full power. Take care especially of the UI that shows some locking information based on what it sees on chain at a givem time. For instance, if you build a transaction just before the upgrade and vote 6x, you may be locked for much longer than expected depending on when the lock expires. So keep this in mind when voting.

2. The staking pallet now handles the case too many nominators (128+) differently than before. The additional ones are put in a queue, but the choice of which is included and which isn't is not purely on stake, but nominators are batched within bags of "similar" stake and ordered according to their time of entry into this bag. So nominator A may have a slightly higher stake than nominator B, but not different enough to be in another bag, so that they both belong to the same bag. If A nominated later, it will have a lower priority to enter the active list than B, even though his stake is slightly higher. Bags themselves are ordered by stake, so by large, the higher stake is still prioritized, and the only exception is within a single bag. Note that this choice applies only when one validator has too many nominators, which is rarely the case on Edgeware.

3. Shankar mentioned that when withdrawing EVM funds, there is now no dropdown menu to choose the unit in the UI. The default unit is the minimal unit of the chain, which is 10^-18 (Wei in ETH). This means that you have to add 18 zeros to a given number expressed in EDG/ETH. For instance, to withdraw 1 EDG you ought to put 1 000 000 000 000 000 000 (1 followed by eighteen 0's) in the box. Bear this in mind if you are using polkadot.js.

4. A specific migration is included in the upgrade to release the funds of the web3 foundation that have been mistakenly burnt during the Edgeware genesis. A recap on this story:

 a. The web3 foundation was allocated 3% of the initial supply (150M EDG) at genesis, as per the whitepaper.

 b. They also got tokens from the locked parity wallet that was signalled. Those were amounting for about 354M EDG. The total of their holding was about 10% of the original supply of 5B EDG since they had then around 504M EDG.

 So during the genesis process, the address of the web3 foundation was listed twice, but instead of adding those numbers together, the genesis allocation logic started to allocate 150M to the address, and then later overrode this number with 354M effectively burning part of their allocation.
 It was only discovered recently during the wallet allocation audit for Kabocha, since the sum of all EDG in the existing wallets was 150M less than what was indicated in the total supply.

 Note that we are not minting new tokens: the total supply indicated onchain already accounts for those 150M, they were just not present in the original wallet. Consequently the total supply number before and after the upgrade will not change.

 Note also that this fix is part of the upgrade because it involves a state migration which is always risky to do and we do a lot as part of the upgrade so getting it performed with others is reducing the number of critical moments we have to care about. This is for convenience only, but I would like to point out that whatever the opinion on the web3 foundation could be, those are their funds according to the whitepaper and it is already very embarrassing that we did not act to release them long before. So this is not a referendum on whether they should be released or not as that's not up to us to decide.
