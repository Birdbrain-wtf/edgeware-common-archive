There are only ~100-200 accounts that have staked KSM towards electing a councillor, and ~50 accounts that have staked EDG. On the other hand, there are ~2400 nominators on Kusama, and ~1000 nominators on Edgeware. Also, people voting for councillor(s) right now are disproportionately large token holders.

This is a proposal to produce a fixed reward of 1-2% per year, to be distributed towards all EDG balances that have been staked towards at least one councillor or councillor candidate.

- For example, if I have 100,000 EDG in an account and have 50,000 EDG locked to vote for a councillor, I would receive this reward on the 50,000 EDG.
- The requirement is that you have to stake towards a legitimate councillor candidate, not that you have to vote for one of the existing councillors. So we might also increase the set of councillor candidates, to e.g. 13 councillors + 13 candidates.


Benefits:
- **Simplicity.** I've spoken with several other people about ways to incentivize governance participation, including awarding EDG to anyone who has voted on a referendum every month. This is the simplest solution by far.
- **Should not increase centralization. **Any governance reward has to be set-and-forget; otherwise the friction of interacting with governance for a 1-2% reward would be high enough that only whales have real incentive to claim the reward.
- **Minimal and predictable effect on token dilution.** The inflation rate is low enough that staking rewards will continue to remain the main source of inflation on the network.


Potential downsides:
- **Could incentivize less informed community members to participate in governance.** I don't think this is necessarily a bad thing; in fact, this change seems like it incentivizes known community members and those with a public reputation to become councillors. The purpose of Edgeware isn't to maintain a purist ideal of governance but to figure out how all the community participants and stakeholders can govern the network most effectively.
- **Could be computationally expensive.** The validator reward code is quite complicated at the moment - rewards are queued up, and must be claimed. This is partly because the number of validators x nominators is huge and previous iterations caused block production to stall at the end of eras. The number of accounts that would receive this reward is low right now, but in the future, we should plan for as many as there are nominators.


The realistic goal here would be to get 30% of the network (or more) involved in selecting councillors, and to decentralize the council by diluting the proportion of councillors that are selected by self-delegation.
