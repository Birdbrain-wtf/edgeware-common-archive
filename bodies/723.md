In the future, it might be cool for an automated mechanism where:

- User launches a collective.
- User onboards members in an on-chain fashion.
- Members verify accounts using Onchain ID.
- Perhaps Walkthrough-style items are completed, similar to Shankar's existing program.
- Once a collective reaches some number of ID'd and active members, the treasury executes a spend to a multisignature account composed of those members to some threshold - requiring cooperation to use those funds, but awarding community organizing and resourcing their initial efforts.
