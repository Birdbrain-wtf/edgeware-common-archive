## **Highlights**

- One of the community validators, [kdpnetstake determined](https://twitter.com/EdgewareDAO/status/1578355962083155968)[a critical bug](https://twitter.com/EdgewareDAO/status/1578355962083155968) in the node client of the v4.0.0 pre-release on the testnet. Thus, we might need to wait for the next pre-release with the inclusion of the client fix which will be collectively tested on the Beresheet v4 testnet before proposing it on the mainnet.
- All the Validators, RPC infrastructure providers, Exchanges and Ecosystem Projects are requested to wait for the next pre-release (and not to upgrade to the [pre-release v4.0.0](https://github.com/edgeware-network/edgeware-node/releases/tag/v4.0.0) if you haven't done already).
