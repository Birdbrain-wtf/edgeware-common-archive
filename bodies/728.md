## Edgeware x Decred community call
**Thurs 22.10.20**

A first EDGxDCR community call happened last thursday and discussed the best ways to kickstart relations between the two projects.

[@dillchen[ @Shankar Warang[ @Thom Ivy[ @Akin Sawyerr](/edgeware/account/mVX17FbkGpGoBZyiHEiufevC35KduZJjDcyygZVjiee6FTB)](/edgeware/account/iJFChQEmukA7CNckr7TLZBjp4QxMxugEnzUVjceaJbPK9Sp)](/edgeware/account/5DiCs4ECYBHrqDDiPad7T3pJaSgxzRz7mQ5GXh5LkSA6UpNi)](/edgeware/account/hcgMhR61jUS55naSR5uW4JjN92zdFp3V91TtSCJmdnuxZAL) attended. 

For those not familiar with Decred, there is a fairly deep rabbit hole, but in summary, it was designed to tackle what the core team saw were the key issues facing Bitcoin - you can read more [here.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/508-lessons-from-decred-on-governance-community-culture-and-proposals)

The project is in many ways a hedge or insurance against certain factors either stunting BTCs growth, or centralising certain aspects of the project and the wider crypto-ecosystem, especially given the arrival of CBDCs and increasing digital surveillance around the world. 

A case in point is last week's [MVP release](https://twitter.com/decredproject/status/1318998573975994373?s=20) of the DCRDEX, a OS project from Decred that utilises atomic swaps and is one of the key pieces in enabling crypto to remain truly permissionless and unstoppable, since there is no single 'product' perse - anyone can download the software, spin up a client with their own server. 

In general 'DEXs' are misunderstood - [this article](https://www.theblockcrypto.com/post/79768/theres-no-such-thing-as-a-decentralized-exchange) by Coincenter gives useful context.

**Opportunities for collaboration**

The two key initiatives identified were:

- **EDG integration into DCRDEX**. Right now BTC, LTC and DCR are supported, with ETH coming soon. [Here](https://github.com/decred/dcrdex#client-quick-start-installation) is a link to the documentation and here is a [simple walkthrough](https://www.publish0x.com/block-commons/how-to-get-on-dcrdex-xolpzmv) from DCR community member RichardRed.


Initially this will not drive major liquidity for either project, but it is a statement of intent, integrating two governance focused projects from two different crypto generations and a stepping stone to opening up Decred's community to the Polkadot ecosystem, with future opportunities for DCR being used as 'collateral' within the ecosystem. 

- **Decred chain integration into [Commonwealth](https://commonwealth.im)'s UI**, putting the community into the eyeline of other projects, and offering a new organisational tool for the community to collaborate on ahead of Pi-Reddit - a DCRtime based reddit clone.


CW is aiming to utilise IPFS/Filecoin on their backend, discussions did touch on leveraging DCRtime but that was considered to be for a later date.

Decred and Edgeware/Polkadot are almost polar opposite in their emerging community cultures - but that doesn't mean one is right and one is wrong. 

Overall, there is value in both approaches and perhaps together, the best of both conservative and progressive mindsets.

Would love to get feedback from the EDG devs and also solicit involvement from DCR community. 

Comments, ideas, pushbacks are encouraged.
