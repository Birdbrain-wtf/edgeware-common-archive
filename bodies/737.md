One easy way to become a leader in the Polkadot gaming chain space is to develop easy libraries, APIs and integrations with gaming engines. Potential targets for both 3D and 2D mobile and platform engines include but are not limited to:

- **[Unity (](https://unity.com/)Top Priority)**
- **Unreal**
- Corona SDK
- [GameMaker Studio 2](https://www.yoyogames.com/gamemaker)
- libGDX
- [RPGMaker ](https://www.rpgmakerweb.com/)
- [Solar2D](https://solar2d.com/) (Previously known as Corona)


Top priorities should be the engines that support multiplatform and perform 2d/3d, and those with an existing robust ecosystem for advertising, analytics, media, and the presence of other blockchain integrations.  Unity clearly takes the cake, and has 4.5 million devs and accounts for [28 billion installs over 3 billion devices.](https://blog.enjin.io/blockchain-sdk-for-unity-coming-to-ethereum-testnet/)

Ethereum integrations with Unity for example, leverage libraries like:
- [Enjin Blockchain SDK](https://blog.enjin.io/blockchain-sdk-for-unity-coming-to-ethereum-testnet/)
- [Netthereum Library](https://github.com/Nethereum/Nethereum)


More, token standards should be examined and deployed to Edgeware that fit the use cases intended.
- [ERC-1155, an improvement on ERC-721](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/issues/1155) for digital gaming.
