**What Working Groups can be created?**
Many types of WG can be valuable:
- Regional groups that serve to promote and use Edgeware and improve problems within certain regions, Asia, Africa, and South America are all great places to open a WG.
- Specific technology applications, Privacy for instance.
- Identity or Community-based solution groups: Minorities, Social Justice, or others.
- Many others! Share ideas, none are bad.
- Partnership groups: Like the Decred x Edgeware WG.

### To launch a Working Group:
- Post a concept to [Commonwealth.im/Edgeware](http://commonwealth.im/Edgeware) to share your idea. Your working Group Idea should include:
- **A mission**: What is your group trying to achieve, build or solve?
- **A vision**: What does the world look like if your group succeeds? Inspire your audience
- **A guide to recruiting:** Who is your ideal member set? Describe them. What skills, interests, connections or resources do they have? This will help your readers both self-identify as potential group members and help members recruit others. These members might not only be people but **organizations** you're looking to onboard or partner with. If you know specifics, share them. **Working groups reach a self-sustainable threshold of about 50-60 members with 7-12 core and very active participants. This is your goal!**
- **A link to a chat group** you've created for your WG. Right now, Edgeware uses both forums and chat rooms for each working group that helps it organize conversation from long-form content.
- You can ask for a Commonwealth #wg-YourWorkingGroup channel in your post, (see the sidebar here,) which is what Commonwealth Labs recommends for long-form and threaded discussion.
- We can also set up a channel in the Edgeware Discord or you can bring your own Telegram or Element group. Just be sure to add the invite link here.
- **Optional: Ideas for Initial Tasks:** These might include growing your group, setting up a first meeting, or reaching out to certain individuals or projects.


If you have questions or recommendations about establishing working groups, ask them here!
