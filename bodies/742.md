### Goal:
Make smart contract platforms like Edgeware popular, meme-like, utilized and valuable.

### Need:
A committed, robust and self-sustaining community of users/developers to use, develop, secure ,maintain and evangelize the platform.
### 
### Problem:
How do you onboard community members reliably, sustainably, such that their presence and efforts are also maximized individually but also as a cohort in order to jumpstart/ bootstrap the arrival of the sustainable robust community in the quickest time possible?

Unsolved challenges:
- How do people enter a flow of introduction to various working groups, in order to find their tribe?

### 
### Prior Art:
- Previously Ambassador programs sought to connect people with highly aligned values or stake in the game with each other, led by a CMO-type role whose task was to organize the activities of Ambassadors, track progress, onboard new ambassadors, and occasionally reward tasks. Ambassador programs are hype-d out, the benefits barely accrued, and tit-for-tat arrangements are legally odious.
- Reversely, organic and decentralized meme-based efforts are more successful in lifting token price, onboarding new speculators, and shillers. If the payoff in the form of token price doesn't hit, these meme-movements crumble and resentment builds. These users have loosely aligned values, namely economic conditions that don't ensure they will cooperate with each other past certain points.


### Solution:

**The Working Group Collective**
Fuse the approaches to create a decentralized set of working groups that are self-organizing but centrally bootstrapped by a community architect.

**Value-aligned at the top, Token-Value aligned at the bottom.**
The Working Group approach aligns people on values, but also produce material, norms and values that can be disseminated organically to a broader community of speculators, who can serve to distribute in turn to the broader web. It's important to have the energy and memes come reliably from value-aligned community because they are resilient to price action. They are also cooperative in the face of economic pressures to refrain from cooperation. They maintain the excitement and momentum even while speculators look around the market under negative light. Resilient momentum flowing from the working groups serves to inspire and maintain speculator hope and interest in a variety of market conditions.

**Users know who is in charge and reputable.**
Ambassador programs at first may have tricked users into believing that they were honored positions, but today most communities know Ambassadors are individuals subservient to the community, and often trading their time for experience in a field they would like to work in, AKA Interns. Under Working Groups, users recognize the autonomy, credit, and monetary awards in the forms of community development grants that can accrue to WG members and coordinators. ****

**Scaled Appropriately**
A community architect bootstraps Working groups by identifying, inspiring, and enlisting a set of initial participants while also considering the networks of those participants and the network's growth strategy -partners, etc.
After the growth of the initial group reaches a boiling point, (which our efforts have shown to be around 60 people in a discussion channel, and 5-12 core regularly contributing members in calls,) the group should select co-chairs and become increasingly autonomous, at the prodding of the community architect.
