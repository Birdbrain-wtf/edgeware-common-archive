Multi-Sig Overview/FAQ


What is a Multi-Sig


A Multi-Signature account is Keyless Account. Where a single Edgeware account key and it’s signatory for transactions are _determined*_ by multiple citizens. The participants (accounts) would be responsible for signing transactions to complete the transfer of funds and other on-chain actions. (ie. declaration of a chain Identity) 


The Multi-Signature account transactions are secured via the set participation**_ _threshold.** This is amount is the required approvers for transactions.

[*These type of account are categorized as **deterministic.**]

Below is an overview of creating a Multi-Sig account on the Edgeware chain using one of our decentralized interfaces. For this following example we’ll use 3 citizens with a threshold of approvers being 2.

Anyone can send EDG to your Multi-Sig The sending of EDG from your Multi-Sig must be approved by the threshold of participants you set step 2c. Below is an example of making a transfer from your Multi-Sig account. 


1)   Select _Send_ from your created Multi-Sig account 
            A. Paste the address or select the account receiving EDG. 
            B. Enter the amount to send from you Multi-Sig
            C. Select _Make Transfer_
            D. **Record** you Call Data and Hash to share with your approvers. This data is required to                  complete the transaction.

1) Approving the transaction 
             A. You’ll receive a notification of number pending transactions 
             B. Click on the Kebab button (3 vertical dots) and select Multisig approvals 
             C. Select the pending **hash** from the dropdown menu, (match this hash to the recorded data from 1d) then _Approve,_ and sign your transaction. 

             D. **If you are providing the final approval:** Select the pending _**hash **_from the dropdown menu, and input the _**call data**_ (match this hash to the recorded data from 1d) then _Approve,_ and sign your transaction.
