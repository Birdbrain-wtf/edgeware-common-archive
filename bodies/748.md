> Polkaswap is a non custodial AMM DEX designed uniquely for the [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) ecosystem and hosted on the [SORA](https://sora.org) v2 network. Near boundless liquidity through one of a kind **Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT)** with the security and convenience of a DEX.  

There is a new working group focused on chain integrations and asset bridges between [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) (funded by a Web3 grant) which is itself an application within the [Sora](https://sora-xor.medium.com/october-30-2020-dev-updates-for-sora-polkaswap-and-fearless-wallet-9ff465a6d751) ecoystem. 

The discussion is currently focused on integrating EDG into Polkaswap and collaboration on an ETH bridge. 

A link to the Working Group is **[here](https://t.me/joinchat/GZzH7kwD4icWIPUjVTL5wQ)**. 

Sora is being developed by [Soramitsu](https://soramitsu.co.jp/) a Japanese blockchain R&D company with an established track record. 

They are also working with [Interlay](https://www.interlay.io/) who are developing [PolkaBTC](https://polkadot.network/bitcoin-is-coming-to-polkadot/).
