Currently, there are no dedicated solutions on mobile for EDG users other than Android-based Mathwallet. Additionally, we are seeing defi projects like Stafi and Sublime announce their integrations with Edgeware. A defi-enabled mobile wallet would unlock value for nearly all stakeholders in the Edgeware ecosystem. This proposal seeks to fund the cost of developing an Android and iOS integration with EDG, and would lay the groundwork for future support including staking and governance actions.
