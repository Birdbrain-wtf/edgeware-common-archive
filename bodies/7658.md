**PURPOSE**:

The purpose of this proposal is to raise a second fund of capital for the financial working group consisting of Advait, Vismay and Ash. This second fund has the same goal as the previous fund and will be used to create a diversified portfolio of blockchain assets (tokens) that appreciates in value (monetary, utility) overtime that Edgeware can use for capital heavy opex (operating expense) or funding future growth.

**REASON:**

Through this entire period of time the diversification group has remained vigilant and evaluated various strategies to stay true to our goal of 20-30% returns that could be captured within the funds timeline. Our core investment goal is to create sufficient value transfer to the Edgeware Treasury and thereby the Edgeware community. 

![](https://commonwealth-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/53cb0f79-dffe-4b8f-bec5-c5c246b491c0.1668278218409)


The fundamentals in studying the macro market vis-a-vis our investment thesis has allowed the group to be vigilant to market pressures; ‘buy’ points that we feel were not truly representative of the value of the asset in the eroding market. We have long suspected before the public fall out of the aforementioned parties and beyond that there are actor(s) obfuscating unknowingly or wilfully the exposure risks / losses; thereby creating artificial floor prices for assets. 

In comparison to better capitalized funds, our group has managed to maintain a large amount of the fund’s capital through responsible and diligent capital allocations. Other DAO’s and firms that invest in the market by comparison have had several liquidity issues, hacks and outright bad investments that we have not fallen prey to.Our approach is similar to VC’s in that the valuations of assets / projects through the tenure of fund 1 is higher than what we would want to allocate to, and as such are cautious in understanding the asset / projects value prop.

As part of our core appraisal of asset prices, the group believes that market appropriate pricing is approaching and that we are in the unique opportunity to allocate capital to assets that could yield great value to the community.
