## Key network services development 

- Finalising new revenue model for Edgeware - which will see the evolution of Network Services, where Decent Partners purchases services priced in EDG from on-chain organisations based on the network (and the same in Kabocha). This will enable Edgeware's token to drive demand and set a price floor as more services and organisations emerge.

 
- Examples include: Community management, moderation, market making etc, but this will expand to include every part of creating, maintaining and launching a network. 


- Moving forward Decent Partners will 'market' these services to other projects inside and outside of the ecosystem, deconstructing key domain expertise into services that enable future networks to launch in a 'no-code' manner, simply plugging into the expertise offered by teams operating in and around Edgeware and Kabocha.


- In the example of above, there will be projects that will pay Decent Partners for network services in fiat -  Decent Partners will then act as an agency, paying an on-chain organisation for their services in EDG.
