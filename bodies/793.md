With the latest upgrade, we now have the ability to use on-chain governance to set the treasury reward rate:
stakingReward.setTreasuryReward
We have over 460m EDG in the treasury, and the minting rate is:
5256000 blocks/year * 95 EDG/block * $0.0055/EDG
= $2.75m/year
The Commonwealth development grant was 50m EDG worth ~$500k at the time. I see the chain deploying up to about $500k/year **at maximum** towards development, and similarly maybe $500k/year towards BD, community, and partnerships (both estimates probably err on the high side).

We may also want to budget around $500k for one-time expenses like exchange listings, initial wallet integrations, etc.

If this analysis holds, our current treasury should be enough to cover the next 2+ years of community expenses. If EDG appreciates, then it will be enough for significantly more than that.

As such, it seems like we should advance this proposal now:

- Set the treasury reward inflation rate to 0. If the treasury continues to accumulate funds from staking inefficiencies, up to a couple % of the chain per year, this is enough to keep us close to a sustainable path indefinitely.


We also have the option to set the treasury reward inflation rate to 5-10% of where it is right now, e.g 7.5 EDG/block ($200k/year). But this seems strictly inferior as an option.

It seems like halting the treasury reward would be a change that other community members support, per [@luckyve](/edgeware/account/mYSJW56dUwhuKGWM1vafo6eYm9jRLr6aFF4TXma5ECwF7Eh)'s [thread](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/434-unfair-reward) from earlier this year.

A concern would be that after halting the treasury reward right now, it would be difficult to turn it on again later, but I tend to believe it wouldn't be a problem so long as the community seems it has been well spent.
