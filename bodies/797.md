We now have a [set of tutorials](https://contracts.edgewa.re/#/) on how to use the WASM and EVM toolchains with Edgeware! These tutorials have been developed by Matej (@yangwao) based on [Parity's tutorial for beginners](https://substrate.dev/substrate-contracts-workshop/#/0/building-your-contract).

If you have Rust experience, or even if you don't, the WASM tutorials just take a few hours. The [Rust guide](https://doc.rust-lang.org/book/index.html) is useful as a companion.
