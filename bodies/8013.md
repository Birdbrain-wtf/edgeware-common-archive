Create Wallet/Deposit/Send Tokens or Directly access Dapp with other wallets
Trade on the decentralized P2P exchange using atomic swap technology.
Buy popular crypto tokens with Fiat currency(Visa/MC) (Adding Edgeware to popular Fiat-on-ramp service providers after Legal Service Provider Proposal)
Non-Custodial Wallet - User’s private keys are stored in his browser’s localstorage, the user must save his keys.
Aggregator Swap - you can use exchanges through [https://0x.org/](https://0x.org/) aggregator for Ethereum, BSC, Polygon networks etc. to find the best prices, but the aggregator can charge fees.
Quickswap - you use direct sources of Uniswap V2 (Ethereum), PancakeSwap (BSC), SushiSwap (Polygon), HoneySwap (xDai), SpiritSwap (Fantom) and Pangolin (Avalanche) liquidity depending on the selected network.

**Farming - ETA 20 Days**

We can allow users to deposit EDG/EDG-20 tokens using a simple “Deposit/Withdraw” interface and earn rewards.
 “Reward” tokens are distributed across all users who stake “staking” tokens, the share of every user calculated personally based on the amount of staked tokens and time his tokens have been staked. For example, if we start distribution of 100 REWARD tokens and 2 users stake 1 STAKE token each they will receive 50 REWARD tokens each. Users can withdraw his part any time before and after the farming period ends.

For example, 365 “REWARD” token and the farming period is 1 year. In this case, every day will distribute 1 token/day across all who deposit “STAKE” token. If user A has deposited 1 STAKE and no one else deposited so this user is earning 1 REWARD/day. IF user B stake 2 tokens (after user A) he will receive 2/3 of rewards ~ 0.7 REWARD/day (and user’s A earnings will down 1 to 0.3)
