**Renamed from Partnerships Lead**

Hey all, as mentioned on past community calls as well on on other [threads on Commonwealth](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/756-chain-contractors-in-the-near-future), the possibility of funding a chain-contractor position for a partnerships / business development lead has been brought up.

In my capacity helping Edgeware, I’ve fielded a fair amount of inbound interest on partnerships, this has also been spread among other contributors like [@Thom Ivy](/edgeware/account/jKauGVPCQkteGTDKuuNwhYhMBrj5QuytgybEC4A5cS4Bki5) , [@mrbulb](/edgeware/account/jXC7ghviUzVcVySg3eD8prB7C9m6VzK6cw2MTyMTDTUye5q) , and [@Akin Sawyerr](/edgeware/account/mVX17FbkGpGoBZyiHEiufevC35KduZJjDcyygZVjiee6FTB) . It seems like this is a relatively well defined area of action that an independent person or team can drive forward. 

At a high-level, this role would be dedicated to building and developing organizational relationships in Edgeware.

- Potential projects that could build on Edgeware or with which it could form partnerships
- Exchanges and other entities that could help drive market development.


A successful candidate in this role, would be able to:

- Help grant and shape the business development roadmap by listening to stakeholders on Edgeware--developers, councillors, and other community participants
- Publicly clearly and concisely on updates to the community
- Recruiting and leading a volunteer led working group that calls regularly to brainstorm and execute on potential opportunities for Edgeware—potentially through the wg-partnerships.
- Manage relationships with exchanges and other entities that can help grow access to the EDG token
- Help navigate the governance process for other projects (if they have an open governance process that is important for success: for example if EDG were to be added as a collateral type on a stablecoin)
- Help potential projects navigate the treasury process if necessary
- Work with existing large Edgeware token holders (I.e funds and crypto holders) to leverage their networks to grow the capacity of the network. 


We frame this role as a chain-contractor opportunity, not a traditional hire, submitted as an request-for-proposal (RFP) response, and paid according to milestone or KPI achievement from the network's on-chain treasury. The ideal candidate possesses the following skillset and is eager to join a growing community to ________ (work with other projects within the community and more)

### **Skillset**
- Demonstrably familiar with OSS Development
- Understanding of incentives and positioning of different projects within the blockchain industry
- Ability to grow a group of community contributors
- Experience with B2B sales
- Great communication and writing skills, fluency in English is a must and any other languages is a plus.

### 
### **Compensation**
We recommend an initial 30-day period at 10 hours per week (42 hours total) at the EDG equivalent of $40/hr. ($40/$0.0070 per EDG ~= 5700 EDG per hour)

For the whole period, we recommend a grant of 239,400 EDG (5,700 EDG/hr*42hr) from the Edgeware Treasury. After this, the role would be renewed every 3 months and milestone grants given accordingly.
### 
### **Milestones**
Here’s a few potential milestones that might be significant to this role. I’d ask the community to contribute to this list

- Work to onboard three projects from Ethereum onto Edgeware
- Conclude an onboarding process to a top 10 exchange
- Build a CRM and reach out to 100 well-regarded projects
