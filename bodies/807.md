As Edgeware is a DAO, we will need to plan for the management of various types assets, for expert opinion on financial proposals, and for a more robust understanding of Edgeware's economic model. 

Although I have drafted many support resources on this matter, including [staking ](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VlzTUDESbbfOggMRz3GyE9-VqR9MlOhNuoekBboKvLw/edit#gid=2011072230)and [inflation estimators](https://www.desmos.com/calculator/1ghpu42zin), [supply curve charts](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1bKuD0GnQr-HZIrPdos6UuVBfOu27MxAuKqF5t2llOHM/edit#gid=0), [token economics pages](https://docs.edgewa.re/edgeware-runtime/economics), there is some uncertainty about the veracity of these - rightfully so, as I am _terrible _at math and have no background in these things other than my experience in crypto.  =-D 

**To that end, I propose the creation of a team of financial and economic thinkers to advise on strategies, propose asset management actions, analyze financial proposals, verify information, correct errors where prudent (staking estimates from 3rd parties being one item that comes to mind, or the functioning of estimators like Furqan recently gifted the community.)**

This "Working Group" will probably need to be more intentionally hired rather than volunteer base at least for the first 1-3 members.  I use the term commission here because it is an ongoing public good that requires prized talent, so the first step likely is to source a Group Lead / Chief Economist to coordinate and form reports. This person ideally has a background in both Dev and Finance/Econ, but a quantitative and technical mind is likely sufficient for resolving current unknowns and leading a group.
