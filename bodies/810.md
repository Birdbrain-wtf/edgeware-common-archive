**dTrade is a platform for synthetic asset trading built on Edgware. **
The key features of our product will be 
- Non-custodial derivatives and margin trading
- Access to a universe of investable assets, crafted by the community through decentralized governance
- Synthetic asset exchange with advanced features like limit and stop-loss orders
- Multi-collateral staking with DAI, EDG, SNX, and more in the future


We are excited at the opportunity to contribute to the DeFi infrastructure of Edgeware and the larger Polkadot ecosystem.
