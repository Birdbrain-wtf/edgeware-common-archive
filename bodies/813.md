So [@Thom Ivy](/edgeware/account/icLHYqouZfis7pPqDfnf7BxjHKG92YeHHTHQSvMXWdVHqGE) first connected with Tristan and Ian from Etherize who are developing an interface/application to enable Edgeware users to link addresses to DAO's in Wyoming and Delaware that would also execute all documentation.

We had a call to discuss the best way to develop user journeys, initial applications and a clear proposal with funding requirements, timelines and deliverables. 

> [Etherize.io](http://Etherize.io) proposes additions to Commonwealth to permit users to wrap crypto-address(es) and DAO proposals in an LLC. 
> By utilizing Wyoming Series LLCs, this process can be…
> Over the course of minutes, generating all necessary legal documents and e-signatures
> At no more than gas costs to start and maintain the entity for one year
> Maintenance of no more than $30/year after the first year

You can view an initial outline of their proposal [here](https://hackmd.io/CDSwvWiHQjuZjdox9Agdng).

The existing DAO/LLC legal frameworks used by Metacartel Ventures and The LAO utilise the [Openlaw](https://www.openlaw.io/) framework (a Consensys venture)  and as Tristan noted the irony is that their contracts aren't that open so the intention would be to look to [Juris](https://getjuris.com/) which is an open source alternative.

Aim would be to: 

- Add some plugins to the Commonwealth repo 
- Enable DAO management through the interface 
- Push Edgeware as the first instance but offer to other chains
- Establish the Brand Working Group as the first address linked LLC
- Enable the BWG to contract with resources in a more assured manner 


**Roadmap**

1. Tristan and Ian are outlining a more detailed proposal
2. Community input into this 
3. Funding request 
4. Would be relatively quick to spin up an MVP
5. Digitising the documentation phase would take up to 4 months
6. Long term aim would be for a multi-jurisdictional interface 


**Other info**

Etherize leverages capability with the [Otco framework](https://otonomos.com/2020/08/otoco-and-the-revolution/) to enable 'mother' and then 'child' companies such that the main entity is registered in Delaware/Wyoming but the children can exist under the first.

This would not negate the need for tax reporting for each, but it might be a useful way to manage a small network of DAOs for Edgeware. 

My instinct is that major initiatives run by separate teams who want to retain more independence would be better served with their own instance. 

We'd probably aim to have a new name since Etherize obviously reflects the ETH community.
