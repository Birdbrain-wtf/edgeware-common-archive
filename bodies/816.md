### **Technology**
- Finality is planned for restoration soon - with less than 2 weeks before the governance-action is executed. The issue, which arose after a recent chain upgrade, prevented Exchanges from permitting some actions and the larger stall issue must be resolved through runtime upgrades executed via this governance process. The new tooling should enable the council to manage this issue if it ever arises again.
- Community developer Furqan Ahmed [shares his port of Yieldscan for Edgeware, allowing users to estimate staking returns.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/803-edgeware-integrated-with-yieldscan-) Additional support will help integrate the treasury economic model and rewards into the estimator.
- You can view live contracts [on the testnet here-](https://contracts.edgewa.re/#/0/live-deployed-smart-contracts) try instancing them!

### Working Groups
- **The DAO Working Group** shares a first major partnership: [funding of Etherize, a tool to connect DAOS to Series LLCs in Delaware and Wyoming,](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/813-etherize-interface-for-creating-daos-legal-entities) bridging the legal gaps and creating a more borderless economy via Edgeware.
- [Thom Ivy announces his support for a **Financial and Economic working group,](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/807-working-group-finance-econ-commission)** with a lead role of a chief economist to help understand and communicate Edgeware's economic model, analyze financial proposals, and manage the chain's sovereign assets.
