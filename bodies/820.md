Putting forth some feelers for changing the times. Lately, as everyone is aware, we have not properly used the fast track mechanism and have had to use the normal 3-week waiting time for proposal, referendum, activation. Is there any interest in shortening these parameters in the next upgrade?

The parameters are defined [here](https://github.com/hicommonwealth/edgeware-node/blob/master/node/runtime/src/lib.rs#L460).
The existing parameter set is:
1. 7 day launch period
2. 7 day voting period
3. 3 day fast track (requires 10+/13 council member threshold)
4. 8 day enactment period.


For starters, we can:
- Reduce the launch period to 2 days
- Reduce the enactment period to 1 days.


Do people have more conservative estimates for what they'd like that are at least faster than what we are currently using?
