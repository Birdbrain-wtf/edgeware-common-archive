**What is Re-Denomination?**
The proposed redenomination is similar to a _reverse_ stock split, (or stock consolidation):  "Similar to a share split, a redenomination ordinarily alters the circulating supply of a token while market capitalisation remains unchanged."

 So, for example, if you had 100 EDG at 5 Billion supply before, you would have 25 EDG at a supply of 1 billion. 
Notice that the value share you hold of the token economy remains the same, but the #s themselves are decreased. 

Previously, Polkadot did this in the reverse (like a normal stock split,) going from 10 million to a round 1 billion. It also reduced the decimal places from 12 to 10- we would do the reverse here, I believe? thats up for an expert to remark on. 

DOT also experienced some issues as large exchanges like Binance and Kraken did not redenominate at the community-prescribed day, resulting in confusion and likely abuse to their users interested in purchasing or selling DOT.
