> As a small update to this proposal. The funds have been transferred to the exchange, and technical integration has proceeded. [@Thom Ivy](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/account/jKauGVPCQkteGTDKuuNwhYhMBrj5QuytgybEC4A5cS4Bki5) or I will keep this thread updated as more information from the exchange is made available to us.

There has been consistent community interest in finding additional venues for liquidity for Edgeware. A top 15 exchange by normalized volume by CMC has approached us about advancing a community proposal for exchange listing. The exchange has asked some aspects of the proposal to remain confidential; however, we think this is one of the strongest proposals we have seen, and it can serve as a blueprint for other exchanges to approach the Edgeware Treasury. 

While Commonwealth has been approached by several dozen exchanges, we think this proposal is one of the strongest proposals. Please note the proposal is still subject to change, and of course up to community discussion (and probably a signaling proposal), before being put up to a treasury vote. 

There are two components to the proposal:

**Integration and Marketing Cost (fee to exchange)**

The exchange has asked for an amount of EDG to cover:

1. Integration Fee
2. Marketing Campaign distributed in a marketing campaign

While the fee is relatively straightforward, we’ve been asked to keep the exact amount confidential. Naturally, this makes a community vote difficult. To balance the dual need for confidentiality and transparency, a treasury proposal will ask for a “buffer” amount of EDG that is between 1.2x - 1.7x the integration and marketing campaign. This buffer amount will be allocated to 2-of-3 multsig, where only one member of the multisig is on the Commonwealth team. This should allow for a third party to monitor any transaction between the multisig and the exchange.

From here, the full amount will be deposited into the exchange. While the integration and marketing will be allocated to the exchange, the remainder of the buffer could be used for market making. After a set amount of time of at least 6 months, the buffer amount would be able to be returned to the Edgeware Treasury.

The proposed cost is well within range with the treasury’s current budget, with the final amount to be included within the treasury proposal. To give a bit more of a concrete number, In the “[Treasury Budget](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/475-treasury-budget)” post, I indicated my thoughts that 20% of the overall treasury could go to growing the exchanges and growth. Right now the treasury stands at ~470m EDG. 

Given the caliber of the exchange and the existing strong support for the Polkadot ecosystem shown. I think this fee is reasonable, although lower is always better 🙂.


**Market Making Deposit (to a market maker + business model)**

Separately, to help foster a healthy market on this exchange and in accordance with generally accepted best practices, Commonwealth Labs has engaged with several professional market makers around ensuring a tight bid-ask spread, of $3k, to the treasury.

There are several ways that this deposit could be funded. First, Commonwealth Labs could use its own portion of the Edgeware supply, and realized PnL would accrue directly to Commonwealth, as part of ongoing market-making operations.

However, the treasury could also accrue this PnL. The Commonwealth team is of the opinion that the community can and should accrue the PnL from market-making, as it is a service that is being provided to and for the community.

The market makers think it would also be exciting to have the deposit come directly from the chain, as it’s an exciting community experiment. At present, the treasury has the funds available, and it is within the range of the Treasury Budget that I outlined earlier this summer. 

The market maker charges a $2k flat fee (in EDG) monthly to provide the service, which could be included in this proposal. I think it’s reasonable to allocate the year budget + the amount of EDG to be used to trade with to be allocated onto the exchange. Having the treasury fund the deposit would be another interesting experiment in terms of the Edgeware Treasury directly accruing profits, and in this case, supporting an activity (better exchange liquidity) that has been highly requested by the community. 

**Next steps**

At this point, we’ll invite community comments for the next five days. If the response from community members is unanimous on all counts, we’ll move to launch a proposal on Monday for the integration and marketing fee. And then later during the week, a proposal for the market making deposit where the market maker(s) can directly answer questions relating to a proposal.

Otherwise, we’ll launch a signaling proposal for each part of the proposal in question, which will run for 7 days, and then we will proceed from there.

We look forward to your comments and input!
