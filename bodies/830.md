The focus of this post ([and the linked full document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1IKWpe4V9JB2TsygE3bbRignjwOLlVnrL2XsetobYyKI/edit#)) is to try and set some bounds so that the community can come to consensus around if and how Edgeware becoming a parachain, some signals to run by the community, and tentative thoughts on making the parachain bonding process more dynamic in the long run. I've TLDR'ed the post here, but there is more context with the Google Doc.

The timing of this proposal going through the treasury is relatively flexible as the parachain bonding process is still rapidly developing--parachain bonding may not be ready until next year. (This would also impact delivery of the Edgeware 2020 Technical Milestone based on this item).

----
Edgeware was launched with the understanding that over time, it would be become a parachain. For the Edgeware community, becoming a parachain has two very direct effects, being able to connect to Acala, Stafi, and other projects with whom Edgeware already has relationships. Moreover, there’s a first mover advantage, this likely means that projects on Edgeware, especially DeFi projects, will be able add DOTs to their protocols which should have a direct beneficial effect on the overall health of Edgeware. Last, becoming a parachain will lower the effective security budget of the chain itself—potentially 2-3x less than the current 10% inflation directed to validators. With Rococo active and Polkadot launched, Edgeware is well on its way to fulfilling that mandate.

In short a potentially viable approach to crowdfund DOTs would be to offer: _**the lower of 1) allocating 1.5 - 3.5% of the Edgeware network or 2) 10- 20% yield in EDG to crowdfund backers. The thought is that we set aside a max portion (3.5%) but if the  EDG/ DOT price fluctuates and the auction is won, then 3.5% may be too much. In this case, individuals should receive a yield of 10 - 20% in EDG. Either way, this should be treasury funded, where EDG is vested over the course of a year.** _There are some additional parameters to tweak that should be voted on by signaling proposals before moving onto treasury and governance votes.

_Please note, this is an idealized plan, and there still technical components up for discussion._
