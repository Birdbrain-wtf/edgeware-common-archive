# **Proposal to Fund Integration of EDG into CryptoLocally**

## **About CryptoLocally**
CryptoLocally provides non-custodial, smart contract based escrows for P2P trading across multiple blockchains. It is a community-governed, decentralized marketplace for fiat-crypto P2P traders.

## **Problem Statement and Proposed Solution**
For a typical internet user, the barrier to entry to most cryptocurrencies remains extremely high. To buy an altcoin like EDG or DOT, users normally need to sign up and wait for verification on a centralized exchange, link a bank and buy bitcoin with fiat, and then pay for slippage converting into altcoins. For those in emerging markets, this process is even more of a headache, given that there is a lack of institutions willing to verify those that are typically unbanked. Our goal is to make cryptocurrencies easily accessible to everyone around the world.

CryptoLocally provides a very unique, easy to use, non-custodial solution for P2P trading. People can trade in their local currency by meeting in person, sending a bank transfer, or using KakaoPay, AliPay, WeChat Pay, Venmo and more. As a non-custodial solution, CryptoLocally helps users eliminate the middleman in their trades, and bypass the onerous fees and deposit/withdraw process imposed by custodial solutions.

We would like to become the first P2P exchange to integrate with the Polkadot ecosystem. Given that our escrows on other chains are smart contract based, we see a lot of potential synergy working with Edgeware. We would be listing EDG with the initial integration, which would allow our 25K+ users and the Edgeware, Polkadot communities to start trading the tokens peer-to-peer. We see EDG getting a lot more exposure in our main user bases in Africa and Southeast Asia through CryptoLocally.

We have already [asked our community for their thoughts on integrating Edgeware into CryptoLocally](https://gov.cryptolocally.com/t/using-edgeware-for-polkadot-ecosystem-integration/44)**,** and their responses have been quite positive.

Afterwards, we would also like to integrate staking/DeFi opportunities on CryptoLocally’s Finance Wallet, which is a simple, non-custodial wallet where users can earn staking rewards on Ethereum-based tokens at the moment. This would incentivize users to buy and hold the tokens through CryptoLocally.

## **Scope**
- **EDG listing on CryptoLocally (trading pairs with all fiat currencies supported on CryptoLocally)**
- **AMA for Edgeware team on CryptoLocally Telegram channel (5K+ members)**
- **Marketing activities (trading competition, giveaways in CryptoLocally GIV)**
