After an unsuccessful attempt to restart finality, I propose we bring back the fallback mechanism until we can be sure of a path to fixing the finality of Edgeware. This is a simple upgrade that just requires changing parameters and is built directly off the currently working master branch of both our node and our fork of substrate.

Additional changes are made to governance parameters and smart contract economic parameters (making them cheaper).

Changes being made:
- Adding Grandpa finality fallback
- LaunchPeriod -  2 days
- VotingPeriod - 4 days
- EnactmentPeriod - 1 days
- FastTrackVotingPeriod - 2 days
- RentByteFee - 0.00001 EDG (1 MilliCents)
- RentDepositOffset - 0.0001 EDG (100 MilliCents)
- SurchargeReward - 0.000015 EDG (15 MilliCents)
