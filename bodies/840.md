This would be a good replacement for the (currently centralized) Commonwealth backend. It's made by Textile, which is one of the companies supported by Protocol Labs to build infrastructure for Filecoin.

They are currently building out ACLs that can be used to moderate and control who can write to a forum or other collection of threads.

Github: [https://github.com/textileio/js-threaddb](https://github.com/textileio/js-threaddb)
