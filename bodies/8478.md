Voting Principles / Perspective

Hello Edgeware Citizens,
	 In anticipation in our future growth I’m providing my 5 voting principles. This is in effort to streamline my cooperation for Edgeware contributors creating funding proposals. The following is a breakdown of the factors towards my voting decision.

The Factors are:

1. **Citizen Reputation/ On Chain Judgment **
2. **Existing Contribution **
3. **Prior Proposal - Project Fulfillment**
4. **Milestone, Deliverables, Timelines  **
5. **Risk to Edgeware**


Bridging of principles: 

1. I cannot be in support of proposals from a citizen that has a bad judgment from a Registrar. 
2. I can only be in support of good faith agreement for citizen proposals with a prior contribution. 
3. I cannot support an additional proposal if the citizen has outstanding proposals. 

                  a. Outstanding proposals are _Project Length > 365 days_
      4.    I cannot support proposals without clear deliverables and fulfilment deadline/timelines for    those commitments.
      5.     I will not support proposals that I believe could subject or encourage Edgeware to experience unnecessary legal scrutiny or impede the long term sustainability.  

Curiosity:

1. This is would be in conflict with the value of our on-chain Identity function and the contributor(s) providing this service. 
2. Good Faith Agreements are built on prior contribution. Faith is equal to the display of good contribution for Edgeware’s citizens.   
3. I will consider citizens with outstanding proposals (Project Length since incentives > 365 days) with an overwhelming workload.  I will not place additional expectations on a contributor with existing chain fulfillments.  
4. Proposals without timelines, appear as having an unplanned execution. It’s possible to miss deadlines to unforeseen occurrences. I cannot build confidence in the contribution without milestones, deliverables and/or having the fulfilment of the proposal planned. 

                            a. Clear contribution for Post Paid Proposals. 
                            b. Reporting provided in the same location as the request for funds.   
      5.    Interpretation of the Law and punishment for infractions are determined by Judges. I will always vote to in effort to preserve our decentralized software and DAO named Edgeware.. Always in adherence and in effort toward legal compliancy. For the protection of our existing and future citizens and contributors of Edgeware. 



We are decentralized. So please, do not interpret this as a call to action. I am conveying my voting principles. 


I’ve been told that you understand a person when you have nothing to offer them. I prefer honest engagement, despite the outcome. I prefer to build a reputation on principles, rather then anything else.
