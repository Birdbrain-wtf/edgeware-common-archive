As part of our wg-builders strategy, we've put together Developers Deck, which should shine some spotlight on developers and attract them to develop on top of Edgeware. 
Here is the view [Edgeware: Developer Deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1bcPgZMTw_ppx35E41-DjJWnTjNQ3iV9E2UA0IjruDWI/edit?usp=sharing) on the slides. 

Main result and aim for this would be pinpoint for developers is 
- Introduce them Edgeware
- What are core features of Edgeware
- How to Develop - Flow & Tooling
- How they can engage and nurture their idea/project among focused working groups (hint on sidebar of this page)
- Funding their project - options to fuel their idea on-chain
- Show governance overview - outline how process works    
- Existing builders, working ecosystem


Show us your love, if you are curious what should be there or what we are missing, **your perspective is more than welcome.**
