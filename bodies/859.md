**Background** 

Edgeware is looking to recruit a PR agency to build awareness around the project in the lead up to a number of key project events including the gradual release of EDG to the 15m+ addresses that participated in the Lockdrop launch campaign, alongside key exchange listings, project announcements and community activities. 

The [attached document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tFRuVFlaSx47ki1Jdz9xNlP55FbxsVmhlwBQ2fPGSS0/edit) outlines three options for building out a cohesive but flexible communications strategy for the project - please add comments or thoughts to the document or comment below.

The three options:

**Option 1 - Community first**
The first option is built around an internal competency that is scaled out with a community focused approach through the addition of a dedicated team working in sync with emerging working groups and project teams. 

**Option 2 - Out-source to established PR agency Market-Across **
Thom Ivy has received a proposal from Market-Across, a well known crypto PR firm who come recommended by Ashley Tyson - the former head of PR at Web3.

You can view the full proposal [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1BR7iLcD3pHIaoP2zpmPRFe3b3z8mW652gtOYGodu76M/edit). 

**Option 3 - A hybrid**
With Edgeware gradually finding its feed as a community owned and operated network we bring in Damoy as the Edgeware PR lead to manage the relationship with Market-Across and sign a short term (3 month) contract that gives us runway to develop, scaleup and test our nascent communications strategy in the lead up to key events in the Edgeware calendar. 

**Note: We are setting a 7 day deadline for comments before a proposal will be put to the council on Wednesday 16th December. **

The 7 days are designed to enable us to refine the choices and decide on a path as well as agree budgets, deliverables and answer any questions. Then we put it to a vote with the council.
