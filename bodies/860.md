While still at the idea stage, this has been floated to several community members and received some positive feedback.

Working Groups, initially spearheaded by [@Thom Ivy](/edgeware/account/jKauGVPCQkteGTDKuuNwhYhMBrj5QuytgybEC4A5cS4Bki5) has been a way to help any group of community contributors can come forward and help with the chain in whatever area that they choose. [@mrbulb](/edgeware/account/jXC7ghviUzVcVySg3eD8prB7C9m6VzK6cw2MTyMTDTUye5q) has characterized these as a few different types (agencies, developer focused, interest groups, among others). They're able to receive funds the treasury

However, the current treasury process can be a bit heavy in some processes, requiring a supermajority of the council, or at least a ~21 day (soon to be 10 day) voting cycle for a democracy vote. For smaller grants, $2500 and under, this poses a high coordination cost.

I'm proposing to change the treasury and council pallet to lower these coordination costs by:

- Creating "multiple" treasury proposal types with different thresholds from the council. For example:
- small proposals of $2500 (or EDG equivalent) and under. These would require only a 4 / 13 approval threshold and a 7/13 threshold for vetoing.
- and anything above this requiring the standard 8 / 13 threshold
- Setting a budget per council member per term. For example: $25000 per threshold (or the equivalent in EDG)


It has the potential for some abuse, with a veto limit that is lower than the coordination cost of a "large" treasury proposal it should be mitigated. This also (as is the current) assumes that council members will vote with the chain's best interest. 

These changes would allow small proposals to pass quickly, and also by enabling working groups to get started on their efforts more quickly, this will also likely have the effect of increasing the experiments that can be run on chain!
