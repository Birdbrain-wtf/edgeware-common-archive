## Requested payments out of scope

The initial outline proposal of the proposal included the ability for the BWG to pay hourly rates for ongoing management work alongside a request to bill for work done to date.

On discussion [@raymondz](/edgeware/account/mae7JWE1EEMLcno2ZSB8dahwPqVPEyh8zik6dgNpQTvvucq) suggested that these management costs be removed.
![](https://commonwealth-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/a297382c-b46c-4ea9-b130-afe012aa7258.1607593988709)
With hindsight we would have preferred the ability to bill for this management time since there has been an enormous amount of time spent behind the scenes that has not been costed: 

- mediating between different groups including the council 
- arranging calls and stewarding meetings
- consolidating feedback
- the development of a wide range of associated initiatives / partnerships that are becoming key parts of the website's sell and indeed central to much of the progress that the community is making.
- Decred Working Group
- Artists Working Group 
- MoCDA partnership discussions
- Zora collaboration (post coming soon)
- Recruiting developers from [Vacuum Labs](https://vacuumlabs.com)
- Comms Strategy and team


In many ways this is becoming a near full-time job for me, on top of responsibilities outside with Lemonade Money and Decent. This suits me well though, since there is broad alignment and value add and so I am keen to continue. 

Having discussed at length with [@Thom Ivy](/edgeware/account/jKauGVPCQkteGTDKuuNwhYhMBrj5QuytgybEC4A5cS4Bki5) and [@Johan](/edgeware/account/m6dDgoheGKuQpghfGVhrTS8rY8x5ejpE5pY1HzMfdVbFdfj) we are also keen to continue as a group to push forward the EDG Agency and are all willing to throw ourselves into this on an ongoing basis. 

Rather than pay ourselves for this work out of our existing funds, we would like to solicit responses since it is not included in the original scope. 

Based on the responses we will either:

- Calculate time spent and bill accordingly from our existing funds with a new hourly rate to be agreed - this will be the subject of a separate post. 
- Submit a further funding request from the council to cover this work.


Going forward we will be submitting a separate funding request for ongoing work as we scale up the ambitions and capabilties of the EDG Agency and its associated initiatives.
