### Community
- Drew Stone opens [a discussion on recruiting developers to the network using a residency program](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/864-recruiting-developers-in-residence-to-edgeware), focusing on tooling, products, and protocols that benefit the public good and rapidly enhance network capabilities. - Dillon Chen discusses [formal modifications to the treasury proposal parameters and process in order to grant smaller amounts of funds to teams building or organizing in Edgeware.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/860-bootstrapping-more-working-groups-with-council-help)


### Governance
- The [proposal to fund the integration of EDG into "Exchange A",](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/827-proposal-top-15-exchange-by-cmc-integration-exchange-a) the pseudonym of a top 15 exchange, is now live on the chain and available for voting by the democracy. Recent discussions through the council encouraged this offer to be decided by the broader Edgeware community. [Voting takes only a few seconds once your account is connected](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/democracyproposal/15-treasuryapproveproposal18) and will have a MAJOR impact on the future of the network, as this could list EDG on its first major exchange. You [can also vote using the Polkadot Apps UI.](https://polkadot.js.org/apps/#/democracy)
- The proposal (Treasury Prop #19) [to fund EDG integration into the mobile wallet and Defi platform Frontier](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/760-proposal-fund-edg-integ-into-frontier-mobile-wallet) is live on the chain, and is ready for seconding. This integration would give EDG its first robust mobile wallet support on cross-platforms and enhance the ability of defi teams to participate with users through wallet-connections.
