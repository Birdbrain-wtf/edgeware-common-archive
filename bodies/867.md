### Governance
- Edgeware at this time has no officially supported exchanges, however Dillon Chen recently brings a [proposal to solve this: an integration for a top-15 exchange.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/827-proposal-top-15-exchange-by-cmc-integration-exchange-a) After discussion, this is going on-chain soon for voting.
- OnFinality [shares their proposal for a public node infrastructure project,](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/852-edg_tp_16-public-shared-nodes-initial-build) subsidized by the treasury for public use, enabling more and quick development activity.
- [DeepDAO offers to integrate Edgeware data into their platform](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/836-deepdao-edgeware-integration), giving access to DAO information and metrics to major venues and research firms, as well as the public.
- CryptoLocally [brings a proposal to integrate EDG into their peer-to-peer non-custodial trading platform, which relies on escrow functions to faciliate trades.](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/834-proposal-integrate-edg-into-cryptolocally)
- [Frontier Wallet Proposal continues to undergo examination](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/760-proposal-fund-edg-integ-into-frontier-mobile-wallet) by the community, and will likely go to on-chain when the team responds to the latest round of questions.
