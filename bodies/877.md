## Introduction 
Edgeware is currently in discussions with a '[Top 15 Exchange](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/827-proposal-top-15-exchange-by-cmc-integration-exchange-a)' proposal that requires treasury funds made up of both a listing fee and a marketing fee. A market-maker is also required as part of the offering. 

As a community owned and operated project with a wide distribution and a large native treasury there are challenges and opportunities when it comes to exchange listings.

For example the fact that compliance will be dealing with a community fund, rather than a foundation with a clear legal structure. On the opposing side, with a wide token distribution and active treasury there are all sorts of novel ways that the project can develop and coordinate community listing initiatives. 

This document seeks to spark ideas and strategies around community led listing campaigns that leverage the project’s unique characteristics - beginning with Binance who were one of the largest signallers in the 2019 lockdrop campaign and therefore a key partner in the ecosystems development. 

## **The background**
Edgeware has one of crypto's widest and fairest distributions - see @Thom Ivy's article[ here](https://docs.edgewa.re/edgeware-runtime/lockdrop/gini-coefficient-of-edgeware) all thanks to the innovative lockdrop[ launch campaign](https://blog.edgewa.re/full-details-on-the-edgeware-lockdrop/) by Commonwealth. 

> _A lockdrop is a new, innovative funding mechanism that aligns token holders of potentially many chains to seed growth for a new chain or project._
> _Users time-lock some or all of their tokens of an approved kind, in a unique, user-specific smart contract for varying amounts of time or signal their intent to participate in the project without locking any tokens, however these signaling users will receive a smaller reward per token._
> _Tokens that are locked within a lockdrop are returned to respective locker after the specified date._

Alongside many individuals, groups and organisations, this design enabled Binance to signal on behalf of the massive stash of ETH that they hold for customers, meaning that they instantly became one of the key partners* of Edgeware's community ecosystem.

* Note there are also other partners such as Aragon who also signalled from their large treasuries and who could also be the subject of strategic community partnerships and initiatives. 

## **The challenge/s**
Unlike many of those who lockdropped their ETH with intention, Binance’s future EDG holders will mostly have no idea what EDG is, why they should care and therefore there is the real chance that many will dump their coins when Binance distribute the coins. 

There have been some conversations with Binance - and now seems like as good a time as any to outline some strategies for not just making future EDG holders aware of their holdings, but also engaged and active within the community. 

**So how do we the Edgeware community, engage these EDG holders?**

The most impactful communications strategy doesn't just build hype, it leverages a project/product's core characteristic, capabilities and advantages - framing them in a way that educates, informs and entertains.

With Substrate's many emerging chains all touting similar capabilities and sideways composability of pallets across the ecosystem there will be a Darwinian development cycle where everyone can rapidly adopt the innovations of everyone else. 

In this competitive environment, the ability to package up core components in a novel and interesting way is key.

## **The concept - Lockdrop2: The NFT mining edition.  **
The lockdrop was a form of liquidity mining - namely, you locked up (or staked) ETH tokens in a contract and received tokens in return. The longer you locked, the greater the bonus on the tokens you receive.

Recently $MEME appeared on the scene - a [community led initiative](https://twitter.com/defidude/status/1294542736725544960?s=20) that tweaked liquidity mining by enabling the minting of NFTs in return for timelocking ETH and ERC20s alongside Uniswap pools. 

With Commonwealth's track record with the lockdrop, Edgeware's development of the[ Artist's Working Group](https://t.me/joinchat/GZzH7hjuK8zMssP45b8AEA), partnership with crypto-artist focused 'Museum Of Contemporary Digital Art ([MoCDA)](https://mocda.org), resident substrate NFT pioneer @yangwao alongside emerging relationships with creator focused[ Zora](https://store.zora.co/manifesto) who are on board to collaborate with[ Lemonade Money](https://lemonademoney.tv) and[ Four To The Floor](https://fttf.tv), there may be an opportunity to push things a bit further.

## **How it (might) work:**

We scale up $MEME's novel approach to minting NFTs for EDG ahead of the Binance distribution, enabling holders to lockdrop EDG in return for receiving unique NFTs that are curated and created by some of the best artists inside and outside of crypto.

These genesis NFTs will confer emerging governance, access and utility rights in Edgeware's emerging Web 2 >< Web3 media ecosystem (all tbc). 

- Innovative campaign that drives awareness and engagement


We (the Edgeware community) can work with Binance to architect a crypto 'first' that will drive awareness inside and outside crypto, therefore giving them additional exposure and benefitting from being part of a true 'community' effort that demonstrates the exciting future of crypto beyond their regular crowd.

It also has the effect of ‘re-launching’ Edgeware into the crypto-ecosystem… 

- Is it cost effective?


We can leverage the Edgeware treasury in cost effective way to drive the launch campaign, alongside value adds from Binance, essentially making the money work a lot harder than just paying for a listing.

It may even be a launchpad for Edgeware to start profitably curating NFTs with the help of Zora. 

- Demonstrates project characteristics


We can throw a bunch of things in here. Smart Contracts. Defi. NFTs. Cross-chain compatibility. Arts and Culture. Community participation, EVM. partnerships, DAOs... 

Its an idea that can really pull the community together around a project that will engage a wide range of expertise.

- Establishes unique project narrative and positioning


We can leverage this as a launchpad to carve out Edgeware's unique positioning in terms of fair and broad distribution, funding and potential to build crypto's most ambitious media ecosystem across Web2/Web3, at a point in time when NFTs and creators are having their moment in the crypto-sun.
