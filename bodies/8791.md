{This framework is currently in the draft stage. Parameters are subject to change based on the discussions by the community members/contributors.}

## **Purpose of the framework: **
To do treasury management/planning for the longevity of Edgeware’s self-sustainability in bearish market sentiment.


## Calculations:

Framework timeline {FT}: 3 years = 36 months
Current Treasury balance {CTB}: 412.6547 Million EDG
Pending incentives {PI}: SCU’s November Proposal + non-SCU contributor’s incentives until November + OnFinality’s Q4 proposal + Dwellir’s Q4 proposal = ~135 M EDG (included buffer)

Treasury spending available per month (without considering the treasury minting due to inflation) {T1 = (CTB - PI) / FT}: 7.71263056 M EDG

Ideal staking ratio {ISR}: 50%
Current % of total supply staked {TSS}: 21.3%
Current Inflation {CI}: 5.7%
Current Total Supply {CTS}: 7.2703 Billion EDG = 7270.3 Million EDG

Treasury spending available per month (considering the treasury minting due to inflation) 
{T2 = T1 +(((ISR-TSS)*CI*CTS/(ISR*100*12): 27.5351036 M EDG

Infrastructural costs {IC}: JelliedOwl + Bharthcoorg + OnFinality + Dwellir + Subscan

Treasury spending available per month deducting infrastructure costs {T}: T2 - IC

Base incentives {B}: 300 USD equivalent in EDG (with reference price equal to whichever is the highest amongst 0.0004 USD per EDG or the 30-day trailing median price)

Number of solo/team contributors: N

Previous incentives per month of an individual contributor: P
Sum of all the previous incentives per month of individual contributors: S


Effective incentives for the contributors: B + [ (T - (B * N)) * P / S ]



## **Proposed Clauses:**
- Contributors are free to level down their contributions and also work under other roles within/outside Edgeverse.
- Contributors can deduct their incentives further (than their corresponding effective incentives) if they want.
- Contributors who don’t scale down their contributions can recoup the total difference in incentives once the market situation improves.
- Except for the infrastructural costs, all other incentives will be re-rated and thus the effective incentives might not reflect the same value in fiat currencies.
- Contributors shall get incentives until November as per their old structure (subject to approval from the community via simpleMajority referendum/referenda). {With/without vesting or locking period?}
- Contributors are advised to liquidate their incentives corresponding to November month via OTC if possible or in the worst-case scenario take help from the DFS contributors to liquidate on spot markets using the limit order method.
- Base incentives mentioned above are not supposed to apply to the regional mods (as their current incentives are lower than that individually) and their incentives will be capped at 125 USD equivalent in EDG per community with the reference price equal to whichever is the highest amongst 0.0004 USD per EDG or the 30-day trailing median price.
