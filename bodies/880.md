As we head into 2020, I wanted to drop this post as a "decentralization roadmap" for Edgeware to accompany [@drewstone](/edgeware/account/hwR8hAatmmdupBLXQSxLUPBa8GhRomLD9hf6iRtFeXs8fcY)'s [Proposed Technical Roadmap](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/878-edgeware-builders-guild-2021-technical-roadmap-ideas). From a token distribution perspective, Edgeware is quite decentralized already. However, this isn’t yet reflected in the network’s core contributors. 

## [Link to the full proposal (with estimated budget, open for comments)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1U5KsZWBrUBZ006J1Rwx4wX8uWLqT4xS8tBDNS2SJBB0/edit?usp=sharing)

We’d like to propose forming a new team of chain-leads, who would then set up BD and developer incentives, to take this step towards the original goal of an on-chain treasury that self-perpetuates the chain.

This would be conducted in an open process, where potential chain leads would post their proposals, and community members would debate them before the proposal goes to a vote. Hopefully, this would resolve any questions around how existing leadership positions are compensated, as [@mrbulb](/edgeware/account/jXC7ghviUzVcVySg3eD8prB7C9m6VzK6cw2MTyMTDTUye5q) has [mentioned](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/861-bwg-now-edg-agency-update-payments). 

Individuals from Commonwealth Labs that want chain-leadership positions would go through this open process, like any other candidate. After all, while Commonwealth Labs helped launch Edgeware, there may be individuals or organizations that may be better suited to driving the project forward. This would also replace any block grants requested by Commonwealth. 

**Chain-leads **would be**:**
- Directly accountable to Edgeware governance. Their compensation and performance would be individually approved by the chain, and paid out from the treasury.
- Paid industry-standard compensation. For a team of 5-6 chain leads, a ballpark figure is ~$500k or 6 * ~$80k, which at current prices is ~71m EDG or ~15% of the treasury budget. This leaves ample room to fund parachain bonding, developer teams, projects, incentives, working groups and more.
- Responsible for outlining a strategy / budget for their leadership area. They would need to propose a budget that enumerates costs for specific projects, incidental expenses, contractors, etc.
- Integrated with existing structures, including working groups and the council. Ideally, a chain-lead would lead an existing working group, and be voted into the council to give them the required network buy-in.


There are a few proposed **chain-leadership positions, **some of which have been taken by Commonwealth or existing chain contractors**:**

- **A GM to help align everyone towards a vision. **This person would help define the vision, governance, and public messaging of the project, working with individual chain leads to align all parties towards a common vision. 
- **A blockchain technical lead.** The technical lead of the project would be overall responsible for core development, new pallets and modules, and maintenance of the chain. They would also serve as a manager and technical leader for our ecosystem of individual contributors. 
- **A developer ecosystem lead**. An individual to help lead the existing developer community, grow the developer ecosystem, and figure out an incentive model for individual developers. (One potential idea for these incentives is to create a public pool that pays contract developers based on fees generated by a contract, similar to the fee earned by yEARN strategy creators.**[ Some discussion has been started here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/798-developer-incentive-dev-mining-by-uma).)**
- **A community lead to support collectives. **The lead should be able to articulate a community strategy to continue to widen engagement across social channels. We need to figure out a process for the ongoing funding and operation of many collectives. 
- **Governance Secretary: **There’s been growing discussion on how the governance process still isn’t coordinated or communicated well, especially as proposals roll out. 
- **BD Lead ([here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/804-grant-proposal-draft-bd-lead)): **The community fields a fair amount of interest on partnerships and deploying on Edgeware, and having more BD capacity will help us close those partnerships and turn them into active builders on the network.
