## Proposal Details Overview
Blockchain infrastructure setup can be a complex and time-consuming process that requires specialised DevOps skills and can be costly to maintain. However, Dwellir's infrastructure offering provides value to your project in several ways:

- Our team has the expertise to handle the technical details of setup, including tasks such as managing SSL certificates, configuring WebSockets, setting up HTTPS, handling firewall configurations, implementing security measures, monitoring performance, reporting on key metrics, and managing upgrades.
- We own and operate our infrastructure, offering fair and sustainable pricing that won't experience the sudden price increases often seen with cloud providers.
- Our innovative cluster stack allows seamless scaling to handle sudden traffic increases, a common occurrence when new projects launch. This ensures that your infrastructure can handle the demands of your users without the need for manual intervention.
- By outsourcing the maintenance of your blockchain infrastructure to us, you can free up valuable time and resources for your team to focus on other aspects of your project. This can be especially beneficial during crunch times when every moment counts.
