## What is Subsocial?
- It is an open protocol for decentralized censorship-resistant social networking on Substrate & IPFS.
- We have a running betanet (1.9m blocks so far) and a web app integrated with the betanet and Polkadot.js Extension.
- Subsocial is supported by Web3 Foundation via grants program, and we are part of Substrate Builders Program by Parity.

## Current features of Subsocial:
- Create a profile for a blockchain account.
- Create a space (similar to a blog or group on other social networks).
- Post in a space.
- Comment on a post, reply to a comment.
- Edit space, post, comment, profile.
- Upvote/downvote a post, a comment.
- Follow a space.
- Follow an account.
- Create different roles and permissions for your space(s) to control who can do what kind of actions (extrinsics) in your space(s), e.g. create Moderator, Editor, Member roles and assign them to the accounts that need these roles. 
- See a personalized feed of new posts from the spaces you follow.
- See notifications about activities related to your account: e.g. somebody followed your space or account, replied to your post or comment, reacted to your post or comment, etc.
- Full-text search over profiles, spaces, posts.
- SEO - search engine optimization - all pages of Subsocial are indexed by Google: profiles, spaces, posts, comments. You can check it with the next Google Search query: https://www.google.com/search?q=site:app.subsocial.network
- All text and images are stored in IPFS.

## Some integration ideas:

### Support Edg. on-chain profiles on Subsocial

If you go to https://app.subsocial.network/ and hover over a post author or go to an account page, you can see their information about a profile: account address, full name (if specified, balance, etc. We could add an extra section that shows on-chain info from Edgware for this account.

### Discuss Edg. on-chain proposals on Subsocial

As I mentioned above, Subsocial already has a set of functionality that can be used to have user-friendly discussions (similarly to what we can do here on CommonWealth), i.e. start discussion threads and see updates in your notifications. For example, one could create a post that describes a tip and others could comment on that, or start a thread to discuss an on-chain proposal, etc.

### Comprehensive support for Edg. on-chain governance

Currently, we do not support integration with on-chain governance, but we would like to. It would be great to have more feature-rich support for on-chain governance, as we have here on CommonWealth or Polkassembly.

### Discuss any Edg. tx on Subsocial

Remember the "Comments" tab on Etherscan pages? The integrated a centralized Discuss commenting system. The idea is to do a similar thing from the UI perspective but in a truly decentralized way.

As an additional feature, we could make our commenting embeddable, so that anyone can attach Subsocial tree-like comments to any entity, e.g. to embed comments on the tx page on blockchain explorers. This means that comments will be on a blockchain explorer, on Subsocial, and anywhere you need them.

### Discuss Edg. councilors and validators on Subsocial

Similar to tx discussions, any other entity can be wrapped in a post and have support for tree-like comments out of the box. The thing is that this should be well thought and designed from the UI/UX perspective, e.g. users navigate to a page "Council" or "Validators" and can start a discussion about the performance of a council member or a validator, ask them a question, discuss their council elections program, or report for some reason so that others can submit their feedback.

### Integration in Subsocial mobile app

This one is a little bit tricky because we don't have a mobile app yet but started R&D this week. Still comparing different approaches: Flutter vs React Native, etc. And comparing a few state management plugins for Flutter. But the mobile app is among the priority features at Subsocial (because we have only a web app as of today). That's why I would like to share with you, that if you are interested, it would be good to consider integration with Edg. into the Subsocial mobile app from day 1 and to put this into its architecture.
