## Positive impacts:
- **Increases security **{As the barrier to getting into the active slots will increase, paired with a significant number of awaiting validators, the network security will strengthen compared to the current situation. This could also result in healthy competition between validators for nominations.}
- **An easier breakeven point for validators **{Validators could experience increased APYs for a short term. Also, individuals/entities hosting multiple validators can shut down a few of their validators to reduce hosting costs and also to give awaiting validators a chance to be part of the consensus.}
- **Performance benefits **{Raw performance will increase and all the cycles will conclude in time (unlike now where several governance-related cycles are taking 3.061% higher time due to unoccupied 3 validator slots). But the increased performance difference will be significantly so small to be noticed by the end users.}


## Negative impacts:
- **Decreases decentralisation **{Not all individuals/entities host multiple validators. So by decreasing the slots, the network security could become less decentralized. Also, the multiple validators with comparatively more nominations belonging to the same individual/entity could choose to not shut down some of their validators which could result in some existing individuals/entities not being part of the network security at all despite having multiple validators but with comparatively fewer nominations. TLDR? Short-term chaos and conflicts between community members.} 
- **Decrease the percentage of total supply staked **{Less active nominators’ nominations could become inactive due to 48 validators going to the awaiting list at once. This could result in a decrease in the percentage of total supply staked. We also don’t have nomination pools yet to tackle such a huge reduction in validators’ slots.}
- **Losing the awaiting validators forever **{Awaiting validators might choose to stop the validation if they run out of the profit margin, especially in such a bear market scenario. Gaining back some of the validators in future could become more difficult than preserving them now. A related discussion worth listening to: [https://youtu.be/Ybewx2kxTm0?t=3799](https://youtu.be/Ybewx2kxTm0?t=3799) (previously highlighted by Zovern)}


## Neutral impacts:
- **Increases treasury minting without affecting the inflation** {Due to the potential decrease in the percentage of total supply staked along with unchanged ideal staking ratio (of 50%) will result in more part of the inflation going to the treasury and less to the validators/nominators as compared to the current levels.}
