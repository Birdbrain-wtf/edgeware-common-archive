If 2020 was the year DeFi took the crypto-headlines then 2021 is looking very much like it will be the year that NFTs go mainstream.

[@Decent Partners](/edgeware/account/ks9tY4vnb5oEGsj5QyrJ1Z66ydjMbjhy623zMBdnCigfGAk) have been working on a collaboration with[ Zora](https://ourzora.com) for the past few months in anticipation of the launch of their full protocol in January. 

The aim is to create a novel new NFT ecosystem that can push experimentation in the space and also pioneer some crypto firsts... specifically profitable curation of NFTs for a community owned and operated DAO such as Edgeware leading to a sustainable way to commission artists, creators and musicians and sustain the development of a new NFT platform and creative network -[ New Futures](https://new-futures.co).

## **What is Zora?**
Existing NFT marketplaces are setting out to create single destinations for NFT drops - re-creating a traditional gallery relationship with artists, taking fees for listings and sales.

Zora is setting out to become a protocol rather than a gallery, with the smart contracts leveraging a token bonding curve. In the future they are moving from the minting of NFTs to the issuance of community tokens that are then retro-actively airdropped to active participants on Zora profiles.

Zora does not take fees, instead preferring the approach of bootstrapping many active profiles and enabling each piece of media to become a market-place. 

They are also thinking ahead of where the market is right now - images, video and audio linked to NFTs to a near future where every asset in the whole media creation stack can be tokenised, composed and remixed, with ownership and value accruing to the atomic units of storytelling.

## **What investors are missing with NFTs**
Well known investors like Anthony Pompliano are driving speculation around crypto-art - suggesting the market can become [bigger than the existing art industry](https://pomp.substack.com/p/the-next-big-bet-digital-art). In the short term there will almost certainly be a hype cycle based around individual artists, but in general the real revolution will be in how the underlying innovations make an entirely new type of creative process possible. 

Rather than the market revolving around single asset based sales in the traditional gallery or musician model, value will flow through narrative networks - each composed of many NFT based elements and their relationships… 

This is the dawn of collective creation and ownership, where rights can be packaged and commercialised in the ‘old world’ but return value back to all those who were part of every part of the creative process. From here we will see story worlds develop, born through borderless creative collaboration - world’s that will one day dwarf the value and cultural influence of today’s most famous IP.

## **How this works for creators**
- NFTs can be assigned to all media in the creative stack - not just ‘finished’ works such as videos, songs or images, which are themselves the end result of a complex combination of creative assets. 
- Once minted the NFT is always for sale, with the new owner inheriting all previous purchase offers.
- The creator can select their preferred bid - not necessarily the highest which opens up interesting ways for curators to purchase assets ahead of the market.
- Creators can add a sell on fee which returns a % of future each sale to them, no matter how many times the NFT is bought and sold meaning value always flows back to them.


## **How this works for curators**
- It is possible to profitably commission via a 'slice' mechanic - curators such as Edgeware can 'advance' an artist funds which gives them ownership of an NFT's market and a % cut of the** first sale.**
- The creator retains their perpetual share of future sales.


## **How this works for collectives/DAOs**
- In the future Zora are looking to launch networks of community tokens, meaning there is the ability for collectives to also have a % of future tokens.
- As mentioned previously, it is important to understand that this is not just about owning single assets, but about owning part of an emerging narrative.

There will also be the opportunity to package up collections of NFTs into collectively owned IP that can then be sold to established media platforms such as Netflix or Spotify. 

For those platforms, collective ownership can simplifies the sourcing and ownership of in demand IP, streamlining the cost structures and offering a chance to scale new projects that bring with them not just an engaged fan-base, but a whole creative community who will benefit from any value creation. 

In time, the expansion of New-Futures and the network of community IP will enable collective bargaining power of the sort enjoyed by large cooperatives - ensuring better terms and ultimately flipping the script on the established vortex of the new media streaming giants. 

## **What is New-Futures?**
New-Futures is building a creative network that will mint and market a unique breed of NFT collectibles based on original ideas and bespoke partnerships. 

The project represents a new collective attitude towards contemporary art and music and releases highly curated drops from both world-renowned and emerging artists that explore and define new possibilities in the NFT industry.

The project will utilise Zora's protocol and both onboard creators and facilitate creative development of ideas that can become iconic ownable moments in culture.

The aim is to bridge mainstream and crypto-media to create era defining assets that will be the launchpad for a waterfall of sustainably funded and collectively owned creative networks.
