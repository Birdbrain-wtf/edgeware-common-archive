We currently have a treasury of 550m EDG worth around ~$4 million USD. All of this has been printed in the 10 months since mainnet launch.

The amount printed remains constant unless adjusted by governance. This means we're on track to print another ~$5m/year from Feb 2021-Feb 2022.

- **We aren't able to deploy $5m/year effectively** towards builders, PR/community, exchanges/market development, and all the other functions related to running the chain.
- **Having a large treasury is a vector for attack. **We already have a large treasury surplus.
- **Treating EDG as something which can be arbitrarily printed out of thin air reduces confidence in the value of EDG. **I don't even think we can sell that much EDG per year.


Speaking as a stakeholder in the network, I would prefer if treasury inflation were either 1) reduced on a regular schedule, or 2) reduced once and then carefully supervised by on-chain governance.

---

## **How much treasury EDG can we effectively deploy?**

I am working off the [budget proposed by Dillon last week](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1U5KsZWBrUBZ006J1Rwx4wX8uWLqT4xS8tBDNS2SJBB0/edit#heading=h.cvbp7r2i0r32) in his Chain Leads post. It allocates 525m EDG for a year of operations:

- $525k/year to chain leads
- $700k/year to builders
- $525k/year to marketing, PR, brand, and community
- $525k/year to market development, e.g. exchanges (but this may not be an expense after Feb-Mar 2021 once key deals are done)
- $350k/year to ecosystem incentives
- $350k/year to BuilDAO (but by my estimates, it would be premature to re-up till early 2022)
- $700k/year to parachain bonding


I think we should try to follow this budget, but it will take time to ramp up to deploying this much EDG effectively, and we will not actually spend that much.

Chain leads will take time to onboard. Projects will take time to spin up. BuilDAO and some other expenses have already been funded.

We should fund high-caliber chain leads and projects, and be comfortable paying a little higher than market, yet even if we do so, my estimate is that **we'll only spend half to two-thirds of this proposed 525m EDG in 2021.**

We should calibrate the treasury reward down accordingly.

## **Proposals for calibrating the treasury reward**

I've advanced three democracy proposals:

- **Democracy Proposal #16** sets treasuryReward inflation to zero.
- **Democracy Proposal #17** sets treasuryReward inflation to 29 EDG/block, or 30% of the current inflation (~$1m/year).
- **Democracy Proposal #18** sets treasuryReward inflation to 62 EDG/block, or 60% of the current inflation (~$2m/year).


_Note that treasuryReward inflation is **in addition to treasury funds collected from the staking module due to staking inefficiencies**, which amounts to an additional ~5% inflation per year, or ~$1.5m/year._

A vote for #16 is a vote to set inflation around $1.5m/year. The continued treasury printing would cover nondiscretionary expenses (parachain bonding, core chain leads, etc.). This is essentially a **restrictive budget** where we would spend down the 550m in the treasury over a year or two.

A vote for #17 is a vote to set inflation around $2.5m/year. We would be able to hire chain leads, hire 5-10 builders, and pay for brand and community projects. This is essentially a **moderate budget** that reduces inflation to around the amount/year required for Dillon's roadmap.

A vote for #18 is a vote to set inflation around $3.5m/year. The treasury would continue to hold 500m+ EDG, which would continue to grow even as we spend the new treasury inflation. This is essentially a **liberal budget** that maintains very high inflation.

**Again, if we pass _any_ of these budgets, we'd still be spending down the current 525m EDG in the treasury.** So under even the most minimalist option, we would be able to pay for the full set of chain leads and operating expenses in 2021, and have $1-1.5m/year in surplus left over afterwards.

And this is assuming EDG does not change in value.
