The [Lemonade Money ](https://lemonademoney.tv)team are [developing original content](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/683-edgeware-original-audio-and-video-development) for Edgeware starting with the new website’s launch film - the initial deliverables are below.

(The below is from the deliverables).

- _Project film treatment and mood film_
- _Builds from the existing BWG initiatives and values_
- _Sets out unique project positioning_
- _Establishes Edgeware as a pioneering network._


We will use this as the starting point for inspiration for the full production of the main film and further original content produced by the network. 

This approach will result in a main film concept that aims to align many different perspectives and will appear on the new project website.

This film is oriented around the high level concept of “what will you build?” which plays to the community oriented nature of the project and the ability of the treasury to kickstart collective initiatives across a wide range of areas.

It will be positioned towards the top of the new website and as such will need to connect with and build from and to the rest of the copy on the site. 

As part of this work we will be conducting filmed interviews with community members in order to ask questions, capture great soundbites and to ultimately bring alive a compelling community film that is created from a diverse set of opinions made up of established and emerging names and faces in the community. 

This builds on some of the research conducted [here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/536-why-are-you-here-edgeware-founding-values-culture-and-focus). 

**Interviews:**
The aim is to conduct a series of 40 min interviews over the next week. 

The Lemonade Money team led by [@jjj](/edgeware/account/jqCACU9fQzGpzRzMkoZLyWKsCwtkG5zTpUgXkYvfPqwZ3Rt) and James Payne will be conducting these. 

They are keen to interview established and emerging voices in the community as well as get some input from those who are keen to start a community or project that might be kickstarted by the treasury.

For this initial research phase we are aiming to limit the discussion to 10-12 people from the community who represent a diverse view of the network - we are currently reaching out to people right now. 

**Timeline (update)**
As stated in the initial proposal the aim was for delivery of 3-4 weeks from the date of payment. 

The Christmas break kind of messed with those plans so we are setting this delivery now to the final week in January when all relevant materials will be shared with the community. 

**Rights and Usage: **
At this stage, we are developing the concept behind the full film, so anything recorded will be shared with a limited number of people via a private Vimeo video. 

Consider this our video ‘alpha’ release, based on this work we will likely conduct another round of interviews for the final film that will also feature original content shot for the sequences.
