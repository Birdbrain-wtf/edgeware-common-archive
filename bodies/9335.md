Recently we had a discussion in one of the Community Calls about the 5% bond when making a proposal.

At the moment the proposer loses the 5% bond if the proposal failed to pass.
So, I just had a thought. which at the moment is all over the place but with all the suggestions could evolve into something useful.

For the 5% bond, I was thinking to make it like tax or, something similar where you’ll lose the bond no matter if the proposal passes or not.

Then have NFTs that can be minted on chain with utilities. So, I can think of **2 utilities** for now. 

1. Getting NFTs for finishing bounties or other community works and get rid of tips. 
This could be like a tier list (For value). So, depending on the tier list, you’ll get NFTs that can be traded for $EDG (From treasury) or it can also be traded for USDT with another person. 
So, someone can sell the $EDG NFT for USDT instantly for whatever price they decide upon (Probably less). 
This utility won’t be of much use now but maybe later when we have many tasks that can be put as bounties, this would make it easier. 

2. We can mint NFTs using $EDG that will give you random NFTs with some utility, I can only think of 1 utility for now. 

**Free bond fee** (Rare NFT, low drop %). This is for the first suggestion I made that is the bond fee as tax. So, no matter how much the proposal is asking for. You can cash in with this NFT. 

Example: I am asking for 30k$ worth $EDG. Bond fee that I’ll be losing is 1500(As tax. pass or fail). So, I could buy this NFT off of someone who has it for like maybe 500$ worth of $EDG. I save 1000$. This NFT is one time use only and will go back in to the collection after use.

We could have lot more other utilities as well with low tier, mid-tier, and high tier in rarity.
