For some time now I've been concerned about certain brand challenges with the current name.

**Ticker Symbol Collision with Edgeless:**
This has lead to..
- Users buying the incorrect token, Edgeless, because they share the same EDG ticker. Sometimes they do not find out it is not an ERC-20 for some time, and usually it occurs in our support channels.
- [API issues with CoiMarketCap due to sharing the same ticker.](https://twitter.com/HeyAxP/status/1346686899503525888)
- Difficulty searching for Edgeware in aggregation sites.
- General confusion about the projects ID and relationship with Edgeless, a gambling protocol.


**SEO and Search Challenges:**
- There exist traditional companies with the name Edgeware, who have offered to sell us their domain and twitter account for 40k USD+. 
- SEO issues with Edgware, UK, a suburb in northern London.
- Our existing domain, Edgewa.re, is difficult to type and recall, increasing friction with users attempting to access our current aged page--- and will interfere with users attempting to access our new, invested-into site design. 


**Branding-Specific Challenges:**
- Originally composed to mean cutting-edge software, this terminology does not describe the function of the software.
- **It increasingly does not map whatsoever to our developing use-case of supporting an open digital economy. **


In light of the overwhelming and growing list of issues with our brand, I would like to open a conversation and advocate for a renaming of the system, currency and ticker symbol to better encompass what it has evolved to, and to resolve the above issues.

The time to do this is now, because:

- Richard and the Agency have scoped a brand refresh and having this change occur before or during that work is natural to avoid wasted effort on refreshing an outdated brand.
- Binance has not yet released the EDG tokens received on behalf of their users via lockdrop signaling, and our brand has not yet reached clear exposure with the majority of the future holders.
- We are unlisted as of yet on a major exchange, but this is approaching.
- It is presumably cheaper to find our own name and domain resources than spending treasury funds on a site that engines and time have associated to a traditional technology company or a London suburb.


I realize we are all affectionately connected to the current name, but I believe our way forward can be much more successful by maximizing the value of the use case development, site design, and exchange partnerships that are rapidly approaching.
