**Intro**
Since blockchains are social systems, the mechanism design underlying the social coordination, incentivisation and management of individuals and collectives is perhaps the single most important barrier to Edgeware’s ecosystem development. 

**Challenge**
There are many different roles, responsibilities, interests and agendas within the project and defining the names, language, reward mechanics, social status, reputation accrual, accountability and roadmaps is imperative if the project is to have any chance at achieving anything close to social scalability. 

**About this document**
The linked document is a work in progress document that attempts to create a simple framework for understanding the different components of the emerging ecosystem through a structure that provides more context than is currently available.

The aim is that it can provide a consistent framework to enable Edgeware to socially scale its collective endeavours, whilst also retaining a flexible and nuanced approach to how different individuals and groups can contribute to the chain.

As noted - this is a working document, so all input / comment is appreciated. 

It is one part of an larger Community Roadmap which will be published soon, that seeks to outline the key opportunities and. challenges facing the network alongside recommendations for the next 12 months activities that will also include a series of already published and soon to be published project proposals.
