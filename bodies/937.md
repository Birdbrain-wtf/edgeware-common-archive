With the new initiative to produce a cryptomedia network funded by our treasury, I've begun thinking about formats and promotional projects.

# Whale $POD
Tokenized Reality TV
> Summary: A reality-tv style show combining personal tokens, markets, and zany drama to allow whales to fight for a scarce resource, with viewers part of the action as decisionmakers in the show's events.

## Stage 1: Pre-Season
## 
**Distribution and Show Announcement**
$POD tokens are distributed to EDG holders according to activity, as proposed by Drew recently. 

**Tokenized Person Offers**
Main-contestants offer to tokenize themselves  and sell ~50% of the $CONTESTANT at competitive rates to holders of the $POD token. 

**Tokenized Contestant Selection**
$POD token holders vote on which main contestant's offer (and their personal tokens) to accept for a season, at the $POD/$CONTESTANT rate set by the offer in the previous stage.

## Stage 2: Game Season

**Whale Buy-In**
Once a seasons' main contestant is selected, $SHOW holders can buy them to participate as Whales, with the top 5-12 whales by amount being selected for the season after a time period of open trading. At any point in the show, a whale may be bumped off the show by a new holder who exceeds their token amount.

**Gaming the Market**
Once the season begins, whale-contestants make deals among themselves and the larger $POD holder market who may also own the personal tokens of the contestant, to accrue the right to determine or influence the actions of the main contestant within some (entertainment-focused bounds,) a la shareholder-style governance. 

**An episode consistent of:**
- A prompt or set of bounds for the tokenized contestant.
- An open-market period where whale-contestants decide how much they want to participate in the decisions.
- A voting period where they vote their tokens of the contestant according to the prompt.
- The tokenized contestant acting out the decision.


**The Goal for Whales**
By the end of the season, the goal of a whale is to maximize their amount of the $CONTESTANT by influencing the market for their tokens over the course of the season and engaging in buys and sells with both co-competitors and the $POD. Volatility is a major factor in success.

## Stage 3: Post-Season

- With $PERSONAL exchangeable for $POD, and $POD priced by the market, $CONTESTANT tokens can increase in value and scarcity significantly for the contestants. 
- By the end of the show, the notoriety of contestants and the likelihood of selection for future seasons may maintain or improve their personal token value. 
- Whales and $SHOW holders will select for contestants that best fit their strategies for shaping the market and also improve $SHOW value.



## Points for Improvement
The tokenization offers, end-game, prompt and decision structure, and winning conditions can be improved or altered significantly to make more sense with the interests of participants and viewers, and possibly the entire scheme can be more simplified and dramatized.
