**Elastic Supply Background**

Blockchains form voluntary markets to bootstrap new public goods. First with bitcoin, used now as a store of value. Now, [elastic supply tokens](https://www.emptyset.finance/) are able to form voluntary markets for a better medium of exchange. They help resolve the set of problems that are listed below:

- Governments face challenges in establishing the price of money.
- Consumers are searching for yield in a zero interest rate environment
- Need to catalyze a wholesale switch, which requires coordination (or regulation) of a variety of ecosystem actors.
- Securing new infrastructure and settlements layer is capital intense (BTC / ETH block reward / miners rewards)


In theory, elastic supply tokens stabilize around a price by matching supply and demand. Individuals try to speculate or arbitrage around this price, allowing the price to fluctuate based around the target price. If the actual price is above the target price, then supply expands, if it is below the target price, then supply shrinks. _Even if the price is above the target price, it may potentially be rational for an individual to get the token if they expect that demand will continue to grow beyond their entry point._ These coins have grown to several hundreds of millions in marketcap in a matter of months.

Variations on this basic model, change the rate and time between supply expansions or contractions, as well as well as whom takes on the risk or reward of supply expansions or contractions. It also effectively rewards early participants whom help popularize the usage of this asset, as more individuals enter the system, by getting the token, the price moves up past the target price, rewarding early holders. Note there are well known potential attack vectors on elastic supply currencies, such as a [Soros Attack](https://ethresear.ch/t/zero-governance-seigniorage-shares-stablecoin-using-uniswap-v2/7471).

From here, holders of the asset can vote in and participate in decision making around governance, pursuing different projects. This same design can be used to bootstrap other voluntary markets for public goods. Here, we discuss using an elastic supply asset to bootstrap carbon removal markets.

**Carbon Removal**

Climate change is a defining challenge of our time, largely led by the accumulation of carbon emissions. Actions on regulating carbon follows a similar breakdown of coordination:

- Governments face challenges in establishing market prices around carbon.
- Requires coordination (or regulation) of a variety of ecosystem actors (NGOs, energy companies, financiers, and consumers).
- Reversing climate change is capital intense
- Yet, there is popular demand for action on climate change with consumers voting with their dollars and voices.


Already, voluntary markets have been established for carbon removal credits. Using something like [Wren](https://www.wren.co/), users can voluntarily offset their emissions at a cost of some ~$20/ton. They have already offset $700k+ of carbon emissions. Individuals can purchase carbon removal credits to offset their own carbon emissions. While these function well in several cases, they still have not seen wide uptake in terms of offsetting or removing large fractions of global carbon emissions.

By borrowing the incentives of elastic supply stable asset, we can potentially help solve some bootstrap demand. The supply of **Carbon (CRBN)** expands to meet (or contracts) to meet the demand for carbon to be removed from the atmosphere at a certain price. Even if the current price for **CRBN** is higher than the target price, _it may potentially be rational for more climate activists to come in, to purchase and support a worthwhile cause._ While the current carbon offset market is $0.5B, some estimate that it will grow to **[$200B by 2050](https://www.spglobal.com/platts/en/market-insights/latest-news/natural-gas/051320-global-carbon-offsets-market-could-be-worth-200-bil-by-2050-berenberg#:~:text=%22The%20global%20carbon%20offset%20market,Berenberg%20said%20in%20the%20note.)**. Purchasing CRBN early may help bootstrap and also capture a large fraction of this market.

We introduce several components:

- **Target carbon removal ($/metric ton): **We establish a price target for carbon removal. This can easily be done by using prices already established by NGOs. All CRBN holders can then vote to lower the price of carbon removal. The network is incentivized to lower the target as the supply of CRBN will expand as the total price is lowered. Additionally, this will force inefficient sources of carbon removal to be priced out of the market.
- **Carbon: CRBN**: Individuals, institutions, and governments whom wish to offset their carbon emissions can voluntarily purchase CRBN. A token that expands and contracts based on fluctuations around the target price. If the price of CRBN is below the target, then the debt limit will increase, forcing **offsetters** to mint GREEN**. **
- **GreenBucks - GREEN**: a token redeemable at one to one with **CRBN. **GREEN are created by **offsetters** to 1) fund opex and Capex of carbon offsetters, they burn CRBN and mint GREEN. They can then sell GREEN to individuals on the open market. 2) if the **CRBN** is below the target price, pricing the supply and demand of CRBN into equilibrium.
- **Watchers:** Watchers vote using their **CRBN to **verify that carbon removal groups (**offsetters**) are legitimate. Watchers earn a small fee for ensuring offsetters are legitimate.
- **Offsetters:** Verified by CRBN carbon removal institutions. **Offsetters** and their backers earn CRBN as the price of CRBN increases beyond the target price. Both those that remove and back those projects. Removers can charge a small commission I.e 10% denominated in CRBN rewards. If a remover is found to be fraudulent, their CRBN will be “slashed” towards a common collective.
- **Backers:** Anyone who holds **CRBN** can “back”. They can further become backers by staking their CRBN towards **offsetters**. Individuals are incentivized to back carbon removal projects that are more efficient, as they will burn less CRBN for GREEN, allowing them to capture more financial upside as supply of CRBN expands.


Even if individuals are not currently using to offset carbon usage, they can purchase carbon. They are incentivized to participate early to increase the supply of carbon removal from the atmosphere, by getting other institutions to join the CRBN system.

**Implementation**

1. An oracle that provides (centralized or backed by some trusted individuals should be okay) that can feed a price to the target carbon price. Target: Wren + [Stripe Climate](https://stripe.com/climate)
2. An oracle that can help the DAO whitelist carbon **offsetters** (so that they can mint **GREEN)**
3. CRBN/USDC and GREEN/USDC pools on AMMs to provide on-chain TWAP oracle, as well as easy off-ramp for carbon removal projects
4. A "verification" mechanism. Offsetters need to tweet from an Twitter account which is verified by DAO holders (a multisig holder first), then they can participate in the DAO.


In theory, this should only augment existing funding sources and aid marketplaces like Wren by providing additional purchasing power on their platforms for such an important public good.

**Future**

Bootstrapping CRBN largely makes sense because we are effectively bootstrapping a market for a known commodity, that should grow to have wide and deep markets.

CRBN forms a voluntary market similar to the bitcoin and crypto ecosystem has grown successively by increasing the utility as well as the reach of each project. Where Bitcoin first was held by cypherpunks and libertarians, then interested tech enthusiasts, now too large financial institutions, and finally to governments and treasuries. The asset has grown to a near trillion dollars.

CRBN can see a similar wave of participation. Where first, motivated carbon offsetters participate, then to green energy companies, then financiers can make a return by “backing” projects with CRBN, and then finally to governments whom can collaborate in this ecosystem by bulk purchasing CRBN to influence the target price, serve as watchers, and fund worthwhile projects.

As this grows, CRBN effectively becomes a global market for carbon. Without needing a government institution to set or regulate prices. The CRBN protocol contributors would be able to allocate a certain number of CRBN towards a specific industry to catalyze a change. They can drop a certain number of CRBN towards the industry, based on their past history of net negative or net neutral carbon emissions. Incentivizing new projects and teams to go net negative.
