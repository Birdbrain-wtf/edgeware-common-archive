### Governance
- Raymond Zhong brings [a proposal to reduce inflation, specifically to the treasury. T](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/886-proposal-calibrating-the-edgeware-treasury-budget)his is intended to improve confidence in EDG's value, reduce attack vectors, and increase the chains motivation to improve the value of the EDG already in existence. Two democracy proposals, #16 and #17, were created on-chain, each representing a different strategy, a less and more restrictive change to the issuance, respectively.
- Drew Stone opens [a discussion on the launch of a canary network, Hedgeware](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/898-hedgeware-a-canary-network-and-parachain), that would bond to Kusama's parachain slots. The primary motivation is to hedge against the consensus changes that might arise through Polkadot bonding. Hedgeware would instead initialize as a parachain to avoid this issue. Additionally, the token distribution would be determined based on contribution and activity in Edgeware, not the original lockdrop distribution.
- Thom Ivy shares [an offer from a third-party](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/897-offer-to-sell-edgewarecom) to sell [Edgeware.com](http://Edgeware.com), and include the transfer of the @ Edgeware twitter handle. The offer is 40k USD, but it's not clear the pricing and will of the community is for purchasing at this time.
- Drew Stone shares [the proposal and cost of integrating EDG into Ledger devices via the services of Zondax.](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Mt5XyzZj6Qj1NdSKR-wtRr3aa62WWF0bMOqr6Kuf-DY/edit#heading=h.sdhffoau43zh) Ledger has faced security breaches recently, but support is a frequently requested item from the community.
- Rich shares [the draft of a collaboration with Zora Protocol,](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/884-edgeware-x-zora-x-new-futures-profitable-curation-of-culture) an NFT service provider empowering artists.
- Thom Ivy [opens the discussion on a rebranding of the network](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/934-conversation-rebrand), sharing several motivations and council weighs the timing and costs.
