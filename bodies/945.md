### Projects and Partnerships
- Tidal Finance, a multi-asset insurance cover protocol allowing users to purchase cover or create custom pools, [announced their launch on Edgeware using the upcoming Tokyo-milestone EVM support.](https://medium.com/commonwealth-labs/edgeware-partners-with-tidal-finance-to-bring-insurance-cover-to-users-and-defi-958ff081114d) The move will help ensure a safer and more rapidly developed Defi ecosystem on EDG.

### Tech
- The [pre-release of the Tokyo version of Edgeware](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/936-tokyo-prerelease-v320) is available for testing and node-upgrades. This release features EVM support as well as treasury and governance parameter changes, the ChainBridge module for bridging to ETH, and expanded functionality of the new assets pallet.
- Core developer Drew Stone [opens a thread on a canarynet for Edgeware, titled 'Hedgeware,'](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/898-hedgeware-a-canary-network-and-parachain) that would launch and aim for a KSM parachain slot as an experimental ground for ironing out any kinks for the still-developing parachain bonding for the EDG <> DOT.
- Last month, [Dillon Chen opened a thread on the parachain strategy for EDG,](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/830-draft-proposal-edgeware-parachain-bonding) and now we're looking for community members to lead this conversation as part of a Parachain Working Group, to ensure that the technology and communications around the process is well-understood and executed.

### Governance
- 4 days are [left to vote in a referendum on whether](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/referendum/21-treasuryrewardsetcurrentpayout620000000000000) to decrease the treasury's share of inflation to 62 EDG per block approximately ~325m EDG per block down from ~500m EDG. This will have the effect of reducing the rate of increase of total EDG.
