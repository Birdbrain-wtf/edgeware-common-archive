Distributed ledgers are capable of coordinating behavior and resources globally.
One hurdle to mass adoption: how do distributed ledgers reliably interact with the legal world of corporations, courtrooms, and bank accounts?

This proposal is a bridge between Edgeware and the "legal" world. Its completion would permit Edgeware users to create legal entities within minutes, for free*; protecting their assets in a low tax jurisdiction. It would similarly allow multisig and DAO users to wrap their projects in legal entities, affording them the ability to create legally binding contracts and open bank accounts.

Edgeware is already situated at a 'bridge': Polkadot. By building a 'LexBridge' to the legal world, Edgeware would be strategically positioned to bring Web3 to the world.

### Deliverables
Using Commonwealth, any Edgeware user may:
- Create a legal entity for free*
- Wrap their Edgeware account, multisig wallet or DAO in a Series LLC or an existing legal entity
- Assign the assets of a legal entity (such as crypto tokens)
- Issue legal agreements as an individual or legal entity
- Wind up their involvement in a legal entity

Total ask, over three milestones: $30k in Edge
*Series LLC. User pays gas cost; otherwise free for first year; no more than $30/year subsequently. Other entity types will be added over time.



## Background
As anyone who has tried to convert crypto to cash can tell you there is always a cost when transferring value between mediums.
We can reduce the energy wasted going back and forth between these two worlds by building a bridge through an appropriate portal.
In a place that [many believe is fiction](https://twitter.com/search?q=#WyomingDoesntExist), just such a portal between the virtual and real worlds has been opened.
This proposed bridge would allow entities to traverse through this portal; reducing the energy needed for legal and decentralized entities to deal with one another. In other words: any DAO proposal would be able to hit the ground running.
The bridge will be anchored in Wyoming and Edgeware, which will act as gateways to the 'real' and 'virtual' worlds, much like the Portuguese trading ports once did in Japan. We have an opportunity for cross pollination if trust can be built between these communities.
Other portals will surely be opening through progressive legislation, and the tools developed during this project will be easily repurposed for bridging other jurisdictions.
Interactions between legal and decentralized entities can be made regular. Exotic hybrids, like DAO/LLCs, can be made commonplace. The real and virtual worlds can be made into one, enabling cooperation at an inconceivable scale.

  
  


## Partnering Organizations

**Etherize**
The principal organization behind this proposal, Etherize has won several hackathons awards for developing solutions for 'hybrid' entities. Over the last year, we've founded numerous DAO/LLCs and DAO/non-profits, and seek to automate this process further.

We are actively working on partnerships with:


- MetaCartel's [DAOHaus](https://daohaus.club/): a Moloch DAO factory for Ethereum, which has had some utilization of Series LLCs for its grants
- [LexDAO](https://github.com/lexDAO/): A guild of crypto-lawyers who will be responsible for drafting the legal agreement templates
- [OtoCo](https://otoco.io/): An entity formation company that has pioneered the Series LLC/crypto-token model; offering free LLC's for those who mint their own token which then acts as proof of ownership. They have created 150+ hybrid entities using the ERC20 tied to Series LLC model. 

  
**Wyoming?**

Why use Wyoming for LLCs?
Wyoming is one of the few states that permits Series LLCs. These legal entities can have thousands of 'child' entities; allowing rapid and inexpensive formation costs for new projects.

Additionally...
  
The State of Wyoming recognizes cryptocurrency as untaxed property[*](https://twitter.com/CaitlinLong_/status/1096116466259116032), holds cryptotokens as potentially equivalent to stock certificates[*](https://wyoleg.gov/Legislation/2019/HB0185), and permits humans to control legal entities by signing statements with their public key[*](https://legiscan.com/WY/bill/HB0101/2018).
Additional legislation has created a sandbox for fintech startups[*](https://wyoleg.gov/Legislation/2019/HB0057) and allowed for special banks[*](https://www.wyoleg.gov/InterimCommittee/2018/09-2018112919LSO-0055v0.8.pdf) to provide fiat services to crypto projects.
