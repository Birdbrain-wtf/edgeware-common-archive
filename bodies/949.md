One of the challenges of Edgeware governance is that it's difficult to effectively measure the community's opinions, when the community is highly decentralized.

EDG holders have expressed that they don't feel knowledgeable enough to express their opinions at length, on Telegram or Commonwealth, or through voting on referenda.

We've gotten good results (30+ votes) from polling the Telegram group, but those results are not high-quality enough to be useful in guiding policy.

Kusama solves this problem by establishing a council of major token holders, who are all highly engaged with the project - however, because Edgeware distributed to a much wider community, this model doesn't work for us.

## CoreDAO

I'd like to propose the formation of a **nonbinding congressional DAO starting with ~15-20 core members.** Basically, a decentralized organization open to any natural person (physical human being) who has contributed to the network in some capacity. [1]

The DAO would field one survey per week, usually a single question, distributed to all the members to vote.

**Everyone in the DAO would be expected to vote, every week** - not missing more than one proposal in a row would be a requirement to maintain "active membership" in the group - but it would be acceptable (and perhaps common) to abstain.

CoreDAO surveys would seek either a 1/2 majority or 2/3 supermajority:

- Resolutions on a single issue ("Should Edgeware fund [a project]?") would seek a 1/2 majority.
- Resolutions for policies the network should follow on an ongoing basis would seek a 2/3 supermajority.
- Resolutions for policies would be repealed by a 1/2 majority.
- Changes to the composition or rules of the DAO would be decidable by a 2/3 supermajority.


This DAO would be deployed to a smart contract, likely written in EVM, with either Snapshot or Commonwealth as the interface.

## Gathering interest

Because the DAO would have no binding authority, it's important that its legitimacy derives from it being truly representative of the core community.

It should not be dominated by any one party or members of any one entity.

And it should have representation from:

- Builders
- Agency members
- Chain contractors
- Core developers
- Applications developers building on the network
- Validators
- Major tokenholders participating in governance decisions


It would essentially be an overlay/addition to the democracy and council modules, that provides an advisory signal.

If there is interest from at least 10-15 diverse members of the community and no blockers, I would push forward with this project.

## Next steps, and future work

Implementation details TBD, but it's not a technically difficult project.

I'd be interested in invoicing the chain for a nominal amount (e.g. $5k) for technical development associated with the project. However, if a treasury proposal doesn't go through but there is still interest, I'd want to push forward with it anyway as I think there is significant value towards having a consistent polling mechanic for the core community.

I believe it would be a good idea for the DAO to provide a stipend to all members who remain in good standing. The cost of having that signal seems worth around $10k/year or even slightly higher than that.

At that point, the DAO should also request a rationale for all votes - a blob of arbitrary text to explain the vote, that could be very simple but would provide a lot of useful context on how governance decisions are being made.

## Final notes

I view this as a way to accelerate Edgeware governance significantly. 

Edgeware has many questions where the "consensus" is unclear, and as such, huge decisions hinge on the actions of a couple of tokenholders (who may not have predictable voting strategies).

If we could gather a quorum of members to vote and provide guidance on where the community consensus stands, it would reduce the confusion and allow us to act more efficiently on many issues.

It would also be a great demonstration of the power of decentralized governance - a tokenholder governed network delegating power to a body of citizens that are generally civil and well informed.
