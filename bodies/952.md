# Overview
We've decided with the Ren team that it's best to work towards RenVM integration once the EVM is live. This makes their integration easier and makes our work more straightforward on Edgeware, giving us insights into the types of pallets we'll want to integrate and start working on in the short-term, such as an asset wrapper between EVM assets and native assets. 

## [Requirements](https://docs.renproject.io/developers/renvm-multihain/multichain-requirements)
These are mandatory requirements and incumbent upon the applying project.
1. Complete the Multichain PR: [https://github.com/renproject/multichain](https://github.com/renproject/multichain)
2. Agree to a binding liquidy mining subsidy of 50K USD to ensure proper liquidity bootstrapping within DeFi. More information [below.](https://docs.renproject.io/developers/renvm-multihain/multichain-requirements#liquidity-mining-requirements)
3. If a destination blockchain (L1) agrees to create and maintain a fully functional [RenBridge](https://bridge.renproject.io/) like UI for your users (prior to release). Projects can simply fork [RenBridge](https://github.com/renproject/bridge-v2) as needed, or utilize [RenJS.](https://github.com/renproject/ren-js)



## Hosting a separate UI
This is something either Commonwealth Labs or myself can take on through Webb. We are building out a small Yearn.finance style UI and can integrate it there as well or another community group can definitely propose handling this too.
