There's been a common call to limit overall inflation for the average stakeholder. However, the treasury is still useful as a long-term resource for the ecosystem. 

Overall, this proposal has the effect of boosting _real_ (not nominal) rate of return from 8% (lower due to treasury inflation) to 16-20% long-term (because treasury inflation will be zero). 

Additionally, the treasury will be able to accumulate rewards by participating in the system itself. At 20-25% of the network participating, it would have a natural “endowment” of 3 - 5% of inflation + fees to run the chain indefinitely and increasing the security of the chain. This can further be tuned by setting the % fees that go to the treasury.

If treasury inflation is used, it should be used to allow for a "perma" slot on Polkadot.

**TLDR Proposal:**

Executed over a few years. 

1. **Grow the treasury to 20-25%** of the chain to the treasury with inflation, like Synthetix or AAVE (DAO). 
2. **Move treasury inflation down** from 62 per block for treasury for two years, then drop to 32 per block, the going to zero.
3. **Use treasury to earn 20% per year by bonding as a validator. **At 20 - 25% of the network, this will let the treasury act as an endowment, earning some of 3 - 5% of the network each year, and continually fund core activities, all while reducing treasury inflation. 
4. **Only turn on any additional treasury inflation for leasing / acquiring DOT. ** We should try to acquire enough DOT / KSM to never lease again.


**In-Depth Proposal:**

We are trying to balance a few interests:

- **Validator Economics: **Make sure that this is high enough, in the absence of fees, that chain security may be guaranteed, I.e 50% of the network or more may be staked. Probably needs to yield some 20% before being moved down as fees pick up in years 2 - 5.
- **General Token-Holders**: limiting overall dilution and giving more certainty around supply if a person is not staked. A person may want to use the tokens across the
- **Treasury / Chain Development:** Needs some 3% of the chain to continue running.
- **Parachain Economics: **Needs a large chunk of the treasury to be able to secure a slot.

 
[Validator inflation](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VlzTUDESbbfOggMRz3GyE9-VqR9MlOhNuoekBboKvLw/edit#gid=1370498247) ([Web3 Documentation](https://research.web3.foundation/en/latest/polkadot/economics/1-token-economics.html#inflation-model)) is minted as a % of the network. In this way, networks don't have a fixed supply. Right now, inflation is high, as a percentage of the network, in year 1, the inflation was 20%. With it dropping to ~16.6% in the second year. This gives network stakeholders anxiety about the overall supply and potential dilutionary effects.

Alongside this, treasury inflation is minted  EDG number. 62 per block is ~350m per year. At 62 EDG per block, In real terms, treasury inflation will be something like 5% of network supply in year 2, and some 4% in year 3. Additionally, any unclaimed validator and network fees will also flow to the treasury, this has yielded some additional 1% per year in supply. The additional treasury inflation has the effect of reducing validator rewards from 20% to 5 - 8%.

Instead, the treasury should just participate in validating. We should use this so that the treasury can get to 25% of the network over the course of the next several years, at which point it should be "self-sustaining". Then all treasury inflation could be directly removed, boosting validator participation. f the “treasury address” can create a controller and stake within the system. 

Turn inflation could be directed towards purchasing DOTs or incentivizing the crowd to participate on behalf of Edgeware. So the 2 - 5% of EDG, would be able to facilitate some $4 - $10m of purchases on a yearly basis. 

**Additional Considerations / Parameters.**

1. **Reduce the treasury % portion for tips / fees. **Currently, [80% of fees go to the treasury.](https://github.com/hicommonwealth/edgeware-node/blob/master/node/runtime/src/lib.rs#L136) if the treasury is participating in validating on the network this can be flipped to 20%. 
2. **Collator Incentives. **Currently, I don't advocate for any changes to inflation to validators as it becomes a parachain, even as the security requirements are lessened. 
3. **Adjusting the ideal staking rate upward or overall validator inflation. **From 50% to 75%, will allow the treasury to earn more.
4. **[Adopting EIP-1559 style burning](https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/blob/master/EIPS/eip-1559.md). **The supply of EDG can further be reduced. The Edgeware fee market should start to become competitive as more protocols onboard. Parachain blocks need to be constructed in 0.5 seconds, or equivalent to 5m gas for 0.5 sec of computation. Right now, the cost to launch a simple contract can eat up 250k gas, so it will be a pretty scarce resource in relatively short order. Of course, there are efforts to allow the EVM to work more efficiently. Developers on Edgeware also have the option of using the WASM contract environment.
