## 
## Current Challenges
- We have increasing organizational and information management challenges as the number of working groups and network participants grow.
- Projects and companies are coming online that overlap with some working groups.
- The latter stages of working groups and their limitations are becoming evident.
- We're in a document, chat room, and decision/information hell, as too many have too many broad categories of info and decisions to track.


## Solution: Use-Case Category "Conglomerates" of WGs and Companies
For instance, Rich has begun to launch Decent Partners, a UK-based corp that intends to interface with Vacuum Labs and other companies to enhance onboarding and coordination for what is clearly a related set of solutions.

What became clear to me in that change was that there are natural exploration and aggregation steps in the development of our ecosystem by which things explicitly become more decentralized through a kind of phase change.

**Expansion of Working Groups**
- Working groups created, funding and business models explored, some are funded, some grow, some dead end or become inactive without a solution or problem to address.


**Contraction to Conglomerates**
- Succesful Working Groups find a fit between their problem solving and capacities, with a use case category.  These existing WG may evolve into a core like Decent, or they may join an existing core, or be bought out/acquired depending on the token structure.


Decent Partners is a great example because it appears fully stacked-out. They've built out development relations, a marketing strategy and staff, and more to solve for their proficiencies and interests in the EDG ecosystem, namely cryptomedia, NFTs, more creative content.  The NFT WG, alongside the artists WG, might be good acquisitions or absorptions for Decent. 

The other use case silos that we might find efficiencies in aggregating to a new layer include:

- Defi: Economics WG, defi wg, NFT
- Privacy: ZK, Commonwealth?
- DAO: existing DAO WG and teams, governance (applies to chain as a whole as well as DAOs)


By permitting and encouraging this new layer or ring of decentralized economic managers, like Decent, we allow people the opportunity to specialize a bit more, as mentioned above, we are too big now and have too many core folks to ask them to track every WG daily.

## What will happen to general-purpose entities like the Agency?
In Crypto, the pace of development of verticals like defi is ever-increasing. As a result, a jack-of-all-trades is inherently limited in their abilities because they cannot easily track all verticals. Instead, fast-moving narratives require a specialist.

In line with that, the Agency will be empowered to address marketing layers that apply to /all/ entities- the health and reputation of the chain in its entirety is a good example, but specific use case elaboration and awareness, I believe, will aggregate and specialize into the conglomerate layer.

Agency and other Core-level Execution orgs (Core Devs) can also serve as capacity builders: to onboard new WGs, Aggregators, and structure their activities effectively using knowledge of how the ecosystem works and a big-picture view of the chain. 

## In Summary

- Mature aggregations of WG and Talents enable further specialization and scalable efficiencies.
- They also reduce the amount of ecosystem-wide information the average chain participant needs to ingest.
- They create clear and decentralized sources of authority and activity on important verticals/use-cases for the network.
- They are flexible on or off-chain, being able to incorporate in legal jurisdictions.
- They place likely partners in a more relevant proximity. 
- They serve as another layer of experimentation in the organization of digital labor, empowered to produce different policies and practices that can be in turn, distributed to other orgs.
- Acquisition patterns for tokenized orgs can be powerful incentives to launch a WG or company in Edgeware-- there is a market for businesses that can evolve.
