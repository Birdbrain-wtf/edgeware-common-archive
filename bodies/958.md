How can we let independent developers earn a living and tap into existing communities without launch new tokens?

**Background**

1. We’ve already seen what [wrapping / rehypothecation ](https://twitter.com/cburniske/status/1351306808065880064)can be used for interest bearing / money like assets. Yearn + CRV and yield farming
2. We’ve seen what happens when everything is open-source (lots of copying)
3. Also, we’ve seen what this looks superfluid liquidity looks like in a potentially zero sum way (I.e [vampire mining](https://www.cryptonative.ch/vampire-attack-an-attack-on-liquidity-dependent-protocols/)). Although this usually ends up speeds up innovation.
4. We’ve seen plenty of forks, can be a money grab, or actually righting a wrong in terms of supply (Monero).
5. And there’s been some [mergers](https://www.dillchen.com/essays/merging-chains) (bumping previous thread from Niraj + I)


_**Yet, we know that we can’t fork community, and brands are extremely sticky**_. How can we let independent developers earn a living and tap into existing communities without launch new tokens?

**Idea**

Right now, I’ll call these subDAOs, basically permission-less subsidiaries. Instead, we can use wrapped governance tokens to create “subDAOs” or innovation zones on different chains, while still accruing fees to existing governance token providers. 

I would be pretty excited to see these pop up on Edgeware, especially as [metatransactions / contract staking allows for feeless DeFi ](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/955-protected-contracts-and-transaction-fee-subsidies)on Edgeware. This could be a cool, collaborative, and incentive aligned way for Edgeware to grow TVL, rather than forking and creating additional protocols. As Edgeware becomes a parachain, "edgCRV" or "edgCOMP" will then be able to take advantage of the existing ecosystem of DOT collateral. 

This makes things value add and permissionlessly collaborative. 

**What does this look like?**

**Step 1. **Create a wrapped asset I.e “riskyUNI” or “riskyCOMP” or “edgCRV”.
- Just like ETH <> WETH, this is a one to one mapping. But could later include fees / bear interest if that gets voted in.


**Step 2. **Deploy another copy of the same contracts onto your platform of choice

**Step 3. **Instead of using a new asset ala SUSHI and UNI, use riskyUNI or edgCRV to govern those contracts.

**Step 4** (Optional). Vote in some portion of fees with “riskyUNI” or “edgCRV” to go to the developer for undergoing this effort.

**Step 5**. Run the protocol, using “riskyUNI” or “eCRV” to govern the project. Tuning different parameters, perhaps adding in new fees, different collateral types or more.

**Step 6**. Now, instead, “riskyUNI” or “edgCRV” token will accrue yield. As the TVL grows within the protocol governed by “riskyUNI” or “edgCRV”, fees will grow. More people will want to wrap into the “riskyUNI” or “edgCRV” to capture the fees.

**Step 7**. For a platform that wants to incentivize “riskyUNI” or “edgCRV”, additional liquidity rewards could be allocated, for example from the Edgeware Treasury to further boost TVL. 

**Step 8**. Ask for a grant from the normal treasury to further grow “riskyUNI” or “edgCRV” and or add new features.
