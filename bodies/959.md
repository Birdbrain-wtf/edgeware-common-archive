Edgeware is a state-of-the-art smart contract chain with a community-managed treasury, decentralized proposal system, and network of DAOs.

Edgeware is a platform for the next generation of decentralized applications. On Edgeware, Ethereum developers can deploy [Solidity/EVM](https://main.edgeware.wiki/edgeware-runtime/evm) smart contracts with few or no changes, while new developers can get started with Rust and [WebAssembly](https://main.edgeware.wiki/edgeware-runtime/wasm).

Builders and organizers can find a place in one of Edgeware’s many collectives funded by the on-chain treasury to expand the network and explore new ideas.

The treasury and chain are governed by a [fairly-launched](https://blog.edgewa.re/the-edgeware-lockdrop-debriefed/), widely distributed set of stakeholders established during the lockdrop, one of the largest Ethereum token generation events in 2018.

## How to get started

- Join the [Edgeware Discord](https://discord.gg/njDnHDk) channel and introduce yourself
- Optional: [create an on-chain identity](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/393-registrar-process-and-verification-tiers-registrar-index-0) (this locks 5 EDG)
- Attend a working groups' weekly call (our [calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0?cid=Y29tbW9ud2VhbHRoLmltX2loYnM4OTJwcXVidm9ndG9pbTZjMWhmN2NrQGdyb3VwLmNhbGVuZGFyLmdvb2dsZS5jb20))
- Attend the bi-weekly community call - Wednesdays from 1-2pm ET, [link to Zoom room.](https://us02web.zoom.us/j/9320459771)
- Ready to start giving to the community? Here's a few ways to help:
- Help establish a partnership between Edgeware and another team, network, or community
- Build on Edgeware - we’re [Wasm](https://main.edgeware.wiki/edgeware-runtime/wasm) and [EVM compatible](https://main.edgeware.wiki/edgeware-runtime/evm)
- Propose new ideas or launch a discussion on Commonwealth (here)
- [Post a meme](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/637-repeating-meme-contest-memedg-tournament)
- [Stake your EDG](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I-jTp4nrfcw) and help [govern the protocol](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-governance)
- Join an DAO


Edgeware’s design enables continual development of the network through our layer-one, community-controlled Treasury.

You can submit proposals to access funds and resources to: bring an existing project onto Edgeware, receive seed-funding for a project idea, or on-board an existing community into our ecosystem.

Check out this [example of a successful proposal ](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/859-edgeware-comms-strategy-discussion-document)and introduce yourself to us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/njDnHDk) to get started!

## Active Collectives, DAOs, and Projects

- The [Edgeware Builders](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussions/wg-builders) group has developed faucets, staking interfaces, smart contracts tooling, and zero knowledge tech for Edgeware. See the [construction-projects](https://github.com/edgeware-builders/construction-projects) Github for projects and bounties
- See the [awesome-edgeware](http://github.com/edgeware-builders/awesome-edgeware/) Github for resources
- The [Edgeware Agency](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussions/wg-brand) includes artists, designers, and community strategists, and works on public messaging for the network & projects on the network. See the [Edgeware Agency](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/643-brand-working-group-proposal-updatelive-council-motion) funding proposal for more information
- Other working groups serve as discussion forums on [crypto art](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussions/wg-artists), [DAOs](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussions/wg-DAOs), [DeFi](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussions/wg-defi), and more
- Some groups are still being set up:
- [Edgeware Alpha](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/discussions/builDAO) is an incubator and for-profit DAO
- [Decent Partners](https://www.decent.partners/) is a 'metadao' that seeks to create crypto-native public media
- The [Zero Knowledge](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/900-zeroknowledge-working-group-call-1) working group develops cutting-edge zk tech


We are working to keep our list of active projects up-to-date, you can find them here at [awesome-edgeware Github](http://github.com/edgeware-builders/awesome-edgeware/) and make a request to add any that are missing.

Together, we can keep this page updated and reflecting all of the great work in our growing community!
