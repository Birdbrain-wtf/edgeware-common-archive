With the latest updates now live on Commonwealth, I'd like to start making proposals to Edgeware, and other networks, to sustainably fund Commonwealth development.

Edgeware governance is somewhat unique because:

- There are many different groups interacting here - protocols like Empty Set Dollar or SushiSwap serve a single purpose, while Edgeware is host to many different communities ranging from: artists, Agency, Builders, and so on.
- We are still building towards a full-scale core team (see: [chain leads](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/880-decentralizing-edgeware-with-chain-leads))


As such we have different needs - we need to reduce communication barriers between different groups, and make it easier for governance to function. For example, one issue I notice often is that projects fall behind because it's unclear what community feedback has been.

In this thread I'd like to make a proposal for **Phase 1 Commonwealth improvements**, **focused on improving signaling and governance clarity**. This includes:

1. Adding the ability to track offchain proposals as they move through different stages
2. Adding a sophisticated off-chain signaling mechanism, to collect signals for approval / disapproval
3. Supporting the bounties module
4. Finishing the Search function, and moving it out of beta
5. Finishing the Members function, and moving it out of beta


**Deliverable #1: Offchain proposal stages. **Currently, there is no way to tell whether a proposal in a discussion thread is in a draft stage, or actively seeking feedback, or proposed on-chain, or completed or abandoned. This deliverable would allow for the thread author & community admins to move a thread through "stages". (See the design below for a rough idea.) Additionally, if a proposal were In Voting, it would show how long the proposal has been in that stage. 

This would also encompass some design updates to how discussion rows are designed, to improve readability. (estimate: 2 weeks across design, engineering, and QA)

**Deliverable #2: A sophisticated off-chain signaling module.** One of the main uses of the Commonwealth forum is to put forward proposals for community feedback. However, many proposals receive limited feedback. Many community members read and decide on an opinion, but do not find commenting a good way to express it.

This deliverable would introduce an offchain poll similar to Polkassembly's poll signals. The creator of a thread would have to opt in** **to showing an offchain poll, and could customize the text in the poll.

- The creator of a thread could also create multiple polls in a single thread, to collect feedback on different parts of a proposal.
- Votes on threads would be made by signing a text string in polkadot-js. The signature would be saved on the frontend, and instructions provided on how to verify them.
- Votes on threads could be made by **logged-in** or **logged-out** users. Depending on spam, we may choose to require that logged-out users hold an on-chain identity (this will be decided later).

**Deliverable #3: Supporting the Substrate bounties module. **This deliverable would allow viewing, creating, and discussing bounties from within the Commonwealth interface. While the Polkadot-JS Apps interface supports bounties, it doesn’t allow for ongoing discussion about them, and bringing our off-chain discussion functions would make it easier for ongoing conversations to happen there.

This would be executed to a fully complete stage (not just "beta"), so any bounty functionality would be expected to land with around the same stage of polish as our current Discussions interface.

(estimate: 2.5 weeks across design, engineering, and QA)

**Deliverable #4: Finish delivering Search. **There is currently a beta version of search implemented right now. However, it is limited in functionality in a few key ways. This deliverable would augment search by identifying and highlighting where search terms occurred in the result text. It would also move Search into the main discussions section, rather than its own page. (estimate: 0.5 weeks across design, engineering, and QA)

**Deliverable #5: Finish delivering Members. **Currently a beta version of the members directory has been deployed to master. This is missing a number of backend optimizations that would be necessary for it to really be "stable" software. This deliverable would address these issues:

- There are duplicate members being shown where members have recently participated in a community.
- The backend logic does not gracefully handle large communities; it tries to show all members at once. Instead members should be loaded from the backend in pages.
- The design could be improved somewhat to show more info about each member, including number of historical posts and comments over time.


(estimate: 0.5 weeks across design, engineering, and QA)

## Pricing

Using Kusama proposals for comparables:

- The first Kusama bounty was to implement a frontend for the bounties pallet. The bounty requested 1165 KSM at a time when the price of KSM was around $50-55.
- The latest interface project on Kusama, a parachain lease offering dashboard, is requesting 523 KSM at $65/KSM. However at the date of the posting, the price of KSM was around $90/KSM or higher.


I am pricing this proposal based on these comparables at 4.75m EDG. The resulting cost would be $66.5k at a price of $0.014 / EDG.

Compared to the Kusama treasury, which is rich because of KSM appreciation, our pricing here is lower on a per project basis - and all together, the proposal is larger and includes more material.

The proposal is scoped to these projects because they are the ones we have developed the most clarity and interest around (they are all features that I believe would be used by the network in critical ways & would unlock new governance possibilities).

## **Possible future work**

There are other proposals that could potentially be advanced within the next 6 months, to continue building out the interface. They might be proposed from within Commonwealth Labs or by myself & other engineers as independent developers:

- Making the overwhelming amount of content posted on Commonwealth easier to follow - I'm considering a Phase 2 proposal focused on managing information overload across different channels, and improving the UX of working groups, but need to do some design work to evaluate what are feasible solutions first.
- Finishing up the governance functionality on Commonwealth. Currently this is at an early alpha/beta stage. The Commonwealth backend successfully indexes referenda, democracy proposals, treasury proposals, and council changes in realtime. However, voting doesn't always work, and data from past proposals is not always saved or shown correctly. The UI could be improved significantly. I'm considering making a Phase 3 proposal focused on delivering a fully complete governance interface, and integrating it with Discussions. This is lower priority.
