If you're reading this from inside the US, you may have received your Yangdollars stimulus check fiat airdrop by now. Or perhaps not quite yet. The distribution of dollars hasn’t gone quite as smoothly as one would hope. It’s with this context that Edgeware heads in 2021, which feels like the true start to the decade. It's never been more clear that society needs an upgrade. 

Lucky or unlucky, the tailwinds for cryptocurrency adoption aren't slowing down. In ten years, crypto may have gone through two additional price-development cycles, where assets in crypto may be a few orders of magnitude greater than they are today. 

So what should Edgeware doing this year to help this future happen faster?

With an upgrade, we should have new ways of organizing squads to create wealth giving everyone ownership, new ways of storing and safely moving money and IP, and new ways of recognizing individual contributions with financial and social rewards. Each of these ideas has a founder, a community, and a set of crypto primitives that are needed to support them. We just need to facilitate these **let new things happen**. 

Basically, I think Edgeware just needs to continue to work on inviting in independent, technical, and creative individuals. If we bring in these people and give them the engineering, community, and financial tools, we are successfully acting as an incubator--an ecosystem for **new things** to start and grow quickly. Think about YC— that’s turned tens of millions into hundreds of billions of startup value. Each YC company is tackling an incredibly difficult problem with very different personalities at the head of each company. There’s a tremendous amount of activity going in on Edgeware, one look at the [Commonwealth starter thread](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/959--new-to-edgeware-start-here) will tell you that. 

In the same way, I think we just need to continue to let the same diverse ecosystem continue to thrive and experiment. I think this happens in two ways:

1. continuing to find the chain leads are able to **disagree but support** new and different ideas
2. making sure the right projects are in motion for 2021 across tech, governance, protocol, and community. 


**The Right Chain Leads**

As new [chain leads](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/880-decentralizing-edgeware-with-chain-leads) apply, I’ll personally be voting for people who can **disagree but support**:

- **Create the right incentives**
- **Removing (technical, funding, and community) friction.**
- **(Again) disagree but support new things.**


For a token as widely distributed as Edgeware, there is no shortage of voices that can shape the conversation, and as long as these voices at their core are in tune with the idea of an incubator. Being able to be in tune with the existing community as well as articulate how their projects fit together. Of course, this is in addition to being to fulfill the actual lead position whether it’s across BD, tech, or community.

While there are a lot of core issues, that need wide buy-in like overall network upgrades, becoming a parachain, or deciding on treasury emissions, or onboarding chain leads, there are times in which things when not everyone needs to agree. Not everyone (I certainly don't) has strong opinions on the specifics for art or DeFi.

**Proposals to Put in Motion in 2020**

There’s been a lot of ideas that have been proposed by the community. These are some of my favorites, that I would be in favor of personally helping champion if necessary. I know that others will disagree and I'm excited to hear feedback. In the spirit of "**Letting new things happen even if they disagree with things”**, this isn't a formal roadmap, rather a map of where my head is this year. Each of these proposals is connected an incubator theme. I’ve noted them in italics.

- Tech
- [Parachain Bonding](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/830-draft-proposal-edgeware-parachain-bonding): _Lets new things happen _by connecting to DOT Collateral and projects
- Bridges ([here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/754-trusted-vs-trustless-eth-bridge) and [here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/954-runtime-module-asset-bridge-between-edgeware-evm-edge-assets)): _Lets new things happen _by connecting to new ecosystems like ETH and Decred
- [Zero-Knowledge Primitives](https://github.com/edgeware-builders/anon): _lets new things happen _by enabling private DeFi or anonymous collectives
- Potential: [Contract Staking](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/955-protected-contracts-and-transaction-fee-subsidies): _removes friction _by creating zero fees for DeFi and other protocols
- Community / Governance
- [Small Proposals for Working Groups](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/860-bootstrapping-more-working-groups-with-council-help): _let’s new things happen_ by seeding new working groups quickly
- [CoreDAO](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/949-coredao-a-consensus-building-entity-for-the-edgeware-core-community): _removes friction_ by providing light touch signaling for the community
- [Chain Leads](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/880-decentralizing-edgeware-with-chain-leads): _creates the right connections_ between new community leaders
- Protocol / Funding
- [Really kick off BuilDAO](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/941-buildao-update-2-11220): _creates the right incentives _to attract new people and projects.
- Getting [Developer ](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/798-developer-incentive-dev-mining-by-uma)and [Protocol Onboarding](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/804-grant-proposal-draft-bd-lead) Incentives Right: _creates the right incentives _to aggressively onboard Developers and new projects
- Developer Experiments / Experience
- [SubDAOs](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/958-subdaos-just-an-idea-for-now) Grants: _Let new things happen_ for new protocols to onboard onto Edgeware.
- Hackathons: _Creating the right incentives_ for new developers to get started
- 100 Developer Grants: _Creates the right incentives _for new developers to get started.


If at the end of the year if we did all this Edgeware would have / be:

- a side chain with zero fees for specific contracts
- Have a new utility for EDG that gives Edgeware as a network the potential to become a stakeholder in protocols building on Edgeware in an aligned manner.
- Have several protocols running live and using DeFi and NFT collateral from other Polkadot, Ethereum, and more.
- And a developer ecosystem, new primitives, and community willing to pick up and work on any new idea.


These are my quick notes on what Edgeware can bring forward the future more quickly. Looking forward to new things happening.
