## **Zora OS call - 27.01.21**

Follows fom the ongoing discussions with Zora - the profitable curation of media. 
[Original thread here](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/884-edgeware-x-zora-x-new-futures-profitable-curation-of-culture). 

**Overview**

We had a follow up call with Breck from Zora (ex Coinbase engineering team) to discuss the Zora ZDK (SDK). 

We worked from simple to more complex / short to long term. 

1. Decent Partners onboarding creators to Zora and getting them experimenting.
2. Funding of creators / projects by Edgeware via a Decent Partners ‘fund’ 
3. Developing front end instances to Zora’s protocol ([FTTF](https://fttf.tv) / [MoCDA](https://mocda.org) / [New-Futures](https://new-futures.co)). 
4. Develop some additional functionality / advances utilising Zora OS
5. Scaling Zora - R&D project with aim of bringing protocol to Edgeware. 


**Some detail:**

- Big innovation in Zora comes with creating a Uniswap style global liquidity pool for NFTs - where each asset has its own market. 


- In this mode you can accept bids into perpetuity as there is no expiration of the market and liquidity remains on the token instead of having a fractured market of liquidity.


- We discussed a number of smart contracts / UI libs, and graphQL indexing that Zora will release open source by the end of this week. 


- Many creators struggle conceptually with what changes when the media is also a financial asset and brings the market with it… needs some leaps of imagination. 


- Discussed a number of use cases ranging from funding artists in the creation of an NFT that will be fractionally owned after that - this[ tokenized essay experiment](https://twitter.com/tomcritchlow/status/1354219313889112069?s=20) is being run on Mirror which is built on Zora and involves a crowdfunding contract. Funds are then tokenised and each own a piece of the minted NFT.


- One question was around the ‘ordering’ of media tied to NFTs - commissioning is how many artists get their start - they outline the idea, then receive funds to create - the asset is only created at the end. Wondered how this might work with Zora. 


- Discussed ways that the media linked could be added / changed at a later date. 


- NFTs currently pinned to IPFS but that storage solution is outside of the protocol and can be amended - Jam mentioned KodaDot as data can be mutable. 


- Some interesting areas for investigation when considering the ongoing funding of emerging ‘stories/narratives’, rather than single asset ‘drops’. Feels ripe for innovation and enables new things - per discussion by Dillon here and where Edgeware should pioneer. 


- We discussed a loan deFi scenario backed by an NFT security - something for the Defi guys to get stuck into, collateralised lending based on order book of market. Reminded me of [this thread ](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/636-announcing-sublime)on Sublime Finance [@dillon](/edgeware/account/iCLdev9eJBSaUSD1qnbq1roqcV6jZmfLpbLJjTdmUocixcY) [@Humza - dTrade](/edgeware/account/oDggT2fMcwHdhUeWnXofgAQCfkDqzNhTt6q53vMLNQJM8e9) 


- We discussed the collection for a museum ([MoCDA](https://mocda.org)) and release of limited editions


- We discussed a data analytics used by collectors and traders built on top of the graphQL service. 


- We discussed possible integrations with Edgeware , given that at the moment the target is focused on Ethereum, @jam10o gave us some useful insight


- Finally we discussed the curatorial involving people skilled in curating art which might eventually evolve in a form of governance /dao - value in this ecosystem is going to accrue to great ‘curators’.


- Example used - would you as a creator prefer to sell your art for $10k to an anon address, or for $100 to Kanye with a creator share that means the work is now worth a lot more owing to who has previously owned. 


- In the future creators should benefit from more granular control over access to data / their rights - giving creator ‘optionality’.
