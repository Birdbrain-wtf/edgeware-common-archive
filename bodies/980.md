What topics, areas of interest would be useful to create explainer films about?

Some suggestions from Shankar, Thom, Therese, Jan, Anett and Ryan. 

**Approach with this mindset:**
- What is the problem?
- What is the solution


[@Shankar Warang](/edgeware/account/5DiCs4ECYBHrqDDiPad7T3pJaSgxzRz7mQ5GXh5LkSA6UpNi) noted there are daily questions and generally three types of user: 

**Daily problems**
- Who is the CEO?
- Who is in charge?


**New to crypto**
- What is a blockchain?
- How does a blockchain work?
- How do I buy EDG?
- How do I earn EDG?
- What wallets can I use?
- What exchanges?
- When moon?


**Those more familiar with the tech**
- How is Edgeware different?
- How they can interact with apps?
- How they can interact with Commonwealth?
- Walkthrough videos


**More experienced**
- What is the primary use case?
- Why should you choose Edgeware as a developer?
- How do I get my project funded?


**We also discussed tutorials**
- How can you join the governance process?
- How does Commonwealth.im work?
- How can I create an on-chain ID?


**NFT space**
- What are NFTs?
- How can a digital file have value?
- How does this relate to artists? 
- How does it benefit them?
- How might NFT's work on Edgeware?
