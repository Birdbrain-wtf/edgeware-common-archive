## Edgeware Communications Strategy - Update 

I had a very interesting call today with [Joon Ian Wong](https://twitter.com/joonian) who is a well respected journalist in the space. His background spans technology and business reporting at Quartz, media startups in Asia, and he was early at Coindesk, launching their Consensus event. He is also one of the founders of the [ACJR](https://acjr.network/) (Association of Crypto Journalists & Researchers) whose Telegram group contains the great and the good of crypto-reporters.

Aside from this he is also deep into the world of Personal / Social / Community Tokens and has been experimenting there as one of the founders of [Seed Club](https://seedclub.xyz/) so he has a very hands on approach.

**Why the discussion was timely **
We are currently finalising the recruitment of [a project PR agency ](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/859-edgeware-comms-strategy-discussion-document)and one of the things we need to balance carefully is how we as a community project balance funding of 'PR' and the funding of 'independent media DAOs' that are born from the chain.

In effect, how do we draw a line between more explicit 'marketing and PR' and the funding of public goods? 

Currently there is no clear definition as to how we separate the two, and it is a distinction that needs to be made - or at least discussed. 

**The independent media challenge **
Traditionally in newsrooms there has always been a clear dividing line between editorial and commercial imperatives, to ensure reporting remains balanced and independent - akin to the separation of church and state. 

In the last few decades, the rise of 24/7 news, digital media networks, branded content and social media that are all the products of advertising models that prioritise attention have seen this dividing line eaten away, leading to an erosion in trust as profits trump purpose and everything becomes about clicks. 

Crypto media in general has been another product of this model - Coindesk was founded on price speculation around Bitcoin - those articles still generate the most attention today.

This is something that really frustrates journalists in the space who would rather focus on more in-depth reporting, rather than just chasing headlines. 

**So what could we do? **
We discussed a few ideas / areas of collaboration:

- The Edgeware treasury putting funds towards **an independent journalism / research DAO **that would be the launch pad for something that could become a public good for the whole space. 
- This DAO would be run independently of Edgeware by the ACJR - perhaps using a Commonwealth.im interface. 
- They would curate grants for journalists to write long form / investigative pieces that may not be funded by the more news-cycle focused press. 
- These pieces would be distributed through established press relationships they are close to, gaining widespread attention for quality research. 
- We could then onboard other on-chain treasuries to ensure ongoing funding (should funding fall away from one project) and no accusations of bias.
- There may also be ways we could experiment with NFTs and funding models that drive value back to the DAO, whilst ensuring the DAO retains editorial independence. 


This is one version of the [MetaDAO research concept](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/513-metadao-pooling-onchain-funding-for-public-good-proposals) that is a part of the funding received through the Agency, alongside the brand / original audio and video. 

This DAO approach has some similarities to the Guardian's 'Scott Trust' based funding model ([read more here](https://www.theguardian.com/the-scott-trust/2015/jul/26/the-scott-trust)) which has enabled it to be commercially viable longer and stay more independent than many other advertising only news orgs.

This feels like an area that we should definitely be pushing towards and also aligns well with the Decent Partners interest in building out a sustainably funded and cooperatively owned [Web3 media ecosystem](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eOE5Ggwf6O5SXM9Bbdthe44aKYQ3mhaZxn63r6h50Eo/edit) on Edgeware, utlising NFTs and new publishing models. 

- We also discussed an idea he has to launch 'DAO News' - a publishing outlet that covers the people and politics of the emerging DAO space, something that aligns very well with the project. 
- This ties into some of the early thinking around Edgeware being a 'Governance' focused chain and connects with the sort of governance research that [@Junte](/edgeware/account/hX8eTbsDWPsEtoWBSjMBmRg3xXkhd2zxVAoBfGjhp57VPtm) has  been [mentioning recently](https://commonwealth.im/edgeware/proposal/discussion/971-edg-grant-for-governance-of-daos).
- You can view a [working document here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1kKdhrsULe3eYrOgyfIdeVE82VPp_SjrZ8LyoxAnppCU/edit#). 


From here we also discussed his experience and work developing conferences / events for protocols - something again that I think would be a worthwhile discussion and could also perhaps align with the DAO narrative.
