As we all noticing, NFTs - non fungible tokens are booming over the course of last months on Ethereum and other blockchain solutions are finding ways how to grab artist attention to their platforms. This post is not about art NFTs although they are "[Next Fucking Thing](https://twitter.com/NateAlexNFT/status/1358843787817193473?s=20)". I outlined the idea of putting those tokens (NFTs) into work.

[POAP](https://www.poap.xyz/) is Proof of Attendance Protocol that was built on Ethereum but now is running on xDai chain (because of high gas fees for token transfers, transaction costs). POAPs were being used on physical events where organisers were giving them away to attendees and people then could either compare their NFTs or just use as an ice breaker for talking about Ethereum conferences that they visited. As event has flipped into virtual space the POAP craze is still going on strongly. Event organisers are giving away POAPs to attendees of their events, hosting [virtual scavenger hunt](https://ethdenver.poap.xyz/ ) or giving them away as reward and motivation if people are [staking into ETH2.0](https://unvetica.com/ethonline/).

Another usability of these tokens that I find very interesting is that POAP NFT holders are allowed to take apart in governance process and vote on proposals see [https://poap.vote/](https://poap.vote/).

It means that people (community) is incentivised to attend events (in Edgeware it can be calls) in order to have a chance to make a change in the governance processes. I find this very interesting as that’s the way how to let people vote on proposals and be apart of the governance process.
