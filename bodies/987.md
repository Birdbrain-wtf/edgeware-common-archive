This thread is for the posting and collaboration on our every-other-week community calls. 

These calls feature discussion and ongoing updates from core development, working groups, and larger organizations in the space. Most of the time is dedicated to exploring governance and existing proposals. 

This thread will post agendas for upcoming calls and members can comment to add or discuss items and their inclusion. Ideally this is a collaborative process.


## Edgeware All-Community Call (EOW)
EOW, Wednesdays on the 2nd and 4th week of every month, ~1 hr long
