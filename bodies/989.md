**Who are we?**
To give you a bit of background we are an independent creative studio based in London making everything from award winning TV documentaries to music videos and social content for brands. 

Behind our work is a genuine passion for storytelling and a desire to share these stories in accessible and emotive ways. We do most of our work within the sphere of youth culture - especially music. 

As well as shooting live music performances and music videos we have always used music as an access point to broader stories - and in the last few years we have focused our unique skill set and imaginative storytelling on ideas around systems change and other ways to live, work and create.

We are recent members of the Edgeware community and also newcomers to the Crypto space, beyond a very basic understanding of tokens and blockchains. 

Over the last 4-6 weeks we have been on a real mission to understand the space and particularly where Edgeware fits and have conducted many research interviews with the community. These are ongoing so we aim to conduct more as part of this process.

For most people ‘blockchain’ really means Bitcoin and maybe Ethereum, which are seen as speculative assets first and then maybe complex technologies second. Other than names like Satoshi Nakamoto and maybe Vitalik Buterin, the people of blockchain rarely get a mention in the mainstream press.

Edgeware really resonated because community and people are at the heart. The idea of a funding and proposal system that is not tied to a single organisation or board of directors - that enables anyone, anywhere to present their own particular vision made real sense.

In many ways our journey of understanding has been vital to the development of the ideas that make up the brief - ultimately we need to connect outside of the existing community, so finding common language and visual metaphors is key. 

_‘What Will You Build?’_ Is a really powerful concept - and opens the door to some exciting opportunities when it comes to bringing things to life.

**Our process**
We are sharing a bit more of our process than we would ordinarily - normally showing our workings to a client is not the done thing, but with a community, it seems showing the process is a much more intuitive way to work. 

We actually prefer working in this way, and very much reflects how we run projects internally with all the different creatives who build our projects.

One of the things we really admire about the open source philosophy of the space is how collaborative it is, how it relies on a self starter mindset and sharing your work in public.

This approach has been exciting, and feels like a new dawn for collaborative work with teams from very different disciplines and area codes finding their home on Edgeware.

**1) Initial Thoughts**

Our key task is to create an introductory film for the website - and the community.  

We were aware from the off that there was a need to make the film appeal to various participants in the network - something which could appeal across the different groups who have made their home around the project but also (as we will discuss) something which could help recruit developers, creators, organisers and investors from beyond the Crypto bubble. 

We were introduced to the core concept - WHAT WILL YOU BUILD? 

Immediately this felt like it could be at the heart of our film. Both as a challenge to those developing in the digital space but also as a concept for real world use cases that also allow us to pull in broader concepts around the idea of ‘community’. 

With this video likely to be the first thing many people will see of the Edgeware project we also wanted to make sure our messaging is clear and simple. 

Having watched many other films for different blockchain projects we felt that often there was too much information being conveyed in a single film which often left us as viewers feeling slightly overwhelmed. 

The core principles which we identified as essential were:

1. **Explain and contextualise blockchain and its potential**
2. **Explaining select technical detail **
3. **Place it in a broader narrative/context**
4. **Simple instructions of how to get involved. **
5. **Deliver a differentiated positioning that connects.  **


To avoid making an overly long, or laboured film we feel there could be separate content strands to really go into detail on points 1 & 2 (more of this later). 

As new members to the community we felt there would be a better service to have a series of films which explain the basic Crypto/Blockchain terms/concepts and a series of Edgeware specific explainers and leave the main website film to really grab people’s interest and attention.

**3) Mood Film**

We also created a mood film to give a taster of our visual and narrative styles. We felt many of the current films in the space are overly corporate or can try too hard to be ‘cool’. 

We want to make something which authentically feels at the bleeding edge whilst remaining accessible and community (aka people) centric. 

To note: We’ve used some voices from the community in the film, but these were pinched from our research calls rather than specifically recorded for the film and are therefore representative of the conversational style we will employ rather than the exact wording that will appear in the finished film. .   

Both the narration and the footage are from our archive rather than being shot specifically but hopefully they illustrate the mood we are trying to create.
