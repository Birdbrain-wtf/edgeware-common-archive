Questions for Validators

1. What is your experience as a validator or node operator?

I'm an individual running validators for Polkadot (Alexander testnet), ChainX, and Harmony.

2. What's your technical setup? (cloud vs. on-premise; software stack; key management)

Google VPS with 8192 MB RAM 100 GB SSD and 4 CPU core.

3. Why are you interested in validating?

We knew Edgeware before it was a thing. We were excited about the vision of governance experimentation so it makes total sense that we want to be a part of the network infrastructure and security from day 1 and utilize our lockdrop reward in a productive way.
