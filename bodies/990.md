Smart contracts gain even more possibilities when they are merged with legal contracts. 
Hybrid contracts consist of both  distributed code and legalese. One example might be the start of a new venture with its own LLC and tokens. The smart contract address for the tokens, and the signed operating agreement, would together make a hybrid contract. 
As part of the LexBridge proposal, Commonwealth users will be able to issue and sign legal agreements; opening up the ecosystem to hybrid contracts.

The initial milestone will cover signing documents as an individual through the Polkadot browser extension. 
Most of this process is already sketched out: a PDF is generated clientside, and the hash of this is signed and stashed on a distributed ledger as proof of signing.  

I am seeking insight on how we might best sign statements using Edgeware; for both individuals and as groups. 

Following signing by individual accounts,  support for multisig wallets and DAOs to sign legal contracts will be the focus of the LexBridge proposal. Most multisig and DAO implementations support signing of statements or signaling votes; sufficient for legal agreements. 

Eventually, though, there will need to be some process by which smart and legal contracts, once signed,  kick off further events; from AWS to Ethereum to Edgeware, opening up a cascade of possibilities. Polkadot supports this bridge, in theory, but it will take the entire community to build it.
