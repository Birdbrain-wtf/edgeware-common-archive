**Why are NFTs such a big deal?**
- "A core insight is that NFTs are much better understood as encapsulating intellectual property rights to the assets they describe." 
- "If NFTs are liquid IP, then holders will eventually own the property’s cash flows. This fact makes non-fungible tokens a financial asset class."
- [All digital media is going on-chain](https://blog.coinfund.io/all-digital-content-is-going-on-chain-ae26a7071657) - Jake Brukh / Coinfund.


- "NFTs have the potential to invert the ownership model of media—offering creators, their audiences, and developers who build for them, a viable alternative to monetization."
- [NFTs make the internet ownable](https://variant.mirror.xyz/T8kdtZRIgy_srXB5B06L8vBqFHYlEBcv6ae2zR6Y_eo)- Jesse Walden, Variant.
